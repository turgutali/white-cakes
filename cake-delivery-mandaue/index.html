Cake Delivery in Mandaue

Across the Mandaue–Cebu bridge with the same three-day bench schedule.

Mandaue City orders share our Cebu production schedule: mixed for your date, crumb-coated, piped, and wrapped for travel. We do not accept next-day requests on a hand-finished cake.

Neighbourhoods and roads

We regularly deliver toward North Reclamation Area, U.N. Avenue, Subangdaku, Magellan, and subdivisions along the coastal highway toward Consolacion. Send the exact pin and a landmark visible from the road — not only a subdivision name.

Bridge and corridor traffic

Crossing between Cebu City and Mandaue moves in ranges. We quote a window when we confirm, accounting for bridge queues and port-area traffic. A bento travels more easily than a tall tier if the ride is long and hot.

Receiving the cake

A person must meet the driver at the gate or lobby. Industrial parks and villages with pass systems need the pass arranged before the driver arrives — note it in the order.

Lead time and ordering

At least three days before the date. Use the order form with delivery selected and a full Mandaue address, or message us on WhatsApp.

Industrial and residential drops

Factory gates, subdivisions with visitor slips, and coastal condos each need different notes. Write the name on the pass list if your village requires it; drivers cannot guess your phase and block.

Combining with Cebu City pickup

Some Mandaue customers collect in Cebu City when their day is tighter than ours. Say so in the order if you are flexible — we will reply with the option that protects the buttercream.

Reclamation, highway, and subdivisions

Mandaue is a short hop from the Cebu City kitchen and a different kind of address. North Reclamation warehouses, plants along the highway, and gated villages near Subangdaku and Magellan do not share one gate routine. A factory may need a visitor slip and a bay number. A subdivision may need the phase, the block, and the name already on the guard’s list. A condo on U.N. Avenue needs the tower and a person who can come down. Write the one that matches your pin.

A Mandaue window

We still quote a range. Late-afternoon queues on the Mandaue–Cebu corridors and around the port shift the drop, but the landmark we need is the gate you actually use, not the word “Mandaue” by itself. If the cake is for an office pantry, say whether reception can receive it or whether a named employee should meet it. Three days’ notice still applies before we mix. Resort gates and island bridges are not this page; those notes live with Lapu-Lapu delivery.
