Cake Delivery in Lapu-Lapu

Mactan deliveries with buffer for bridge traffic and resort gate checks.

Lapu-Lapu and Mactan Island sit across the bridges from our Cebu kitchen. We deliver when the date allows enough bench time and when you can receive the cake at a resort, village, or condo gate.

Common pins

Maribago, Pusok, Mactan Newtown, Punta Engaño approach roads, and barangays along the M.L. Quezon corridor. Resort deliveries need the guest name on the order notes so security can match the delivery.

Travel time

Bridge traffic is variable. We quote a window, not a minute-precise time. Bento cakes handle a longer ride more easily than a tall marble tier — mention your transport when you order.

Heat

Island afternoons are hot. Receive the cake into air-conditioning when you can, and read our journal note on buttercream in Cebu heat linked from the home page.

Tell us your date. We confirm within a day.
