What Is a Bento Cake? A Small-Cake Guide for Cebu

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 18 November 2026

Read this guide in Cebuano or Tagalog.

Quick answer: A Bento cake is a compact cake made for a small celebration. White Cakes’ Bento is 4 inches across and is estimated at two generous or three modest slices after a meal. The menu lists ₱449 through January 15, 2027, then ₱599 from January 16, 2027; confirm the current price and date availability when ordering.

“Bento cake” describes a small, boxed celebration cake. Size and decoration vary by bakery, so use the actual product details rather than assuming every Bento is identical. Here is what the Cebu menu currently means by Bento and how to decide whether it fits your occasion.

What comes with White Cakes’ 4-inch Bento?

The listed design is a round 4-inch cake finished with ivory buttercream, hand-piped shell details, a blush ribbon and one rose. Flavours offered by White Cakes include chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet; ask which flavours are available for the Bento on your date. The decoration does not determine the flavour or filling.

How many people does it serve?

Plan for about two generous servings or three modest slices after a meal. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed number of portions: appetite, slice size and other desserts affect how far it goes. It works best for two people, a small gift or a few shared bites. If everyone at a larger gathering should have a full slice, share the guest count and ask about a larger cake. Our Cebu cake-size guide explains what details to compare.

What does a Bento cost in Cebu?

The 4-inch Bento launch price is ₱449 through January 15, 2027 inclusive. The listed regular price of ₱599 begins January 16, 2027. The quote should confirm the cake, selected flavour, requested decoration and delivery or pickup arrangement. Ask before adding custom colours or details, because they may change the scope or price. Delivery charges depend on the address and are confirmed separately.

Will a message or custom design fit?

The top is small, so choose one main idea: a short greeting, a name, a simple motif or a limited colour palette. Send the exact spelling and punctuation in writing. If the phrase is long, decide whether the words or decoration matter more and ask what can fit before confirming. A reference image helps communicate one feature; it is not a promise of an exact copy. See the cake inscription guide for more wording tips.

How to order and arrange delivery

White Cakes asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes; the date is reserved only after the bakery confirms availability and the order details. Send the event date, flavour, exact message, design reference and whether you want arranged pickup or delivery. For delivery, include the full address, landmark, access instructions and receiver’s phone number. Coverage includes Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City; the exact delivery window and charge are confirmed for the address. Use the Bento product page or send an order enquiry to check your date.
