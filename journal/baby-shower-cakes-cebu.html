Baby Shower Cakes in Cebu: Colours, Sizes, and When to Order

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 18 November 2026

Quick answer: For a baby shower cake in Cebu, choose a size for the gathering, then send the invitation palette, a short message, flavour, date and delivery or pickup details. White Cakes makes hand-finished buttercream cakes with at least three days' notice. For a weekend party, ask earlier so the date can be confirmed.

Choose a cake size for the gathering

A 4-inch Bento Cake is a small option for a couple or as a gift for the parents-to-be; it is sized for two generous slices or three modest ones. For a family gathering, compare the 6-inch and 8-inch custom starting sizes, or ask about a two-tier design. Share the guest count and how the cake will be served before choosing. The custom cake page lists current sizes and starting prices, and the Cebu cake price guide explains how quotes work.

Plan the colours and message

Send a photo of the invitation, a colour palette or one reference cake. Soft pastels, a simple ivory design or colours that match the party are all useful starting points. You do not need to know the piping technique; tell us the look you want and we will confirm a hand-piped buttercream version for your date.

Keep the wording short enough to read clearly on the top. A family name, “Welcome Baby,” or the celebration date can be easier to place than a long sentence. Check spelling before confirming the order. If the parents are keeping the baby's name private, use a neutral message and share only the details you want on the cake.

Pick a flavour guests will enjoy

White Cakes offers chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. If you are choosing for a mixed group, tell us whether the guests have a favourite or whether you want a familiar option. Read the guide to cake flavours in Cebu for notes on choosing among the five. A 4-inch Bento is one flavour; ask us whether a larger cake can be divided before placing a deposit. Planning to reveal the baby's sex at the party? See our gender reveal cake guide for Cebu and confirm any coloured centre before booking.

Coordinate the delivery with the venue

Delivery is available in Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City. For a venue, send its full name, exact entrance, floor or hall, any gate or lobby instructions, the time someone can receive the cake and a reachable mobile number. We confirm a delivery window; we do not provide venue setup or cake cutting.

For a home party, arrange for someone to receive the cake and keep it cool until serving. Check our Metro Cebu cake delivery guide and send the complete delivery address when requesting a quote.

When should you order?

Three days' notice is the minimum for a made-to-order cake. Baby showers often happen on weekends, so message earlier while the date is still flexible. We confirm availability before you pay; a 50% deposit confirms an order. The cake ordering timeline explains what to send and why extra notice helps.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make a baby shower cake in the party colours?

Send the invitation colours or a reference. We can confirm a hand-piped buttercream design for the date and explain which details will work clearly on the cake.

How much is a baby shower cake in Cebu?

The 4-inch Bento has a published price. Custom 6-inch, 8-inch and two-tier cakes have starting prices; the final quote depends on the size and design. Delivery is quoted for the full address. See the current price guide.

Can you deliver a cake to a baby shower venue?

We deliver in Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu. Send the venue entrance, receiving contact and delivery time so we can confirm the route and window. We do not set up the cake at the venue.

How early should I order?

Three days is the minimum. For a weekend celebration, ask earlier to confirm the date before paying the deposit.
