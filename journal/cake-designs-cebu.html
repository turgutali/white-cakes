Cake Designs in Cebu: Marble, Bouquet and Bento Styles

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 18 November 2026

Quick answer: White Cakes makes three signature styles in Cebu: a 6-inch Signature Marble with dusty-blue marbling, a Bouquet Cake piped to look like flowers, and a 4-inch Bento with a blush ribbon and rose. All are finished in buttercream. Choose by occasion and guest count, then send the date, flavour, inscription, and delivery details for confirmation.

Compare the three signature cake designs

Signature Marble: a centrepiece for a shared table

Signature Marble is the 6-inch design for a birthday, family dinner, or celebration where the cake sits in the middle of the table. Its ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and single rose create a composed finish without a tall topper or printed image. See sizes and current starting prices on the custom cakes page.

Bouquet Cake: a flower-shaped gift

A Bouquet Cake uses hand-piped buttercream flowers, sage leaves, a pleated wrapper, and a bow. It is a gift and dessert together: the flowers are part of the cake, not a separate bouquet. Bouquet describes the finish rather than one fixed size, so send the guest count and ask for a quote on the Bouquet Cakes page.

Bento: a small cake for two or three

The 4-inch Bento is the compact option. It serves two generously or three modestly, and its blush ribbon, piped shells, and single rose suit a desk, date, or small celebration. Choose one flavour for this size. The Bento Cakes page has current pricing and ordering details.

Choosing a custom cake design

Start with the occasion and where the cake will be served. A table centrepiece, a gift delivered to someone else, and a cake for two call for different formats. For a larger guest list, tell us how many people will eat cake; the Cebu cake size guide lists the confirmed Bento portions and explains what we need before recommending a larger size.

Then choose a palette and a short inscription. One clear reference photo is more useful than a folder of mixed examples. Tell us which details matter most: colour, border, flower placement, or wording. The custom cake briefing guide shows what to send.

White Cakes works in hand-piped buttercream. We do not make fondant sculptures or printed edible sheets. If a reference relies on a character figure, edible photo, or a finish outside that range, ask before paying a deposit; we will confirm what can be adapted. Read the buttercream and fondant comparison before choosing a reference.

Flavour, price and delivery

The listed flavours are chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet. A flavour name does not guarantee a separate filling or topping; mention any specific expectation and wait for confirmation. Compare them in our Cebu cake flavour guide.

Prices depend on the selected design and size. Signature Marble and Bento have published starting prices; Bouquet Cakes are price on request. See the current cake price guide before you place an order.

Orders need at least three days' notice. Send the date, design, flavour, inscription, guest count, and whether you need arranged pickup or delivery. For delivery, include the complete address, a nearby landmark, and the name and number of the person receiving the cake. White Cakes delivers in Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu; check the Metro Cebu delivery guide for hand-off details.

Cake design questions

Which cake design works for two or three people?

The 4-inch Bento is the confirmed small format: it serves two generously or three modestly. For a larger guest list, send the count so we can recommend a size.

Can I send a photo of a cake design I like?

Send one clear reference, the date, colour palette, flavour, and wording. We work in hand-piped buttercream; fondant figures and printed sheets are outside our range. We confirm a custom design before payment.

How early should I order a cake in Cebu?

Orders need at least three days' notice. Weekends, holidays, and detailed custom designs may need more lead time, so message earlier when your date is fixed.

Where can you deliver?

Delivery is available in Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu. Send the complete address and a receiver's contact number so we can confirm the route and hand-off.
