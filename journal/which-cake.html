Marble, bouquet, or bento

Three cakes, three kinds of day. The right choice is about the table and who is eating, not about height alone.

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 14 November 2026

We keep three designs on the bench so an order can start without a long brief. Signature Marble, Bouquet Cake, and Bento. All three are ivory buttercream, finished by hand, and made for a date you name at least three days ahead. They are not three sizes of the same cake. If none of them is the shape you need, order Custom and tell us what the day actually is.

Signature Marble

The Signature Marble is a 6-inch cake. The side is ivory cream with dusty-blue marble. A shell border runs around the top, and a single rose sits with a few sage leaves. This is the cake that belongs in the middle of a table: a birthday, a family dinner, a small party where several people will be served from one centerpiece. There is room on top for a name or a short line. Order it when the cake itself is the event, and when you want guests to see one cake rather than a cluster of flowers.

Do not order a 6-inch marble for two people who will be embarrassed by the leftovers, and do not ask it to feed a hall. It is a small table, not a wedding. If you need it larger, say 8 inches in the order and we will confirm whether that date can take it. The 6-inch is the one we mean when we say Signature Marble.

Bouquet Cake

The Bouquet Cake is lower, and the top is piped roses with sage leaves rather than a field of marble. It reads as flowers you can cut. Order it when the cake is a gift, an anniversary, or a lunch where the look should be soft and the height should stay modest. It is the one to send across Cebu to someone else, with a line on the top and their name in the notes. Tell us the size of the table if you know it. We confirm the scale when we reply, because “bouquet” describes the finish, not a single diameter.

Choose bouquet over marble when the person would rather be given flowers than a birthday cake with candles. Choose marble over bouquet when children are waiting to see their name on a cake that looks like a cake.

Bento

The Bento is 4 inches. Shells, a blush ribbon, one rose. It is for two plates, a desk, a Tuesday that still deserves a cake, or a gift you will carry yourself through traffic. It is the easiest of the three to take home in an ordinary car, and the one we will point you to if you mention a motorcycle. It is not a miniature of the Signature meant to be stretched. If the guest list is a family, step up to the 6-inch.

If you are between them

A birthday with a table: Signature Marble, 6 inches. Two people, or a small private celebration: Bento, 4 inches. A gift, especially one we deliver: Bouquet. Anything that needs a different color, a different flower, or words that will not fit the top of a bento: Custom, with a photo if you have one. You can open the order form with the cake already chosen. Change the flavor there. The design is only the starting point, and we would rather you switch it before we bake than live with the wrong cake on the day.
