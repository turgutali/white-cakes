Cebu Cake Calendar 2027: Key Dates and Ordering Guide

A practical calendar for planning cake orders around Cebu celebrations, official holiday dates and the hand-off.

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Quick answer: Plan upcoming Cebu cake dates around Christmas 2026, New Year’s Day (Friday, 1 January 2027), Sinulog’s listed Grand Parade (Sunday, 17 January), Chinese New Year (Saturday, 6 February), Valentine’s Day (Sunday, 14 February) and Mother’s Day (Sunday, 9 May). White Cakes needs at least three days’ notice; ask earlier for holidays and wait for date and delivery confirmation before paying a deposit.

Holiday calendars help you choose a date, but they do not reserve a cake or guarantee a delivery slot. This page brings the next known dates together with the details our Cebu kitchen needs to confirm an order. School graduations, office parties and family gatherings follow their own schedules, so check the venue or organiser before choosing a cake date.

Upcoming cake occasions in Cebu

Christmas and New Year 2026–2027

Christmas Day 2026 is Friday, 25 December. New Year’s Eve falls on Thursday, 31 December, with New Year’s Day on Friday, 1 January 2027. If the cake is for Media Noche, tell us whether you need it before midnight on New Year’s Eve or during New Year’s Day; those are different hand-off plans. See the Christmas cake guide for the current seasonal offer and holiday delivery details. For the following week, read our New Year cake guide for Media Noche.

Sinulog 2027 — Sunday, 17 January

The Sinulog Foundation lists the 2027 Grand Parade for Sunday, 17 January at Cebu City Sports Center. The full activity schedule and route maps may change; check the official Sinulog Foundation page and current road advisories before planning a venue delivery. Our Sinulog cake guide explains what to send when asking about a festival-week cake.

Chinese New Year — Saturday, 6 February

The Philippine government’s 2027 holiday proclamation lists Chinese New Year on Saturday, 6 February. If your family or office is gathering, send the exact serving date, guest count and venue. A holiday listing is not an indication that our kitchen or a delivery route has an open slot; we confirm each request. The date appears in Proclamation No. 1427 in the Supreme Court E-Library.

Valentine’s Day — Sunday, 14 February

For a couple’s celebration, the 4-inch Bento is sized for two generous or three modest servings after a meal. A Bouquet Cake has hand-piped buttercream flowers; it is a cake, not a fresh-flower bouquet. See the Valentine’s cake guide for design, inscription and delivery planning.

Mother’s Day — Sunday, 9 May

Mother’s Day in 2027 falls on Sunday, 9 May. Decide who will receive the cake and where they can keep it cool before you send the order details. Our Mother’s Day cake guide compares a Bouquet Cake, Bento and custom cake for Mom or Lola.

Graduations, birthdays and family milestones

Graduation ceremonies are set by each school, and birthday dates are personal. Confirm the actual event date, venue access and serving time first. Then use the graduation cake guide, kids’ birthday guide or debut cake guide to plan the design and size.

Choose a cake for the group and occasion

Start with how many people will actually share the cake. A 4-inch Bento suits two generous servings or three modest servings after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches and is the usual choice for a family table. For larger groups, ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake and include the guest count; slice size and other desserts affect how much you need. Our Cebu cake-size guide explains the current size estimates.

Choose from the listed flavours — chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet — and say if you need a particular filling confirmed. White Cakes finishes cakes by hand in buttercream. Send one clear design reference and point out the detail you like; printed edible image sheets and fondant sculptures are not available. Check the custom cake briefing guide if you are still collecting ideas.

Order timing and delivery checklist

Three days’ notice is the minimum, not a promise that a date is still open. For a public holiday, festival, weekend or detailed design, message sooner. We reply within 24 hours with availability, a question or a quote. A 50% deposit confirms the order after the date, design, price and delivery plan are agreed.

Delivery is arranged across Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City. Send a complete address, barangay, landmark, gate or lobby instructions, receiver’s name and mobile number. Holiday road access and the delivery window depend on the actual destination. We confirm a workable window rather than an exact arrival minute.

  1. Exact date and when the cake will be served.
  2. Occasion, guest count, cake size and flavour.
  3. Short inscription with names and spelling checked.
  4. One design reference and the details that matter most.
  5. Full delivery address, landmark and receiving contact, or a pickup request.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I order a cake for a 2027 holiday in Cebu?

Three days is the minimum notice for a cake, not a guarantee that a holiday date is available. Message earlier with the date, address and design so we can confirm before you pay a deposit.

Can you deliver cakes on holidays in Cebu?

Delivery is available in Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City by arrangement. Holiday availability, route access, delivery charge and a workable time window must be confirmed for the full address.

What cake sizes can I order for a holiday gathering?

A 4-inch Bento suits two generous or three modest servings after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches. Ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake for a larger gathering and include the guest count.

What information should I send with a holiday cake enquiry?

Send the exact date and serving time, cake or flavour, guest count, inscription, one design reference, full delivery address, landmark and receiver’s mobile number.
