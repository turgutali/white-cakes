Sinulog 2027 Cakes in Cebu: Date, Ordering and Delivery Guide

Plan a hand-piped cake for the celebration, venue and delivery window you actually have.

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 18 November 2026 · Updated 19 November 2026

Quick answer: Sinulog Foundation lists the 2027 Grand Parade for Sunday, January 17, at Cebu City Sports Center. Its full activity schedule and route maps are still marked “Coming Soon.” For a cake, send your event date, guest count, venue and one design reference early. White Cakes needs at least three days’ notice and must confirm event-date delivery against the actual address and road access.

Sinulog is a season of family gatherings, office celebrations and events across Cebu. A cake order has to fit the gathering and the hand-off, not just the festival colours. Our Cebu kitchen makes cakes to order, so the most helpful first message includes the date, guest count, venue and a clear design direction.

Start with the event date and venue

The Sinulog Foundation currently lists the Grand Parade for Sunday, January 17, 2027, at Cebu City Sports Center. The full 2027 activity schedule, route maps and parade participants are still marked “Coming Soon” on its official page. Check it and current road advisories close to your date; do not assume every Sinulog celebration falls on Grand Parade day. A home gathering before a parade and a party after an event need different cake hand-offs.

Tell us when the cake will be served, when someone can receive it, and whether the address is a home, office, hotel or event venue. During busy festival days, roads and access arrangements can change. We agree on a delivery window rather than an exact minute, and we need a complete address, a landmark and a receiving contact to confirm whether the route is workable.

Choose a cake size for the people sharing it

A 4-inch Bento is a small cake for two generous servings or three modest servings after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches and is the usual choice for a family table. For a larger group, ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake. We do not list a fixed serving count for those sizes: the slice size and the rest of the food affect what the cake needs to cover. Include the number of adults and children and whether there will be other desserts.

For current starting prices and what changes a custom quote, see the Cebu cake price guide. Delivery is quoted separately for the full address.

Send a design brief that can be piped

Pick one reference photo and say what matters most: perhaps the palette, a short greeting, or one simple motif. A few clear details are more useful than a long folder of unrelated images. Tell us if the cake is for a family table, office celebration or event photo, because the layout and inscription space need to make sense in that setting.

Our cakes are finished by hand in buttercream. We do not make fondant sculptures or printed edible image sheets. If your idea depends on a sculpted figure or a printed design, it will not fit our finish. Send it anyway if you are open to a simpler piped interpretation; we review the reference and confirm what we can make before you place a deposit.

Pick the flavour and inscription

Choose from chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Tell us the flavour in the first message; if you want a different filling from the cake, ask whether it is available for the date. Keep a cake-top line short enough to read in a photo. Send names, spellings and numbers exactly as they should appear and double-check them before paying.

Give the cake enough lead time

Three days before the date is the minimum notice. Festival week and weekends can fill earlier, so check availability as soon as you know the celebration date. We reply within 24 hours with availability, a question or a quote. A 50% deposit confirms an order after the design, price and delivery details are agreed.

Use the order form or WhatsApp with the event date, guest count, flavour, reference, inscription, full address and receiver's mobile number. Our Cebu cake lead-time guide explains why three days is the minimum.

Keep buttercream cool after delivery

Arrange a cool place for the cake and keep it out of direct sun. If it will be served later, ask where it can be refrigerated and who will move it safely. Do not leave it in a parked car while guests attend an event. Read the buttercream and Cebu heat guide for practical carrying and storage advice.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make a Sinulog-themed cake in Cebu?

Send one clear reference, the colours and the details you want. We hand-pipe buttercream and confirm which parts of a custom design can be made. We do not make fondant sculptures or printed edible image sheets.

What size cake should I order for a Sinulog gathering?

A 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches for a family table. Ask us about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake for a larger gathering and share the guest count; we confirm the right size rather than promise a fixed number of servings.

How early should I order a Sinulog cake?

Three days before the event is the minimum notice. Ask earlier for festival week and weekends so we can confirm the date and delivery plan before you pay the deposit.

Can you deliver during Sinulog events?

We deliver to Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City, but the route and time window depend on the full address and current road access. Send the venue, landmark and receiver contact early. We confirm a delivery window, not an exact minute.

When is the Sinulog 2027 Grand Parade?

Sinulog Foundation lists the Grand Parade for Sunday, January 17, 2027, at Cebu City Sports Center. The full activity schedule and route maps are still marked “Coming Soon.” Check the official Foundation page and current road advisories for the activity and delivery route you plan to use.
