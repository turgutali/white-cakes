Affordable Bento Cake in Cebu: What ₱449 Includes

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Read this guide in: English · Cebuano · Tagalog

Quick answer: The current 4-inch Bento launch price is ₱449 through January 15, 2027; delivery and requested design changes are quoted separately, and three days’ notice is required.

If you are comparing affordable bento cakes in Cebu, start with the guest count and the final cost, not only the headline price. The White Cakes 4-inch Bento is a small, made-to-order buttercream cake for two generous or three modest slices after a meal. Here is what the current price covers and what to confirm before you order.

Current Bento price

The listed launch price is ₱449 through January 15, 2027. The regular price is ₱599 from January 16, 2027. It is the lowest-priced cake on the current menu. The price is for the listed 4-inch style; design changes and delivery are quoted separately. Ask us to confirm the current total for your date and address before paying.

Think about the price per serving

At ₱449, the cake works out to ₱224.50 for each of two generous servings, or about ₱149.67 for each of three modest servings after a meal. These figures divide the cake price only; they do not include separately quoted delivery or requested design changes. The 4-inch Bento is not intended to serve a large family table.

What comes with the listed style

The listed Bento has ivory buttercream, hand-piped shell details, a blush ribbon, and one rose. Choose from the menu’s chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet flavours. Send the inscription exactly as it should be written; a long line may not fit the small top, so we confirm the text with you.

Know what is and is not included

Delivery is quoted for the complete address and is not included in the displayed cake price. A design change may also affect the final quote. A 50% deposit confirms the order after the date and details are agreed. White Cakes does not keep ready-made Bento cakes and needs at least three days’ notice; same-day and next-day requests are not available.

When to choose a different size

If more than three people need a proper slice, compare the 6-inch and 8-inch custom options instead of stretching the Bento. Our Cebu cake-size guide explains the planning estimates. For current sizes and starting prices, read the Cebu cake-price guide or visit the Bento cake page.

How to place the order

Send the date, flavour, exact inscription, pickup or full delivery address, and a reachable receiver’s number. We confirm availability and the total before the deposit. Use the order form or message us on WhatsApp.
