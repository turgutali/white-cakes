Welcome Cake for a New Colleague in Cebu: Office Planning Guide

White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Read this guide in: English · Cebuano · Tagalog

Quick answer: Choose a serving time that fits the team’s workday, confirm how many colleagues will eat cake and keep the welcome message short enough to read at a glance.

A cake can mark a new colleague’s first day, first week or a team welcome gathering. Ask the person coordinating the event which timing feels comfortable; a welcome does not need to interrupt onboarding or a busy shift. White Cakes makes custom buttercream cakes to order in Lahug, with delivery across Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu by arrangement.

Choose a time that works for the team

Check the new colleague’s schedule and the team’s break or meeting time. Confirm who will meet the delivery at the office entrance, lobby or approved receiving point. In an office tower or coworking space, ask the building contact about rider access before choosing a delivery window. The office cake guide covers the order details to send.

Estimate how many people will have a slice

Count the team members who expect to eat cake, not everyone in the building. Decide whether other snacks or desserts will be served. The 4-inch Bento is estimated at two generous or three modest portions; it will not suit every group. Compare sizes in the Cebu cake-size guide and confirm the serving estimate with the bakery.

Make the greeting personal but work-appropriate

Use the colleague’s preferred name and keep the line easy to read. Examples include “Welcome to the Team, [Name],” “Glad You’re Here,” or “A Sweet Start, [Name]!” Confirm the person is comfortable being named before placing their name on a cake. For a whole department, use the team name or a general welcome instead.

Confirm flavour and any ingredient questions

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask the team coordinator whether a particular flavour is preferred and whether anyone has an ingredient question. Do not promise that a cake is free from an allergen; ask the bakery for current ingredient information before confirming if this matters to a guest.

Send one complete office order

Share the date, serving time, number of servings, flavour, exact inscription, full office address, building entry instructions and name and number of the receiver.
