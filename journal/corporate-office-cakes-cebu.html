Cebu runs on offices. There are BPO towers at Cebu IT Park, headquarters in Cebu Business Park, warehouses and showrooms in Mandaue, and factories in the Mactan Economic Zone. All of them celebrate: birthdays every month, promotions, anniversaries, Christmas parties, client visits. This guide is for the admin, HR lead or team captain who has been asked to “handle the cake.”

What do offices usually order: one big cake or several small ones?

There are two common approaches, and the right one depends on how your team actually gathers.

One shared cake. For a team huddle around a pantry table or a boardroom celebration, a 6-inch Signature Marble or a Bouquet Cake makes a clear centrepiece. Ask us about a larger size if your team is big. We confirm what the bench can do for your date.

Several bento cakes. For hybrid teams, night-shift and day-shift splits, or client gifts, 4-inch bento cakes work better. Each person gets a finished cake with their name, and nobody fights over the last slice at 3 a.m. Each bento is piped separately, so send a list with names and flavours if they differ.

Flavours for both are chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Chocolate is the safest for a mixed office. Ube makes the Filipino teammates happy.

Can you put our company logo on a cake?

We don’t print logos. We pipe in buttercream and don’t use edible photo sheets. What we can do is take your brand colours as the palette for a custom cake, and pipe a short line like the company name, “Happy 5th Anniversary, Team” or “Welcome to Cebu.” Send reference images and we will tell you honestly what translates to buttercream. What should you include on a corporate cake order?

A complete first message saves a day of back-and-forth:

  1. Company name and the name of the person ordering
  2. Contact mobile number (one that is answered during the delivery window)
  3. Delivery address: tower, floor, unit, street and city, plus a landmark
  4. Lobby or gate instructions: visitor pass, loading bay, which entrance
  5. Receiving person: name and number of whoever will come down
  6. Date and time range, at least three days ahead
  7. Quantity, cake type and flavours, with a list of names if each cake is personalised
  8. Words for each cake, spelled exactly

Important: security guards usually can’t accept or hold a cake for you, and buttercream shouldn’t sit in a warm guardhouse. Have someone ready to receive it.

How much lead time and what about billing?

Lead time: at least three days per order, even for many bentos, because every cake is piped by hand. For monthly team birthdays, send all the dates for the month in one message. For Christmas parties in December, message us as soon as the date is set, since December fills quickly.

Billing: bento cakes are ₱449 each until January 15, 2027 (regular price ₱599). Other launch prices are on the menu. Bouquet cakes are price on request. A 50% deposit confirms the order. If your procurement team needs a formal quotation, ask on WhatsApp.

Are cakes good client gifts in Cebu?

They can be, if they travel well. A bento cake is easy to hand over at a client lunch. A Bouquet Cake works for a client anniversary or a thank-you to a partner company, delivered with their name on top. For gifts that go to a client’s home or office, give us the recipient’s details and mark it a surprise. We text the window to you, not to them.

For Christmas client gifting, start early. A dozen hand-piped bentos for the same December week needs real bench time.

Five Office Cake Ordering Mistakes to Avoid in Cebu

Office celebrations involve more people and logistics than a home cake order. These five avoidable gaps can turn a straightforward cake into a stressful event-day task.

1. Using the company headcount as the serving count. The number of employees on the roster may include people who work remotely, are on another shift, or will not attend. Estimate who will actually be there when cake is served, then ask the bakery to match that number to current size and portion guidance.

2. Sending a logo without explaining the design goal. A logo file does not tell the decorator whether you want the exact mark, a colour cue, or a general corporate feel. White Cakes uses hand-piped buttercream and does not list printed edible logo sheets in its current information. Explain the priority and ask what adaptation is possible before you confirm.

3. Leaving the inscription and spelling until the last message. A short, approved message is easier to fit and less likely to be copied incorrectly. Send final wording in writing, including capitalization, names, and punctuation. Ask for it to appear in the confirmation.

4. Treating the delivery address as enough. Office towers may have a delivery entrance, visitor pass, lobby rule, or receiving contact. Provide the full address, landmark, floor or handoff point, recipient’s name, mobile number, and building instructions. Confirm someone can receive the cake during the delivery window.

5. Paying before the full order is clear. Confirm date, cake and flavour, design direction, message, complete price, pickup or delivery details, and payment terms in writing. The current White Cakes ordering information states a 50% deposit; check the quote for the applicable amount and method.
