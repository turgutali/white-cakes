Cake Sizes in Cebu: 4-Inch, 6-Inch, 8-Inch and Two-Tier Guide

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 18 November 2026

Quick answer: As a planning estimate, a 6-inch round cake gives about 8–12 party slices, an 8-inch about 16–24, and a common 6-inch plus 8-inch two-tier cake about 30–36. The 4-inch Bento is sized for two generous or three modest slices after a meal. These are estimates, not guaranteed portions: cake height, design, and slice size change the count.

Searching for a cake size in Cebu usually means trying to solve two things at once: how much cake the table needs and which design fits the occasion. Diameter helps you compare options, but it does not tell the whole story. Slice size, whether dessert is served after a meal, and the finished design all affect the plan.

How many people does each cake serve?

Use these as starting estimates for party-size slices: around 8–12 from a 6-inch round, 16–24 from an 8-inch round, and 30–36 from a common 6-inch plus 8-inch two-tier cake. The 4-inch Bento has its own smaller serving plan: two generous slices or three modest slices after a meal. The estimates assume a typical round cake and ordinary celebration cuts; taller cakes can be cut differently, and a wedding-size slice is smaller than a dessert slice.

These ranges help with early planning, but they are not a fixed guarantee for every White Cakes design. Height, decoration, and the way the cake is cut affect the total. Choose a size by the occasion

Two people or a small gift

The 4-inch Bento has a clear serving plan and a compact hand-piped finish. It can carry a short inscription and is easy to bring to a small celebration. If you need a cake for more than its stated portions, compare a 6-inch cake instead of assuming the Bento can stretch to a larger table.

A family birthday table

The 6-inch Signature Marble is the named design: ivory buttercream with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border and one rose. Custom cakes are also available at 6 inches. A larger gathering or a tiered look

The menu lists 8-inch and two-tier custom cakes. A two-tier format changes the look and dimensions, so share the reference and the place where the cake will be displayed. What to send for a size recommendation

  1. Your date and the number of guests expected to eat cake.
  2. Whether there will be another dessert and the portion size you have in mind.
  3. Your preferred format: 6-inch, 8-inch, or two-tier; for Bento, note its stated portions.
  4. Your flavour, one design reference, and the details you want piped on top.
  5. Pickup or delivery, plus the full address and a landmark if delivery is needed.
