Cake Flavours in Cebu: Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango & Red Velvet

Choose from five cake flavours in Cebu: chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Find a flavour for your guests, celebration and serving plan.

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 14 November 2026

The cakes on this site are finished in ivory buttercream. Marble, roses, and shells sit on that cream. Choose from five listed cake flavours: chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet. The flavour name does not specify a separate filling or fruit topping; tell us what you have in mind and we will confirm the details for your order. How to choose from the Cebu cake flavours

Start with what the people at your table already enjoy, then send the exact flavour name with your order. A menu flavour tells you the listed cake flavour; it does not automatically include a separate filling, frosting, fruit pieces, or topping. If one of those details matters, name it in your request and wait for confirmation before paying a deposit.

  1. Choose one of the five listed flavours.
  2. Tell us the guest count and cake size so we can confirm whether a second flavour is possible.
  3. Write down any specific filling, fruit or outside decoration you expect; these need confirmation and are not promised by the flavour name alone.
  4. Send the date and pickup or full delivery details for an availability check and quote.

If you are still deciding, compare the listed flavours with the preferences of the people who will eat the cake. Our Cebu cake size guide explains the confirmed Bento portions and what to share for a larger serving recommendation; the cake price guide covers current starting prices.
