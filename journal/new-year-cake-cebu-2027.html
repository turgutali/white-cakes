New Year Cake in Cebu: Planning for Media Noche 2026–2027

Plan a cake for Media Noche around the serving time, people at the table and a delivery slot we can confirm.

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Quick answer: Media Noche is on the evening of Thursday, 31 December 2026, ahead of New Year’s Day on Friday, 1 January 2027. White Cakes needs at least three days’ notice; for a December 31 cake, message by December 28 at the latest, and earlier for a holiday date. Delivery is only confirmed after we check the full address and route.

New Year’s Day is listed as a regular holiday in the Philippine government’s 2027 holiday proclamation. For cake planning, the more useful detail is when Media Noche will actually be served and when someone can receive the box. We bake to order in Cebu, so give us the occasion date and hand-off plan before choosing the finish.

Choose the cake size for your Media Noche table

For two people sharing dessert after dinner, the 4-inch Bento is sized for two generous or three modest servings. Signature Marble is 6 inches and is the usual choice for a family table. If more guests will have cake, ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake. Tell us the number of adults and children and whether there will be other desserts; slice size changes how much cake a group needs. Our cake size guide for Cebu shows the size estimates.

For a larger family gathering, use the guest count to plan before you settle on a design. If the cake is mainly for a photo and a small ceremonial slice, say so too. We will recommend a size based on the gathering, but will not promise a fixed number of slices for larger custom cakes.

Pick a flavour and describe the design

The listed flavours are chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Send the flavour you want with your first enquiry. If you prefer a filling or detail that is not shown on the menu, ask us to confirm it rather than assume it is included.

White Cakes finishes cakes by hand in buttercream. Send one clear reference photo and name the details that matter — for example, a colour, a short greeting or one simple motif. We review the idea and confirm which parts can be piped for your date. Printed edible image sheets and fondant sculptures are not available. For current options, see the custom cakes page and our guide to briefing a custom cake.

Choose a short New Year cake message

A cake-top inscription is easiest to read when it is brief. These lines suit a family table or a workplace gathering:

  • Happy New Year 2027
  • Welcome, 2027!
  • Our First Media Noche
  • Cheers to New Beginnings
  • Love, Peace & Cake
  • New Year, Same Sweet Family
  • Best Wishes from Team [Name]
  • Here’s to Us in 2027

Send the exact wording and spelling in your enquiry. For more ideas for other celebrations, see our birthday cake message guide.

When to order a New Year cake in Cebu

Three days before the event is the minimum notice. For a cake needed on December 31, that means sending an enquiry by December 28 at the latest. It does not guarantee there is still an open slot; holiday dates and detailed piping can fill earlier. Message us as soon as your plan is set so we can confirm availability, design, quote and delivery before you pay.

We reply within 24 hours. A 50% deposit confirms the order after the date, design, price and delivery details are agreed. Check the Cebu cake price guide for current starting prices and quote terms; the final custom quote depends on the design.

Plan the December 31 hand-off

For delivery, send the complete address, barangay, landmark, gate or lobby instructions and the name and mobile number of the person receiving the cake. We arrange delivery in Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City. The route, charge and workable time window depend on the full address and holiday access; we confirm a delivery window, not an exact arrival minute. A delivery area listing does not mean a particular New Year slot is open.

If you want the cake for midnight, tell us whether it must arrive well before the gathering or if another person can receive it earlier. We do not promise midnight or same-day delivery. Keep the boxed cake cool after hand-off, out of direct sun and away from a parked car. See our buttercream care guide for Cebu heat.

Send this information in one message:

  1. December 31 or January 1, plus the planned serving time.
  2. Guest count, cake size, flavour and short inscription.
  3. One reference photo and the design details you want us to focus on.
  4. Full delivery address, landmark, access instructions and receiver contact, or a pickup request.

Use the order form or message us on WhatsApp. You can also check the Cebu cake calendar for 2027 for the next holiday and festival dates.

Frequently asked questions

When should I order a New Year cake in Cebu?

For a cake needed on December 31, the three-day minimum means enquiring by December 28. That is a minimum, not a guarantee of an open holiday slot. Message earlier so we can confirm the date and hand-off.

Can you deliver a cake on New Year’s Eve?

Delivery is arranged across Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City. New Year’s Eve availability, route access, the delivery fee and a workable time window must be confirmed for the full address before you pay.

What size cake works for Media Noche?

A 4-inch Bento suits two generous or three modest servings after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches and is the usual choice for a family table. Ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake for a larger group and share the guest count.

Which flavours can I choose?

The listed flavours are chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Tell us your choice in the enquiry. If you want a particular filling or a change from the usual cake, ask us to confirm whether it is available for your date.

What should I write on a New Year cake?

Keep the inscription short so it is easy to read on top. “Happy New Year 2027,” “Welcome, 2027!” or “Our First Media Noche” are simple options. Send the exact wording and spelling in your enquiry.
