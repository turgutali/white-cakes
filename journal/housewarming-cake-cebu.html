Housewarming Cake in Cebu: A Sweet Welcome to a New Home

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Quick answer: Match a housewarming cake to the number of people who will share it, then send the new address, barangay, landmark and a receiver’s mobile number. White Cakes offers 4-inch Bento and custom sizes; orders need at least three days’ notice for Cebu City, Mandaue or Lapu-Lapu delivery.

A housewarming can be a quiet first meal in a new condo or a larger family visit. Before choosing a design, decide when the cake will be served and how many guests will actually have a slice. That helps you choose between a small gift cake and a cake for the table.

Pick a size that fits your guest list

The 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly after a meal and can work as a small congratulations gift. For a family gathering, compare the 6-inch and 8-inch custom cakes using the Cebu cake size guide. The number of slices depends on the cut and design, so tell us the guest count and whether there will be other desserts.

A hand-piped buttercream finish can be kept simple: a short “home sweet home” line, a name, or colours that match the new space. Send one reference image and the words exactly as you want them written. We make the listed flavours chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet; ask first if a particular filling or topping is essential.

Make the new address easy to find

For delivery, include the complete address, barangay, building or subdivision, tower or unit where applicable, a nearby landmark and the recipient’s mobile number. Check whether security needs a resident name, visitor pass or lobby hand-off. A driver needs a person who can receive the cake; buttercream should not be left in a warm lobby or vehicle.

We deliver in Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu. The delivery fee and window depend on the exact address. We agree a time range rather than promising a precise minute. Read how cake delivery works across Metro Cebu and our notes on keeping buttercream cool.

Ordering checklist for a housewarming

  1. Choose the date and the time you expect to cut the cake.
  2. Estimate how many people will eat cake and choose a size to discuss.
  3. Send flavour, inscription and one design reference.
  4. Confirm the new address, building access and receiver.

Orders require at least three days’ notice. We confirm the design, availability and final price before a 50% deposit confirms the order. Pickup is arranged after confirmation; see the custom cake collection or send the details through the order form.

Frequently asked questions

What cake size works for a housewarming?

It depends on the number of people who will eat cake. The 4-inch Bento suits two generous or three modest slices; for a larger group, discuss the 6-inch or 8-inch options and serving plan.

Can you deliver to a condo or subdivision?

Delivery is available in Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu. Send the full address, building or subdivision details, access instructions, landmark and receiver’s number so we can confirm the window and charge.

Can you add a “home sweet home” message?

Send the exact inscription with your request. A short line is easiest to read on a small cake; we confirm what fits the selected design.

How early should I place a housewarming cake order?

At least three days before your date. Message earlier for a busy weekend or a more detailed custom design.
