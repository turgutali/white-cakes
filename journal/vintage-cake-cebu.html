What does “vintage cake” mean?

Vintage-inspired cakes often use piped buttercream borders, repeated shells or swags, small rosettes, ruffles and a short message. Some designs are deliberately dense; others use one ornate edge on a smoother cake. A reference photo helps because two people can use “vintage” to mean completely different shapes and finishes.

When you message, point out the parts you want us to keep: for example, the piped border, two-colour palette or short lettering. Also mention anything you do not need. A focused brief makes it easier to confirm which details fit the cake size and the time available.

How to request a vintage-inspired design

  1. Send one main reference. Tell us which detail matters most: the border, shape, colours or lettering.
  2. Name the date and guest count. This helps us check availability and whether the listed 6-inch size suits your table. See the cake size guide for serving estimates.
  3. Choose a flavour and exact message. Include correct spelling and capitalisation; short wording leaves room for piping.
  4. Share the delivery plan. Give the full address, barangay, landmark and a mobile number for the person receiving the cake.
  5. Wait for confirmation. We confirm the date and design before the 50% deposit secures an order.

White Cakes decorates with buttercream by hand. Edible print sheets and fondant sculptures are not offered. If a reference depends on a printed portrait or a sculpted character, send it anyway and we can tell you whether there is a simpler piped detail that fits the request.

Lead time and delivery in Cebu

Orders need at least three days' notice. That is a minimum, not a promise that every date remains open. Ask earlier for weekends, Christmas, New Year and detailed piping. White Cakes delivers in Cebu City, Mandaue City and Lapu-Lapu City by arrangement; the delivery fee and time window depend on the address. Someone should be present to receive the cake.
