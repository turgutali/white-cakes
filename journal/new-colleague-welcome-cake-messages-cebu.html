What to Write on a Welcome Cake for a New Colleague: Short Ideas

White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Read this guide in: English · Cebuano · Tagalog

Quick answer: Use the new colleague’s preferred name, choose a friendly line that fits your workplace and get their name checked by the team coordinator.

A welcome cake is usually shared during a break, team meeting or first-week gathering. Keep the wording positive and comfortable for a new person who may not yet know everyone. These short ideas can be adapted to the tone of your office.

Simple welcome messages

  1. Welcome to the Team, [Name]!
  2. Glad You’re Here, [Name]
  3. Welcome Aboard
  4. A Sweet Start to Your First Week
  5. Here’s to Great Work Together
  6. Happy First Day, [Name]!
  7. Great to Have You with Us
  8. New Team, New Adventures

Cebuano and Tagalog options

  • Cebuano: Maayong Pag-abot sa Team, [Ngalan]!
  • Cebuano: Nalipay Mi nga Kauban Ka
  • Cebuano: Welcome sa Atong Team
  • Tagalog: Maligayang Pagdating sa Team, [Name]!
  • Tagalog: Masaya Kaming Kasama Ka
  • Tagalog: Welcome sa Ating Team
  • Tagalog: Magandang Simula, [Name]!

Keep the greeting thoughtful

Confirm whether the colleague wants their name on a shared cake; use a team or general welcome if you are unsure. Avoid jokes about job title, age, personal life or workload unless you know the person welcomes them. Keep the line short so it remains legible with hand-piped buttercream.
