How to order a custom cake in Cebu

What to send, why the date has to be three days out, and how pickup differs from a delivery across the city.

A cake from White Cakes starts as a message, not a visit to a counter. We bake in Cebu, to order, and we finish every cake by hand. That takes time, so the useful rule is simple: write to us at least three days before you need the cake. We answer within 24 hours, with a confirmation or with a question if something in the order is still open.

Send the order from the form on this site, or on WhatsApp if that is easier. Either way, include the same facts. We need your name and a mobile number we can actually reach. We need the date, written as a calendar day, not “next weekend.” We need the design — Signature Marble, Bouquet Cake, Bento, or Custom — and the size if you know it. We need the flavor: chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet. If there are words for the top, write them exactly as you want them piped, including the spelling of a name. Say whether you will pick the cake up in Cebu or whether it should be delivered. If it is a delivery, give the full address and a landmark. A photo is optional, and it helps most when the cake is custom.

Three days is the minimum, not a suggestion we bend on a busy Saturday. The cake is mixed and baked for your date. It has to cool before it can be crumb-coated. Marble, shells, and roses are piped after the cream is on, and they need a little time to set before the cake can travel. If we accepted an order for tomorrow, something else on the bench would be rushed, and the finish is the part we will not rush. If your date is inside three days, say so plainly. We would rather tell you we cannot than promise a cake we cannot finish properly.

Pickup

Pickup and delivery are both in Cebu. There is no case of cakes waiting, and we do not publish a street counter. When we confirm the order, we confirm a time and a meeting point for pickup. Come as close to serving as you reasonably can. Buttercream does not enjoy a long sit in a hot room, and Cebu afternoons are hot. Carry the cake home on a flat seat, not on a lap and not in a sunlit trunk. A bento travels more easily than the tall marble cake if the ride is tight.

Delivery

Delivery needs a person at the address. Traffic across Cebu — through the city, toward Mandaue or Lapu-Lapu, or up into the hills — moves in windows, not in exact minutes, so we agree a range when we confirm. Leave a mobile number for the person who will open the door. If no one is there, the cake sits in a warm vehicle, which is the thing we are trying to avoid. A condo, a village, or a gate that needs a pass should be named in the notes. A barangay alone is not enough for a driver to find a building.

After you send the form, leave the details as they are until we reply. We read every order ourselves. Within a day you will have a yes, a revised time, or a question about flavor, size, or the line on the cake. If you need to change the inscription, tell us before the day we pipe it. A change of date is sometimes possible and sometimes not; ask, and we will say which. The handover, and how you will pay, are settled in that confirmation rather than guessed from this page.

If you already know the cake, start the order with it selected. If you are still deciding, read the notes on flavor and on which of the three designs fits the day, then come back to the form. The date is the fact that matters most. Give us three days, and we will tell you within one.
