Welcome Home Cake for an OFW in Cebu: Arrival Planning Guide

White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Read this guide in: English · Cebuano · Tagalog

Quick answer: Do not base cake delivery on a flight arrival minute. Choose a confirmed home or gathering location, appoint a receiver and leave room to adjust if travel timing changes.

Planning a welcome home cake for an overseas Filipino worker or balikbayan often involves several people: the returning guest, family, a host and whoever receives the cake. Keep the cake handoff separate from airport pickup logistics. White Cakes arranges delivery within Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu; confirm the exact address and receiving time before placing the order.

Plan for the gathering, not just the flight

Ask the host when the returnee is expected at the home or celebration venue, and whether the welcome will happen immediately or after the traveller has rested. Travel schedules can change, so a cake delivery to the final gathering location may be easier to coordinate than tying it to an arrival time. White Cakes does not list airport delivery; ask about the specific destination and access.

Name one receiver and one backup contact

Give the bakery the name and mobile number of the person who will meet the delivery. Choose someone already at the destination, not the person who is driving to meet the arriving guest. Share a backup contact if the host may be unavailable. Include the address, barangay, landmark, gate or building instructions and a delivery window.

Choose a message the family wants to display

Short lines can include “Welcome Home, [Name],” “Safe Travels, [Name]” or “So Happy You’re Home.” Confirm the returnee’s preferred name and the exact wording with the host. If the gathering is a surprise, keep the message discreet in shared chats and make sure the person receiving the cake understands the plan.

Estimate servings and pick a flavour

Count family and friends who will actually share the cake. A 4-inch Bento is estimated at two generous or three modest servings; compare larger sizes in the cake-size guide. White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Name the preferred flavour and ask about current ingredient details for any dietary concern.

Confirm the order and handoff

Send the date, location, serving estimate, flavour, reference, exact inscription and receiver details together. Orders need at least three days’ notice and remain subject to availability. Confirm the final time, quote and delivery details before the 50% deposit secures the order. For a general surprise checklist, see the welcome home cake guide; send a request through the order form.
