Wedding Cake Message Ideas in Cebu: Short Lines for Couples

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Read this guide in: English · Cebuano · Tagalog

Quick answer: Choose one short greeting, verify both names and the date, and keep the inscription readable on the selected wedding cake.

A wedding cake inscription is usually read in photos as well as at the reception table. Choose wording that fits the couple’s style, then confirm the names and date before approving the design. These short examples work as a starting point; a card or programme can carry a longer personal message.

Simple lines for the couple

  • Congratulations, [Name] & [Name]!
  • Best Wishes to the Newlyweds
  • With Love, [Name] & [Name]
  • [Name] + [Name] · [Date]
  • Here’s to Your Next Chapter

If the cake will be photographed from across the room, keep the main line short and easy to read.

English and Cebuano options

  • Malipayong Kasal, [Ngalan]!
  • Congrats sa Inyong Duha!
  • Uban sa Gugma, [Ngalan] ug [Ngalan]

Families may prefer Cebuano, English, or a mix. Confirm the exact phrase with the couple instead of translating a personal message word for word.

Tagalog options

  • Maligayang Kasal, [Pangalan] & [Pangalan]!
  • Pag-ibig at Pagbati sa Bagong Kasal
  • Para kina [Pangalan] at [Pangalan]

Use the couple’s preferred names and spelling. If their invitation uses a particular form, copy it exactly.

Fit two names and a date

Decide which matters most on the cake top: both full names, a date, or one greeting. Initials or first names can leave room for the design; put the full names and longer dedication in a card if needed. Send punctuation, accents, capitalization, and the date in writing and have one person proofread the final line.

Match the message to the cake and reception
