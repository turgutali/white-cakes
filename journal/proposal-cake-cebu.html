Proposal Cake in Cebu: A Small Cake for the Big Question

By White Cakes · Lahug, Cebu City · Published 19 November 2026

Quick answer: For a proposal cake in Cebu, decide whether the cake is a small reveal for two or a centrepiece for family afterward. Send the date, size, flavour, short inscription and exact hand-off plan. Orders need at least three days’ notice, and delivery is confirmed for the full address and agreed window.

A proposal cake has two jobs: look right in the moment and still be easy to receive, keep cool and serve afterward. The best design brief starts with where the question will happen, who knows about the delivery, and how many people will share cake. A small surprise for two is a different order from a family engagement dinner.

Choose a size for the moment after the question

The 4-inch Bento is sized for two generous slices or three modest slices after a meal. It suits a private proposal or a simple gift. If family will join immediately, compare the 6-inch and 8-inch custom options and tell us how many guests will eat cake. The cake size guide for Cebu explains the published planning estimates; we confirm the portion plan for the actual design.

For a larger gathering, a custom cake can carry the couple’s colours or a short phrase. The hand-finished style uses buttercream details such as shells, roses and marbling. Send one or two reference photos and identify the one feature that matters most, so we can confirm what is practical for the date.

Keep the surprise private and the message readable

If the recipient should not see order messages, use the organiser’s name and mobile number as the contact. Tell us who can receive the driver and whether the cake should arrive before the venue booking or dinner begins. For an inscription, send the exact spelling and capitalisation. A 4-inch top has limited writing space, so keep the line short; for longer words, ask before finalising the design.

Choose one flavour from chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango or red velvet. The flavour name does not guarantee a separate filling or topping; ask us to confirm any specific expectation. See our cake flavour guide if you are comparing options.

Plan delivery around the venue

We deliver in Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu. Send the complete address, barangay, venue name, entrance instructions and a reachable receiver. Hotels, restaurants, condos and gated venues may have their own hand-off rules, so check who can accept the cake. We confirm a delivery window, not an exact minute, and do not provide venue setup. Keep a buttercream cake in a cool place until serving; our guide to handling cake in Cebu heat covers the ride and storage.

What to send for a quote

  • Proposal date and the time you plan to serve the cake.
  • Guest count and whether it is a private reveal or family gathering.
  • Size, flavour, exact inscription and one design reference.
  • Delivery address, landmark, receiving contact and any surprise instructions.

Orders need at least three days’ notice. We confirm availability and the final design price before a 50% deposit confirms the order. Start with our custom cakes in Cebu or 4-inch Bento page for the available formats.
