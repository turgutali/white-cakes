Why these cakes

Three things we actually do

Hand-finished

Buttercream is smoothed by hand. Marble, shells, and roses are piped, not printed on a sheet. The finish is the work.

Fresh to order

We bake for the date you name. Send the order at least three days ahead so the cake is made for that day, not pulled from a case.

Cebu pickup or delivery

Collect in Cebu, or give us an address and we deliver. We confirm the time when we reply. There is no walk-in counter.

How to order

Three steps

We need a name, a mobile number, and a date at least three days out. We confirm within 24 hours.

  1. 01

    Choose design

    Signature Marble, Bouquet, Bento, or Custom. If it is custom, say the mood and send a photo if you have one. The journal note on which cake fits which day is the short version.

  2. 02

    Send order

    Tell us the flavor (chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet), the size, the words for the cake, and whether you will pick up in Cebu or need a delivery address. Orders inside three days are too tight for a hand-finished cake.

  3. 03

    Pick up / delivery

    We reply within a day with a yes and a time. Pickup is in Cebu. Delivery needs a full address and someone to receive it. Keep buttercream cool on the way — how to hold a cake in this heat.
