Cake Delivery in Cebu City

From Lahug and IT Park to downtown and the port area — agreed windows, not exact minutes.

We deliver hand-finished buttercream cakes within Cebu City from our cloud kitchen. There is no storefront to visit; we confirm a delivery window when your order is accepted — not an exact minute, because traffic across the city moves in blocks.

Areas we often serve

Lahug, IT Park, Banilad, Capitol Site, Mabolo, the Ayala vicinity, downtown Cebu City, Sambag, and barangays along the usual north–south corridors. If you are unsure whether your pin is in range, send the barangay and a landmark in the order form and we will confirm in reply.

Condos, offices, and villages

Include lobby instructions, floor and unit when allowed, and a mobile number for whoever meets the driver. A barangay name alone is not enough for a building search. Buttercream should not wait in a sunlit lobby longer than necessary.

What to expect on the day

Someone must receive the cake at the door. If nobody is there, it sits in a warm vehicle — the scenario we are trying to avoid. Collect as close to serving time as you can; read storage in heat if the party is hours later.

Pickup alternative

If your schedule is tight, ask for pickup in Cebu City and collect after confirmation. We still need three days’ notice on the order.

Traffic and windows

Port area, flyovers, and school zones shift delivery windows without warning. We text inside the agreed range when the cake leaves the kitchen — not an hour before. Have someone reachable on the mobile number you gave.

Same-day expectations

We are not a same-day delivery catalogue. Services advertising free same-day city delivery operate on a different model. Our delivery page is for cakes ordered at least three days ahead, finished by hand.
