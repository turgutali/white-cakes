Cake Delivery in Cebu City

From Lahug and IT Park to downtown and the port area — agreed windows, not exact minutes.

Order a hand-finished buttercream cake for delivery in Cebu City. We make each cake for your named date in our cloud kitchen; there is no storefront or same-day catalogue. Send your request at least three days ahead. We reply within 24 hours, confirm the final cake price and delivery charge, and agree on a delivery window rather than an exact minute because traffic across the city moves in blocks.

Cakes available for delivery

  • 4-inch Bento cake: ₱449 through January 15, 2027 (regular price ₱599); sized for two generous or three modest slices after a meal.
  • Birthday cakes: choose a design for your guest count and send the date and delivery address to confirm availability.
  • Custom cakes: through January 15, 2027, 6-inch cakes start at ₱1,399, 8-inch at ₱1,999 and two-tier at ₱3,399. Final prices depend on the design.
  • Bouquet Cake: hand-piped buttercream flowers; price on request.
  • Christmas Vintage cake: 6-inch seasonal design at ₱650 through December 25, 2026; regular price ₱1,650 from December 26.

Delivery is quoted separately for the complete address; there is no single citywide fee. See the Cebu cake price guide for current starting prices. To check a date, use the order form with your design, flavour and delivery barangay.

Areas we often serve

Lahug, IT Park, Banilad, Capitol Site, Mabolo, the Ayala vicinity, downtown Cebu City, Sambag, and barangays along the usual north–south corridors. If you are unsure whether your pin is in range, send the barangay and a landmark in the order form and we will confirm in reply.

Condos, offices, and villages

Include lobby instructions, floor and unit when allowed, and a mobile number for whoever meets the driver. A barangay name alone is not enough for a building search. Buttercream should not wait in a sunlit lobby longer than necessary.

What to expect on the day

Someone must receive the cake at the door. If nobody is there, it sits in a warm vehicle — the scenario we are trying to avoid. Collect as close to serving time as you can; read storage in heat if the party is hours later.

Pickup alternative

If your schedule is tight, ask for pickup in Cebu City and collect after confirmation. We still need three days’ notice on the order.

Traffic and windows

Port area, flyovers, and school zones shift delivery windows without warning. We text inside the agreed range when the cake leaves the kitchen — not an hour before. Have someone reachable on the mobile number you gave.

Same-day expectations

We are not a same-day delivery catalogue. Services advertising free same-day city delivery operate on a different model. Our delivery page is for cakes ordered at least three days ahead, finished by hand.

Pins we can place from the note

A useful Cebu City note names the barangay, the building or village phase, and a landmark a driver can see from the street. IT Park and Cebu Business Park towers need the tower name and who will come to the lobby. Talamban and the uphill barangays take longer at school-out than a Lahug condo near the kitchen. Say which kind of drop it is so the window matches the road. This page is the city itself. Bridge crossings to Mandaue and the island ride to Mactan are written up on their own delivery pages, because those gates and queues are different.
