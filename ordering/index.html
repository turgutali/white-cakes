Ordering & delivery

The White Cakes - Cebu · Updated 17 November 2026

We bake to order in Lahug, Cebu City. There is no storefront or walk-in counter; pickup is arranged after we confirm the order.

Lead time and confirmation

  • Please request your cake at least three days before the date you need it. December dates can fill earlier.
  • We aim to reply within 24 hours to confirm availability, design details, and the final quote.
  • A 50% deposit confirms the order. We will confirm the payment method and remaining balance details in our reply.
  • Custom cake prices are starting prices. The final design price is confirmed before you pay. Bouquet cakes are quoted on request.

Pickup and delivery

Delivery is available in Cebu City, Mandaue City, and Lapu-Lapu City. Send the full address, a landmark, and the receiver’s mobile number. We confirm a delivery window rather than an exact minute. Delivery charges are not listed as a flat rate; ask for the charge for your address and have it confirmed with your quote before paying a deposit.

Changes and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change a date or order detail. A date change depends on the bench schedule. Because cakes are made to order and perishable, returns are not accepted.

The site does not set a general refund amount or refund timeline for cancellations after a deposit. Please ask us to confirm the cancellation and deposit terms for your specific order in writing before sending the deposit.

Allergens and care

Our cakes contain gluten and dairy unless we agree otherwise in writing. Tell us about allergies before ordering so we can confirm what is possible. Keep buttercream cakes out of direct sun and hot cars; refrigerate if they will be held for several hours before serving.

For a quote, send your cake choice, flavour, size, message, date, pickup or delivery choice, and (for delivery) the full address. Use our order form or contact us on WhatsApp.

Ordering from overseas for someone in Cebu? Read our guide to sending a cake to Cebu from abroad for the details to confirm before paying.
