Frequently asked questions

Made-to-order cakes in Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu — no walk-ins.

How far in advance should I order?

At least three days before the date you need the cake. We bake to order and finish piping after the crumb coat sets.

Do you have a storefront or walk-in counter?

No. We are a made-to-order cloud kitchen in Cebu. Pickup is by arrangement only after we confirm your order.

Where do you deliver?

Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu. Send a full address, landmark, and someone to receive the cake at the door.

What sizes can I order?

Our menu includes a 4-inch Bento (two generous or three modest servings after a meal), a 6-inch Signature Marble, 8-inch custom cakes, and two-tier custom cakes. As planning estimates, allow about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch round, 16–24 for an 8-inch round, and 30–36 for a common 6-inch plus 8-inch two-tier cake. Actual portions vary with height, design, and cut. Bouquet is a floral finish rather than a fixed size. Read our Cebu cake size guide and share your guest count so we can confirm a suitable size.

What flavors are available?

Chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet. If you need something else, message us and we will confirm whether we can do it on your date.

Can I send a reference photo for a custom design?

Yes. Upload an inspiration photo on the order form or send it on WhatsApp. We pipe by hand — we will say if a detail needs adjusting for buttercream.

How should I store the cake before serving?

Keep buttercream out of direct sun and hot cars. Short time at room temperature is fine; refrigerate if you collected hours before serving, especially in Cebu heat. See our journal note on heat.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit confirms the order. The payment method is confirmed when we reply. If you are ordering from overseas, see our guide to sending a cake to Cebu from abroad and ask which payment option is available to you.

Do you list allergen information?

Our cakes contain gluten and dairy unless we agree otherwise in writing. Message us about allergies and we will confirm what we can safely offer.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Contact us as soon as you need a change. Whether we can move a date depends on the bench schedule. Read our ordering and delivery terms and confirm any cancellation or deposit terms with us in writing before you pay.

How fast do you reply?

Within 24 hours on orders sent through the website form or WhatsApp, often sooner.

What are your prices?

Launch price — until January 15, 2027. The struck-through amount on the menu is the regular price from January 16, 2027. A 4-inch bento is ₱449 (regular ₱599). Custom cakes start at ₱1,399 for 6-inch (regular ₱1,799), ₱1,999 for 8-inch (regular ₱2,599), and ₱3,399 for 2-tier (regular ₱4,299). A 6-inch Christmas vintage cake is ₱650 (regular ₱1,650), a Christmas special — until December 25, 2026. Custom designs are starting prices; the final price depends on the design. Bouquet cakes are price on request. A 50% deposit confirms the order.

Still unsure? Send the date and the cake you have in mind.
