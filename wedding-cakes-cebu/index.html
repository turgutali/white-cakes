Wedding Cakes in Cebu

Small wedding and engagement cakes finished by hand — tell us the guest count and the mood.

We are a made-to-order cloud kitchen in Cebu, not a hotel pastry shop with display tiers. For intimate weddings, engagement dinners, and rehearsal nights we finish buttercream cakes to your brief — often Signature Marble or a custom design in ivory, blush, dusty blue, and sage.

Planning the brief

Send guest count, venue name, whether the reception is air-conditioned or open air, and any reference photos. Tall multi-tier cakes need more bench time than a single 6-inch; message us early and we will say honestly what your date allows.

Heat and buttercream

Cebu garden receptions and afternoon photo sessions matter for buttercream. Read our notes on keeping a cake in heat and tell us when the cake will be cut, not only when it is delivered.

Delivery and setup

We deliver within Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu. We do not provide on-site cutting service or venue setup crew. Someone at the venue must receive the cake; resorts need a guest name on the order for gate clearance.

Quotes

We do not publish prices online. Message us with the date and brief; we reply within 24 hours with what we can do and how payment is confirmed for that order.

Tiers and servings

Tell us realistic guest count and how the cake will be served — after dinner, during cocktails, or only for photos. We do not publish tier diagrams; we reply with what size and finish the bench can do for that date.

Venue coordination

Ask your venue about cake tables, power for AC near an outdoor setup, and whether they allow outside cakes. We deliver to the address you give; we do not negotiate corkage with the hotel — that stays between you and the venue.

What we will and will not promise

We finish a buttercream cake for an intimate wedding, an engagement dinner, or a rehearsal. We do not staff a cutting table, and we do not build a ballroom display from a catalogue. If the guest list is a hall, send the number early. We will say whether the bench can do that date, or whether a smaller cake for the couple’s table is the honest answer.
