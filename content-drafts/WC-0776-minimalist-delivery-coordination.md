## Coordinate a minimalist cake delivery with a clear receiver

Choose someone who can meet the driver during the agreed window and place the cake on a level surface. Send White Cakes the complete address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any building or gate instructions. Confirm the delivery fee and timing for that destination; a simple design still needs a precise handoff.

If it is an office or venue delivery, check who can accept the cake and where it can wait before serving. Keep it away from direct sun and a hot vehicle. Ask the bakery about care if the cake will sit for several hours, especially if the finish includes buttercream.

Send the event date, flavour, size, inscription, and one design reference with the delivery request. Confirm the minimalist layout and price in writing; do not assume a simple appearance means a shorter production slot. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
