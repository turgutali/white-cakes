## Show the colour movement you like and identify the parts that can change

For a marble cake, choose a reference that makes the colour palette and pattern easy to see. Tell the bakery which part matters—soft movement, a particular colour, or a border—and which details are flexible. Avoid combining several unrelated marbling photos without explaining the difference.

Share the requested size, date, flavour, inscription, and budget. Ask how the reference can be adapted to the selected cake and whether the finish is available for that order. Confirm the final design in writing; a photo from another baker is direction, not a guarantee of an exact copy.

Add these instructions to the custom brief guide. A separate case needs a real reference-to-finished comparison, original images, and permission.
