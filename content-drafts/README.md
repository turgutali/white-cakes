## Publishing guidance

Most roadmap candidates should strengthen existing canonical guides as useful sections or FAQs. Follow `content-index.csv` and the calendar's canonical target; do not create 950 thin standalone URLs. Do not copy drafts to production or request indexing until the editorial set is complete and target pages have been updated and reviewed.

Never invent prices, serving counts, customer cases, routes, delivery rules, available ingredients, or photo evidence. Recheck time-sensitive business details before publication.
