## Publishing guidance

Most candidates should strengthen existing canonical guides as useful sections or FAQs. Use `content-index.csv` and the calendar for target pages. Do not copy drafts into production or request indexing until target pages are updated and reviewed.

Never invent prices, serving counts, customer cases, routes, delivery rules, available ingredients, or photo evidence. Recheck time-sensitive business details before publication.
