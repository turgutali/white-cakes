## Send the inscription early enough to confirm its fit

White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. When the design includes custom lettering, send the exact inscription with the first enquiry rather than deciding it after the cake style is agreed. Include the name, punctuation, and capitalization, then ask whether the line fits the selected size.

For a 4-inch Bento, a short phrase is more practical because the writing area is limited. A larger custom cake gives the baker more layout options, but the border, flowers, and other piped details also use surface space. One reference photo and one clear message help the baker confirm a design that stays readable.

Give the date, flavour, size, guest count, and delivery or pickup details in the same message. For delivery, include the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. White Cakes must confirm availability, design, quote, and logistics; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after that reply.

Use this as a section in the shared lead-time and custom-brief guides. A separate timeline page for inscription orders would duplicate the same minimum notice and order-confirmation rules.
