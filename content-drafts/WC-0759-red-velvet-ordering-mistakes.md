## Confirm the flavour, size, and design rather than relying on an old photo

An old red velvet cake photo may not show the current menu, price, or available decoration. Ask whether the flavour is available for your date, what the selected option includes, and how many portions the chosen size is expected to provide. If you want a custom finish, explain the design priority and ask what can be accommodated.

Send the exact inscription, event date, delivery or pickup location, and recipient contact in one complete brief. Confirm the total quote and deposit instructions in writing before payment. If there are allergies or dietary restrictions, ask directly about ingredients and accommodation.

Keep flavour availability in the live flavour guide and booking steps in the order guide. Avoid a standalone red velvet article that repeats current details without adding verified value.
