## Prepare a clear brief for a children's birthday cake

Start with the date, number of children and adults expected to eat cake, preferred size, flavour, and delivery or pickup plan. Ask the child for one main design idea and send a reference image that shows it clearly. If the child has several favourite themes, decide which one should lead instead of asking the bakery to combine every idea.

Include the exact inscription, colours, and any detail that must not appear. Ask White Cakes whether the requested character-inspired or themed design can be adapted; a reference photo does not guarantee an exact copy. Confirm the design and quote before paying.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Tell the bakery whether children and adults will share it and whether there are other desserts. If any child has an allergy, ask the bakery directly about ingredients and preparation rather than assuming a cake is safe.

Allow at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after date, design, and delivery details are settled.
