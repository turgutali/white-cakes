## Confirm the address, complete quote, and order details first

Before paying, confirm the family event date, cake size, flavour, design, inscription, and requested delivery or pickup plan. For Lapu-Lapu, provide the full destination and a receiving contact, then ask the bakery whether the route is available and what the current delivery fee is. Do not assume a location is covered without confirmation.

Request the complete amount in writing, including custom work and delivery. White Cakes currently states a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order; verify the exact amount and current payment channel. Save the receipt and order summary so the family coordinator can check all agreed details.

Keep the payment instructions in one central order guide. Recheck the live process before publication because payment details and delivery coverage may change.
