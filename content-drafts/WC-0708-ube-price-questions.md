## Request a current quote for an Ube cake

When asking for the price of an Ube cake, include the size, flavour, design, date, and serving plan. White Cakes lists Ube among its current flavours, but confirm that it is available for the requested size and production date. If you want a custom finish or lettering, include those details so the quote reflects the actual work.

The current custom menu shows 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799, and 8-inch cakes at ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. These rates are time-limited; check the live menu before using the amounts. Ask whether the final design or delivery adds a charge.

White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. These are approximate yields. Give the bakery the number of people expected to eat cake and ask which size is sensible. Confirm the date, price, delivery fee, and timing; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order after details are settled.

Keep current Ube availability and prices in the central menu guide rather than creating a flavour-specific page with figures that can go stale.
