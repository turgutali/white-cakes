## Compare the options using guest count, table space, and the event style

Begin with the number of guests who will actually have cake and whether dessert is served after a meal. A small shower may suit a compact design, while a larger gathering can need more portions. Ask the bakery to confirm servings for the selected size and cutting style instead of assuming one cake diameter always serves the same number.

Then compare shape and decoration against the venue setup. If the cake will be a focal point on a dessert table, share a photo of the space or describe its colours. Decide whether a short inscription, floral detail, or simple finish matters most, and check current flavour and price availability.
