## Confirm these birthday-cake details before the deposit

Before sending the deposit, make sure the bakery has confirmed the date and production slot. An enquiry is not a reservation. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, so send the date early and wait for a clear reply that it is available.

Read back the full order in one message: cake size, flavour, design and colour notes, exact inscription, pickup or delivery, and the amount quoted. If you supplied reference photos, ask the bakery to confirm the feasible interpretation rather than assuming every pictured detail is included. For delivery, verify the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, delivery window, and address-based fee.

White Cakes' current ordering process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Check how the balance is to be paid and when, using the instructions provided with the quote. Do not rely on an old screenshot or a general price article for a current amount; the written order confirmation should state the terms for your specific cake.

Save the confirmation and payment proof so both sides can refer to the same details. This checklist belongs in the central ordering guide and should be reviewed if the bakery changes its deposit process.
