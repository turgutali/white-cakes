## Request a baby-shower cake quote with the key details

Include the event date, requested size, flavour, design, inscription, number of expected cake eaters, and delivery or pickup location. If the cake will be part of a dessert table, say so. A custom reference photo may involve work beyond a listed option, so ask White Cakes for the total price for the actual design.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16. The 8-inch listed amount is ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. These figures are temporary and need a live-menu check before publication. Confirm whether decoration or delivery adds to the quoted total.

Ask whether the size suits the serving plan. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm date availability, delivery fee, and timing. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep current amounts and quote factors in the central price guide. Update temporary price dates when the bakery revises its menu.
