## What to ask about the price of baby shower cake in Cebu

Request a written quote for the exact date, size, flavour, piping detail, inscription and delivery address. A reference image by itself cannot establish the price because scale and hand-piped detail affect the work; ask what the quoted amount includes.

Prices and availability can change. The listed launch prices for Signature Marble sizes are date-limited through 15 January 2027 and do not set the price for every custom design. Confirm current pricing directly before announcing a budget. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
