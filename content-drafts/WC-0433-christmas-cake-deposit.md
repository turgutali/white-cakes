## Christmas-cake details to confirm before the deposit

Holiday dates require an availability check before you assume the bakery can accept the order. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice, and its Christmas guidance asks customers to plan earlier because December dates can fill. Send the date you need the cake and wait for a reply that confirms the slot before paying a deposit.

Confirm the cake style, size, flavour, message, and design reference in writing. If you are ordering a seasonal design, identify the exact style or photo you mean and ask whether it is available for your date. A photograph can guide a hand-piped interpretation; it is not a guarantee of an exact copy. Ask for the current quote and any seasonal details rather than relying on an old screenshot or a previous year's price.

If the cake will be delivered, confirm the complete address, nearby landmark, receiver's mobile number, and the agreed delivery window. Holiday traffic and venue access can affect the practical handover, so name a person who can receive the box and keep it out of direct sun. The bakery confirms an address-based delivery charge with the quote; do not assume a flat rate.

The ordering page says a 50% deposit confirms an agreed order. Before paying, make sure the date, total, payment method, remaining balance details, and delivery or pickup plan are clear. Keep this seasonal checklist within the existing order and Christmas delivery guides; a separate deposit article would repeat the same order rules.

Humihiling ang White Cakes ng hindi bababa sa tatlong araw na paunawa, at hinihiling ng patnubay nito sa Pasko ang mga customer na magplano nang mas maaga dahil maaaring mapuno ang mga petsa ng Disyembre. Kung nag-o-order ka ng pana-panahong disenyo, tukuyin ang eksaktong istilo o larawang ibig mong sabihin at tanungin kung available ito para sa iyong petsa. Ang isang litrato ay maaaring gabayan ang isang hand-piped na interpretasyon; hindi ito garantiya ng eksaktong kopya.
