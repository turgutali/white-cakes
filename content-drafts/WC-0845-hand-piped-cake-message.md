## Keep lettering clear beside hand-piped details

On a custom buttercream cake, the message and the decoration need room to be seen. Choose a short line and send the exact text with spelling and punctuation checked. Then tell the baker which piped feature matters most—perhaps a border, flower, or colour accent—so the lettering does not have to compete with every element in the reference photo.

The 4-inch Bento has a small writing area and suits a brief phrase. Larger sizes give a different layout, but the usable space depends on the design. Ask White Cakes to confirm that the requested message fits before paying the deposit. A digital logo is not printed as an edible sheet; colours or shapes may be interpreted in hand-piped buttercream if the bakery confirms they are feasible.

Include the date, size, flavour, guest count, and delivery or pickup preference in your brief. If delivered, provide the receiver's full address, landmark, mobile number, and access notes. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice.
