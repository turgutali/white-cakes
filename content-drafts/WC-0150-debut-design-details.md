## Choose debut-cake details that work together

Begin with the celebrant's main colour or event palette. Pick one focal detail—such as a floral accent, a clean border, or a short inscription—and let the remaining choices support it. A design brief with a few priorities is easier to interpret than a collection of unrelated references.

If the cake will be displayed as part of the venue styling, share a photo of the table or describe the backdrop. Tell White Cakes whether the cake should blend in or stand out, and ask how the design translates to the chosen size. A reference image is guidance, not a guarantee that every element can be copied; confirm the feasible design and price.

Estimate servings with the bakery. Current ranges are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. Give the actual number of cake eaters and note other desserts. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm the date, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Add an original close-up only when it shows a real White Cakes debut design, the technique is verified, and image permission is documented. Otherwise, this belongs in the existing design guide.
