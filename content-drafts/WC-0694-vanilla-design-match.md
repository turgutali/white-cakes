## Match a vanilla cake design to the occasion

Vanilla is one of White Cakes' current flavours. Decide how the design should feel—simple, floral, playful, or formal—and choose one focal detail. A clear colour palette and short message can make a cake feel personal without crowding its surface. Ask the bakery whether the chosen decoration is available for the requested size and date.

Send one reference image and explain what to adapt. A photo may show techniques or materials that are not offered, so ask for the feasible version and final quote rather than assuming it can be copied exactly. If the recipient has a dietary restriction, confirm ingredients with White Cakes directly; the flavour name alone does not establish suitability.

Share the serving plan, event date, inscription, and pickup or delivery details. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use original approved photos for a vanilla-specific design example. Otherwise, add the general guidance to the central design guide and link to the flavour menu.
