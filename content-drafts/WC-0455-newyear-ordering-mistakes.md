## Avoid last-minute problems with a New Year cake

Do not wait until the holiday week to ask about availability. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and New Year's dates may fill sooner. Confirm the production slot before building the celebration plan around a cake.

Avoid providing only an event name when delivery is involved. Give the full address, landmark, local receiver's mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Ask whether the delivery route, fee, and window are available for that date. Holiday traffic or venue restrictions should be confirmed, not guessed.

Do not send an incomplete design request. Include flavour, size, serving estimate, reference photo, and exact inscription together. Confirm the feasible design and price in writing. Current serving estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; ask the bakery to confirm the size.

The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after details are settled. Keep the seasonal reminders in the main order guide and update them only with current bakery information.
