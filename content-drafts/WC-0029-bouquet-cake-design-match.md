## Match the Bouquet Cake colours and message to the recipient

White Cakes' Bouquet Cake is built around hand-piped buttercream flowers, including rosettes and sage leaves, with pleated wrapper detail, pearls, and a bow. When ordering, decide whether you want the established floral look or a variation in colour and finish. Send one reference image and name the feature that matters most; the baker can confirm what can be interpreted without treating the image as an exact-copy promise.

Consider the recipient's preferred palette and the place where the cake will be presented. A short inscription may fit differently depending on the floral arrangement and scale. Share the exact wording and ask where it can go on the design. If the cake is for a shared table, include the guest count and ask for a serving recommendation; Bouquet Cake scale is confirmed for the request rather than assumed from a photo.

The price is quoted on request because the finish and scale vary. Provide the event date, flavour, recipient or venue details, and delivery or pickup preference. At least three days' notice is requested; ask earlier for detailed piping or a holiday date.

Use approved Bouquet Cake photos on the existing product page. A separate photo case should describe a real, occasion-specific cake and a visible design decision, not simply repeat the bouquet style description.
