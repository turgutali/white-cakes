## Choose a design that can be clearly briefed from overseas

When ordering for family in Cebu from abroad, send one reference photo and state which detail you want the baker to interpret: a colour, border, flower, or inscription. Avoid assuming that the photo's entire finish can be reproduced exactly. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and confirms what will fit the selected cake size and production schedule.

Keep the message short and include exact spelling. If the cake is a 4-inch Bento, the top has limited room; ask whether your line fits. Choose the flavour from the current menu and ask separately about any filling, topping, or special decoration you expect. A listed flavour does not automatically include those extras.

Give the bakery the event date, guest count, size, and delivery address. The receiver's mobile number, a local landmark, and gate or lobby instructions help coordinate the handover. Since the buyer may be in a different time zone, confirm who will answer delivery questions and who will receive the cake during the agreed window.

This is practical guidance for the overseas-order page. A photo-led design case needs an approved image of a real cake and a documented design decision; without that proof, do not present a generic image as a commission for a Cebu family abroad.
