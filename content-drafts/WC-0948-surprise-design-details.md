## Choose surprise-cake details the recipient will recognize

Think about something the recipient genuinely likes, such as a colour, hobby, or shared memory. One small motif and a short message can make the cake personal without creating a crowded design. If you are unsure, ask a trusted family member to help select the main idea.

Send White Cakes one reference photo and explain what to adapt. Ask whether the design fits the selected size and what the quote includes. A reference image is not a guarantee of exact reproduction. Confirm the inscription and spelling in writing, especially if the cake is part of a surprise.

Choose a local receiver who can accept the cake without revealing the plan. Include the delivery address, landmark, mobile number, and the time window. Keep the cake away from direct sunlight and heat after delivery. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use an original close-up only when it shows a real White Cakes design with permission. Otherwise, merge general design guidance into the existing guide.

Mag-isip tungkol sa isang bagay na talagang gusto ng tatanggap, tulad ng isang kulay, libangan, o nakabahaging memorya. Ang isang maliit na motif at isang maikling mensahe ay maaaring gawing personal ang cake nang hindi gumagawa ng masikip na disenyo. Kung hindi ka sigurado, hilingin sa isang pinagkakatiwalaang miyembro ng pamilya na tumulong sa pagpili ng pangunahing ideya.

Magpadala ng White Cakes ng isang reference na larawan at ipaliwanag kung ano ang iaangkop. Itanong kung ang disenyo ay umaangkop sa napiling laki at kung ano ang kasama sa quote. Ang isang reference na imahe ay hindi isang garantiya ng eksaktong pagpaparami. Kumpirmahin ang inskripsiyon at spelling sa pagsulat, lalo na kung ang cake ay bahagi ng isang sorpresa.

Pumili ng lokal na receiver na maaaring tumanggap ng cake nang hindi inilalantad ang plano. Isama ang delivery address, landmark, mobile number, at ang time window.
