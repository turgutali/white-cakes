## Ask for a quote for a small gathering cake

Share the date, number of people who will eat cake, requested size, flavour, design, and inscription. For two or three servings, ask whether the 4-inch Bento is suitable; it is planned for two generous portions or three modest ones. If the group is larger, a 6-inch or 8-inch cake may be more appropriate, depending on slice size.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16. The 8-inch listed price is ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 afterward. Check the live menu before relying on these date-specific amounts. Ask whether the requested design or delivery changes the total.

Confirm that the flavour and design are available for the date. For delivery, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's number, and preferred window; verify the fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep current prices in the central guide and update them when the bakery changes its menu.
