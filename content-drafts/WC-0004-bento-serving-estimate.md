## Decide whether you want two generous portions or three modest ones

White Cakes describes its 4-inch Bento as serving about two people generously or three modestly. Treat that as an estimate rather than a guaranteed number; appetite and cutting style affect the result. If more people will share the cake, tell the bakery the actual group size and ask which available product is more suitable.

Consider whether the cake is served after a meal, whether other desserts are available, and how large you want each slice. Confirm the current product description and date availability directly. If you plan to add a message or decoration, ask whether it affects the size choice or total price.

Keep this serving guidance in the central size guide and on the current Bento listing. Recheck it with the bakery before publishing.
