## English

# How to estimate servings for 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

A 4-inch Bento is estimated at about two generous or three modest servings after a meal. Treat that as a guide, not a promise for every event: appetite, slice size, and other desserts matter. If everyone needs a full portion, ask the bakery whether a larger cake is more appropriate.

Confirm the actual Bento product and serving assumption before ordering. Do not select it for a group based only on its compact appearance in photos.
