## Confirm venue access, shade, and the person receiving the cake

Give the bakery the full address, entry instructions, requested handoff time, and a reachable receiver’s number. Explain whether the outdoor venue is shaded, how long the cake will wait before serving, and where it will be placed. Ask whether delivery to the location is available and confirm the fee and timing.

The receiver should take the cake promptly, keep the box level, and follow the bakery’s advice for the specific product. Do not assume a decoration is heatproof. If the event time changes, contact the bakery for updated care guidance.

Keep route and handoff steps in the delivery guide and verified storage information in the heat-care page. Do not imply a route or environmental guarantee without evidence.
