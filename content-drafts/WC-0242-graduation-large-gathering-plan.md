## Plan graduation cake portions for the actual celebration

Graduation may include a ceremony, a family meal, or a larger party. Estimate how many people will eat cake at the moment it is served, and note whether there are other desserts. The people attending the ceremony may not all gather around the cake, while family members who were not at the ceremony may join the meal.

White Cakes' current size guide estimates 8–12 party portions from 6 inches, 16–24 from 8 inches, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges and depend on cutting style. Ask the bakery to confirm a configuration based on your event plan; do not extrapolate exact numbers for a larger group.

Share the date, flavour, design, inscription, and venue with the order request. If it will be delivered to a school or office, confirm who can receive it and what building instructions apply. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm the current quote and delivery fee, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Keep graduation examples in the shared serving guide and link to the current product and delivery details.
