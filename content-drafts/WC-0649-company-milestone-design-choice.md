## Make the milestone visible while keeping the cake readable

For a company anniversary, launch, or team achievement, decide what colleagues should recognize at a glance: the milestone number, a short company name, or one brand colour. Trying to fit a logo, slogan, date, and several decorations on one surface can make the result difficult to read. Choose the essential feature and treat the rest as optional.

Share a suitable logo file or colour reference if the company has approved its use. Tell the bakery how many people may share the cake and where it will be displayed, then confirm whether the design and serving plan fit the selected size. Get permission before sharing any internal event photograph, logo, or company name publicly.

Add the planning advice to the existing design guide. Publish a separate company case only when there is a real finished cake, a distinct design lesson, and written approval for the details and imagery.
