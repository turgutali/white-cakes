## Avoid common anniversary-cake order mix-ups

Do not wait until the event is close to check whether the date is available. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and a weekend date may need earlier planning. Get a confirmed slot before assuming the cake is booked.

Avoid sending several conflicting design references. Choose one visual direction, identify the detail that matters, and provide the exact inscription. Confirm whether the requested design is feasible for the selected size and ask for the final price. A reference image does not establish a fixed quote or guarantee an exact replica.

Do not estimate servings from the RSVP number alone. Ask how many people will eat cake and whether other desserts are planned. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; confirm the size with the bakery. For delivery, verify the address, receiver, window, and fee.

The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all terms are clear. Use this checklist in the central ordering guide, not as an invented account of customer mistakes.

Kinukumpirma ng 50% na deposito ang napagkasunduang order pagkatapos na malinaw ang lahat ng tuntunin. Gamitin ang checklist na ito sa gitnang gabay sa pag-order, hindi bilang isang imbentong account ng mga pagkakamali ng customer.
