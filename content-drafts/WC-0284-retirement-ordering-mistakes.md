## Prevent common retirement-cake order problems

The first avoidable problem is waiting until the event is close to confirm the date. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and a retirement celebration may have a fixed office schedule. Ask about availability as soon as the event time is set.

Do not assume the cake must serve every person on the invitation list. Ask how many colleagues will eat cake and whether there are other desserts. White Cakes estimates around 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; these ranges vary with serving size. Share the plan and ask the bakery to confirm the best size.

Avoid sending conflicting logo and message references. Confirm the exact wording, any approved company mark, flavour, design, size, total price, and receiving contact in one written order. For office delivery, verify building access, delivery window, and fee. The current process uses a 50% deposit to secure the agreed order after details are settled.
