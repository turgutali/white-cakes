## English

# How to prepare for delivery of 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

For Bento delivery, confirm the full address, building entry, contact, and delivery window. Ask how the compact cake should be carried and where it can be received safely. A small size does not mean it can be tilted, carried casually, or left outside.

Name a receiver and confirm delivery coverage for the exact Cebu address. Send access details in writing.
