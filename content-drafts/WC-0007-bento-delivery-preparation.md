## Confirm the handoff details before the delivery day

Provide the complete address, requested arrival window, recipient’s name and phone number, and any gate or building instructions. Ask whether delivery to the location is available and confirm the fee and timing with the bakery. If the Bento cake is going to an office, café, or event space, include the exact entrance or receiving point.

Have someone ready to take the cake directly. Keep its box level and follow White Cakes’ instructions for the specific product, especially if it will wait before serving. Do not assume a 4-inch cake can be carried or stored like a boxed pastry without asking about its decoration.

Add the Bento example to the central delivery guide. Route availability, delivery fees, and care details should be confirmed before publication.
