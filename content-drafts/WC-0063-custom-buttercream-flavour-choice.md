## Choose a flavour and confirm it for the custom design

White Cakes' current flavour list includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Choose based on the recipient or group's preference, then confirm the flavour is available for the selected size, date, and buttercream design. Do not assume every flavour works with every configuration.

If you have dietary restrictions to consider, ask the bakery directly about ingredients and preparation. A flavour name alone does not establish that a cake is safe for an allergy or fits a particular diet. Avoid making ingredient claims unless White Cakes confirms them.

Plan the serving count and design at the same time. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Send the event date, one design reference, exact inscription, and delivery plan. Ask about care if the cake will sit in warm conditions, since buttercream can soften.

Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order. Keep menu facts in the central flavour guide and link it from custom design content.
