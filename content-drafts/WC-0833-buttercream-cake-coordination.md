## Coordinate the handover and storage for a buttercream cake

Choose a receiver who can meet the driver during the agreed delivery window and move the cake to a suitable place. Send White Cakes the complete address, a landmark, the receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instruction. The bakery confirms a delivery window and the charge for the actual address.

After handover, keep the cake out of direct sun and hot cars. If it will be held for several hours before serving, the ordering guidance says to refrigerate it. Do not leave the box unattended at a guardhouse or outside the venue. If the cake will wait through a long programme, tell the bakery the serving time and ask about the practical plan.

Include the event date, cake style, flavour, size, guest count, and exact message in the order request. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice and confirms availability before the order is booked. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed details.

Keep the general handover checklist in the Cebu delivery guide and the heat advice in the shared care page. A buttercream-by-city article would repeat the same storage facts unless new route evidence changes the instructions.
