## Choose a marble design with a clear colour direction

Marbling can mean different patterns and colour combinations. Decide on the base colour and one accent colour first, then send a reference showing the movement or contrast you like. Tell White Cakes whether you want a subtle effect or a more visible pattern and ask what it can create on the selected cake size.

The Signature Marble product is currently described as a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. If that is the design you mean, confirm the current listing and availability. If you want a custom marble finish, do not assume the Signature Marble changes are included at the listed price; request a confirmed quote.

Share the event date, flavour, number of cake eaters, and delivery or pickup plan. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches, but actual portions vary. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the agreed order before paying the 50% deposit.

Use real approved White Cakes photos for any standalone marble-design case. Otherwise, keep the advice in the main design guide and link to Signature Marble.
