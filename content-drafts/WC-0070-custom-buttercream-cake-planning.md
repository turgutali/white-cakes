## English

# Planning custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Plan a custom buttercream cake by fixing the date, guest count, venue, size, flavour, palette, and message before selecting extra decoration. The bakery makes each cake for the requested calendar date; detailed designs and multi-tier enquiries should be sent earlier than the three-day minimum.

Confirm the design, price, and delivery in one written order record.

## Cebuano

# Pagplano sa custom buttercream cake sa Cebu

Planoha pinaagi sa petsa, bisita, venue, gidak-on, flavour, kolor ug mensahe sa dili pa dugangan og dekorasyon. Gihimo ang cake alang sa petsa; sayo pa ipadala ang detalyado o multi-tier kaysa tulo ka adlaw nga minimum.

Kumpirmaha sa usa ka sinulat nga order ang disenyo, presyo ug delivery.
