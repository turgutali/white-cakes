## Plan the custom buttercream cake in three decisions

**Choose the size from the people eating cake.** The current guide gives about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, 16–24 from an 8-inch, and roughly 30–36 from a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier cake. A 4-inch Bento is for two generous or three modest servings. Share the guest count and ask the bakery to confirm the portion plan for the requested design.

**Choose one design direction.** White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream. Send one reference photo and describe the colour, border, floral detail, or lettering that matters most. Ask what can be adapted to the selected size; a reference does not guarantee an exact copy.

**Choose the flavour and event logistics.** The menu lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet. Ask separately about fillings or toppings. Include the date, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery details. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice and confirms availability, quote, and address-based delivery charge before the 50% deposit.

Keep this three-part decision path on the existing custom-cake and size pages so customers can refer to one current menu and serving guide.
