## Design details to consider for Sinulog cake in Cebu

Choose one visual direction that connects to the occasion: a palette, motif or meaningful detail. A cake does not need to repeat every element in the room; a restrained design often reads more clearly in photographs and keeps the celebrant as the focus.

White Cakes makes custom work with hand-piped buttercream rather than printed sheets or fondant sculpture. Share one overall reference and prioritize the details that matter; confirm what can be adapted to the selected size. For a Sinulog gathering, confirm venue access, road or event restrictions and the actual service time with the organizer before setting delivery expectations. Use a palette or motif the bakery confirms it can hand-pipe; do not assume a parade route or road is accessible.
