## Make one Sinulog-inspired design cue the focus

If you want a Sinulog-inspired cake, choose one colour or visual idea that matters to your celebration and explain it in a brief. A single palette or piped accent gives the baker a clearer direction than asking for every festival symbol at once. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; share one reference photo and point out the part to interpret, then ask what will fit the selected cake size.

Keep the inscription short and send the exact spelling. If the cake is for a family table, include the expected number of cake-eaters and use the current size guide to discuss portions. A compact 4-inch Bento and a larger custom cake are different formats; the choice should follow the guest count and the type of celebration.

Send the date early and ask about availability. The bakery requests at least three days' notice, but festival schedules and venues may need advance planning. If delivery is to a venue, give the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any entrance or gate instruction. Avoid assuming a particular road closure or delivery route; confirm the practical arrangements for that year's event.

Use this design brief on the existing Sinulog page. A close-up design breakdown requires an original, approved cake image and a detail actually visible in it.
