## Choose Lapu-Lapu family-cake details that feel cohesive

Start with the family gathering's style and choose one focal idea. A palette that matches the table, a short message, or one small motif can make the cake feel personal without adding unrelated decorations. Ask the host which feature matters most before sending design references.

Send White Cakes one image and explain what it should adapt. If the family wants a local or cultural detail, describe it accurately and ask the bakery whether it can be represented. Confirm the final design and quote; a reference photo is not a promise of an exact copy.

Include the event date, flavour, size, serving estimate, and Lapu-Lapu delivery address. Add a landmark and receiver's mobile number, then confirm the delivery window and fee. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow three days' notice and confirm the 50% deposit for the agreed order.

Use an original, permission-cleared close-up for a specific local design case. Otherwise, merge general guidance into the existing design page.

Ang isang palette na tumutugma sa talahanayan, isang maikling mensahe, o isang maliit na motif ay maaaring gawing personal ang cake nang hindi nagdaragdag ng hindi nauugnay na mga dekorasyon. Magpadala ng White Cake ng isang larawan at ipaliwanag kung ano ang dapat nitong iakma. Kung gusto ng pamilya ng lokal o kultural na detalye, ilarawan ito nang tumpak at tanungin ang panaderya kung maaari itong katawanin. Kumpirmahin ang huling disenyo at quote; ang isang reference na larawan ay hindi isang pangako ng isang eksaktong kopya.
