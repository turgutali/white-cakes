## Send the vintage reference early enough to confirm the piping details

Choose the date and size first, then send a clear reference showing the desired vintage style. Identify the border or piping detail that matters and confirm the exact message. Detailed decoration may need discussion before the bakery can confirm availability for the date.

White Cakes’ current guidance asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Contact the bakery earlier for a detailed vintage finish or busy date. Request the full quote, flavour availability, delivery or pickup plan, and final design summary before following the stated 50% deposit process.

Keep lead-time advice in one authoritative order guide and confirm the current policy before publishing. Do not treat the reference image as a promise of identical piping.
