## Identify the piping feature you want the bakery to focus on

Hand-piped work may refer to a border, lettering, flowers, or another decorative detail. Choose one reference photo that clearly shows the feature and explain what you like about it. Ask the bakery how it might adapt that feature to your cake’s size, colour palette, and requested date.

Keep the brief practical: include the occasion, guest estimate, flavour, exact inscription, budget, and delivery or pickup arrangement. Confirm the full design summary before paying. Do not assume a close-up from another bakery represents a finish White Cakes can reproduce exactly.

Add the guidance to the Cebu design page. A standalone technique article needs genuine close-ups of White Cakes’ own work and a specific, confirmed explanation of the piping shown.

## Tukuyin ang tampok na piping na gusto mong pagtuunan ng pansin ng panaderya

Maaaring tumukoy ang gawaing hand-piped sa isang hangganan, titik, bulaklak, o iba pang detalye ng dekorasyon. Pumili ng isang reference na larawan na malinaw na nagpapakita ng feature at ipaliwanag kung ano ang gusto mo tungkol dito. Kumpirmahin ang buong buod ng disenyo bago magbayad. Huwag ipagpalagay na ang close-up mula sa ibang panaderya ay kumakatawan sa isang tapusin na maaaring magparami nang eksakto ang White Cakes.
