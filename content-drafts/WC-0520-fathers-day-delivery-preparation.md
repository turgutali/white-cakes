## Prepare a Father's Day cake handoff

Choose a family member or venue contact who can receive the cake during the agreed window. Give White Cakes the complete address, landmark, mobile number, and any gate or building directions. If it is a surprise, tell the bakery whether the recipient can be contacted and provide a second local contact if needed.

Confirm delivery availability, timing, and the fee for that address. Decide where the cake will be kept after arrival; a stable shaded place is better than a sunny table or hot vehicle. If there is a long wait before serving, follow White Cakes' storage guidance.

Include the event date, size, flavour, design, and inscription in the order request. Ask about date availability early, since made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice and holiday capacity may vary. Confirm the design and total quote before paying the 50% deposit that secures the agreed order.

Keep these delivery steps in the shared guide. Add any Father's Day-specific capacity or route advice only when the bakery verifies it.
