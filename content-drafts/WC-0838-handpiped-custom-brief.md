## Brief a cake with hand-piped decoration clearly

Start with the event date, size, flavour, and number of cake eaters. Then name the one or two piped details that matter most—such as flowers, lettering, or a border. If everything is marked essential, the bakery cannot tell which feature should lead the design.

Attach one reference image and explain what to adapt. Ask White Cakes whether the technique is available for the chosen size and what it will cost. A reference photo may show work from another baker and does not guarantee an exact replica. Confirm the final inscription and check that it fits the surface.

Include delivery or pickup details and the time the cake will be served. Ask about storage if it will wait several hours, since buttercream can soften in warm conditions. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design and terms are settled.

Keep this technique-specific prompt in the central custom brief guide. Use a dedicated page only when original photos and distinct information support it.
