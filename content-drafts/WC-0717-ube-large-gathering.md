## Confirm how many people will be served and whether ube is available

Estimate the guests who will actually take cake and whether the gathering includes a meal or other desserts. Tell the bakery whether you want modest or party-size slices and ask which available cake size fits. The flavour choice does not determine the number of servings.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate. Confirm portion expectations and current ube availability for the date before ordering.

Keep serving ranges in the central size guide and the current flavour list in the flavour guide. Link the two pages rather than duplicating the same estimates in an ube-specific article.
