## How to choose a flavour for baby shower cake in Cebu

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask what the selected flavour means in the current menu: the name alone does not promise a separate filling, fruit layer or topping. Confirm the cake construction and raise any ingredient or allergy question directly.

For a mixed-age gathering, choose from the bakery’s confirmed options and tell the host what flavour is being served. Do not assume an unlisted combination or special dietary version is available without written confirmation. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
