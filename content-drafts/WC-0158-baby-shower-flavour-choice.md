## Choose a baby-shower cake flavour with the guests in mind

Ask the parents-to-be whether they have a favourite flavour, then consider how many guests will share the cake. White Cakes' current menu lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm the chosen flavour is available for the date and size; do not assume every option works with every design.

If the cake is part of a large dessert table, the host may want a flavour that most guests will recognize. Ask White Cakes for current menu details rather than inventing descriptions or pairings. If anyone has an allergy or dietary restriction, contact the bakery directly about ingredients and preparation; do not infer safety from the flavour name.

Plan size using the actual cake-eater count. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. These are approximate. Include the date, design, inscription, delivery address, and receiver's contact in the order request. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified flavour options in the central guide and link to the size page rather than making a separate baby-shower flavour article.
