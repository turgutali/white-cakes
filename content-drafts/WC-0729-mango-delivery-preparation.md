## Coordinate the address, handoff, and storage plan before delivery

Share the full address, requested handoff window, recipient’s name and phone number, and any building or gate instructions. Ask whether delivery to that location is available and confirm the fee and timing. If the cake is going to an event venue, explain when it will be served and who can receive it.

Ask White Cakes for care instructions for the particular mango cake, since handling depends on the cake and decoration. Have the recipient take it promptly, keep the box level, and protect it from direct sun while waiting. Do not assume a storage time based only on the flavour name.

Add general handoff steps to the delivery page and confirmed storage advice to the cake-care guide. Verify routes and product handling with the business before publication.
