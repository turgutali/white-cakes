## Estimate portions without spoiling the surprise

Use the people who will actually eat cake as your starting number. If you cannot ask the recipient, check with a family member or event organizer. Consider whether there are other desserts and whether guests will have small tastes or full slices. Keep the cake plan discreet, but make sure White Cakes has a reliable local contact.

Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate and depend on cutting style. For two or three guests, the 4-inch Bento may be enough; it serves two generously or three modestly. Ask the bakery to confirm the size for your plan.

Include the event date, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery or pickup instructions. If delivery is a surprise, provide the recipient's address and a trusted person who can answer calls without revealing the occasion. Confirm the window and fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Do not infer a guest count or promise a serving number. Use this as a short example in the central size guide.
