## Prepare a clear custom brief for Red Velvet cake

Start with the event date, number of people who will eat cake, size preference, and Red Velvet flavour. White Cakes lists Red Velvet on its current menu; confirm availability for the requested date and design. Do not assume ingredients or dietary suitability from the flavour name—ask the bakery directly if those details matter.

Describe the design with a few priorities: main colour, one focal detail, and exact inscription. Send one reference image and explain what should be adapted. Ask whether the design is feasible for the chosen size and what the quote includes. A photo is not a guarantee of exact reproduction.

Current serving estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Tell White Cakes whether other desserts will be served and ask it to confirm a size. Include delivery or pickup details and the receiver's contact. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour availability in the current menu and use this prompt inside the central custom-brief guide.
