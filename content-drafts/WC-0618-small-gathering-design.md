## Match a small-gathering cake design to the table

A cake for a few people does not need a large design brief. Choose one visual direction that suits the table: a short inscription, one colour palette, a floral finish, or a simple piped border. Send one clear reference photo and tell the baker which part matters. The 4-inch Bento has limited room for writing and decoration, so ask what will remain readable on its surface.

Use the guest count to decide whether the compact size is enough. White Cakes lists the 4-inch Bento for two generous or three modest servings. For a group that needs more portions, use the existing size guide to compare the 6-inch and larger formats, then ask the bakery to confirm the estimate for the selected design.

Include the date, flavour, exact message, and pickup or delivery preference. If delivery is needed, provide the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery must confirm availability and the final quote.

This checklist belongs in the shared cake-design and size guides. A separate photo case needs a real approved image of a small-gathering cake and a design decision specific to that occasion.
