## Prepare the monthsary cake handoff

If the cake is a surprise, choose a trusted local person who can receive it and give White Cakes that person's mobile number. Provide the complete address, landmark, and gate or building instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee, and decide whether the driver may call the recipient or should contact someone else.

Make sure the receiver can bring the cake to a stable place away from direct sunlight and heat. For a 4-inch Bento, the box is small but still needs a level surface and careful handoff. If the cake will wait several hours, follow the bakery's care instructions and arrange refrigeration if advised.

Include the event date, flavour, short inscription, design, and pickup or delivery plan in your order. Confirm availability and final terms. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep general handoff advice in the Cebu delivery guide. Do not promise special surprise delivery handling unless White Cakes confirms it.
