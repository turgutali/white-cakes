## Pick the flavour for the people sharing this small cake

White Cakes describes the 4-inch Bento as about two generous servings or three modest ones. For a cake shared by a couple or a small family, consider what the people eating it already enjoy and ask which flavours are available for your date. The current listed choices have included Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm the live menu rather than relying on an old post.

Tell the bakery about allergies or dietary needs and ask directly whether it can accommodate them. A flavour name does not confirm ingredients or allergen safety. Confirm the cake’s design, message, price, and pickup or delivery plan separately.

Keep flavour and serving details in the main guides and cross-link them from the Bento product page. Do not create a standalone flavour article for every product size.
