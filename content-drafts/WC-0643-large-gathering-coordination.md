## Coordinate a cake delivery for a large family gathering

Name one person as the cake receiver and share their mobile number with White Cakes. Give the full venue or home address, landmark, gate instructions, and the agreed delivery window. A large family event may have several people arriving and different entrances; make sure the driver knows who to call and where the handoff should happen.

Confirm the serving plan and cake size before delivery. Current estimates are 8–12 portions for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. They are approximate, so ask the bakery to confirm based on how the family will cut the cake. Tell the receiver where to place it away from direct sun and heat.

Share the event date, flavour, design, and inscription in the order enquiry. Confirm delivery availability and fee for that address. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Publish a local delivery case only if the actual venue route and handoff facts are verified. Otherwise, keep the instructions in the central delivery guide.
