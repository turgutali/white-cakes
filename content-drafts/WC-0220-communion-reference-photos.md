## Which reference photos help when ordering First Communion cake in Cebu

Choose one image that shows the overall silhouette, then add a separate close-up only for a detail that matters. Explain what you like in each image—colour, border, flower placement or lettering—and identify what is flexible.

A reference is a direction, not a promise of an exact replica. White Cakes adapts designs to hand-piped buttercream, cake size and practical constraints. Ask for the bakery’s written interpretation before approving the final brief. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
