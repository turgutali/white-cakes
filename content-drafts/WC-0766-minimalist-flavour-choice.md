## Choose the flavour for the recipient, not from the cake’s simple appearance

A minimalist finish does not indicate what flavour or filling is inside. Ask White Cakes which current options are available for your date and what each includes. Its menu has included Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; verify the live list before choosing.

Consider the recipient’s preference and how many people will share the cake. Mention allergies or dietary requirements and ask directly whether the bakery can accommodate them. Confirm size, message, and design separately; the simple exterior does not determine servings.

Keep current flavours in one maintained guide and link to the minimalist design article for appearance. Do not make a separate flavour URL for each finish.
