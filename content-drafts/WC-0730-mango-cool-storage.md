## Ask for care instructions for the exact mango cake you ordered

Storage advice can depend on the filling, frosting, decoration, and time before serving. Ask White Cakes how to handle the specific mango cake and tell the bakery how long it may wait at the venue. If the event is outdoors or the room is warm, mention that when requesting guidance.

Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight while waiting for service. Arrange for a named person to receive the cake promptly and follow the baker’s instructions about refrigeration or room temperature. Do not use a generic safe-time estimate for every mango cake.

Add verified handling advice to the central Cebu cake-care page. Check that any ingredient or storage statement is approved by the bakery before publishing.
