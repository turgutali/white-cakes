## Confirm vanilla availability and the handoff date before approving the design

Choose the event date first and ask whether the current vanilla option is available. Then share the size, guest estimate, design, exact wording, and pickup or delivery arrangement. Ask the bakery what the flavour includes rather than assuming from the name.

White Cakes’ current guidance asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Contact earlier for detailed decoration or a busy date and wait for confirmation. Request a complete quote and final order summary before following the stated 50% deposit process.

Keep the order timeline in one central guide and flavour availability in the live menu. Verify both before publication to avoid outdated instructions.
