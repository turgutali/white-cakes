## Identify the vintage details you want adapted to the cake

Vintage references may show different piping, borders, palettes, and lettering. Select one image for the overall look and tell the bakery which details matter most. If a particular border or message is essential, state it clearly; mark the other parts as flexible inspiration.

Include the date, size, serving estimate, flavour, budget, exact inscription, and handoff plan. Ask whether the chosen design is possible for the cake dimensions and production schedule, then confirm the final description and quote in writing. A reference photo does not guarantee an identical reproduction.

Keep the checklist in the shared custom-brief guide and link to current design examples. Use only photos of real, approved cakes when showing vintage finishes.
