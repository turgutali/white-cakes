## Care for a custom buttercream cake before serving

Keep the boxed cake level and away from direct sunlight, warm appliances, and a hot vehicle. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so choose a cool place for it to wait. If the cake will be held for several hours, follow White Cakes' storage guidance for the specific finish and ask whether refrigeration is recommended.

Tell the person receiving the cake where to place it and when it will be served. Avoid moving the box around or opening it repeatedly. If the event is outdoors, identify an indoor or shaded holding place and check with the bakery before leaving the cake in the heat. Do not rely on an assumed safe time at room temperature.

When ordering, mention the design, delivery location, and expected time between arrival and serving. Confirm the final care instructions with White Cakes, along with the date and quote. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep confirmed storage rules in one central heat-care guide, not repeated across each custom-cake page.
