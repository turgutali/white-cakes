## Estimate New Year cake portions from the people who will eat

New Year gatherings often have several dishes and desserts, so the guest count alone may not tell you how much cake to order. Estimate how many people will actually take a slice and whether you want small tastes or a full party portion. Share that plan with the bakery and ask for a size recommendation.

White Cakes' current guide gives approximate ranges: 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These figures depend on how the cake is cut; they should not be stretched into an exact promise for a specific gathering. If the count is near the top of a range, confirm the plan before ordering.

Holiday dates also need early scheduling. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, but New Year's demand may mean you should enquire earlier. Include the date, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery address. Confirm availability and final terms before the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.
