## Plan bridal-shower cake servings from the actual guest count

Count the guests who will eat cake and decide whether it will be served as a full party slice or as a smaller dessert after a meal. White Cakes' guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, 16–24 from an 8-inch round, and roughly 30–36 from a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier cake. The 4-inch Bento is much smaller: two generous portions or three modest slices. All these numbers are planning ranges; the actual cut and design can change the count.

If the shower is a small gathering, a Bento may suit a table of a few people. For a larger group, share the headcount and ask whether a 6-inch, 8-inch, or two-tier cake is a better fit. Mention if other desserts will be served or if you want slices to take home.

Choose a design after settling the size. Bouquet Cake offers a floral finish, while custom buttercream can follow a clear colour palette and reference. The actual scale and quote should be confirmed before paying. Include the event date, flavour, short inscription, delivery address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number in your enquiry.

Use this portion advice in the existing cake-size guide and link it from the bridal-shower page. A separate serving chart by occasion would repeat the same size estimates and risk giving inconsistent counts.
