## How to plan Sinulog cake in Cebu

Decide the cake’s job first: feed the gathering, mark a candle or photo moment, or do both. Note when it will be served and what other desserts are planned before choosing size and design.

Put one person in charge of the final brief and handoff. Confirm date, serving count, wording, quote, delivery recipient and care plan together so the event team works from one agreed version. For a Sinulog gathering, confirm venue access, road or event restrictions and the actual service time with the organizer before setting delivery expectations. Use a palette or motif the bakery confirms it can hand-pipe; do not assume a parade route or road is accessible.

Pilia ang usa ka tawo nga modumala sa final brief ug pagdawat. Kumpirmaha dungan ang petsa, serving, teksto, quote, modawat sa delivery ug pag-atiman aron usa ra ka bersyon ang gamiton sa team. Gamita ang palette o motif nga makumpirma sa panaderya nga mahimo sa hand-piping; ayaw paghunahuna nga agianan ang parade route.
