## Confirm the Mango cake order details before paying

Check whether White Cakes has confirmed Mango for the selected size and date. The flavour appears on the current menu, but availability should be checked for the actual order. Do not assume the word Mango specifies fresh fruit, a particular filling, or other ingredients; ask the bakery if those details matter to you.

Review the written confirmation for the event date, cake size, flavour, agreed design, exact inscription, total price, and pickup or delivery plan. If you sent a reference image, make sure the final interpretation is clear. For delivery, verify the full address, landmark, receiving contact, time window, and address-based fee.

White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Its current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in the current quote and keep the receipt with the confirmation. Ask the bakery about changes or balance timing before paying if those terms are not clear.

Keep flavour-specific checks in the central ordering guide and verify payment terms before publication.
