## Make sure someone can receive the cake at the agreed time

Provide the complete address, requested handoff window, receiver’s name and phone number, and any gate or building access details. Ask whether delivery to the location is available and confirm the fee and timing. If the anniversary dinner is at a restaurant, share its receiving rules and who will meet the delivery.

Ask the bakery for handling advice for the exact cake and schedule. The receiver should take it directly, keep the box level, and follow instructions before serving. If the dinner starts later than the delivery, tell the bakery when you inquire.

Add general coordination steps to the central Cebu delivery guide. Do not promise a restaurant or venue route without confirmation.
