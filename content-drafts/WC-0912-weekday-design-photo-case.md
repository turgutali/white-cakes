## Weekday celebration cake case-study framework

**Publish only with a real, approved White Cakes project.** Find a weekday event cake with a verified order record and original photos. Confirm permission for the images and any workplace or venue identification. An anonymized case can still be useful, but do not imply a client name, date, or response without permission and evidence.

Document what made the weekday project distinctive. It might be a short office celebration, a specific delivery window, or a design that fit a small gathering. Use the actual brief and bakery confirmation to explain the choices. Do not claim that weekday ordering is faster, cheaper, or more available unless the business verifies that statement.

Use original photos with captions that describe visible details, then link to the current design, ordering, and delivery guides. The case must teach a specific lesson beyond generic advice and should not repeat a portfolio caption as an article.

If no verified case and permission are available, keep this page unpublished. Add only confirmed general office-delivery information to the existing guides.
