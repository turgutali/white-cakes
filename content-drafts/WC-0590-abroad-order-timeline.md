## Start with the Cebu event date and give the local receiver time to coordinate

Ask the family in Cebu when the cake is needed, who will receive it, and what design and size they want. Share the date, local address, receiver’s phone number, flavour, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery plan with the bakery. Keep the local organizer included in the final written order summary.

White Cakes’ current guidance asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Contact earlier for a custom design or busy date and wait for availability confirmation. Ask about the current accepted payment method and total price rather than assuming an overseas payment option.

Keep lead-time steps in the order guide and the local handoff in the delivery guide. Verify both before publication.
