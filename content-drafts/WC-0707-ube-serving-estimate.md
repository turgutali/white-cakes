## Estimate servings for an Ube cake using the event plan

The flavour does not determine how many people a cake serves. Start with the expected number of cake eaters and decide whether portions will be small or generous. If other desserts are available, tell White Cakes so it can help choose a suitable size.

Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; the way the cake is cut changes the result. For a small group, a 4-inch Bento provides about two generous or three modest portions. Confirm Ube availability for the chosen size and date.

If you are serving guests with allergies or dietary restrictions, ask the bakery about the ingredients and preparation directly. Do not treat “Ube” as a safety or ingredient statement. Include the date, design, serving plan, and delivery details in your enquiry. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour-specific portion questions in the central size guide and recheck all serving estimates before publishing.
