## Keep the decoration distinct from assumptions about the cake inside

When planning a red velvet cake design, decide whether the outside should use a simple colour accent, a border, or a short inscription. Share a reference and identify the detail that matters most. The decoration’s colour does not confirm the cake’s ingredients, filling, or flavour; check the current product description with the bakery.

Tell the bakery the date, size, serving estimate, budget, and exact wording. Ask which finish is available and how it fits the selected cake shape. Confirm the current quote and handling advice before ordering.

Add the design prompt to the central Cebu guide. A separate close-up article requires genuine White Cakes photos and a verified hand-finished detail that helps readers.
