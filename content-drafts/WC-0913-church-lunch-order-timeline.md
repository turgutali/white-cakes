## Plan the cake around the service time and the lunch venue

Confirm the ceremony date, lunch location, and time the cake should be served. If the cake moves between venues, name the person who will receive it and share the exact handoff point. Settle the size, flavour, design, and message early enough for the bakery to confirm availability.

White Cakes’ current guidance calls for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Inquire sooner for a custom design or busy date, and ask about the full quote and delivery availability. The stated deposit process uses 50% to confirm the order; verify current instructions before paying.

Keep the timeline in one authoritative guide and verify all current booking details before publication.
