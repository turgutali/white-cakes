## Avoid common mix-ups when ordering Ube cake

Do not assume Ube is available for every size or custom design just because it appears on the menu. Confirm availability for the date and configuration. If you need to know ingredients, filling, or dietary suitability, ask White Cakes directly rather than inferring from the flavour name.

Avoid requesting a design from a photo without explaining which detail you want. One reference and a short note about colour or decoration give the bakery a clearer brief. Ask what can be adapted and what the quote includes; an image does not guarantee an exact copy or fixed price.

Do not wait until the last minute. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice. Confirm the size, serving plan, flavour, exact inscription, delivery address, receiver, and fee. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; ask the bakery to confirm which is appropriate.

The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after details are settled. Use these reminders in the central order guide, not as an invented customer story.
