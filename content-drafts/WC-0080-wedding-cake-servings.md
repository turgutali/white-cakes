## English

# How to estimate servings for wedding cake in Cebu

Estimate wedding cake servings from confirmed reception guests and how slices will be served. After dinner, during cocktails, or for photos only can each change the amount needed. White Cakes does not publish tier diagrams; share the count and ask what size and finish the bench can do for the date.

Do not infer portions from a wedding cake photo. Confirm the serving plan directly.
