## Estimate wedding-cake servings from the dessert plan

Use the number of people expected to eat cake, not the total RSVP count by itself. Ask whether every guest will be offered a slice, whether there are other desserts, and whether the couple wants generous portions. These details can change the size more than a single generic chart suggests.

White Cakes' current guide estimates around 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. The numbers are planning ranges, not a wedding guarantee. Serving style, cake height, and slice size affect yield. Share the actual guest and dessert plan with the bakery and ask it to confirm an appropriate configuration.

For a wedding enquiry, also include the event date, venue address, delivery window, flavour preferences, design references, and the person who will receive the cake. Ask about delivery availability and the fee for that location. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, but a wedding date should be discussed earlier so design and logistics can be confirmed. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the details are settled.

Do not publish a more detailed tier-by-tier serving chart until White Cakes verifies it against its actual cake dimensions and cutting method. Add the wedding context to the central size guide and link to the wedding-cake page.
