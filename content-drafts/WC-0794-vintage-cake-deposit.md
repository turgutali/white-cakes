## Confirm the vintage-cake brief before the deposit

“Vintage” can mean different things to different people: piped borders, layered colour, rosettes, or a particular lettering style. Before paying, send White Cakes one reference image and point out which detail you want interpreted. The bakery hand-pipes buttercream; a reference informs the brief but does not guarantee an exact copy. Ask whether the requested finish can be made on the chosen size and for your date.

Confirm the date, size, flavour, exact inscription, and full design direction in writing. Share the guest count so the bakery can help you choose a sensible size, then ask for the current quote. A vintage look does not set a fixed price because the design and finish can vary. If delivery is needed, confirm the address-based charge, delivery window, full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number.

White Cakes requests at least three days' notice. The date is not reserved until the bakery replies with availability and confirms the order details. Its ordering guidance says a 50% deposit confirms an agreed order. Before paying, make sure the amount, payment method, remaining balance details, and pickup or delivery plan are clear.
