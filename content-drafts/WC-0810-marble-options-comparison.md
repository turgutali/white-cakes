## Compare the current product description with the design changes you want

First check which marble cake options the bakery currently offers, including size, colour palette, border, and inscription. If a listed product already matches your needs, ask whether it is available for your date. If you want a variation, state the specific change and confirm any effect on price or production time.

Compare size and serving needs independently from the marble effect. Ask the bakery to confirm expected portions, flavour availability, and whether the requested pattern suits the cake shape. Use a reference image to explain the direction, not to guarantee an exact copy.

Update the existing product-comparison page with current options and link to the design guide. Do not split one marble comparison into several near-identical URLs.
