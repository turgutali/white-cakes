## Size the cake for the children and adults who will have a slice

Estimate how many guests will still be present when the cake is cut. A children’s party may include adults, siblings, and other desserts, so the invitation count alone may overstate or understate portions. Tell the bakery whether you want modest slices or larger servings and ask it to confirm a practical size.

The current guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake; a 4-inch Bento is about two generous or three modest servings. These are approximate. Confirm portions for the design and date, and keep the cake away from children until an adult is ready to serve it.
