## Keep a mango-cake design clear and balanced

When planning a mango cake, first decide whether the flavour or the decoration should lead the design. A simple colour palette and one focal detail can make a custom finish feel considered without crowding the surface. Tell White Cakes what matters most—a particular colour, a short message, or a decorative element—and ask what is feasible for the chosen size.

White Cakes lists Mango among its current flavours. Confirm its availability for the date and configuration you want; the site should not imply fresh fruit, a specific filling, or a flavour profile unless the bakery has verified those details. If you send a reference photo, identify the one feature you want adapted rather than asking for an exact copy.

Share the serving plan too. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, though actual yield changes with slice size. Include the event date, inscription, and delivery or pickup plan. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
