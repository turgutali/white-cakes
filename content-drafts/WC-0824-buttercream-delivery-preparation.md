## Give the bakery enough information to plan a careful handoff

Share the full destination, preferred delivery window, event start time, and recipient’s phone number. Note building access, parking, gate, or reception instructions that could affect the handoff. Ask whether the bakery can deliver to the address and confirm the final fee and timing before the order is booked.

Buttercream decorations can be affected by heat, direct sun, and a long wait after arrival. Ask the baker how this particular cake should be transported and stored, and arrange for someone to receive it promptly. Keep the box level and follow the product-specific care instructions instead of relying on a generic rule.

Keep route and handoff guidance in the delivery page, with verified storage advice in the care guide. Do not promise that a design is heatproof or name a delivery time without bakery confirmation.
