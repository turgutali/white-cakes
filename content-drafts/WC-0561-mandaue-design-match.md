## Match a family-cake design to the Mandaue gathering

Start with the recipient or family's preferences and the event setting. A home lunch, milestone birthday, and reunion may call for different visual emphasis. Choose one main idea—colour, floral detail, or a short family message—and let the rest of the design support it.

Send one reference image and identify what should be adapted. If the family has several preferences, agree on the main direction before sending the brief. Ask White Cakes whether the details are feasible for the requested size and what price is confirmed. Do not assume that an online image can be copied exactly.

Include the event date, flavour, inscription, serving estimate, and complete Mandaue delivery address. Add a landmark and receiver's mobile number; confirm the delivery window and address-based fee. The current guide estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the agreed order with the 50% deposit.

Use original, approved photos for a Mandaue design case. Otherwise, add only the general principles to the existing cake-design guide.
