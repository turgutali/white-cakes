## Match a birthday-cake design to the celebrant

Start with something the celebrant genuinely likes: a colour, hobby, flower, or simple theme. Choose one main feature and let the other decoration support it. A cake can feel personal without reproducing an entire invitation or combining every reference image.

Send one or two photos and explain what to adapt. Ask White Cakes whether the requested details are feasible for the selected size and what the quote includes. A reference does not guarantee an exact copy. If you want lettering, provide the exact words and ask whether they fit the planned design.

Consider where the cake will be displayed and who will share it. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Tell the bakery the number of cake eaters, flavour, date, and delivery plan. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the 50% deposit after the order is agreed.

Use an original, approved White Cakes photo for a specific birthday design feature. Otherwise, add general advice to the existing design guide.
