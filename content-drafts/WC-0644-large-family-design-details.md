## Keep a large family cake design readable

A larger cake has more room for decoration, but adding every family member's suggestion can make the design hard to read. Agree on one main idea—such as a colour palette, floral detail, or short family message—and let the remaining details support it. Choose a focal point that can be seen from where the cake will be displayed.

Send a reference and state which parts are essential. Ask White Cakes to confirm that the design works for the chosen size and what it will cost. If the cake includes names or a family phrase, provide the final wording and ask how it can fit without overwhelming the decoration.

Size should follow the number of cake eaters. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions from 6 inches, 16–24 from 8 inches, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. The figures are approximate; confirm the serving plan and the effect of the requested design. Include the date and delivery details, allow at least three days' notice, and secure the agreed order with the 50% deposit.

Use original close-ups only when they show a real White Cakes cake and the design detail is verified. Otherwise, add the general principle to the existing design guide.
