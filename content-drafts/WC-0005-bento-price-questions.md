## Confirm what the Bento price includes for your date

When asking about a 4-inch Bento price, share the requested date, flavour, design, and inscription. Ask whether the current price includes the decoration shown on the product listing or whether custom changes affect the total. Confirm whether pickup or delivery is planned and whether any delivery fee applies.

White Cakes describes this size as serving about two generously or three modestly, but confirm the current product details with the bakery. Prices, flavours, and availability can change. Request the full amount in writing before paying the stated deposit.

Keep the live price on the product or central price page and update it when the menu changes. Avoid repeating a stale figure in a separate Bento pricing article.
