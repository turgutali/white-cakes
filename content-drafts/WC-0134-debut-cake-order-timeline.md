## How to plan the order timeline for debut cake in Cebu

Choose the date first, then request a production slot. White Cakes asks for at least three days’ notice; a debut cake with several tiers or detailed piping should be discussed much earlier. A message is a request, not a reservation: wait for written confirmation of the date and design.

For an efficient first inquiry, send the event date, venue area, estimated guests, preferred size, one design reference and the time the cake must be ready. Confirm delivery window and recipient before paying the 50% deposit on the agreed quote.
