## Build a debut-cake timeline around the confirmed event date

Start by sending the exact debut date to White Cakes and asking whether it is available. The bakery requests at least three days' notice, but a multi-tier or detailed design should be discussed earlier when possible. Do not assume that a date is reserved until the bakery confirms the production slot and order details.

Share the expected cake-eaters, preferred size, flavour, and one clear design reference. If you have a colour palette, dress detail, or short inscription in mind, describe it separately from the photo. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, so the reference is interpreted within the chosen cake size and finish. Ask what can be adapted before treating a particular look as confirmed.

Give the venue address, landmark, a reachable receiver, and any gate or loading instructions if delivery is needed. Confirm the delivery window and address-based charge with the quote. Make sure the person responsible for the cake table can receive the box and keep it out of direct sun until the programme reaches dessert.

The 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the date, design, price, and logistics are clear. This event-specific timing note can extend the existing debut and general lead-time guides; it should not become a separate page unless search data shows a distinct debut-ordering question.
