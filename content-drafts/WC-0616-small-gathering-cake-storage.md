## Care for a small cake before the gathering

Even a compact cake can soften if it waits in a warm room. Keep the box out of direct sunlight, away from a hot car, and on a stable level surface. If the cake will be held for several hours, refrigerate it following White Cakes' care guidance. Avoid putting heavy items on the box or moving it between warm and cold places repeatedly.

For a small gathering, decide where the cake will be served and when. If it arrives early, identify refrigerator space before delivery and tell the person receiving it how to handle the box. Ask the bakery whether the cake should sit out briefly before serving; the right timing can depend on the cake and room temperature, so do not apply a generic time rule.

When ordering, confirm size and servings as well as the care plan. A 4-inch Bento is intended for two generous servings or three modest portions, while a 6-inch cake is estimated at 8–12 party slices. These are planning estimates. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and the agreed order is confirmed with a 50% deposit.

Keep product-specific storage details in one heat-care guide and publish only handling instructions the bakery has confirmed.
