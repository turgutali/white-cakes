## Confirm what the bakery currently means by its mango option

Ask whether the mango flavour is available for your requested date and what the current cake option includes. Do not infer the filling, fruit pieces, or ingredients from a product name or an old photograph. If a specific mango preparation matters to you, ask the bakery directly before placing the order.

Choose based on the recipient’s preference and who will share the cake. Mention any allergy or dietary concern and ask whether the bakery can accommodate it; never assume ingredient safety from a flavour label. Confirm portions and size separately from flavour.

Keep the current menu and clarification questions in one maintained flavour guide. Update product details only after checking with the bakery.
