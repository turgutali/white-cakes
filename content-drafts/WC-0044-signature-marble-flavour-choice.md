## Verify the listed Signature Marble flavour before planning the order

Signature Marble refers to a particular product design, but the current listing should be checked to confirm the flavour included. Ask White Cakes whether the product can be ordered for your date and whether any flavour change is possible. Do not infer the inside of the cake from its ivory and blue exterior.

Choose based on the recipient’s preference and who will share it. If anyone has an allergy or dietary restriction, ask the bakery directly about ingredients and accommodations. Confirm the size, serving plan, exact message, current price, and delivery or pickup separately.

Keep current flavour details in the flavour guide and verified Signature Marble description on its product page. Update them from the live menu before publication.
