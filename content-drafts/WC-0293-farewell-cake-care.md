## Keep an office farewell cake cool before the team gathers

If the cake arrives before the farewell event, arrange a cool, level place to store it. Keep the box away from direct sunlight, warm equipment, and a parked car. Ask White Cakes whether the specific design should be refrigerated and follow its advice if several hours will pass before serving.

Choose a colleague to accept the delivery and move it to storage. Share the building address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any lobby or loading instructions with the bakery. Confirm the delivery window and fee; an office contact should be available rather than expecting a driver to wait through a meeting.

Include the date, flavour, size, design, inscription, and number of cake eaters in the order request. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; confirm the size with the bakery. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified storage guidance in the central heat-care article. Do not publish an exact safe holding time without bakery confirmation.
