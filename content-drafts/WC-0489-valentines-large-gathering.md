## Decide who will share the cake before choosing a size

A Valentine’s cake may be a private dessert for two or part of a larger family or office celebration. Count the people who will actually eat cake and consider whether it follows a meal. Tell the bakery whether you want modest portions or party slices, then ask which current size fits.

The 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous or three modest servings; confirm this and current availability for the date. For a larger group, use the central size guide’s approximate ranges and ask the bakery to confirm. Valentine’s dates can be busy, so inquire early.
