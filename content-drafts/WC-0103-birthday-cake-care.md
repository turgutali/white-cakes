## Keep a birthday cake cool until it is time to serve

Choose a level place for the cake, away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm appliances. If the cake arrives early, follow White Cakes' care instructions and arrange refrigerator space if the bakery recommends it. Do not assume every cake can wait for the same amount of time in a warm room.

Ask a family member or venue contact to receive the cake and move it inside. Keep the box closed and avoid carrying it around before serving. If the party is outdoors, find a shaded or indoor holding location and discuss the plan with the bakery before the event.

When ordering, include the event date, delivery time, location, receiver's mobile number, and expected serving time. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep care instructions in one current heat guide and verify product-specific storage details with White Cakes before publication.
