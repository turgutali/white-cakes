## English

# How to keep safe and cool until serving birthday cake in Cebu

Ask how to store the exact birthday cake before serving and whether refrigeration is appropriate for its finish. Keep it level and away from sunlight and heat. If the venue cannot provide the recommended conditions, discuss delivery timing with the bakery.

Give the instructions to the person receiving the cake and avoid leaving it unattended in a car.

## Cebuano

# Giunsa pagpabiling luwas ug bugnaw hangtod i-serve ang cake para sa adlawng natawhan sa Cebu

Pangutan-a unsaon pagtipig ug angay ba ang refrigerator sa finish. Ipatag ug ipahilayo sa adlaw ug kainit. Kon walay storage ang venue, hisguti ang delivery.

Ihatag ang instruksyon sa modawat ug ayaw ibilin sa sakyanan.
