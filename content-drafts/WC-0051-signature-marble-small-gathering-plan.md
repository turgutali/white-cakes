## Plan Signature Marble for the number of guests

Signature Marble is currently listed as a 6-inch cake, with an estimated 8–12 party portions under the general size guide. If only a few people will share it, they may prefer fuller slices; if there are more guests, ask White Cakes whether another size or cake design is available. Do not assume Signature Marble can be scaled up without confirmation.

The current listing describes an ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. Check the live product details and ask whether any requested changes are possible. If you want a different colour or inscription, request a confirmed quote for the new design.

Share the event date, flavour, serving plan, and pickup or delivery address. Confirm availability and the fee for the destination. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified product portions in the central size guide and link it from the Signature Marble page. Recheck the product listing before publication.
