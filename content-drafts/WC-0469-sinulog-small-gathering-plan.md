## Plan a Sinulog cake for the people who will actually share it

Decide how many people will sit down to eat cake. A Sinulog gathering may include street food or other desserts, so the event headcount does not automatically equal the cake-serving count. Tell White Cakes whether you want small portions or full party slices and ask for a suitable size.

Current planning ranges are 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. The actual count depends on cutting style. If only a few people will share cake, the 4-inch Bento provides about two generous or three modest portions.

Check date availability early because a festival may affect the bakery schedule and city access. Include the delivery address, landmark, receiver contact, flavour, and design. Confirm any route limitations, delivery fee, and handoff window rather than assuming usual conditions. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep the size advice in the central guide and publish festival logistics only when verified with the bakery.
