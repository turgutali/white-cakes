## How to compare options for graduation cake in Cebu

Compare options on the same criteria: servings, current total price, achievable finish and handoff logistics. Ask for one or two sizes that fit the guest count rather than comparing unrelated photographs or designs with different levels of detail.

Clarify whether the cake is meant to feed every guest or serve as a ceremonial centrepiece. That answer can change the useful size more than a decorative detail; confirm all options against the actual event plan. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
