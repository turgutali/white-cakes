## Compare graduation-cake options by guests, design, and delivery

Use the actual cake-eater count to compare sizes. White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party slices from 6 inches, 16–24 from 8 inches, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; the event may include other desserts or smaller portions. Ask the bakery to confirm the best fit.

Compare the design based on the graduate's preference and school or family context. A colour palette, short message, or one approved school detail may be enough. If using a logo, get permission and provide clean artwork. Ask what custom decorations are feasible and whether they change the quoted price.

For school, office, or venue delivery, compare the handoff plan too. Provide a full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, access instructions, delivery window, and fee confirmation. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once design and logistics are settled.
