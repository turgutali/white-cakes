## Plan a company milestone cake around the room and the receiving team

Before choosing a design, estimate how many colleagues will actually share cake and whether everyone will gather at once. One shared cake may suit a reception or pantry table; several Bento boxes can work when individual portions are easier to distribute. White Cakes' corporate-order guidance asks companies to send the date, quantity, flavours or messages if they vary, and the office delivery details so the kitchen can confirm what it can complete.

Use a short inscription that names the milestone or team. A company colour palette can guide a custom buttercream brief, but White Cakes does not print an edible sheet from a logo file. Send one visual reference and identify the colour or piped detail that matters. Ask what can be recreated by hand before treating the design as confirmed.

For an IT Park or other office delivery, give the building address, a landmark, lobby or floor access instructions, and a named receiver's mobile number. Arrange any pass with reception in advance. The bakery agrees a delivery window rather than an exact minute, so the receiver should be available throughout that period.

Request the current quote and any address-based delivery charge before paying. The stated 50% deposit confirms an agreed order. Keep company-specific planning in the corporate-cake guide and link to the size and price pages for details that apply to every customer.
