## Schedule a weekday cake order around the event time

Start with the date and the time you need the cake, then ask White Cakes to confirm a production slot. A weekday does not automatically mean the bakery is available. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; send your enquiry earlier if the date is important or the design needs custom work.

Include the size, flavour, serving estimate, short inscription, and one design reference in the first message. If the event is at work, state the full building address, floor or receiving point, contact mobile number, and any visitor instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee, and assign a colleague to receive the box.

Ask the bakery to confirm the design, price, and order details in writing. If the cake arrives before the event, decide where it will be kept away from direct sunlight and heat. The current 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after availability and terms are settled.

Keep weekday and event lead-time guidance in the central order timeline. Avoid suggesting a shorter notice period unless the bakery confirms a specific order can be accommodated.

Ang hinimo-sa-pag-order nga mga cake nanginahanglan labing menos tulo ka adlaw nga pahibalo; ipadala ang imong pangutana sa sayo pa kung ang petsa importante o ang disenyo nagkinahanglan og custom nga trabaho.
