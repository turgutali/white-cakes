## Decide whether you want the listed design or a custom variation

The current Signature Marble description has been a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. Check the live product page before ordering to see whether those details are still current. If you want a variation, identify the exact change—such as colour, inscription, or border—and ask the bakery what it can accommodate.

Use a reference only to explain the adjustment, not as a promise of an identical result. Confirm the final design, flavour, requested date, price, and delivery or pickup details in writing. A custom change may affect the quote or availability.

Keep verified product details on the product page and design inspiration in the existing guide. Do not publish an outdated product description as an evergreen article.
