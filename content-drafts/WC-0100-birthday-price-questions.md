## Request a quote that reflects the size and design you want

When asking about a birthday cake price, provide the date, approximate servings, preferred size, flavour, and design idea. Mention the exact inscription and whether you need delivery. This helps the bakery quote the cake you are considering instead of a general starting price that may not include custom details.

Ask whether the amount includes the requested decoration and delivery fee. Prices and flavour availability can change, so confirm the final total in writing before paying. If you have a budget, share it early and ask which current option might fit.

Keep live rates in one maintained Cebu price guide. Birthday-specific pricing should link there rather than repeat values that may go stale.
