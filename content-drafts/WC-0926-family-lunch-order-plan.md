## Choose the cake size and receiver before the church service

Count the family members who will eat cake after lunch and decide if they will have full slices or smaller dessert portions. Use White Cakes' size guide to compare the 4-inch Bento, 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier estimates, then ask the bakery to confirm the best fit. Do not size the cake by the entire invitation list if some guests will not stay for dessert.

Choose a person who can receive the cake while the family is at church or travelling. Share the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or venue instructions. The bakery agrees a delivery window, so the receiver should be available during that time and able to move the cake to a suitable place.

Buttercream cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars. If they will be held for several hours before serving, the ordering guidance says to refrigerate them. Tell the bakery the expected serving time and ask whether the delivery arrangement is practical.

Keep this scenario within the existing size and delivery guides. A separate family-lunch page needs a documented venue constraint that changes the recommendation.
