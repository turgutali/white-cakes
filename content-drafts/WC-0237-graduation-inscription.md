## What to write on graduation cake in Cebu

Keep the message short, legible and correctly spelled. Send the exact text, capitalization and punctuation in writing; compare names and dates with the invitation or programme before approving the final order.

Ask whether the wording fits the selected cake size and hand-piped finish. If several names or a long phrase matter, decide early what can be shortened so the lettering remains readable in person and in photographs. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
