## Confirm the private contact, order summary, and handoff before paying

For a surprise celebration, choose one organizer to communicate with the bakery. Confirm the date, size, flavour, design, inscription, delivery or pickup details, and which phone number the bakery should call. Make sure the person receiving the cake can be reached without contacting the guest of honour.

Ask for the complete total, including delivery and custom decoration, and confirm the current payment method. White Cakes’ stated process uses a 50% deposit to secure an agreed order; verify the exact amount against the final quote. Save the receipt and written summary so the organizer can keep the surprise on schedule.

Keep these steps in the central order guide. Do not treat an inquiry as a confirmed booking, and do not put sensitive surprise details in messages visible to the recipient.
