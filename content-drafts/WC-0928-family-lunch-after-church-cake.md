## Coordinate a cake handover around a family lunch

When a family goes to church before lunch, delivery and serving may happen at different times. Choose one person who can receive the cake and another who knows where it will be kept until everyone arrives. Tell White Cakes the planned serving time and ask for an order and delivery plan that fits the schedule. Buttercream cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars; if they will be held for several hours, the bakery's guidance says to refrigerate them.

If the cake is delivered to a home or venue, send the full address, a nearby landmark, the receiver's mobile number, and any gate or entrance detail. The bakery agrees a delivery window rather than an exact minute. A named receiver should be ready during that window and should move the cake to a suitable cool place after the handover.

For portions, count the people who will eat cake after lunch and decide whether you want full slices or smaller servings. Use the existing size guide for the 4-inch Bento, 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier estimates, then ask White Cakes to confirm the size for the design. Do not count everyone invited if some will not stay for dessert.

This is a useful family-event scenario for the general delivery and size guides. A separate church-lunch page needs a real, documented venue or timing detail that changes the advice; otherwise the same handover checklist belongs in the canonical guides.
