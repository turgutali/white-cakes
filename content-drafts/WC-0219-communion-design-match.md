## How to match the design of First Communion cake in Cebu

Choose one visual direction that connects to the occasion: a palette, motif or meaningful detail. A cake does not need to repeat every element in the room; a restrained design often reads more clearly in photographs and keeps the celebrant as the focus.

White Cakes makes custom work with hand-piped buttercream rather than printed sheets or fondant sculpture. Share one overall reference and prioritize the details that matter; confirm what can be adapted to the selected size. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
