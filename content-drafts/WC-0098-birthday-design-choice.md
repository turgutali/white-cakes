## Start with the celebrant and select one visual direction

Ask what the birthday person enjoys and choose one detail to guide the cake: a favourite colour, hobby, flower, or short message. Share a reference image and identify which part matters most. The bakery can then advise how the idea might fit the chosen size and production schedule.

Confirm the exact name and wording, flavour, guest estimate, date, budget, and pickup or delivery plan. If there are dietary restrictions, ask directly about ingredients and whether the bakery can accommodate them. The outside design does not determine the cake’s flavour or serving count.

Keep general advice in the central Cebu design guide. Any gallery should show actual White Cakes work with original, approved images and clear captions.
