## Plan the right cake size for family in Cebu while you are abroad

Ask a family member in Cebu how many people will actually share the cake. The person paying from abroad may not know the final guest count or whether other desserts are planned. A local contact can also confirm the preferred flavour, event time, and where the cake should be delivered.

White Cakes' current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. For a smaller gathering, a 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm the right size against the actual meal plan.

Send the recipient's full Cebu address, landmark, and local mobile number, along with the event date and delivery window. Confirm that a trusted person will receive it. Ask about accepted payment methods and currency separately; never assume an overseas payment option. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and confirms the agreed order with a 50% deposit.

Keep portion advice in the central size guide and update overseas logistics only after the bakery confirms its current process.
