## Confirm the retirement cake and delivery terms before payment

Verify that White Cakes has confirmed the event date and production slot. For a made-to-order cake, allow at least three days' notice and check earlier if the retirement event has a fixed office schedule. A quote request by itself does not reserve the date.

Read the order details: size, flavour, design, exact message, total price, and pickup or delivery. If it is being delivered to a workplace, confirm the full address, building entrance, receiving contact, delivery window, and fee. Make sure someone will accept the cake and move it to a cool place before the program.

The current White Cakes process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Pay using the instructions in the current quote and keep the receipt with the confirmation. If a company logo or message is included, check that it is approved and that the final text is correct.

Keep payment and deposit details in the central order guide and recheck them against the current process before publishing.
