## Choose an image that shows the design direction, not an old seasonal menu

For a New Year cake, use a reference to show the colours, message style, or overall mood you want. Tell the bakery which part of the image matters and whether it is flexible. A photo from a past holiday can inspire an idea, but it does not prove that the same product, price, or finish is available this year.

Include the event date, size, serving estimate, flavour, exact wording, and delivery or pickup plan. Ask about the current seasonal schedule and confirm any design adjustments in writing. If the cake will be served at midnight, give the bakery the actual handoff window.
