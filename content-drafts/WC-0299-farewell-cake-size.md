## Matching a farewell cake to the people sharing it

For an office or family send-off, count the people who will actually take cake and decide whether you want full party slices or smaller portions after a meal. The current White Cakes size guide uses these planning ranges: about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, about 16–24 from an 8-inch round, and roughly 30–36 for a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier arrangement. A 4-inch Bento is much smaller: two generous servings or three modest slices. These are estimates, not guaranteed counts; slice size and the design affect the result.

Use the guest count to choose a starting size, then ask the bakery to confirm the plan for the actual cake. Tell them whether several people may want a slice to take home and whether another dessert will be served. If attendance is uncertain, ask what size gives the most sensible buffer rather than ordering by the total invitation list.

For a farewell message, keep the wording short and send the exact spelling. A name, team, or brief thank-you is easier to read than a paragraph. If the design is for a workplace, one colour cue and one reference photo are enough to explain the mood. The cake is made to order, so provide the date and allow at least three days' notice; the kitchen must confirm availability before the order is treated as booked.

If the cake is delivered, provide the office address, landmark, lobby instructions, and a reachable receiver. White Cakes agrees a delivery window and confirms the address-based charge with the quote. This practical portion note belongs in the existing size and corporate-cake guides, where it can remain consistent with the current serving estimates.
