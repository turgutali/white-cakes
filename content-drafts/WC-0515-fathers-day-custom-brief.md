## Give the bakery a clear idea of the recipient and the occasion

A useful custom brief says who the cake is for, what is being celebrated, how many people may share it, and when it is needed. Add the recipient’s interests or favourite colours, plus one reference image if you have one. Explain what you want the cake to communicate: a simple greeting, a hobby-inspired design, or a family joke.

Separate required details from flexible inspiration. For example, the name and message may need to be exact, while the baker can adapt decorative elements to the cake’s surface and production time. Include your preferred flavour, requested size, and delivery or pickup plan, then ask the bakery to confirm availability, price, and the final design summary.

Use this checklist in the shared custom-cake briefing guide rather than publishing another holiday-specific page with the same instructions.
