## Choose for the graduate and the people sharing the cake

Start with the graduate’s preference, then consider who else will have a slice and whether the event includes other desserts. White Cakes’ current listed flavours have included Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet, but the live menu should be checked for the event date. Ask what the chosen flavour includes rather than assuming from its name.

Tell the bakery about allergies or dietary requirements and ask directly whether it can accommodate them. Confirm cake size, portions, design, and message separately; flavour does not determine servings. If the graduation schedule is busy, check availability early and coordinate pickup or delivery with the family organizer.

Keep flavour choices in the maintained guide and link to the size and timeline pages. Do not publish a separate graduation-flavour URL that repeats generic selection advice.
