## How to choose a flavour for graduation cake in Cebu

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask what the selected flavour means in the current menu: the name alone does not promise a separate filling, fruit layer or topping. Confirm the cake construction and raise any ingredient or allergy question directly.

For a mixed-age gathering, choose from the bakery’s confirmed options and tell the host what flavour is being served. Do not assume an unlisted combination or special dietary version is available without written confirmation. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
