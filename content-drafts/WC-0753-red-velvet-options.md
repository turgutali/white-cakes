## Compare the confirmed cake options when choosing red velvet

Red velvet is one of the five flavours listed for White Cakes custom cakes. When you compare it with chocolate, vanilla, ube, or mango, start with what the people at your table prefer. The menu does not specify a particular crumb colour, frosting, or filling for red velvet, so ask about any detail you expect instead of relying on a general cake-shop convention.

Separate the flavour choice from the format. A 4-inch Bento is a compact cake for two generous or three modest servings and is listed with one flavour. Larger 6-inch, 8-inch, or tiered custom cakes are planned using the size guide; if you want more than one flavour in a larger design, ask whether the split is possible for that date and finish.

Send the exact flavour name, date, guest count, size request, and any custom filling or topping as separate details. White Cakes can confirm the menu flavour and answer whether additional requests are available. Wait for that answer and the current quote before paying the 50% deposit.

Use the red velvet page for details the bakery can verify about that flavour, and let the central flavour guide handle comparisons common to all five listed choices. Do not publish a flavour-vs-flavour page unless it adds first-hand tasting notes or another original comparison from White Cakes.
