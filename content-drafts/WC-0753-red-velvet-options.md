## English

# How to compare options for red velvet cake in Cebu

Compare only red velvet options the bakery confirms are currently available. Ask what the name describes, which filling or finish is included, and how size and decoration change the total. Do not compare a simple current quote with a detailed cake in an unrelated photo.

Choose by the event’s priorities—taste, appearance, serving count, or budget—and record the confirmed combination in writing. Request ingredient details directly if needed; flavour names are not a substitute.

## Tagalog

Itanong kung ano ang inilalarawan ng pangalan, aling filling o finish ang kasama, at paano nagbabago ang kabuuan ayon sa sukat at dekorasyon. Huwag ihambing ang simpleng kasalukuyang quote sa detalyadong cake sa ibang larawan.

Pumili ayon sa prayoridad ng event—lasa, itsura, serving, o budget—at itala sa sulat ang kumpirmadong kombinasyon. Direktang humingi ng detalye sa sangkap kung kailangan; hindi kapalit ang flavour name.
