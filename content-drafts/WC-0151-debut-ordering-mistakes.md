## Avoid preventable problems when ordering a debut cake

Do not begin with a design image and leave the date, serving plan, and delivery venue until later. Send the event date, time the cake must arrive, location, approximate number of cake eaters, and desired size together. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; a milestone event deserves an earlier availability check.

Avoid treating a reference photo as a fixed quote. Explain what you want to keep—perhaps the palette, silhouette, or one decorative detail—and what can change. Confirm the flavour, inscription, size, feasible design, and final price in writing. If there are multiple event references, select one primary image so the brief does not point in conflicting directions.

Do not estimate portions solely from the number invited. White Cakes' current planning range is about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are estimates. Tell the bakery whether cake is the only dessert and how you plan to serve it.

For delivery, give the complete venue address, landmark, receiving contact, and agreed window, then verify the fee. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after availability and terms are settled.
