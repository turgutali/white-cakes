## Choose a flavour for the family lunch table

Ask the host which flavours the family enjoys and consider the rest of the meal. White Cakes' current menu lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm availability for the requested date and cake size; not every flavour may be offered for every custom design.

If lunch includes several desserts, the host may want to consider how the cake will be served and how many people will actually eat it. Ask White Cakes for a size recommendation as well as flavour availability. If a family member has an allergy or dietary restriction, ask directly about ingredients and preparation rather than relying on the flavour name.

Include the event date, preferred size, design, inscription, and delivery location in the enquiry. For a church-to-lunch schedule, identify who will receive the cake and where it will be kept before serving. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour facts in the central menu guide and link it from family event content.
