## Compare buttercream cake options by finish, size, and care

First compare the size against the number of cake eaters. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm the best choice for your serving plan.

Then compare the finish. A simple buttercream look, hand-piped flowers, or a custom colour palette can involve different design work. Send one reference image, identify the detail you want, and ask what is feasible and included in the quote. Confirm the flavour from the current menu and whether it is available for that cake configuration.

Consider the setting as well. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so ask about care if the cake will wait before serving or be displayed outdoors. White Cakes' current custom prices are date-sensitive; check the live menu for the current amount. Allow at least three days' notice and use the 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Keep design, price, and care information linked from one cake-choice guide rather than duplicating stale tables.
