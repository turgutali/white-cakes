## Choose reference photos that show the Bouquet Cake details you want

White Cakes' Bouquet Cake is a floral design with hand-piped buttercream rosettes and sage leaves, pink pleated wrapper detail, pearls, and a bow. If you send a reference, choose a photo that makes the detail you want easy to see. Then name that detail in your message: flower shapes, palette, bow, or overall scale. A picture without an explanation can leave the bakery guessing which part matters most.

Use one main image rather than several designs that compete. The bakery can interpret a reference in its hand-piped finish, but an image is not a promise of an exact copy. If you want to change the colours or include a short message, state that separately. Bouquet Cake size and price vary with the requested finish, so provide the date and guest count and ask for the confirmed quote.

If the cake will be delivered, send the receiver's full address, landmark, and mobile number. At least three days' notice is requested; weekends and detailed piping are reasons to ask earlier. Wait for availability and the agreed design before paying the 50% deposit.

Keep this guidance in the existing Bouquet Cake and custom-brief pages. A standalone reference-to-result case should use a real, approved White Cakes cake and explain one visible change made from the inspiration image.
