## English

# Which reference photos help when ordering Bouquet Cake in Cebu

For a Bouquet Cake reference, show the floral arrangement, wrapper detail, or colour palette that matters. The White Cakes design uses buttercream rosettes and sage leaves with a pleated collar, pearls, and bow; identify whether you want to keep that signature arrangement or change a detail.

Send one clear image and note what may be adapted. Avoid references with artificial stems or wafer-paper flowers if you expect hand-piped buttercream.

## Cebuano

Sa Bouquet Cake reference, ipakita ang arrangement, wrapper o kolor nga importante. Buttercream rosettes ug sage leaves ang design uban sa pleated collar, pearls ug bow; isulti kon sundon ba ang signature arrangement o usbon ang detalye.
