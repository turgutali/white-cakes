## Prepare the delivery handoff for red velvet cake

Before confirming delivery, choose someone who can receive the cake at the agreed time. Provide White Cakes with the complete Cebu address, landmark, mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee for the destination. The receiver should know where the cake can be placed after handoff.

Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight or a hot vehicle. If the cake arrives well before serving, follow the bakery's storage advice and use refrigerator space if directed. Do not assume that Red Velvet needs different care from other cakes unless White Cakes confirms that for the product.

Include the event date, requested size, Red Velvet flavour, design, inscription, and serving estimate in the order enquiry. Confirm flavour availability, price, delivery area, and timing. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified handoff and care instructions in the shared Cebu delivery and cake-care guides. Do not add a flavour-specific handling claim without first-party confirmation.
