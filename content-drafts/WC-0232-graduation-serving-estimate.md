## How to estimate servings for graduation cake in Cebu

Estimate portions from the serving plan, not from a photograph. White Cakes’ guide gives about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch round, 16–24 for an 8-inch round, and 30–36 for a common 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate and vary with slice size, height and design.

Tell the bakery whether cake is the main dessert, one of several sweets, or a ceremonial cut. Share the guest count and whether everyone needs a full slice; ask the team to confirm the best size for the exact design. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
