## Use the celebration headcount at cake time, not the ceremony total

Graduation guests may move between the ceremony, photos, and a family meal. Estimate how many people will be present when dessert is served and whether the cake is the only sweet. Tell the bakery whether you want modest dessert portions or party slices, then ask it to recommend a size.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; confirm the actual plan and cutting style with the bakery. Do not assume these sizes cover an entire large graduation event.

Keep verified ranges in the central size guide and link the delivery guide for venue scheduling.
