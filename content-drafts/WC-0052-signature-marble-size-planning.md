## English

# How to plan Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Plan a Signature Marble order by confirming the date, listed 6-inch design, flavour, message, and delivery address. If you need a different size or colour, explain that before requesting the quote because it may become a custom design.

Confirm the price and availability for the calendar date, then follow the bakery’s deposit instructions.
