## Plan the 6-inch Signature Marble for the right group

Signature Marble is currently listed as a 6-inch cake. The general guide estimates 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake, but slice size and the rest of the menu affect the count. Tell White Cakes how many people will actually eat cake and ask whether the listed size fits your plan.

If the group expects more than the estimate, ask whether another size or a different cake is available. Do not assume the same Signature Marble design can be made at a larger size without confirmation. Check the current listing for its ivory base, dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose, then request a quote if you want to change any feature.

Include the date, flavour, inscription, and delivery or pickup plan. Confirm availability and the final price. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep confirmed product servings on the Signature Marble page and link to the central cake-size guide. Recheck the product listing before publication.
