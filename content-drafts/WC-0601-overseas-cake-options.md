## Compare cake options with help from a family contact in Cebu

If you are ordering from abroad, ask someone local to confirm the recipient's preference and the number of cake eaters. Compare the 4-inch Bento for about two generous or three modest servings with larger choices: 6 inches is estimated at 8–12 party slices, and 8 inches at 16–24. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm the right size.

Compare flavour and design using current menu details. White Cakes lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet, but availability should be checked for the actual date and configuration. Send one design reference and ask what can be adapted. Confirm the quote for the exact request.

For delivery, the local contact should provide the complete Cebu address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and handoff instructions. Check the delivery window and fee. Ask White Cakes directly which payment methods and currencies it accepts from abroad; do not assume international processing is available. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all terms are clear.

Keep overseas payment and delivery specifics current, and do not create separate local pages that repeat the same menu options.
