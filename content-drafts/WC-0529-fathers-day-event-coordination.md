## Choose one family contact to manage the holiday handoff

Give the bakery the complete address, requested delivery window, receiver’s name and mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Ask whether that location is covered for the Father’s Day date and confirm the fee and timing. If it is a surprise, specify which family member can be contacted.

Have the receiver available to take the cake promptly and follow product-specific care instructions. Confirm the final design, flavour, size, and inscription before the holiday. If a family meal is at a venue, share access details early and do not assume delivery works like an ordinary weekday.
