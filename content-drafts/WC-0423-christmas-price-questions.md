## Ask for a confirmed Christmas cake quote

Share the holiday date, size, flavour, design, inscription, number of cake eaters, and delivery or pickup location. Christmas designs can vary from a simple colour palette to custom decoration, so ask White Cakes for the total price for the actual scope. A seasonal photo alone does not establish the current product or quote.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16; 8-inch cakes are ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 afterward. These rates are temporary and should be checked on the live menu before use. Confirm whether custom details or delivery change the total.

Ask about date availability early; made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. Current portion estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm serving size, delivery fee, and window. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep price updates in the maintained menu guide and remove expired seasonal rates.
