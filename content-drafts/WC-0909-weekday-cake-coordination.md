## Coordinate the weekday cake handoff with the host

Choose someone who can receive the cake during the agreed delivery window. Give White Cakes the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any office or building instructions. If the celebration is at work, confirm the correct entrance and whether deliveries are accepted during the event time.

Confirm delivery availability and the fee for the exact location. Ask the host where the cake should be placed and who will move it after the driver leaves. Keep the box away from direct sunlight and heat; if several hours pass before serving, follow the bakery's storage guidance.

Include the date, size, flavour, design, inscription, and serving count in the order request. A weekday does not guarantee open capacity, and custom work still needs at least three days' notice. Confirm the final quote and handoff plan; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.
