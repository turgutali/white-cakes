## Order a mango-flavoured cake around the date you need it

Mango is one of the flavours listed for White Cakes custom cakes. The kitchen requests at least three days' notice for a made-to-order cake, but a three-day lead time is not a promise that every date is available. Send the date as early as you can, especially for weekends or holidays, and wait for a reply confirming the production slot.

In the same enquiry, state the cake format or size, flavour, expected guest count, and any inscription. If you expect fresh mango pieces, a separate filling, or another mango-specific finish, ask about it explicitly; the menu flavour alone does not confirm those additions. The bakery should tell you what is possible for the selected design before you pay.

For delivery, provide the full Cebu-area address, a nearby landmark, and a mobile number for the receiver. The bakery confirms the address-based delivery charge with the quote and agrees a delivery window. If the cake is for an event, name someone who can receive it and keep it out of direct sun.

The order is confirmed after availability, design, price, and logistics are agreed and the 50% deposit is received. Keep this flavour-specific reminder within the shared Cebu ordering timeline guide and the existing mango page; do not create a second generic lead-time article for each flavour.
