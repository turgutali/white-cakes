## Plan Mother's Day cake portions for the family gathering

Estimate how many people will actually eat cake, not just how many are invited to the meal. Some relatives may share a small portion, and other desserts may be available. Tell White Cakes whether you want full slices or smaller servings and ask the bakery to recommend a size.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges. A 4-inch Bento offers about two generous or three modest servings for a smaller moment. Confirm the count against the family plan rather than stretching the chart.

Mother's Day is a popular date, so enquire early and include size, flavour, design, and delivery location. Confirm date availability, current price, and the handoff plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
