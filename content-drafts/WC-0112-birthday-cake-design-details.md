## Choose one birthday-cake detail to lead the design

A birthday cake becomes easier to brief when you choose one feature to lead: the colour palette, a short inscription, a piped border, or one decorative element. Adding every inspiration at once can make the cake difficult to read, especially on the 4-inch Bento. Send one reference photo and tell the baker which detail matters most; the rest can be adapted to the chosen cake size.

White Cakes offers a 4-inch Bento, Signature Marble, Bouquet Cake, and fully custom buttercream designs. These formats create different visual starting points. Bento is compact, Signature Marble has its established dusty-blue marbled finish, and Bouquet Cake is decorated with hand-piped flowers. Custom cakes follow a brief within the hand-piped style. Ask what can be changed and what affects the quote before confirming.

Include the birthday date, flavour, expected cake-eaters, and exact message. Use the size guide to plan portions rather than selecting from a photograph. If delivery is needed, send the complete address, a landmark, receiver's mobile, and any venue access detail.
