## Confirm the current ube option and the complete order summary

Before paying, ask whether the bakery can make the requested ube cake for your date and confirm what that flavour option includes. Then check the size, design, exact inscription, pickup or delivery arrangement, and full price. If the cake is custom, ask what can be adapted and what is included in the quote.

White Cakes’ stated process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Verify the amount and current payment channel in writing, and save the receipt. Do not treat an inquiry about flavour or date as a booked order until the bakery confirms it.

Keep the payment flow centralized in the order guide and update the current flavour list separately. Recheck both before publishing.
