## English

# What to confirm before paying a deposit for ube cake in Cebu

Before paying a deposit, make sure the quote identifies what “ube” means, the exact cake size, design, message, date, delivery location, and total. A requested flavour should not be assumed available just because it appears in an old photo or draft menu.

White Cakes’ published guidance says a 50% deposit follows agreement on final details and price; confirm current payment terms directly. Keep the approved quote, payment instructions, and any company invoice requirements together, then ask for written acknowledgement after payment.
