## Assign one organizer to manage the farewell cake handoff

Give the bakery the exact venue, requested handoff time, organizer’s name and mobile number, and any office reception or building access instructions. Ask whether delivery is available to that location and confirm the fee and timing. If the cake is a surprise, use a coordinator contact rather than the person leaving.

Have the receiver ready to take the cake and follow the bakery’s care advice for the specific design. Confirm the inscription and final order details before the event day. If the venue’s program changes, contact the bakery rather than assuming the delivery time can move.

Add these logistics to the central Cebu delivery guide. A separate route story needs a documented real delivery and customer permission.
