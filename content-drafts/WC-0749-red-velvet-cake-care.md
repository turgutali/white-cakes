## Store a red velvet cake safely until serving

Keep the cake in its box on a stable, level surface. Avoid direct sun, a hot car, and warm equipment; heat can soften a buttercream finish. If the cake will wait several hours, follow White Cakes' care instructions and use refrigerator space if the bakery advises it. Do not apply a fixed room-temperature rule without knowing the specific cake and conditions.

Choose a receiver who can bring the box inside promptly. If delivery is at a condo, office, or event venue, share the full address, landmark, mobile number, and the right entrance with the bakery. Confirm the delivery window and fee, and make sure the person receiving the cake knows where it should be stored before guests arrive.

Red Velvet is on White Cakes' current flavour list; check availability for the date and requested size. Include the serving estimate, inscription, event date, and delivery plan when ordering. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep storage guidance in one care article, and publish only handling details verified by White Cakes.
