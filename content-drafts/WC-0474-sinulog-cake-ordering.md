## Common ordering mistakes to avoid with Sinulog cake in Cebu

Avoid booking assumptions: a message or reference photo is not a confirmed slot. Also avoid a brief with no guest count, venue or serving time, choosing tiers from appearance alone, and leaving spelling or delivery arrangements until event day.

Use one written summary for the final size, flavour, design, wording, quote and handoff. Confirm current availability and care instructions with the bakery; these checks reduce last-minute confusion without adding unnecessary complexity. For a Sinulog gathering, confirm venue access, road or event restrictions and the actual service time with the organizer before setting delivery expectations. Use a palette or motif the bakery confirms it can hand-pipe; do not assume a parade route or road is accessible.
