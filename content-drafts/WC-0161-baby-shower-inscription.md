## Keep a baby-shower cake message simple

A short greeting is easy to read and leaves room for the cake's design. You might use the parents' names, a brief welcome, or a simple phrase such as “Baby on the Way.” These are wording ideas, not actual White Cakes customer orders. Ask the bakery whether your preferred words fit the size and design.

Send the exact spelling and punctuation in one message. If you want the message in Cebuano, Tagalog, or another language, provide the final wording yourself or have a fluent speaker check it; do not assume a machine translation will preserve the intended tone. Confirm the text before the cake is finalized, especially if names or dates are included.

Include the date, flavour, size, design reference, and delivery plan in the order request. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake; these are approximate. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use these examples in the existing message guide. A standalone baby-shower gallery needs original photographs and distinct, permission-cleared examples.
