## What to write on baby shower cake in Cebu

Keep the message short, legible and correctly spelled. Send the exact text, capitalization and punctuation in writing; compare names and dates with the invitation or programme before approving the final order.

Ask whether the wording fits the selected cake size and hand-piped finish. If several names or a long phrase matter, decide early what can be shortened so the lettering remains readable in person and in photographs. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
