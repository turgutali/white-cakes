## Coordinate the receiver, reveal time, and delivery point privately

For a proposal, choose one organizer to communicate with the bakery and receive the cake. Give the exact address, requested handoff time, a discreet mobile number, and instructions about whom the rider should contact. Check whether the bakery can reach the location and confirm the plan directly; do not assume a particular route or time.

The organizer should keep the cake level and follow the baker’s storage guidance until the reveal. If the venue has security or setup rules, arrange access before the delivery day. Confirm the design and message in writing so the surprise plan does not depend on last-minute changes.

Add this coordination checklist to the delivery guide. Publish a location-specific proposal story only after a real order, route details, and customer permission are documented.
