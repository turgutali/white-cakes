## Treat the inscription as one of the design elements from the beginning

Decide what the cake should communicate and choose the exact words before finalizing the decoration. A short name or greeting can be the main focal point; a longer phrase may need a simpler border or a separate card. Share one reference and explain which part should guide the design.

Ask whether the wording fits the selected cake size and where it can be placed. Confirm the date, flavour, serving plan, budget, and handoff details separately. Check spelling and punctuation in the final order summary before production.

Add the planning advice to the central design guide. A case-specific article needs a real approved cake photo showing a distinct layout decision.
