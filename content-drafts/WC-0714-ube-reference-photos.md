## Use a photo to explain the design, then verify the ube flavour separately

Choose a reference that clearly shows the decoration, colour, or lettering you want adapted. Tell the bakery which part matters and which details are flexible. A purple cake photo does not confirm the ingredients or flavour inside, so ask whether ube is currently offered and what that option includes.

Include the size, serving estimate, event date, exact message, budget, and delivery or pickup plan. Confirm the final design and current quote in writing. If you have allergy or dietary questions, discuss them directly with the bakery rather than inferring from an image.
