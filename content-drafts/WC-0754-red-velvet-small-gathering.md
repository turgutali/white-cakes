## Match the cake size to the small group and confirm the flavour

Count the people who will actually share cake and decide whether you want modest dessert slices or a generous portion each. Tell the bakery if there are other desserts and ask which current size fits. Confirm that red velvet is available for your date; do not rely on an old photo.

The 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous servings or three modest ones. Ask the bakery to confirm that estimate and whether the design you want fits the size. Flavour does not determine portion count, so discuss servings and menu options separately.

Keep size ranges in the central guide and the current flavour list in the flavour guide. Verify both before publication.
