## Pick a baby-shower reference with one clear design cue

Choose one photo that shows the part of the baby-shower cake you want the baker to understand: a colour palette, floral detail, border, or lettering style. Then name that element in your message. A large collage of competing ideas can make it harder to agree on one hand-piped finish.

White Cakes offers Bouquet Cake with piped flowers, Signature Marble with its established marbled look, a compact Bento, and custom buttercream designs. These options have different sizes and visual styles. If the reference shows a theme, say whether you want its colours or a specific decoration interpreted. The bakery can confirm what fits the chosen size; the photo is not a promise of an exact copy.

Include the date, flavour, guest count, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery preference. Use the current size guide to decide whether the cake can serve the group. If delivery is needed, provide the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number so someone can meet the driver during the agreed window.
