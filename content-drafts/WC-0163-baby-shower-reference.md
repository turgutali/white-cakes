## Which reference photos help when ordering baby shower cake in Cebu

Choose one image that shows the overall silhouette, then add a separate close-up only for a detail that matters. Explain what you like in each image—colour, border, flower placement or lettering—and identify what is flexible.

A reference is a direction, not a promise of an exact replica. White Cakes adapts designs to hand-piped buttercream, cake size and practical constraints. Ask for the bakery’s written interpretation before approving the final brief. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
