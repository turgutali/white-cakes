## Store vanilla cake safely until serving

Keep the boxed cake level, away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm equipment. If it will wait several hours before serving, follow White Cakes' care guidance and ask whether refrigeration is recommended for the specific cake. Do not infer storage requirements from the Vanilla flavour name.

Choose a person to receive the cake and bring it to a stable place promptly. If it is delivered to an office or venue, confirm that someone can accept it and that there is a suitable holding location. Avoid leaving the cake in an exposed lobby or repeatedly moving it between heat and cold.

Ask the bakery whether the selected design has particular handling instructions. Include the delivery window and expected serving time in your order enquiry. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use only bakery-confirmed storage guidance in the shared heat-care article. Do not publish a flavour-specific holding time without evidence.
