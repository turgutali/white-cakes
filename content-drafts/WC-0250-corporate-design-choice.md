## Choose a corporate-cake design that stays readable

Start with the reason for the cake: a team milestone, company anniversary, or a welcome for a new colleague. Decide whether the design should feature a logo, a short message, or a team colour. A simple focal point can look intentional without turning the cake into a crowded brand board.

Get approval for company marks and wording before sending the brief. Provide a clean logo file only if the company has authorized its use. Send one reference photo and point out the detail to adapt. Ask White Cakes what it can make for the chosen size and what the quote includes; a reference image is not a fixed product or exact-copy promise.

Share the event date, number of cake eaters, flavour, inscription, and office delivery details. Confirm the size and price, and assign a colleague to receive the cake. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Use original, permission-cleared White Cakes photos to illustrate an actual corporate design. Otherwise, add the advice to the existing design guide.
