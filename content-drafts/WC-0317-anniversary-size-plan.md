## Estimate who will share the cake before selecting a size

An anniversary celebration might be an intimate dinner or a gathering with relatives and friends. Count the likely cake eaters, consider whether the cake follows a meal, and mention any other desserts. Ask the bakery to recommend a size based on the portions you want instead of starting with the anniversary number alone.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. A 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous or three modest portions. Confirm these approximate ranges and current availability directly; cutting style affects the result.
