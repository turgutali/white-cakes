## Share a reference that clarifies the office design and logo use

For an IT Park office cake, send one image that shows the overall look and a separate file for a logo only if the company approves its use. Explain whether the reference is for colour, message placement, or decoration. A photo of the office party table can also help if available, but do not share coworkers’ images without their permission.

Tell the bakery the guest estimate, requested size, date, flavour, exact inscription, delivery building or tower, and receiver’s contact. Ask whether the design fits the cake and the building’s access process. Confirm the quote and handoff plan in writing.

Keep the reference checklist in the custom brief guide and link to verified delivery advice. A separate office case needs company approval and original photos.
