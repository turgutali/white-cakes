## Match a Valentine's cake design to the kind of gift you want to give

For a floral statement, White Cakes' Bouquet Cake is shaped and wrapped to read like a bouquet, with hand-piped buttercream rosettes, sage leaves, pleated wrapper details, pearls, and a bow. Choose it when the recipient would enjoy flowers presented as a cake to share. Its scale and finish vary, so ask for a quote based on your date and guest count.

For a smaller surprise, the 4-inch Bento is a compact cake with space for a short message. It serves two generously or three modestly according to the current product guidance. Signature Marble is a 6-inch design with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and a single rose. Fully custom buttercream work can follow a concise reference brief within what can be hand-piped.

The design should fit the way you plan to give it. If it is a doorstep surprise, confirm the delivery address, receiver's number, landmark, and delivery window with the person who can accept it. If it will be carried to dinner, ask how to keep it out of direct sun and a hot car. Keep the inscription short and send the exact spelling before the order is confirmed.

Request availability at least three days ahead and earlier for a holiday date. White Cakes should confirm the chosen format, quote, and delivery details before the 50% deposit. The Valentine's guide can compare these actual options and their photos; a separate design page should only be added when it has original, occasion-specific cake evidence.
