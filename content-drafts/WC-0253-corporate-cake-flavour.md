## Choose an office-cake flavour with the team in mind

For a shared office cake, consider whether the team has a known favourite or whether you need a familiar option for a mixed group. White Cakes' current menu lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm which flavour is available for the event date, size, and custom design before you promise it to colleagues.

Do not claim an option is suitable for allergies or dietary restrictions based only on its name. Ask White Cakes directly about ingredients and handling if anyone has a restriction. If the bakery cannot confirm suitability, do not label the cake safe for that person.

Share the number of colleagues expected to eat cake and whether it is the only dessert. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Ask the bakery to confirm size. Include the office address and a receiver's mobile number; confirm delivery timing and fee. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep workplace flavour advice and verified menu information in one central guide rather than creating separate corporate pages for each flavour.
