## How to choose a flavour for corporate cake in Cebu

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask what the selected flavour means in the current menu: the name alone does not promise a separate filling, fruit layer or topping. Confirm the cake construction and raise any ingredient or allergy question directly.

For a mixed-age gathering, choose from the bakery’s confirmed options and tell the host what flavour is being served. Do not assume an unlisted combination or special dietary version is available without written confirmation. For a corporate cake, send the approved brand colour and exact wording, and confirm who can authorize logo use. Keep the delivery contact reachable at the office or venue; do not assume reception can accept a cake without prior arrangement.
