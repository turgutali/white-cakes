## Keep a Bouquet Cake away from heat before serving

Bouquet Cake has hand-piped buttercream flowers, so plan where it will wait before the celebration begins. Use a stable surface away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm appliances. Do not carry the box around unnecessarily or open it repeatedly to inspect the decoration.

If the cake will be held for several hours, follow White Cakes' refrigeration guidance. Ask the bakery how to handle the specific design and when to remove it from the refrigerator before serving; do not guess a fixed room-temperature period, especially in Cebu's warm conditions. Arrange refrigerator space before the cake arrives if a long wait is expected.

When ordering, confirm the event date, flavour, size, design, delivery window, and receiving contact. Ask for the current quote and delivery fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep handling advice in one cake-care guide and verify any product-specific exception with the bakery. Do not imply that floral piping guarantees the cake will withstand outdoor heat.

Gumamit ng matatag na ibabaw na malayo sa direktang liwanag ng araw, mainit na sasakyan, at maiinit na kagamitan.
