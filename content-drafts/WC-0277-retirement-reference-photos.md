## Send a retirement-cake reference that explains the idea

Choose one photo that shows the visual detail the team wants: a palette, work-related motif, lettering style, or simple arrangement. Tell White Cakes which element to adapt and what can change. If colleagues have different ideas, select one primary direction before sending references.

For a company logo or uniform detail, confirm permission to use the image. Ask whether the design is feasible for the chosen cake size and what the quote includes. A reference photo does not guarantee exact reproduction; the bakery should confirm its interpretation.

Include the event date, flavour, serving plan, inscription, and office or venue delivery details. Current portions are estimated at 8–12 for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size and delivery window, allow at least three days' notice, and secure the agreed order with the 50% deposit.

Keep reference advice in the custom brief guide and publish a before-and-after only with original images and approval.
