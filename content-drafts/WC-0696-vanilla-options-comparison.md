## Compare vanilla cake options by size and design scope

Vanilla is one of the flavours on White Cakes' current menu. First confirm it is available for your event date and requested design. Then compare sizes by the number of people expected to eat cake: the current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges, so ask the bakery to confirm the configuration.

Next, compare the design scope. A simple finish and short message may fit a listed option; a detailed custom reference may require a separate quote. Send one clear image and identify what matters most. Ask whether it can be adapted and what the confirmed price includes. Do not infer ingredients, taste notes, or dietary suitability from the word Vanilla.

Include the date, inscription, serving estimate, and pickup or delivery plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm the final design and terms; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep the comparison in the central cake-choice guide, with current menu details linked rather than duplicated.
