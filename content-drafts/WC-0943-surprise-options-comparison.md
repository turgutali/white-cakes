## Compare the serving plan and the reveal logistics before choosing

First estimate how many people will be present when the surprise is revealed and whether the cake is the main dessert. Ask the bakery to match current sizes to that number, then compare flavours and design options available for the date. A cake for two and a cake for the family group are different plans.

Consider who can receive it privately and where it will wait before the reveal. Confirm delivery or pickup, the receiver contact, total price, and any design changes in writing. If the surprise is outdoors or at a venue, ask about the handoff and care instructions.
