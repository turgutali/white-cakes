## Keep First Communion cake details balanced and readable

Choose the family palette and one focal detail before adding decorations. A short inscription, a simple border, or one symbolic motif can connect the cake to the occasion without competing for space. Ask the family which element matters most.

Send one reference image and identify the part to adapt. If you request a religious symbol, be precise about the design and have the family approve the wording. Ask White Cakes whether the details are feasible for the selected size and how they affect the quote. A reference does not guarantee an exact copy.

Include the date, flavour, size, serving estimate, and delivery plan. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm the final design, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Use original close-up photos only when they show a real White Cakes finish and publication is approved. Otherwise, add the practical advice to the existing design guide.
