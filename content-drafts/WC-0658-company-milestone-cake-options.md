## Compare company-milestone formats by how colleagues will share them

For a team milestone, choose between one shared cake and individual boxes based on how colleagues will gather. A 4-inch Bento can be handed to one person or placed at a desk; a larger custom cake suits a common table. White Cakes' serving guide provides estimates for 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier formats. Share the attendee count and ask the bakery to confirm portions for your plan.

The format also changes the design options. Signature Marble has a defined 6-inch marbled look, Bouquet Cake has a piped floral finish, and custom buttercream can use company colours or a short team message as inspiration. White Cakes does not print edible logo sheets from a file; ask what can be piped by hand.

For multiple Bento cakes, state the quantity and whether names, flavours, or inscriptions differ. The bakery must confirm what its schedule can accommodate. For an office tower, provide access instructions and a named receiver so the handover can happen during the agreed window.
