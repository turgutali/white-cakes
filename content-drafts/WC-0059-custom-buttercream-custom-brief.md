## Give the bakery the event details and one clear design priority

Include the celebration date, recipient, expected servings, requested size, flavour, delivery or pickup plan, and budget. For the design, share a reference image and explain what it means to you: perhaps the palette, lettering, border, or a particular motif. Mark which detail must remain and which parts can be adapted.

Ask whether the proposed buttercream finish suits the cake size and schedule. If the event is outdoors or warm, tell the bakery and request handling advice for that exact cake. Confirm the inscription’s spelling and the final quote before paying the deposit.

Keep the reusable briefing checklist in one custom-cake guide. Product-specific design and storage instructions should be confirmed by White Cakes rather than inferred from online photos.
