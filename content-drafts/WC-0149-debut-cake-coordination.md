## How to coordinate debut cake in Cebu

Assign one family member or event coordinator as the cake contact. That person should hold the final specification, know the delivery window, answer the rider and alert the venue team. Multiple relatives sending different revisions can create uncertainty about which colour, wording or size is approved.

Create a short handoff note with the recipient’s name and phone, venue landmark, access instructions, cake-table location and the time it can be moved there. Share it with the host and venue coordinator, then keep the bakery’s confirmed order summary as the source of truth.

## Tagalog translation (editorial review pending)

Magtalaga ng isang kapamilya o event coordinator bilang cake contact. Siya ang magtatago ng final specification, may alam sa delivery window, sasagot sa rider, at magbibigay-alam sa venue team. Kapag maraming kamag-anak ang nagpapadala ng magkakaibang rebisyon, nalilito kung aling kulay, teksto, o laki ang aprubado.

Gumawa ng maikling handoff note na may pangalan at numero ng tatanggap, landmark ng venue, tagubilin sa pagpasok, lokasyon ng cake table, at oras kung kailan ito maililipat doon. Ibahagi sa host at venue coordinator, at gawing batayan ang kumpirmadong order summary.
