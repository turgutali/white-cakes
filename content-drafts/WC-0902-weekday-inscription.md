## Keep a weekday message focused on the recipient or team

For an office or after-class celebration, choose a name, short congratulations, or simple group greeting. A brief message is easier to read during a quick meeting and leaves space for decoration. Decide whether the cake is for one person or the whole team before choosing the wording.

Send the exact spelling and punctuation to the bakery and ask whether it fits the selected design and size. If you want a longer note, use a card. For a workplace cake, confirm that any company name or logo is approved for use.

Add weekday examples to the shared message guide only where they offer distinct wording or layout advice. Use actual approved photos if illustrating a finished cake.
