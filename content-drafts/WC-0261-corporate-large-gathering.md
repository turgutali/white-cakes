## Estimate the colleagues who will actually join the cake break

A company’s full headcount is rarely the same as the number of people attending one celebration. Ask the organizer how many coworkers will be present at serving time and whether the cake is the main dessert. Tell the bakery whether portions should be modest or party-size and request a size recommendation.

The current size guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; ask the bakery to confirm the likely yield for the event. A much larger group may need a different arrangement.

Keep serving information in the central size guide and link to corporate delivery details. Do not imply a standard cake size serves an entire company without checking.
