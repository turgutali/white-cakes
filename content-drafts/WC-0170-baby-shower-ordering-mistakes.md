## Confirm the event plan and spelling before the bakery starts

A common source of confusion is leaving the date or venue uncertain while requesting a detailed theme. Confirm where and when the shower will take place, who will receive the cake, and how many guests may share it. Then send the chosen size, flavour, design references, and exact inscription together.

Choose one organizer to approve the final brief, especially when the cake is a surprise. Ask the bakery which details can be made for the requested size and schedule, confirm the complete price and delivery fee, and follow the current deposit process. Double-check the guest of honour’s name before confirming.

Keep these checks in the shared order and custom-brief guides. Publish a case-specific example only if it documents a real order and has customer permission.
