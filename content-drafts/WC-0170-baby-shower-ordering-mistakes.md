## Common ordering mistakes to avoid with baby shower cake in Cebu

Avoid booking assumptions: a message or reference photo is not a confirmed slot. Also avoid a brief with no guest count, venue or serving time, choosing tiers from appearance alone, and leaving spelling or delivery arrangements until event day.

Use one written summary for the final size, flavour, design, wording, quote and handoff. Confirm current availability and care instructions with the bakery; these checks reduce last-minute confusion without adding unnecessary complexity. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
