## English

# How to prepare for delivery of custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Custom buttercream cakes are hand-finished and should be delivered upright with a receiver ready. Share the full address, building or venue entrance, contact number, and delivery window. Confirm who can take the cake to the venue table; White Cakes does not provide an on-site cutting or setup team.

Ask the bakery to confirm route coverage and any product-specific transport instructions.
