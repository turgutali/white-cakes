## Prepare the handoff for custom buttercream cake

Choose a receiver who can meet the driver during the agreed delivery window. Give White Cakes the full address, landmark, mobile number, and any gate, lobby, or building instructions. Confirm the fee and delivery timing for that destination; do not assume the cake can be left unattended.

Plan a stable, cool place for the cake after arrival. Keep it away from direct sunlight and a hot vehicle because buttercream can soften in warm conditions. If there will be several hours before serving, follow the bakery's storage instructions for the specific design and arrange refrigeration if advised.

Include the event date, size, flavour, design, inscription, and serving estimate in the order. Confirm that the requested finish is feasible and that the quote includes the agreed decoration. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep general handoff and care instructions in the shared delivery guide. Publish special handling only when White Cakes confirms it.
