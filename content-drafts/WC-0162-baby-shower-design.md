## Match a baby-shower cake to the party palette

Begin with the table or invitation colours, then choose one cake detail that will carry the theme. A restrained palette with a clear focal point is easier to brief than a collage of unrelated inspiration. Send one or two references and identify what you are borrowing from each—colour, shape, border, or flowers—so the decorator understands the priority.

Decide whether the cake is meant to be a quiet part of the dessert table or its main visual feature. That choice affects how much decoration and inscription you need. Ask White Cakes whether the requested design can be adapted to the chosen size and flavour, and get the final scope and price confirmed before paying.

Choose size by the number of people likely to eat cake. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; actual yield depends on slice size and other desserts. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice and confirms the agreed order with a 50% deposit.

This is planning guidance, not a record of a particular baby shower. A photo case study needs a real White Cakes cake, verified design choices, and permission to publish the images. Without those, merge the design advice into the main Cebu cake-design guide.
