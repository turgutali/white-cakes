## Describe the colour and decoration you want without assuming the flavour from a photo

Choose the visual direction for the ube cake—perhaps a simple colour palette, border, or short message—and identify one focal feature. Share a reference and tell the bakery what should be adapted. The outside colour alone does not confirm the ingredients or flavour inside.

Ask whether ube is currently available for the requested date and what the option includes. Confirm the size, serving estimate, exact wording, design price, and handoff plan. Discuss allergy or dietary questions directly with the bakery.

Add design advice to the central guide and current flavour information to the live menu. Use only original approved images to show actual cake finishes.
