## Brief an IT Park office cake so it can be delivered smoothly

An office cake enquiry should include the practical handoff details as well as the design. Give the event date, time the cake should arrive, number of cake eaters, building and office address, recipient's mobile number, and any lobby or visitor instructions. IT Park buildings can have their own access process; confirm the right receiving point with your office contact before requesting a delivery window.

For the cake itself, specify size, flavour, short inscription, colours, and one reference photo. If the cake is for a team, decide whether everyone is expected to take a slice. White Cakes' guide estimates 8–12 party portions from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches, but the actual count depends on serving size. Ask the bakery to confirm which option fits your plan.

White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm design feasibility, price, delivery availability, fee, and arrival window before paying the 50% deposit that confirms the agreed order. Share the written handoff plan with the colleague who will meet the driver.

Use this as an office-specific example within the general custom brief guide. Do not claim special IT Park access or delivery guarantees without a verified first-party case.
