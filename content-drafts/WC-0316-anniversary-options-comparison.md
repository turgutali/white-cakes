## Compare size and design separately so neither choice is rushed

First decide how many people will share the cake and whether it is served after a meal. Ask the bakery which current size fits that estimate and how many portions it can reasonably provide. Then compare flavours and decoration options for the actual date; the anniversary year alone does not tell you what size is needed.

For design, consider one personal reference such as a favourite colour, shared place, or short message. Ask whether the idea works on the selected cake shape and confirm any custom cost. Verify delivery or pickup availability and the total price before paying.
