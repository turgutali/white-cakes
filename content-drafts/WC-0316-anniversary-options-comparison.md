## English

# Comparing Anniversary Cake Options in Cebu

The best anniversary cake option depends on the setting. A couple celebrating at home may want a small cake for after dinner; a family party needs to serve more people; a larger gathering may call for tiers or more than one cake. Start with the guests who will actually eat cake and when it will be served, then compare the available products.

Use the current size guide as a planning reference:

- **4-inch Bento:** about two generous or three modest portions after a meal.
- **6-inch round:** about 8–12 party slices.
- **8-inch round:** about 16–24 party slices.
- **Common 6-inch plus 8-inch two-tier:** about 30–36 party slices.

These are approximate counts. Cake height, decoration, and slice size affect the final number, so confirm with the bakery for the product you choose. A Bento may suit a couple’s dinner but is not designed for a room full of guests. A bigger round or tiered cake offers more portions and display presence, but needs table space and a practical cutting plan.

Compare the design surface and flavour options too. A longer anniversary message or multiple names may need more room. Check the current menu rather than relying on old photos, and ask if the chosen flavour is available at the size you want. For a surprise, include the person who will receive the cake and keep delivery logistics private from the couple.

Ask White Cakes for one complete quote with size, flavour, decoration, inscription, event date, pickup or delivery, and any address-based fee. The [cake comparison guide](/journal/which-cake.html) and [anniversary cake guide](/journal/anniversary-cake-message-ideas-cebu.html) can help you prepare the details.

- **4-inch Bento:** mga dalawang generous o tatlong modest na portion pagkatapos kumain.

- **8-inch round:** humigit-kumulang 16–24 party slices.
