## Coordinate the Signature Marble cake delivery

Choose a receiver who will be available during the confirmed delivery window. Provide White Cakes with the full address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any gate, lobby, or venue instructions. Confirm the delivery fee for that exact location and make sure the cake will not be left unattended.

Plan where the cake will be placed after handoff: a stable, shaded surface away from direct sunlight and a hot vehicle. If it will wait several hours, ask the bakery whether refrigeration is required for the specific design. The current product description is a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose; confirm that these details and availability are current.

Include the event date, flavour, inscription, and pickup or delivery preference in the enquiry. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm design, price, and delivery details in writing; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep the product note in the central delivery guide and update it only when the current listing or bakery confirms a change.
