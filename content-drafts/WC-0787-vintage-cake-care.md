## Keep a vintage-inspired cake cool before serving

Vintage-inspired cakes may have piped buttercream details that can soften in warm conditions. Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, or warm appliances. Ask White Cakes how to store the specific design if it will wait several hours; follow its refrigeration instructions rather than applying a generic time limit.

Choose someone to receive the cake and bring it to a cool place promptly. If the event is at a venue, confirm there is a safe holding spot and that the contact can reach it. Avoid leaving the cake unattended or moving it repeatedly between heat and refrigeration.

When ordering, mention the expected time between delivery and serving and ask whether the selected finish needs special care. Confirm the size, flavour, date, and delivery plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use only bakery-confirmed handling details in the central heat-care guide. Do not claim vintage piping has a special temperature tolerance without evidence.
