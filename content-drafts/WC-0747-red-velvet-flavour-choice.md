## Choose Red Velvet for the people who will share the cake

Red Velvet is one of the flavours on White Cakes' current menu. Confirm that it is available for your chosen size and date, especially if the cake has a custom design. Do not infer ingredients, flavour intensity, or allergy suitability from the name; ask the bakery for verified details if those affect the order.

Estimate portions from the actual group. Current guidance is 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. The 4-inch Bento is designed for about two generous servings or three modest portions. These are approximate; ask White Cakes to match the size to your guest and dessert plan.

Include the date, flavour, reference image, inscription, and delivery or pickup plan in one enquiry. Confirm the design and quote, and allow at least three days' notice for made-to-order work. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once terms are settled.

Keep flavour facts in the current menu guide and avoid unsupported taste or ingredient descriptions.
