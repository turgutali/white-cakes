## Keep the red velvet flavour and outside design as separate choices

Red velvet is one of the flavours White Cakes lists for custom cakes. The outside design is a separate decision: choose a colour palette, piped border, inscription, or reference photo and ask what can be created in hand-piped buttercream. Do not assume a red exterior, specific frosting, filling, or topping from the flavour name alone; ask the bakery to confirm any detail you expect.

If sending a reference, identify what matters most. One image showing the desired border or lettering is easier to interpret than several conflicting styles. A reference guides the design but does not guarantee an exact copy. Ask whether the requested detail will fit the chosen cake size and how it affects the quote.

Include the event date, size, guest count, exact inscription, and delivery or pickup plan. Use the size guide for portions and ask for a recommendation for the actual design. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery must confirm availability before the 50% deposit.

Keep flavour details on the red velvet guide and shared styling advice on the cake-design page. A separate photo case should use a real approved red velvet cake image and a documented design choice.
