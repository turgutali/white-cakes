## English

# Common ordering mistakes to avoid with wedding cake in Cebu

Avoid choosing a wedding cake by photo before sharing the venue, guest count, and service plan. Do not assume a tiered cake includes delivery setup or cutting staff. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and focuses on intimate weddings; it will confirm what is feasible rather than promise a ballroom display.

Check the venue’s outside-cake rules, cake table, storage, and responsible cutter before paying.
