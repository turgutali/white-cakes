## Keep a company-milestone inscription short and approved

Choose the milestone wording first: an anniversary number, a team name, or a brief congratulations message. A short line is easier to read on a cake and leaves room for the design. Ask the company contact to approve the final text before sending it to White Cakes, especially if a logo or trademark will be included.

Send the exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation in one message. If the cake includes a logo, provide approved artwork and confirm that the company allows it to be used. Ask whether the wording and mark fit the selected cake size and design. Do not assume a low-resolution web image can be reproduced cleanly.

For an office celebration, include the event date, number of cake eaters, flavour, and delivery contact. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm size, delivery window, and fee; allow at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

These are practical message tips, not examples from actual companies. Keep them in the existing inscription guide.

Ang isang maikling linya ay mas madaling basahin sa isang cake at nag-iiwan ng silid para sa disenyo. Hilingin sa contact ng kumpanya na aprubahan ang huling text bago ito ipadala sa White Cakes, lalo na kung may isasamang logo o trademark.

Itanong kung ang mga salita at marka ay akma sa napiling laki at disenyo ng cake. Huwag ipagpalagay na ang isang mababang resolution na imahe sa web ay maaaring kopyahin nang malinis.

Para sa isang pagdiriwang sa opisina, isama ang petsa ng kaganapan, bilang ng mga kumakain ng cake, lasa, at contact sa paghahatid.
