## Choose a wedding-cake flavour with the guest list in mind

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet for custom cake orders. To choose for a wedding, begin with the couple's preferences and the people who will actually eat cake. If the couple has a favourite, that is a reasonable place to start; if guests have mixed preferences, ask the bakery whether more than one flavour can be accommodated in the chosen size and design.

Do not assume that a flavour name includes a separate filling, frosting, fruit, or topping. The menu lists the flavour itself; any additional ingredient or finish should be named in the enquiry and confirmed for the date. A flavour sample or tasting should not be promised unless White Cakes confirms that service is available.

Send the guest count, planned slice size, date, cake format, and flavour request together. The size guide gives estimates for a 6-inch, 8-inch, and a common two-tier cake, but the final count depends on the design and cut. Weddings and multi-tier requests should be discussed earlier than the minimum three-day notice when possible so the bakery can check the date and production plan.

Use the wedding guide for the couple's event choices and link to the central flavour and size guides for shared facts. A separate wedding-flavour URL needs original, confirmed tasting details beyond the same menu list.
