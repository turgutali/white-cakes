## Keep a child's birthday cake safe between delivery and the party

Choose an adult to receive the cake and keep track of it until the birthday meal or activity is finished. White Cakes' guidance says buttercream cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars. If the cake will be held for several hours, refrigerate it. Do not leave it unattended at a gate, beside a sunny window, or in a vehicle while children are playing.

If delivered to a home, school, or venue, provide the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and entrance instructions. A named adult should meet the driver during the agreed window and move the cake to a suitable place. Tell the bakery when you expect to serve it and ask if the plan raises any concerns.

For the cake itself, confirm size and portions for the number of children and adults who will eat. Send the date, flavour, message, and design reference early. The 4-inch Bento has a limited writing area; larger formats need a serving discussion using the current size guide.

Keep this handling advice in the shared cake-care guide and link it from the children's birthday page. The storage instructions apply to buttercream cakes generally, so separate product-by-product pages would repeat the same guidance.
