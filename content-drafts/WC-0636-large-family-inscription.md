## Choose wording that speaks to the family without listing every name

For a large family gathering, a short greeting such as “Family Day,” “Welcome home,” or “Salamat sa tanan” may suit everyone better than a long list of names. Decide whether the cake is for a specific person or the whole family, and keep the wording easy to read from the table.

Send the exact message and spelling to the bakery. Ask whether it fits the selected size and decoration, especially if several colours or borders take up the writing area. If the family wants a longer dedication, use a card or separate sign rather than squeezing the text onto the cake.
