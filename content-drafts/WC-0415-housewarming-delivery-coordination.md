## Coordinate a housewarming cake at a new address

Confirm that someone will be home or at the venue during the delivery window. A new address can be hard to locate, so give White Cakes the full unit or house number, street, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and gate or building instructions. Ask the host to confirm the directions and identify where the driver should stop.

Choose one person to accept the cake and move it inside to a stable place away from direct sunlight or heat. If the celebration begins hours later, follow the bakery's storage advice and arrange refrigerator space if recommended. Do not leave the cake unattended at a gate or in a hot vehicle.

Include the event date, flavour, size, design, and inscription in the request. Confirm route availability, delivery fee, and timing for the specific address. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the details are settled.
