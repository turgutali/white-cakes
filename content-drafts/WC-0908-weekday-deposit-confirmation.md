## Confirm weekday availability and delivery before paying

Do not assume a weekday date is automatically available. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; ask the bakery to confirm the production slot and delivery timing. If the cake is for an office, check the building access and contact schedule too.

Review the written order for size, flavour, design, exact inscription, price, and pickup or delivery plan. Verify the full address, entrance, receiver's mobile number, delivery window, and fee. Make sure someone can meet the driver and move the cake to a suitable place before serving.

The current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow payment instructions in the current quote and save the receipt with the order confirmation. Resolve open questions about office access, wording, or design before paying.

Keep deposit details in one maintained ordering guide and recheck them whenever White Cakes updates its terms. Do not imply a faster weekday lead time unless the bakery confirms it.

Nangangailangan ang White Cake ng hindi bababa sa tatlong araw na paunawa para sa mga made-to-order na cake; hilingin sa panaderya na kumpirmahin ang puwang ng produksyon at oras ng paghahatid. I-verify ang buong address, pasukan, numero ng mobile ng tatanggap, window ng paghahatid, at bayad. Siguraduhing may makakakilala sa driver at ilipat ang cake sa angkop na lugar bago ihain.

Lutasin ang mga bukas na tanong tungkol sa pag-access sa opisina, pananalita, o disenyo bago magbayad.

Panatilihin ang mga detalye ng deposito sa isang pinapanatili na gabay sa pag-order at suriin muli ang mga ito sa tuwing ina-update ng White Cakes ang mga tuntunin nito. Huwag magpahiwatig ng mas mabilis na lead time sa araw ng linggo maliban kung kinumpirma ito ng panaderya.
