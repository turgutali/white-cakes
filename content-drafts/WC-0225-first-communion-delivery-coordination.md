## How to coordinate First Communion cake in Cebu

Assign one cake contact who has the confirmed order summary, delivery window and venue details. That person should answer the rider, notify the venue coordinator and make sure the cake reaches its planned table without being left unattended.

Share a short handoff note with the receiver’s name and phone, landmark, entry instructions, cake-table location and time of service. Avoid parallel revisions from several people; send any change through the single contact and get it reconfirmed. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
