## Confirm the Bento order before sending the deposit

Check that White Cakes has confirmed the date and production slot. A message asking whether a Bento is available is not the same as a reserved order. The made-to-order minimum is three days' notice; for a busy date, ask earlier and wait for the bakery's written reply.

Read back the exact 4-inch Bento flavour, design, inscription, price, and pickup or delivery plan. The serving expectation is about two generous portions or three modest ones. If more people will share it, ask whether a larger size is more suitable before confirming. For delivery, verify the address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, delivery window, and fee.

White Cakes' current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in your current quote and keep the payment proof with the order confirmation. Do not rely on an old social post if the bakery has given you updated terms.

Keep the Bento-specific check as a subsection of the main order guide. Verify deposit details whenever the bakery changes its process.
