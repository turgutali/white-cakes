## English

# How to plan the order timeline for Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Signature Marble is a defined 6-inch cake with ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. Confirm its availability for your date and work backward from serving time, allowing at least three days’ notice and time for payment and delivery coordination.

If you want a change to the signature look, ask whether it becomes a custom design and how that affects the quote.
