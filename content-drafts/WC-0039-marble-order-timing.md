## Timing an order for the Signature Marble cake

White Cakes' Signature Marble is a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. It is a defined design, but the bakery still needs to confirm the date and production slot. For a made-to-order cake, allow at least three days' notice. Contact the bakery sooner for a weekend, holiday, or a date that cannot move.

Include the date and the time you need the cake, how many people will share it, the flavour you prefer, and whether you need delivery. The current serving estimate for a 6-inch cake is about 8–12 party slices, but slice size changes the count. If the cake is for a larger group, ask whether this size is suitable rather than assuming it will serve every guest.

If you want to change the Signature Marble's colour, border, flower, or inscription, describe each change and ask whether the bakery can accommodate it. A custom adjustment may affect the confirmed design and price. Wait for the written availability and terms; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep this product-specific timing note in the main Cebu order-timeline guide and link to the Signature Marble listing for the current design. Recheck product details before publication if the design or availability changes.
