## Estimate the portions first, then ask how the vintage design fits

Vintage-style borders and piping affect the decoration, but they do not determine the number of servings. Count likely cake eaters and explain whether you expect small dessert slices or larger party portions. Mention other desserts and whether the cake will be cut for a seated meal or a casual gathering.

The current guide gives approximate party-slice ranges: 8–12 from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. Ask the bakery to confirm what size works for your guest count and whether the requested vintage details are suitable for that size. Treat the ranges as estimates, not guarantees.
