## Estimate cake servings from the weekday guest list

The day of the week does not change the cake's serving count. Count the people who will actually eat cake and decide whether they will have a full slice or a smaller dessert portion after a meal. White Cakes' guide estimates two generous or three modest servings from a 4-inch Bento, about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, and 16–24 from an 8-inch. A common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier arrangement is planned for roughly 30–36.

Treat those numbers as planning ranges; the cake height, design, and slice size affect the actual count. If colleagues arrive at different times, consider how the cake will be cut and stored between groups. Share that plan with the bakery and ask whether the selected size will suit it.

Send the date, guest count, size request, flavour, and design. At least three days' notice is requested, and availability must be confirmed. If the gathering is at an office, add the delivery address, lobby instructions, and a receiver who can meet the agreed window.

Keep serving estimates on the central size guide. A separate weekday serving page would repeat the same size math and could imply that weekdays change portions or price when they do not.
