## Keep buttercream cake cool before serving

Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so keep the boxed cake away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm appliances. Place it on a stable, level surface and avoid moving it around. If there will be a delay of several hours, follow White Cakes' care instructions for the specific cake and ask whether refrigeration is recommended.

Choose a receiver who can move the cake indoors promptly. If it will be displayed outdoors, identify a shaded place and discuss the expected serving time with the bakery. Do not leave it in the sun or assume every design can remain at room temperature for the same duration.

When ordering, share the venue, date, size, flavour, design, and how long the cake will wait before serving. Confirm the delivery window and handoff. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified handling steps in one maintained heat-care guide and link from product pages.
