## English

# Design details to consider for ube cake in Cebu

At the chosen size, prioritize one clear detail that distinguishes the ube design—perhaps the occasion wording or a simple decorative feature—only after the bakery confirms the product and finish. Avoid adding many tiny elements that may not read well. Ask how the design will work with the actual cake colours.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and does not offer edible printed logo sheets. Submit approved artwork if required and ask what can be made by hand. A unique gallery entry also needs an original, rights-cleared close-up; do not create a case from a reference image.
