## Let the requested finish and the flavour remain clear

When discussing an ube cake design, tell the bakery whether you want the colour to appear in the cake itself, the outside decoration, or both. The appearance of a reference image may depend on lighting and editing, so describe the look you want in plain words and identify one detail that matters most. Ask the bakery to confirm which finish is possible for the actual order.

Keep colour and flavour expectations separate. A purple decoration does not by itself confirm the cake’s ingredients or taste. Check the live menu for the current ube option and ask directly about ingredient or dietary questions before paying a deposit.
