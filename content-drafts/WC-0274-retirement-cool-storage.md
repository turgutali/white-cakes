## Plan care for the time between the office handoff and the celebration

Ask the bakery how to store the exact retirement cake, since its filling, frosting, and decoration may affect the instructions. Tell the baker whether the cake will arrive at an office before the program and when it will be served. For a warm venue or outdoor gathering, ask specifically what handling is recommended.

Arrange for a colleague to receive it promptly, keep the box level, and protect it from direct sun or heat. Follow the bakery’s advice about refrigeration and when to bring the cake out. If the program runs late, contact the bakery rather than assuming the original storage plan remains suitable.

Keep confirmed care advice in the central Cebu heat guide and link from the order timeline. Do not publish generic storage durations for all cake types.
