## Plan servings for a large family cake

Count the relatives who will actually eat cake, then consider other desserts and the planned slice size. A large guest list does not always mean everyone will have a full portion. Share those details with White Cakes so the bakery can recommend a size based on the event rather than an assumed headcount.

Current estimates are 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges, not guarantees. If the family plans generous slices or the guest count sits near the upper end, confirm the configuration with the bakery.

For a larger gathering, also think about how the cake will be displayed and served. A tiered design may need a stable table and a person assigned to cut it. Confirm the venue delivery, receiving contact, and storage plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep portion facts in the central sizing guide. Do not add unverified tier combinations or exact yields.
