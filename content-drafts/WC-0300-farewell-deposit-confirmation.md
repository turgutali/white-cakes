## Confirm the recipient, approved message, and venue contact before paying

Check who the farewell cake is for, the event date, size, flavour, exact message, design, and delivery or pickup location. Choose one organizer to approve the wording and receive the cake. For a workplace order, confirm permission before using a logo or sharing the company name publicly.

Ask for a complete quote, including custom decoration and any delivery fee. White Cakes’ stated process uses a 50% deposit to secure an agreed order; verify the current amount and payment method in writing. Save the receipt and final order summary.

Keep payment instructions in the central order guide. Do not treat an inquiry as a confirmed booking.
