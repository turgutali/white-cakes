## English

# What to ask about the price of mango cake in Cebu

A useful mango cake quote specifies the actual product, size, design, inscription, date, and destination. Ask whether any fruit decoration or special finish is included and whether delivery is separate. A picture alone cannot show the size, labour, or current availability behind a price.

White Cakes’ published guidance says a 50% deposit follows agreement on final price and details; verify current payment terms directly. Ask for the total in writing and request a revised quote if the design or delivery address changes.
