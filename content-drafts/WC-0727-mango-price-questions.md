## Ask what the current mango cake option includes in the quoted amount

Share the date, preferred size, flavour, serving estimate, design, and exact message when requesting a price. Ask whether mango is available for the date and what the current option includes. If you expect a particular filling or fruit detail, confirm it directly rather than inferring from the product name.

Request a complete quote, including custom decoration and delivery if needed. Rates and offerings can change, so confirm the amount in writing before paying. If you have a budget, tell the bakery early and ask which available option might fit.

Keep current rates in the maintained price guide and mango availability in the flavour guide. Do not create a separate price page for each flavour.
