## Choose debut-cake references that communicate one clear idea

Select one photo that shows the feature the celebrant wants—perhaps the palette, silhouette, or floral detail. Tell White Cakes which element to adapt and what can change. If the family sends several images, agree on the primary direction so the brief does not combine conflicting looks.

If the cake should match a dress, invitation, or venue, share only the details that matter. Ask whether the requested design works at the chosen size and what the quote includes. A reference image does not guarantee an exact copy. For logos or branded marks, confirm permission and provide approved artwork.

Include the event date, size, flavour, serving plan, exact inscription, and delivery venue. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size and handoff plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep reference advice in the custom-brief guide and use original, permission-cleared photos for any public before-and-after example.
