## Plan a custom buttercream cake from the guest count outward

Start with how many people will eat cake, then choose a size that can serve them. White Cakes' guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round and about 16–24 from an 8-inch. A common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier configuration is planned for roughly 30–36. These are estimates, not guaranteed portions; the design and slice size change the count. Share your guest count and how the cake will be served so the bakery can confirm the plan.

Next, describe the design. Choose one reference photo and name the colour, piping, border, or lettering detail you want interpreted. White Cakes works in hand-piped buttercream, so the final design must suit the chosen size and available finish. A digital logo is not printed onto edible paper; company colours can guide a piped palette.

Pick one of the listed flavours—chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet—and ask separately about any filling or topping you expect. Send the event date, inscription, and delivery or pickup preference. At least three days' notice is requested, and availability must be confirmed before the order is booked.

The product page lists starting prices for the current sizes; the final quote depends on the requested design. Confirm the price and address-based delivery charge before the 50% deposit. Keep these details in the existing size and custom-cake pages so the menu remains the single source for current options.
