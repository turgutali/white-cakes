## Size the cake for the people who will share the proposal moment

If the proposal is private, decide whether the cake is only for the couple or will be shared with family and friends afterward. Count likely cake eaters and tell the bakery whether you want modest or generous slices. A small cake can suit a private moment, but confirm its actual portions for the chosen design.

The current guide describes a 4-inch Bento as about two generous servings or three modest ones. Ask whether that size and the requested inscription or decoration are available for the date. If more people will join after the proposal, request a size recommendation instead of assuming the small option will be enough.

Keep portion advice in the central guide and link to proposal design and delivery details. Do not promise an exact yield without bakery confirmation.
