## Confirm the mango option and describe the design request separately

Include the event date, requested size, serving estimate, inscription, budget, and pickup or delivery plan. Ask the bakery whether its current mango option is available and what it includes; do not assume a particular filling or fruit detail from the name. Then describe the visual design and share one reference if useful.

Tell the bakery which parts of the reference are essential and which can be adapted. Confirm the exact message, full quote, and availability in writing. Raise allergies or dietary concerns directly and ask about ingredients rather than assuming from the flavour.

Keep reusable briefing advice in the custom-cake guide and current menu details in the flavour guide.
