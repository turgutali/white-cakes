## English

# Common ordering mistakes to avoid with Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Avoid assuming that Signature Marble is any cake with a marbled pattern; the listed version is a defined 6-inch design with ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose. Do not expect a different palette or size without confirming it as a custom request.

Check current price dates, flavour, serving need, and delivery address before paying.

## Cebuano

Susiha ang petsa sa presyo, flavour, servings ug address sa dili pa mobayad.
