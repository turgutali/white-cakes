## Avoid common mistakes when ordering Signature Marble

Do not assume the listing guarantees a date. Ask White Cakes to confirm availability for your event and leave at least three days' notice for made-to-order work. A specific weekend or holiday may need earlier planning.

Another common mismatch is expecting the listed design to include custom changes. Signature Marble is described as a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. If you want a different colour, flower, border, or message, state each change and request a confirmed quote. Do not assume modifications are included at the listed price.

Check the serving plan too. A 6-inch cake is estimated at 8–12 party portions, but actual yield depends on slice size. If more people will eat cake, ask about another size rather than hoping small slices will stretch the cake. Confirm flavour, date, pickup or delivery, and the final design in writing.

The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once the bakery replies with availability and terms. Keep this product-specific reminder in the main ordering guide, and recheck the product details before publication.
