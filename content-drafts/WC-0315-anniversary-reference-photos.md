## Show the bakery one personal idea and explain what the image means

For an anniversary cake, a useful reference might show a colour palette, a flower, or a simple style that suits the couple. Tell the bakery which part you want adapted and whether it is essential or only inspiration. One well-explained image is usually clearer than a group of pictures with different themes.

Include the date, size, guest estimate, flavour, exact inscription, and delivery or pickup plan. If you want a detail from a personal photograph, share it only with the people’s permission and explain what should be represented. Ask whether the idea fits the cake surface and current quote.
