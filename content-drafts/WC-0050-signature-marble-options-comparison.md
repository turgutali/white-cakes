## Compare Signature Marble with other cake options

Signature Marble is currently listed as a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. If those details fit the occasion, confirm current availability and ask about the flavour choices for the date. If you want a different colour, flower, or border, request a quote for the custom changes rather than assuming they are included.

Compare size against the number of people sharing cake. The current estimate for 6 inches is 8–12 party portions. If your group needs more, ask White Cakes whether another size or product can work; do not assume the Signature Marble design scales to a larger cake.

You can also compare flavour and delivery details. Confirm the selected flavour, event date, pickup or delivery address, receiver, delivery window, and fee. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep the current product details on the Signature Marble page and link to the central cake-choice guide rather than copying stale specifications.
