## English

# How to compare options for Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Compare Signature Marble with custom designs by listing what stays fixed: 6-inch size and the stated ivory, dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and rose. A custom design changes the brief and may change the price. Ask whether the current starting price applies to your requested version.

The choice is between the listed look and a design built from your references; confirm both flavour and delivery separately.
