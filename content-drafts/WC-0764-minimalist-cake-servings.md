## A minimalist finish does not change the cake's serving size

“Minimalist” describes a visual style, not a diameter or a number of slices. Estimate servings from the actual size: the White Cakes guide lists about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, about 16–24 from an 8-inch round, and roughly 30–36 for a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier arrangement. The 4-inch Bento is for two generous servings or three modest slices. These are ranges; the slice size and cake design affect the result.

Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and whether it follows a meal. If you want smaller portions because there will be several desserts, say so. A simple exterior does not mean the cake should be smaller than your guest count requires, and a photograph cannot show how the cake will be cut at your event.

For a minimalist custom brief, send one reference image and identify the detail you want kept—perhaps a quiet colour palette, a clean border, or short lettering. The baker can confirm what will fit on the chosen size and how the finish affects the quote. Include the date, flavour, and delivery or pickup details when asking.

Keep this distinction in the existing minimalist design guide and point readers to the shared size page for portions. A separate serving article for every visual style would repeat the same size facts without answering a different question.
