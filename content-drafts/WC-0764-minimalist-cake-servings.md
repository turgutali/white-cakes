## English

# How to estimate servings for minimalist cake in Cebu

Do not choose a minimalist cake size based on the visual impression of a photo; simple decoration can make scale hard to judge. Count the likely eaters and decide whether slices are modest or full party portions. Published estimates: 6-inch round 8–12 slices; 8-inch 16–24; common 6+8-inch two-tier 30–36.

Height and cutting affect portions. Tell the bakery whether other dessert is served and ask which currently available size matches your count. Keep the written serving estimate with the quote.

## Tagalog

# Paano tantiyahin ang servings para sa minimalist na cake sa Cebu

Huwag pumili ng sukat ng minimalist cake batay sa itsura sa larawan; mahirap tantiyahin ang scale kapag simple ang dekorasyon. Bilangin ang posibleng kakain at magpasya kung maliit o full party portion ang hiwa. Tantiya: 8–12 slice ang 6-inch na round; 16–24 ang 8-inch; 30–36 ang karaniwang 6+8-inch na two-tier.

Nakaaapekto ang taas at paghiwa. Sabihin kung may ibang dessert at itanong kung aling sukat ang babagay sa bilang. Isama sa quote ang nakasulat na serving estimate.
