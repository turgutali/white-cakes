## Prepare a Bouquet Cake brief around the flowers

Start with the event date, size, flavour, number of people who will share the cake, and preferred handoff. Bouquet Cake is identified by hand-piped buttercream flowers, so describe the colour palette and the arrangement you have in mind. Choose one or two priorities rather than requesting every flower style in different references.

Send one clear image and explain which part to adapt. If you want an inscription, include the exact words and ask whether it fits without covering the flowers. Ask White Cakes to confirm the available floral design, price, and any size limitations. Do not assume an online photo is an exact product or copy guarantee.

If delivery is needed, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and access instructions. Confirm date availability and delivery fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the design and terms are settled.
