## Choose a reference photo for a church-to-lunch cake order

For a family lunch after church, the cake design can reflect the meal or family without needing to copy a religious symbol. Pick one clear reference that shows a colour or decorative idea and explain what you want adapted. If the family has an invitation or table decor, share only the details that help the bakery understand the look.

Do not send a collection of conflicting photos. Agree on one main direction and mark optional details. Ask White Cakes what is feasible for the chosen size and price. Provide the final inscription separately, with spelling checked by the family. A reference photo does not guarantee an exact replica or permit public use of someone else's cake image.

Include the date, serving estimate, flavour, pickup or delivery address, and receiver's contact. Confirm the handoff if the cake goes to a church hall or family venue. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; ask the bakery to confirm the fit. Allow at least three days' notice and secure the agreed order with the 50% deposit.
