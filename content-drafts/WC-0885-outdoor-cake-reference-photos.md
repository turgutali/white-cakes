## Choose an outdoor-cake reference that suits the real serving setup

If the celebration is outdoors, a reference photo should help explain the design, but the serving plan matters just as much. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; its guidance says cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars, and be refrigerated if held for several hours. Before ordering, decide whether the venue has a cool indoor place for the cake until dessert.

Choose one reference showing a colour or piped detail you want adapted. Tell the baker which element matters, then ask whether it fits the selected size and the practical serving plan. A photo does not confirm that the cake can remain outside for the whole event or that every detail will be copied exactly.

Include the date, serving time, guest count, cake format, and venue address in your enquiry. If delivered, provide a landmark, receiver's mobile number, and access instructions. Ask the bakery to confirm the delivery and storage plan before paying the deposit.

Use this advice in the shared custom brief and cake-care guides. A separate outdoor reference case requires an approved photo of a real cake and a distinct design decision.
