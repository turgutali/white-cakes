## Use a reference photo to show the lettering style you want

Choose a reference that makes the lettering or message placement clear. Tell White Cakes which part you like—font feel, alignment, colour, or surrounding decoration—and which details can change. One primary reference is easier to follow than multiple images with conflicting layouts.

Send the inscription in its exact final wording with spelling and punctuation checked. Ask whether the text fits the selected cake size and design. A photo may show a font or technique the bakery does not offer, so confirm what it can adapt and what the price includes.

Include the event date, flavour, size, serving estimate, and pickup or delivery plan. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the right size with White Cakes, allow at least three days' notice, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Only publish a reference-to-finished comparison with original photos and permission. Otherwise, keep these instructions in the central custom brief guide.
