## Choose a flavour the family will enjoy and confirm the Lapu-Lapu details

White Cakes currently lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet for custom cakes. Ask the family which flavour they prefer and send that name in the order enquiry. The flavour does not automatically promise a separate filling, fruit pieces, topping, or frosting; ask about those details separately and wait for confirmation.

Include the date, guest count, size, exact inscription, and delivery address. Use the current serving guide to estimate portions and ask White Cakes to confirm the size for the actual design. If guests have different preferences, ask whether the chosen format can accommodate more than one flavour; the 4-inch Bento is listed as one flavour.

For delivery in Lapu-Lapu, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and subdivision or venue entrance instructions. Confirm the delivery charge for the actual address and make sure someone can receive the box during the agreed window.

Keep menu flavour details on the central guide and local handover instructions on the Lapu-Lapu delivery page. A flavour-by-city URL would repeat the same menu choices without adding a different answer.

Tanungin ang pamilya kung aling lasa ang gusto nila at ipadala ang pangalang iyon sa pagtatanong ng order.
