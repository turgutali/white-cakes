## Confirm the child’s details, party plan, and full cost

Before paying a deposit, check the child’s name spelling, age, event date, requested size, flavour, design, and exact inscription. Confirm the venue address, receiving adult’s name and phone, and whether delivery is available. Ask for a complete quote that includes custom decoration and any delivery charge.

White Cakes’ current process states a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Verify the amount and payment method with the final written quote, then keep the receipt. Do not assume that an inquiry or design reference reserves the date; wait for the bakery’s confirmation.

Keep the deposit steps in the central order guide. Verify all current booking and payment details before publishing.
