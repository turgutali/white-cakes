## Compare Christmas-cake options by serving plan and design

Before choosing a festive design, decide how many people will eat cake. White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate portions, so tell the bakery whether there will be other desserts and whether you want generous slices.

Then compare the look and effort involved. A flavour choice, a colour palette, and one holiday detail can make a cake feel seasonal without crowding its surface. The current flavour list includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm what is available for the size and date. If you request a custom illustration or a detailed festive scene, send one reference and ask for a quote based on the actual work.

Holiday availability can be limited. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, but a Christmas date should be checked much earlier. Confirm design, size, flavour, delivery plan, and price in writing before paying the 50% deposit.

Keep the comparison inside the central cake-choice guide, with a link to the current menu. Do not publish an assumed seasonal product or stock claim without confirmation.
