## English

# Questions to Ask About an Anniversary Cake Quote in Cebu

An anniversary cake quote is easier to compare when every bakery is pricing the same order. Before asking for a figure, share the event date, cake size or guest estimate, flavour from the current menu, design direction, exact message, and whether you need pickup or delivery. A quote for a simple cake with a short inscription is not comparable to one for a larger custom design.

Ask what is included in the amount. Confirm whether it covers the selected cake size, flavour, hand-piped decoration, inscription, and any requested topper or special detail. White Cakes decorates with hand-piped buttercream and its current information does not list edible logo printing; if you want a company or personal logo, ask what interpretation is possible and whether it changes the price.

Check the delivery cost separately from the cake. Provide the complete Cebu address, landmark, receiver’s mobile number, and any gate or building access instructions. Ask whether the venue is within the service area, what delivery window is available, and whether the charge changes with the address. If pickup is an option, confirm the collection time and who will transport the cake to the event.

Before paying, ask for the total, deposit amount, accepted payment method, and terms for changes or cancellation in writing. Current White Cakes ordering information states a 50% deposit after the date, design, and final price are agreed; confirm the applicable terms in your quote. Keep the approved message, design notes, and price confirmation together so the anniversary order has one clear record. See the [Cebu cake price guide](/journal/cake-prices-cebu.html) and [anniversary cake guide](/journal/anniversary-cake-message-ideas-cebu.html).

Dili maparehas ang quote sa yano nga cake nga mubo ang mensahe ug sa mas dako nga custom design.

Pangutan-a unsay apil sa kantidad. Kumpirmaha kung apil ang napiling gidak-on, flavour, hand-piped nga dekorasyon, inskripsiyon ug topper o espesyal nga detalye. Hand-piped buttercream ang dekorasyon sa White Cakes ug wala sa kasamtangang impormasyon ang edible logo printing; kung gusto og logo sa kompanya o personal, pangutan-a unsay mahimo ug kung mausab ba ang presyo.

Susiha nga bulag ang bayad sa delivery. Ihatag ang kompletong address sa Cebu, landmark, mobile number sa modawat ug instruksyon sa gate o building. Pangutan-a kung apil ba ang venue sa service area, unsang delivery window ang available ug kung mausab ba ang bayad base sa address. Kung pwede ang pickup, kumpirmaha ang oras ug kinsa ang modala sa cake sa event.

Sa dili pa mobayad, pangayoa sa sinulat ang total, kantidad sa deposito, gidawat nga paagi sa pagbayad ug lagda sa pag-usab o pagkansela. Nag-ingon ang kasamtangang ordering information sa White Cakes og 50% nga deposito human magkasinabot sa petsa, disenyo ug kataposang presyo; kumpirmaha sa quote ang kondisyon para sa inyong order.
