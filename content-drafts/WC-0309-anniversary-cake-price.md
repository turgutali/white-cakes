## Ask for the current anniversary-cake quote, not a guessed package price

The price depends on which cake format and design you choose. White Cakes publishes starting prices for listed custom sizes; a Bouquet Cake is quoted on request because its scale and finish vary. A custom design can change the final quote, so an anniversary cake does not have one universal price just because of the occasion.

In your enquiry, state the date, cake size, flavour, inscription, reference photo, and how many people will share it. Ask whether the quoted amount is a starting price or the final price for that exact design. If the cake will be delivered, also ask for the address-based delivery charge and the agreed delivery window. Include the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number.

Menu prices are time-sensitive. Check the live price guide when ordering and do not reuse a previous celebration's screenshot as a current quote. The bakery should confirm availability, design, total, and payment details before you pay the 50% deposit.

Keep the current amounts in one maintained Cebu price page, where they can be updated when the menu changes. The anniversary guide can explain which formats suit different celebration sizes and link to that source rather than publishing a separate price page that quickly goes stale.
