## Balance the family’s preferences and confirm what is available now

Ask the host or family members which flavours the group tends to enjoy, then check the current menu with White Cakes. Its listed flavours have included Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet, but availability may vary by date. Do not assume that an old photo shows the current options.

If many relatives will share the cake, mention any known allergies or dietary needs and ask the bakery directly whether it can accommodate them. A flavour name does not confirm ingredients or safety. Decide the size based on servings separately and confirm the exact product details.
