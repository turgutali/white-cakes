## Choose for the person celebrating and check what is available now

For a birthday, begin with the celebrant’s preference. If the cake will be shared by a group, consider whether guests have familiar favourites and tell the bakery how many people are expected. White Cakes’ current listed flavours are Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm the live menu for availability on the requested date.

Ask what the chosen flavour includes and whether it can be paired with the design or filling you want. If anyone has an allergy or dietary restriction, explain it directly and ask the bakery whether it can accommodate it; a flavour name alone does not confirm ingredient safety. Confirm size and portions separately from flavour.
