## What to include in a custom brief for corporate cake in Cebu

A useful brief names the occasion, date, serving time, venue, approximate guest count, budget range and the cake’s role. Add the preferred size, flavour, exact message and one clear design reference; mark which details are essential and which can change.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, so describe the mood and a few specific details instead of asking for an exact copy of a screenshot. Ask the bakery to confirm an achievable interpretation before treating the design as final. For a corporate cake, send the approved brand colour and exact wording, and confirm who can authorize logo use. Keep the delivery contact reachable at the office or venue; do not assume reception can accept a cake without prior arrangement.
