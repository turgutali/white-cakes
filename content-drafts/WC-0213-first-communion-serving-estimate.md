## Estimate First Communion cake portions with the host

Ask the family how many guests will actually take cake and whether another dessert is planned. The number attending the service may not match the number at the meal. Also decide whether you want small slices or generous party portions; this changes how far one cake will go.

White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are planning ranges rather than guaranteed counts. If your expected number sits at the edge of a range, give the bakery the full serving plan and ask it to recommend a size.

Include the event date, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery or pickup plan in your enquiry. Confirm the size and current quote. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and availability should be confirmed before paying the 50% deposit. For venue delivery, provide the full address, landmark, and receiving contact.

Use this occasion example in the central size guide. Do not publish exact yields for every design without bakery verification.
