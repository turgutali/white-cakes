## How to estimate servings for First Communion cake in Cebu

Estimate portions from the serving plan, not from a photograph. White Cakes’ guide gives about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch round, 16–24 for an 8-inch round, and 30–36 for a common 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate and vary with slice size, height and design.

Tell the bakery whether cake is the main dessert, one of several sweets, or a ceremonial cut. Share the guest count and whether everyone needs a full slice; ask the team to confirm the best size for the exact design. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
