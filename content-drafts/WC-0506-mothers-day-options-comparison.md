## Compare Mother's Day cake options by size, flavour, and handoff

Start with how many people will actually eat cake. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. A 6-inch cake is estimated at 8–12 party slices, while an 8-inch is estimated at 16–24. These are approximate ranges; ask White Cakes to confirm based on the meal and other desserts.

Compare flavours from the current menu: Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm availability for the holiday date and chosen design. A custom flower or inscription may change the quote, so compare the total for the exact size and decoration rather than relying on a product photo.

Compare the delivery plan as well. Confirm the address, receiver, window, and fee, especially if it is a surprise. Check date availability early; made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep product choices and current prices on the central cake-choice guide and link the Mother's Day page for seasonal information.
