## Coordinate delivery for marble cake

Choose a person who can receive the cake during the agreed window. Provide White Cakes with the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Confirm the delivery fee and timing for the exact location; do not assume the driver can leave a cake unattended.

Plan where the cake will wait after handoff. Keep it level and away from direct sunlight and a hot vehicle. If serving is delayed for several hours, ask the bakery about storage for the specific design. If you mean Signature Marble, confirm its current product details and whether any custom changes affect care or price.

Include the event date, flavour, size, design reference, and inscription in the order. Confirm availability and the delivery plan in writing. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
