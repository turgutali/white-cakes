## Avoid unapproved logos and conflicting instructions from several colleagues

Before sending the brief, appoint one colleague to coordinate and approve the design. Confirm whether the company allows its logo or brand marks on the cake. Send one clean reference and explain whether the milestone number, company name, or short message is the priority.

Share the event date, size, serving estimate, flavour, exact wording, venue address, and receiver’s contact together. Request a complete quote, including delivery and custom decoration, then follow the current booking process. Confirm office access and handoff timing rather than assuming a rider can enter the building.

Keep these checks in the shared order guide. A corporate story requires company permission and a real cake example.
