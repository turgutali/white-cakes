## Decide which detail matters most in a minimalist cake

A minimalist cake depends on spacing and a few visible choices. Choose the base colour, a short inscription, and one focal element—such as a border or small flower. If every optional detail is added, the design may stop feeling simple. Tell White Cakes which element has priority and which can be left out.

Send one reference image with a note about what you like in it. Ask whether the finish and lettering are feasible for the chosen size and what they will cost. The bakery should confirm the final layout; a photo does not guarantee an exact copy or indicate the current price.

Include the date, flavour, serving plan, and delivery or pickup details. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

An original close-up can illustrate a real minimalist finish only if it is a White Cakes photo with publication permission and accurate technique details. Otherwise, add this advice to the existing design guide.
