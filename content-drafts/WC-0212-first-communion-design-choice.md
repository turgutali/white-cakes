## Choose a First Communion cake design that fits the celebration

Ask the family whether they prefer a simple cake for the meal or a more decorative centrepiece. Choose a restrained palette and one focal detail, then use the invitation or table decor as a reference if it helps. A cake can feel suited to the occasion without trying to reproduce every symbol or detail from the event.

Send one or two clear references and point out what should guide the design. Confirm with White Cakes whether the requested finish is feasible for the selected size and flavour. If you want a specific religious symbol or logo, confirm it is appropriate and provide a clean reference. Include the exact inscription and ask whether it fits the surface.

Estimate servings with the bakery: current ranges are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. The actual count depends on serving style. Share the event date and delivery plan, allow at least three days' notice, and confirm the quote before the 50% deposit secures the order.

Use original, approved White Cakes photos for a distinct First Communion design feature. Otherwise, add the advice to the general design page without inventing an event case.
