## Choose the flavour separately from the vintage-inspired finish

“Vintage” describes a visual style, not a flavour. Start with what the recipient likes, then check White Cakes' current menu: Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm availability for the requested date, size, and piping design before choosing a flavour.

If the cake is for a group, consider how many guests will eat it and whether there are other desserts. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Ask the bakery to confirm that the size suits the plan. If anyone has a dietary restriction, ask directly about ingredients and preparation; do not infer suitability from a flavour name.

Send the event date, size, flavour, one design reference, and exact inscription. Ask whether the vintage-inspired details are feasible and what the quote includes. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour choices in the central menu guide and vintage styling in the design guide. Avoid pages for every style-and-flavour combination.
