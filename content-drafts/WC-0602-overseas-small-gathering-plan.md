## Plan a small family cake from abroad with a local contact

Ask someone in Cebu how many people will share the cake and whether other desserts are planned. If only two or three relatives will have cake, the 4-inch Bento may fit: it serves about two generously or three modestly. For a larger group, ask about a 6-inch or 8-inch option.

White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Those are approximate ranges, not a guarantee. A local family member can clarify the expected serving style and help choose a size. Share the event date and flavour preference after confirming the person who will receive the order.

Give the bakery a full Cebu delivery address, landmark, receiver's local mobile number, and gate or building instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Ask directly which payment methods are accepted from abroad and what currency applies; do not assume. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep portion advice in the central size guide. Update overseas-payment instructions only after the bakery verifies the current process.
