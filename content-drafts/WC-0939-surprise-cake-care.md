## Keep the cake safe while the surprise is being arranged

Choose a receiver who knows a cake is arriving, even if the recipient does not. Give that person the delivery window, the full address and gate instructions, and a mobile number for the order. The receiver can keep the surprise intact while making sure the box is not left unattended at a guardhouse, in direct sun, or inside a hot car.

White Cakes' ordering guidance says buttercream cakes should be kept out of direct sun and hot cars, and refrigerated if they will be held for several hours before serving. Ask the bakery about the plan if the cake must wait during a long event programme. The person holding the box should know where the cake can be kept and when it will be brought out.

When ordering, send the buyer's contact for questions and a separate receiver's number for delivery. Include the occasion date, cake format, flavour, message, and guest count. At least three days' notice is requested, and the date must be confirmed by the bakery. The 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the quote and logistics are clear.

Keep the storage instructions in the shared cake-care guide and the receiver checklist in the delivery guide. Do not promise that the bakery can conceal a surprise from the recipient; agree directly who may be contacted and when.
