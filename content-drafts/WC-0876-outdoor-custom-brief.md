## Share the outdoor setting and how the cake will be protected

An outdoor celebration affects more than the design reference. Tell the bakery where the cake will be kept, when it will be served, and whether the setup is shaded or exposed to sun. Ask for advice about which finish and handoff timing suit the conditions. Do not assume a particular decoration can withstand heat without confirmation from the baker.

Your brief should also include the date, guest estimate, preferred size and flavour, inscription, design priorities, and delivery or pickup plan. Identify one must-have detail and mark the rest as flexible. Arrange for a named person to receive the cake and follow the product-specific storage instructions.

Keep the reusable checklist in the custom brief guide and verified handling advice in the heat-care guide. Avoid promises such as “heatproof” unless the bakery specifically supports them.
