## Short cake messages for a small gathering

On a small cake, one clear line is usually easier to read than a long quotation. Consider a name with “Happy Birthday,” a short congratulations, or a few words that the group will recognise. Send the exact wording to White Cakes with spelling and punctuation checked, and ask whether it fits the surface of your chosen cake.

The 4-inch Bento is the smallest listed format and has limited room for lettering. Its current serving guidance is two generous or three modest portions. If the gathering has more people, ask about a larger size and use the serving guide to plan. The message should fit the cake that serves the group rather than forcing the design into a smaller surface.

Send one reference photo if you want a particular colour or border. Tell the baker which detail matters, then let the rest adapt to the size. Include the date, flavour, and pickup or delivery preference. For delivery, share the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number.

Keep original message examples on the existing cake-message guide and the sizing note on the size page. A separate gallery needs original lines and photos demonstrating how each fits a real cake top.
