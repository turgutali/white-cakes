## Request a quote that includes the outdoor event plan

When asking for a quote, include the event date, cake size, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery address. Tell White Cakes the celebration is outdoors and when the cake will be served. The selected design may affect the price, and the bakery may need to advise on whether it suits the setting.

Current menu prices are time-sensitive: 6-inch cakes are listed at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 afterward; 8-inch cakes are ₱1,999 through that date and ₱2,599 from January 16. Verify the live menu before using these amounts. Ask whether custom decoration or delivery changes the total.

Confirm the delivery window, fee, and a receiver who can move the cake to a shaded place. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so ask what care instructions apply. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Keep current price and outdoor care information in their maintained guides. Do not imply a cake is suitable for outdoor heat without bakery confirmation.
