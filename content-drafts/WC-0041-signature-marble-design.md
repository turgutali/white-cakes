## English

# How to choose a design for Signature Marble cake in Cebu

The Signature Marble design already has a dusty-blue marbled finish, shell border, and one rose. If that look suits the occasion, order the listed 6-inch version; if you want other colours or motifs, ask about a custom brief instead.

Do not infer that every reference can be added to the signature design without changing the scope or price. Confirm the final design and current availability.
