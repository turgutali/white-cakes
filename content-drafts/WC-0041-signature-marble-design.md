## What is fixed and what can be discussed on Signature Marble

Signature Marble is White Cakes' named 6-inch design: ivory buttercream with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. That identity makes it different from a fully custom cake, where the brief and reference photos guide a hand-piped design within the bakery's capability. If you want the Signature Marble, start with its established look; if you want different colours or piping, ask whether the request should be quoted as a custom variation.

The design still needs to fit the occasion and the people sharing it. The cake-size guide gives a planning estimate of about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round. Actual portions vary with the cake height, cut, and finish, so send the guest count and ask for confirmation if the event needs a particular number of servings.

When you enquire, include the date, flavour, inscription, and whether you want the standard Signature Marble or a custom change. A photo can help explain a variation, but it does not guarantee the exact reference will be copied. Ask for the current quote and delivery charge before the 50% deposit confirms the order.

Use the actual Signature Marble photo and fixed design description on the product page. Keep design advice on the existing cake-design guide unless there is a specific, approved photo showing a genuinely different Marble variation.
