## Match the graduation cake design to the graduate

Ask the graduate what they want the cake to feel like: classic, playful, school-themed, or focused on a personal achievement. Choose one main detail and a palette, then keep the remaining decorations simple enough that the message is readable.

Send White Cakes one reference image and explain what you want adapted. If you want a school logo, confirm that it is approved for use and provide clean artwork. Ask what the bakery can make for the selected size and whether the design changes the quote. A reference photo is not a guarantee of an exact copy.

Include the event date, flavour, serving estimate, inscription, and delivery plan. The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Confirm the size with the bakery and allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use original, permission-cleared White Cakes photos for a specific graduation design feature. Otherwise, merge these principles into the general cake-design guide.
