## Plan a minimalist cake for a small gathering

Choose the serving size before settling on the minimal design. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. A 6-inch cake is estimated at 8–12 party slices, and an 8-inch at 16–24. These are approximate ranges; tell White Cakes how many people will eat cake and whether there are other desserts.

For a compact cake, a short inscription and one focal detail may be enough. Ask the bakery whether the preferred layout fits the selected size and confirm the final design and price. A simple look still needs a clear brief and a confirmed production slot.

Include the date, flavour, serving plan, and pickup or delivery location. Confirm availability and the agreed handoff. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and the 50% deposit confirms the order after terms are settled.
