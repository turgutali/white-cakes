## Match a minimalist cake size to the serving plan

Choose size based on how many people will eat cake; a minimal design does not change the portion estimate. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. A 6-inch cake is estimated at 8–12 party slices, and an 8-inch cake at 16–24. These are approximate ranges, so tell White Cakes about the guest count, other desserts, and slice size.

Ask the bakery whether the simple layout works on the selected size. A short inscription and one focal detail may suit a compact cake, while more wording could require a larger surface. Confirm the flavour and design are available for the date and request a final quote.

Include the event date, delivery or pickup plan, and receiving contact. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; the current 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after details are settled.

Keep portion estimates in the central sizing guide and discuss minimalist layout in the design guide rather than publishing overlapping size charts.
