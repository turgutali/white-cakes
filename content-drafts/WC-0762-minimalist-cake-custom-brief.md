## A minimalist-cake brief with one clear priority

Begin with the cake size and guest count, then choose one visual detail to lead the design: a quiet colour, a small piped border, a single flower, or short lettering. Send one reference photo and identify that feature. If the reference includes extra decorations you do not want, say so; this keeps the final cake from becoming busier than intended.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream. Signature Marble has an established dusty-blue marbled look; fully custom work can follow a palette and brief within the selected size. Ask what can be simplified or adapted and how the choice affects the quote. A photo is a guide, not a promise of identical reproduction.

Send the date, flavour, exact inscription, expected servings, and pickup or delivery details. At least three days' notice is requested. The bakery confirms availability and final design before the 50% deposit confirms the order.

Keep general brief advice in the custom-cake guide and minimalist examples on the existing style page. A new brief page should add a real design case, not another copy of the same instructions.
