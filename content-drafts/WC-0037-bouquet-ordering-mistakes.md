## Confirm the actual Bouquet Cake design and delivery needs

Do not rely on an old social photo to assume the current product details, price, or availability. Check the live Bouquet Cake listing and confirm its size, current design description, flavour options, requested date, and total cost. If you want a change, state it clearly and ask whether it is possible before paying.

Because the Bouquet Cake is described as hand-piped buttercream flowers, ask how the finished cake should be transported and stored. Share the receiving address, event time, and a reachable contact. Confirm the inscription exactly and allow enough lead time for the bakery to check availability.

Keep product facts on the product page and general booking advice in the order guide. Update both from current business information rather than multiplying near-identical product pages.
