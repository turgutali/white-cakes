## Avoid these small mistakes when ordering a thank-you cake

**Leaving the message vague.** Send the exact short line and check the recipient's name. A short message is easier to fit on a small cake and less likely to need last-minute changes.

**Asking for an unconfirmed design.** Send one reference photo and say what to interpret. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, so a reference guides the finish rather than guaranteeing an identical copy.

**Forgetting the guest count.** Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and whether it follows a meal. Use the size guide to choose between Bento and larger formats; do not estimate servings from a picture.

**Sending delivery details at the last minute.** Include the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instruction. Confirm a delivery window and have someone ready to receive it.

White Cakes requests at least three days' notice. Confirm the date, flavour, design, quote, and delivery charge before paying the 50% deposit.
