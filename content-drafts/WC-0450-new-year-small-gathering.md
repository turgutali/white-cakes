## Estimate the New Year portions and confirm the date early

For a small New Year gathering, count the people who will share dessert and consider whether the cake follows a full meal. Tell the bakery whether you want generous portions or a small celebratory slice. If some guests will arrive after midnight or leave early, use the expected serving-time headcount rather than the total invitation list.

The current guidance lists about two generous or three modest servings for a 4-inch Bento. Larger options have approximate party-slice ranges in the central size guide. Ask the bakery to confirm the appropriate size and whether your requested date is available; holiday schedules may differ from ordinary weeks.

Keep portion estimates in the size guide and New Year booking reminders in the order guide. Recheck the current seasonal schedule before publishing.
