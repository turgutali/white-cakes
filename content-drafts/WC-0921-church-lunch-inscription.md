## Choose a short message for the family lunch cake

For a family lunch after church, a simple message can connect the cake to the gathering without taking over the design. Consider the celebrant's name, a family greeting, or a short phrase. These are suggestions rather than real White Cakes customer messages. Decide which detail matters most before sending the order.

Send the final text with spelling, capitalization, and punctuation checked. If the family wants wording in Cebuano or Tagalog, have a fluent relative check the phrase and tone first. Ask White Cakes whether the message fits the chosen size and decoration; a small surface may not leave room for a long line.

Include the date, flavour, size, serving estimate, and delivery or pickup plan. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size with the bakery and provide the receiver's contact if delivery is needed. Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
