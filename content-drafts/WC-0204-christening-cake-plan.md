## Plan the christening cake around the meal and the handover

Start with the number of people who will eat cake and when it will be served. A family lunch after a ceremony may have a different portion plan from a cake served later at a reception. Use the current size guide to compare Bento, 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier estimates; tell White Cakes whether you expect full slices or smaller dessert portions after a meal.

Choose the inscription and design next. Send the child's name with spelling checked and keep the line short enough to fit the cake top. If you have a reference photo, point out the colour or piped detail to interpret. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and can confirm what is practical for the selected size.

For delivery, give the church or reception venue's full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any access instruction. Choose someone who can meet the agreed delivery window and keep the cake out of direct sun. If the family will be away during the service, plan the receiver and storage before placing the order.

At least three days' notice is requested; the date and quote must be confirmed before the 50% deposit. Keep the serving plan in the size guide and the ceremony context on the existing christening page.
