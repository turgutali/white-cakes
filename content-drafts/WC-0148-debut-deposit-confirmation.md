## Confirm the debut-cake order before sending the deposit

Make sure White Cakes has confirmed the event date and production slot. A debut has a fixed schedule, so check the date early and leave at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. An enquiry or design discussion alone does not reserve the date.

Review the full order in writing: size, flavour, final design, exact inscription, price, and delivery or pickup plan. If the cake is going to a venue, confirm the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, access instructions, delivery window, and fee. Ask the venue who can accept the cake and where it can be stored before serving.

The current White Cakes process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in the current quote and keep the receipt with the confirmed order. Ask the bakery to resolve any unclear design or payment term before sending money.

Keep deposit instructions in the main ordering guide and recheck them against the current business process before publication.
