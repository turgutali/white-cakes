## What to confirm before paying a deposit for debut cake in Cebu

Before paying, check that the written summary names the debut date, cake size, flavour, design direction, exact inscription, total price, delivery arrangement and person who will receive it. Correct spelling and venue details while changes are still easy to confirm.

The stated 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after its details and price are settled. Ask how the remaining balance is handled and what happens if the event timing changes. Keep the confirmation and payment record together; do not rely on a social message that omits the final specification.
