## Prepare the delivery handoff for Valentine's Day

Valentine's Day is a date when a recipient may be away from home or at work. Before confirming delivery, check that someone can receive the cake during the agreed window. Give White Cakes the full address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any gate, lobby, or office instructions. If the cake is a surprise, make sure the driver has a way to reach the recipient without revealing the message to the wrong person.

Ask about delivery availability and the fee for the exact address. Confirm who will receive the box and where it can be kept out of direct sunlight and heat. If it will sit for several hours, follow the bakery's refrigeration guidance. Do not assume a driver can wait indefinitely or leave a cake in an unsecured place.

Send the order date, flavour, size, short inscription, and design request early. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and a Valentine's date should be checked well in advance. Confirm the agreed order details; the 50% deposit secures the confirmed order.

Add only verified seasonal delivery instructions to the shared Cebu delivery guide. Avoid promising exact arrival times or special handling that the bakery has not confirmed.
