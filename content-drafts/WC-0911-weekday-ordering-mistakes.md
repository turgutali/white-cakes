## Avoid weekday cake-order problems by confirming the handoff

Do not assume a weekday date is automatically available. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and the requested design still needs confirmation. Ask about the date and production slot before planning the event around delivery.

If the cake is going to an office, do not provide only the company name. Include the full building address, tower or entrance, floor or receiving point, contact mobile number, and visitor instructions. Confirm who will meet the driver and whether the building accepts food deliveries at that time.

Avoid sending incomplete design details in separate messages. Provide the size, flavour, exact inscription, reference photo, serving estimate, and final pickup or delivery preference together. Confirm the feasible design, total quote, window, and fee. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after these terms are clear.

This is a general checklist rather than a documented mistake by a real customer. Place it in the central order guide and add weekday building details only when verified.
