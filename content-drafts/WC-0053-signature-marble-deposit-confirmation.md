## Confirm the Signature Marble product and order terms

Before paying, confirm that Signature Marble is available for the required date. The current listing describes a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose. Check that the design and price are current, and list any requested change—such as colour, flower, or inscription—so the bakery can confirm whether it is feasible and costs extra.

Review the full order: flavour, size, serving estimate, exact wording, pickup or delivery, total price, and delivery fee if applicable. A 6-inch cake is currently estimated at 8–12 party portions; confirm that the size suits your group. Provide the full address, landmark, and receiving contact for delivery.

White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Its current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Pay according to the current quote and save the receipt with the order confirmation.

Keep the product-specific note in the single ordering guide, and recheck the listing and deposit process before publication.
