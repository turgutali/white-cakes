## Choose a company-milestone cake flavour for the team

Ask whether the recipient or team has a known preference, then check White Cakes' current menu. Its listed flavours include Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm which options are available for the chosen date, size, and design before announcing the flavour to colleagues.

For an office group, consider whether the cake is the only dessert and how many people will eat it. Ask the bakery for a size recommendation. If someone has an allergy or dietary restriction, request ingredient and preparation information directly; do not assume a menu flavour is safe for that person based on its name.

Include the event date, serving count, office address, and receiving contact. Confirm flavour availability, delivery window, and fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all details are settled.

Keep verified flavour details in the central menu and link to corporate ordering information. Avoid unsupported taste descriptions or separate pages for each occasion-flavour combination.
