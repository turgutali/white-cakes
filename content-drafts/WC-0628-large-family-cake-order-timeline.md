## Plan a large-family cake early enough to confirm size and design

Start by estimating how many people will eat cake and when it will be served. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice, but a large family gathering may need earlier discussion so the bakery can confirm the size, design, and date. A short-notice enquiry is not a confirmed production slot.

Use the current size guide to compare Bento, 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier estimates. Tell the bakery whether you expect full slices, small dessert portions after a meal, or slices to take home. Ask for a recommendation for the actual design instead of relying on a photograph or total invitation count.

Send the event date, flavour, size request, inscription, and one reference photo in the first message. For delivery, include the full address, landmark, gate instructions, and the receiver's mobile number. Confirm the quote and delivery window before the 50% deposit.

Keep one central Cebu ordering-timeline page and add this family-size example as a section. If the guest count changes, update the bakery before production rather than assuming the original size will stretch to the new number.
