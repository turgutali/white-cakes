## Compare cake options by the message and serving plan

If the inscription is the main feature, compare sizes by how much surface the wording needs. A 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions, but it also has a compact writing area. For larger cakes, current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Ask White Cakes whether the exact message fits the chosen design.

Compare design scope as well. A short line may suit a simple layout; longer wording may require fewer decorations or a different size. Send the text exactly as it should appear and one lettering reference if needed. Ask the bakery what can be adapted and request a quote for the actual work.

Include the event date, flavour, serving estimate, and pickup or delivery plan. Confirm current price and date availability. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep message, size, and price facts linked from one cake-choice guide rather than creating separate pages for every inscription length.
