## Choose birthday-cake references that clarify the request

Pick one image that shows the main design idea: colours, decoration, shape, or lettering. Tell White Cakes which part you want to adapt and what can change. If the birthday celebrant has several inspirations, agree on the priority first instead of sending a folder that suggests different cakes.

Do not assume the reference is an exact product or a guaranteed copy. Ask the bakery whether the technique and details are feasible for the selected size and what they will cost. If you use the photo online later, do not repost another baker's image without permission; take your own photo of the finished cake.

Include the event date, flavour, number of cake eaters, inscription, and pickup or delivery plan. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions; the current 6-inch estimate is 8–12 party slices. Confirm the size and date with White Cakes, allow at least three days' notice, and use the 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Keep reference tips in the custom-brief guide and link to the birthday page for occasion-specific details.
