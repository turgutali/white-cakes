## Confirm the inscription and full custom order before paying

Read the approved inscription carefully before paying the deposit. Check spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and dates. Ask White Cakes to confirm that the text fits the chosen design and size; if it recommends shortening the message, agree on the final wording in writing rather than leaving the choice open.

Review the rest of the order too: date, size, flavour, design reference, final quote, and pickup or delivery plan. If delivery is needed, verify the complete address, landmark, receiving contact, delivery window, and fee. Confirm the requested date is available; an enquiry is not a booking.

White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes and currently uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Follow the current payment instructions and keep the receipt with the confirmation. Resolve any unclear detail before sending payment.

Keep inscription checks and deposit terms in the central order guide. Verify payment terms whenever the bakery updates its process.
