## Choose a First Communion cake flavour from the confirmed menu

White Cakes currently lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet for custom cakes. Ask the family which flavour the people at the table enjoy, then send the chosen name exactly. The menu flavour describes the cake flavour; it does not automatically include a separate filling, topping, fruit pieces, or frosting.

If the celebration follows a church service and lunch, share the guest count and how the cake will be served. If guests have different preferences, ask whether a larger cake can accommodate more than one flavour. The 4-inch Bento is listed as one flavour, and a split in another size needs confirmation for that design and date.

Send the event date, cake size, inscription, flavour, and delivery plan in one message. If you expect a specific ingredient or finish, ask about it separately before paying. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice, and the date must be confirmed by the bakery.

Use this note on the existing First Communion or christening guide and link to the central flavour page. A separate occasion-specific flavour article would repeat the same five choices unless first-hand tasting or a distinct product detail is available.
