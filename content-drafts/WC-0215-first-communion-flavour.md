## How to choose a flavour for First Communion cake in Cebu

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask what the selected flavour means in the current menu: the name alone does not promise a separate filling, fruit layer or topping. Confirm the cake construction and raise any ingredient or allergy question directly.

For a mixed-age gathering, choose from the bakery’s confirmed options and tell the host what flavour is being served. Do not assume an unlisted combination or special dietary version is available without written confirmation. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
