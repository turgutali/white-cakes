## Plan the cake around who will share Father's Day dessert

Begin with the people who will actually eat cake. A 4-inch Bento from White Cakes serves two generously or three modestly, while the current planning guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round and 16–24 from an 8-inch round. A common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier design is planned for roughly 30–36. These counts vary with the cut and the design, so ask the bakery to confirm the portions for the cake you choose.

Think about the occasion before choosing a finish. A short message on a compact Bento suits a small family surprise; Signature Marble provides a more classic 6-inch buttercream style; custom designs can use a clear colour or reference brief. If the cake is for a larger gathering, share the guest count and ask about the right size rather than choosing a tier from a photo.

Send the date, flavour, exact message, size request, and pickup or delivery preference in one enquiry. For delivery, add the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby note. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice, but holiday availability must be confirmed. The cake is not reserved until the bakery replies with the agreed details and the 50% deposit confirms the order.
