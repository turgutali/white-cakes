## Plan Christmas cake size from the family table

Before selecting a holiday design, count the people who are likely to eat cake and decide when it will be served. White Cakes' size guide estimates two generous or three modest servings from a 4-inch Bento, about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, and about 16–24 from an 8-inch. A common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier arrangement is planned for roughly 30–36. These are planning ranges, not fixed guarantees.

Tell the bakery whether the cake follows a full meal, whether there will be other desserts, and whether some guests may take slices home. Ask for a size recommendation for the actual design. A photograph of a festive cake cannot show the serving count.

December dates can fill earlier than ordinary weeks. Send the date and flavour early, then confirm availability, design, and current price. For delivery, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's number, and gate or lobby instructions. Agree who will receive the cake and where it will be kept before the meal.

Keep the size estimates on the shared guide and the seasonal schedule on the Christmas page. Recheck the price before publication each holiday season so no outdated offer is presented as current.
