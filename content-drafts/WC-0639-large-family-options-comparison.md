## Compare large-family cake options by portions and design

Start with the number of people who will actually eat cake and whether other desserts are planned. The current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; ask White Cakes to confirm which option fits the way your family will serve it.

Compare design scope next. A simple family message may fit a single-tier cake, while a milestone motif or tiered centrepiece could need a custom quote. Send one reference and explain what can be adapted. Confirm the flavour and design are available for the requested date.

Compare the handoff plan too. Provide a complete home or venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit secures the agreed order after details are settled.

Keep the comparison in one cake-choice guide and link to the sizing, price, and delivery pages instead of duplicating them.
