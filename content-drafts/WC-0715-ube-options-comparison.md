## Compare the available size and design after confirming ube is on the menu

Ask whether ube is currently available for the requested date and what the option includes. Then compare sizes against the number of people who will share the cake, and ask the bakery to confirm portion estimates. The flavour does not determine servings.

If you are choosing between designs, compare the finish, inscription space, current price, and pickup or delivery plan. Use a reference to explain the look, but do not assume it guarantees an exact copy or an unchanged product. Raise allergy and ingredient questions directly with the bakery.
