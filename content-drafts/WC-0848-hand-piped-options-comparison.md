## Compare the cake size and the visible piping feature separately

First estimate servings and ask the bakery which current sizes fit the guest count. Then compare the hand-piped details you want, such as a border, lettering, or floral accents. Ask which of those finishes can be made on the selected cake and whether the reference is adaptable.

Consider how much writing space remains, the event date, flavour availability, and the complete price. Use original White Cakes photos to compare actual work, not images from another bakery as a promise. Confirm the final design and handoff in writing.

Update existing product and design pages with current options. Link to the central size guide rather than repeating portion counts in each design comparison.

Pagkatapos ay ihambing ang mga hand-piped na detalye na gusto mo, gaya ng border, lettering, o floral accent.
