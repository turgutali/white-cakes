## Send a Father's Day reference that shows the detail you want

Choose one photo with a clear colour, piping, or lettering detail and explain what you want White Cakes to interpret. If the recipient prefers a quieter look, a simple palette and short message may communicate more than several decorations. Avoid sending a collage of styles that point in different directions.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream. A reference can guide the design, but it does not guarantee an exact copy or a printed edible logo. If you want a company logo, a sports symbol, or another specific image, ask what can be represented by hand before the cake is confirmed. Keep the inscription short enough to fit the chosen size.

Include the event date, guest count, flavour, and cake format in the same enquiry. Use the current size guide to choose a portion range. For delivery, share the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instructions.

This checklist belongs in the custom brief and Father's Day guides. A separate photo case requires a real, permission-cleared cake and a distinct design choice visible in the finished result.
