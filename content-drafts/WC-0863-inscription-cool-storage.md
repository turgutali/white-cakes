## Protect the cake while it waits for the event

Ask the bakery how to store the specific custom cake, including its filling, frosting, and lettering finish. Tell it when the cake will arrive and when it will be served. If the venue is warm, outdoors, or far from the bakery, mention that when asking for product-specific handling advice.

Arrange for someone to receive the cake promptly, keep the box level, and keep it out of direct sun. Follow the bakery’s instructions about refrigeration and serving time; an inscription does not indicate whether the cake can stay at room temperature. If the schedule changes, ask the baker before changing the storage plan.

Keep verified care instructions in the central Cebu heat guide and link from the custom-design content. Avoid a one-size-fits-all storage duration.
