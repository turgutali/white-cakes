## Prepare the receiving point for Signature Marble delivery

Signature Marble is a 6-inch cake with ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. The person receiving it should know when the delivery window is and where to meet the driver. Send White Cakes the complete address, a nearby landmark, and the receiver's mobile number. Add a gate, condo lobby, office reception, or venue entrance instruction where relevant.

Once the cake is handed over, keep the box upright and out of direct sun and hot cars. If it will be held for several hours, the bakery's ordering guidance says to refrigerate buttercream cakes. Make sure someone can move the cake from the reception point to an appropriate place rather than leaving it unattended.

When you order, give the event date and ask whether it is available. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice, then confirms the quote and the delivery charge for the actual address. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order. The delivery window is a range, not an exact-minute appointment, so the receiving person should remain reachable during it.

This product-specific note can sit in the existing delivery guide next to the general handover and cake-care instructions. A separate marble delivery page would repeat the same route and handling facts without answering a different question.
