## English

# How to match the design of minimalist cake in Cebu

Match the minimalist cake to one clear idea, then remove details that do not support it. A small motif, a restrained colour choice, or clean lettering can each lead; combining all three may no longer feel minimal. Ask the bakery how spacing and placement will work at the confirmed size.

Review an actual approved White Cakes example only as inspiration, not a guaranteed replica. Confirm the finish, exact wording, and focal element in writing before approving the quote.

## Tagalog

# Paano itugma ang disenyo ng minimalist na cake sa Cebu

Itugma ang minimalist cake sa iisang malinaw na ideya at alisin ang detalyeng hindi nakatutulong. Maaaring manguna ang maliit na motif, payak na kulay, o malinis na lettering; kapag pinagsama lahat, maaaring hindi na ito minimalist. Itanong kung paano aayusin ang spacing at puwesto sa kumpirmadong sukat.

Tingnan ang totoong aprubadong White Cakes example bilang inspirasyon lang, hindi garantisadong kopya. Kumpirmahin sa sulat ang finish, eksaktong teksto, at focal element bago aprubahan ang quote.
