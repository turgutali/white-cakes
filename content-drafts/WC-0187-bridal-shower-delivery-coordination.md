## How to coordinate bridal shower cake in Cebu

Assign one cake contact who has the confirmed order summary, delivery window and venue details. That person should answer the rider, notify the venue coordinator and make sure the cake reaches its planned table without being left unattended.

Share a short handoff note with the receiver’s name and phone, landmark, entry instructions, cake-table location and time of service. Avoid parallel revisions from several people; send any change through the single contact and get it reconfirmed. For a bridal shower, treat the couple’s or host’s palette as a guide and avoid reproducing private or copyrighted details without permission. Confirm who approves the inscription and final design so the surprise stays coordinated.
