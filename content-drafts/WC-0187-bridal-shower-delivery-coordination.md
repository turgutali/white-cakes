## Coordinate the bridal-shower cake with the venue contact

Choose one person who can receive the cake during the agreed delivery window. Give White Cakes the venue's full address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any gate, lobby, or function-room instructions. Confirm where the driver should meet the contact and whether the venue can accept a cake at that time.

Plan where the cake will wait before serving. Keep it on a stable surface away from direct sunlight and heat; ask the bakery about refrigeration if several hours will pass. Do not leave a cake unattended in a lobby or assume that venue staff know who ordered it.

Include the event date, size, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery contact in the order enquiry. Confirm availability, fee, and window for the actual location. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after details are settled.

Use a real venue case only with verified route information and permission for photos. Keep general coordination steps in the shared Cebu delivery guide.
