## Choose a flavour that fits the hand-piped cake order

Pick a flavour from the current White Cakes menu, then confirm it is available for the requested size and design. The listed choices include Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Do not assume a particular flavour has a specific filling or texture unless the bakery confirms its current recipe details.

The decoration and flavour need to be considered together when you discuss the order. Explain the event, number of cake eaters, and whether the cake will be served immediately or held for a while. If the design is detailed, ask whether the requested size and decoration are feasible and whether any handling advice applies. Do not infer a special storage rule from the flavour alone.

Send the date, exact inscription, one design reference, size, and delivery or pickup plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm the final quote and terms; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep flavours in the central menu guide and hand-piped options in the design guide. Avoid one thin page for each flavour-decoration combination.
