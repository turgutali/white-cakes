## Ask for a current Father's Day cake quote

Share the event date, size, flavour, design, inscription, serving estimate, and delivery or pickup location. Father's Day designs may range from a simple message to custom decoration, so ask White Cakes for a quote based on the exact request rather than assuming a seasonal image matches a listed price.

The current custom-menu prices are time-limited: a 6-inch cake is ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16; an 8-inch cake is ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 afterward. Check the current menu before using those amounts. Confirm whether custom decoration or delivery changes the final total.

Ask which size fits the number of people who will eat cake. The current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm availability early, allow at least three days' notice, and verify delivery timing and fee. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep temporary rates in the central price guide and update or remove them when their effective dates pass.

Ang mga disenyo ng Father's Day ay maaaring mula sa isang simpleng mensahe hanggang sa custom na dekorasyon, kaya humingi ng isang quote sa White Cakes batay sa eksaktong kahilingan sa halip na ipagpalagay na ang isang pana-panahong larawan ay tumutugma sa isang nakalistang presyo.
