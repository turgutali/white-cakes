## Coordinate a cake order for family in Cebu while abroad

The person paying and the person receiving the cake may be in different time zones. Send the celebration date, recipient's full name and mobile number, complete Cebu address, landmark, and a local contact who can answer delivery questions. Confirm with your family that someone will be present during the agreed delivery window; a surprise can fail if the recipient is away or the driver cannot enter the building.

Choose the cake with the recipient in mind. Give the number of people who will eat it, a flavour from the current menu, one design reference, and the exact message. Ask White Cakes to confirm the final quote, available date, delivery fee, and handoff details. Do not assume international payment methods, currency conversion, or delivery coverage; ask the bakery to confirm what it can accept for this order.

White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Wait for written confirmation of the full order and the available payment instructions. The current process uses a 50% deposit to secure the agreed order. Keep the order reference and share the recipient's contact details only as needed for delivery.

Add a real overseas-order case only if the payment and delivery details are verified and the customer consents. General coordination belongs in the Cebu delivery guide.
