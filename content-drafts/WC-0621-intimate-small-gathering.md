## Decide whether a small cake provides enough portions for the gathering

Count who will actually share dessert and whether the group expects a small taste or a generous slice. Tell the bakery whether the cake follows a meal and if other sweets will be served. Ask which current size fits that plan and confirm the serving estimate.

The 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous or three modest servings. Confirm this for the current product and date. If more friends or family may join, ask about a larger option before choosing a design.
