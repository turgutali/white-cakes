## Choose Bento details that fit a four-inch top

The White Cakes Bento is a 4-inch round with a compact surface. Its current example has ivory buttercream, piped shells, a blush ribbon, and one rose. Those elements create a finished look while leaving limited space for a message. If you want a different colour or border, send one reference photo and identify the detail to adapt.

Keep the inscription short. A name and brief greeting are easier to fit than several lines. Send the exact wording with spelling checked, then ask the bakery to confirm that it remains readable with the requested piping. The cake is listed for two generous or three modest servings, so it is designed for a small group rather than a large birthday table.

White Cakes hand-pipes the design and makes the cake to order. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery confirms the date, flavour, and current quote before the 50% deposit. Include pickup or delivery details with the design request.

Keep these size-specific choices on the existing Bento page and use a product photo that matches the current finish. Update the description if the actual design changes.
