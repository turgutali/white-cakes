## Share the weekday building access and the person receiving the cake

Provide the exact address, requested delivery window, receiver’s name and phone number, and any office, school, or condo access instructions. Ask whether the bakery can deliver to that location and confirm the timing and fee. A weekday does not guarantee a route or arrival time.

Have the receiver ready to accept the cake directly and follow the product-specific storage directions. If the cake will arrive well before a meeting or dinner, ask the bakery how to care for it. Keep the box level and away from direct sun or heat.

Add these practical points to the central Cebu delivery guide. Verify building access and current delivery availability before publishing.
