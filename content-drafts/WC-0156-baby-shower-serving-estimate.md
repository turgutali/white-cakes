## Estimate baby-shower cake servings from the guest plan

Ask the host how many people will actually eat cake and whether the shower will have other desserts. Some guests may take a small slice while others may skip cake. Decide whether you want a tasting portion or a full party slice, then share that with White Cakes.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges and are not exact guarantees. Ask the bakery to confirm which size fits the expected group and serving style.

For a very small shower, a 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. Include the event date, flavour, design, and delivery location in the request. Confirm size and serving guidance for the exact cake. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit secures the agreed order.
