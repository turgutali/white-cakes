## Estimate office-cake servings from the team plan

Count the colleagues who are likely to eat cake, then decide whether the office will serve small slices or full party portions. If the cake is one item on a snack table, fewer people may take a slice. If it is the only dessert after a meeting, the host may want to plan for more.

White Cakes' current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are ranges, not guaranteed yields. Share the team count, other desserts, and serving style with the bakery so it can confirm a size.

For a larger group, ask whether a tiered configuration is appropriate rather than extrapolating from a single tier. Include the office address, receiving contact, and delivery timing in the order. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep these portion estimates in the central cake-size guide and link to the corporate order page. Recheck the serving data before publication.
