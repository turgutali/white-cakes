## Prepare a Christmas cake handoff before holiday traffic

Choose a receiver who will be available during the agreed delivery window and give White Cakes that person's local mobile number. Provide the complete address, landmark, and any building or gate instructions. Confirm the delivery route, window, and fee for the destination; do not assume holiday traffic or access will match a normal day.

If the cake is for a surprise, decide who can answer the driver without revealing the occasion. Make sure that person knows where the cake should go after handoff. Keep it on a stable, shaded surface and follow the bakery's care instructions if it arrives hours before serving.

Check the date early. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and holiday availability may be limited. Confirm flavour, size, design, inscription, and delivery details in writing. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the terms are settled.

Use only verified seasonal delivery instructions in the central Cebu delivery guide. Do not promise a holiday arrival time without confirmation.
