## Confirm office access and cake details before paying

Before paying a deposit, confirm the cake date and production slot with White Cakes. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, but the office schedule and building access should be checked early. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Review the order summary for cake size, flavour, design, exact inscription, final price, and delivery or pickup. If delivery is planned to IT Park, verify the tower, entrance, receiving point, colleague's mobile number, visitor instructions, delivery window, and fee. Make sure the colleague can meet the driver and bring the cake inside.

The current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in the current quote and save the receipt with the written confirmation. Resolve any open question about the logo, message, or delivery access before sending payment.

Keep deposit terms in one current ordering guide. Do not claim IT Park access rules unless verified from the specific building or delivery.
