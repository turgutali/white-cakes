## Ask for a quote for the size, date, and finish you want

“Vanilla cake” alone is not enough information for a reliable quote. Share the requested size, date, serving estimate, design, and whether you want an inscription or added decoration. Ask what the bakery’s current vanilla option includes and whether it is available for your date.

Confirm the total, including any custom finish or delivery fee, before sending payment. A base menu price may not include every design request, and prices can change. If you have a set budget, say so early and ask which available size or simpler decoration might fit it.
