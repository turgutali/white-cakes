## Give the bakery the date, serving plan, and one personal design idea

Include the monthsary date, how many people will share the cake, preferred size, flavour, and whether pickup or delivery is needed. For the design, choose one detail that feels personal to the couple, such as a short phrase, a favourite colour, or a small symbol. Mark what is essential and what can be adapted.

Send the exact inscription and spelling. If the cake is a surprise, name one organizer who can approve the design and receive the order. Ask whether the idea fits the selected size, confirm the total price, and follow the bakery’s current booking process.

Keep this as a concise use case in the custom-cake guide. A separate story needs a real approved order and original photography, not just a new occasion label.
