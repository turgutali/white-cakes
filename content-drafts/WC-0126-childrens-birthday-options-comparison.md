## Compare size and design against the child’s party plan

First estimate the children and adults who will eat cake and whether the party includes other desserts. Ask the bakery which available size fits the expected portions. Compare designs separately: a clear theme or focal detail can work better than a crowded assortment of decorations.

Confirm the current flavour, inscription, date, and price. If you have a character or logo reference, ask what the bakery can create and do not assume it will reproduce protected artwork exactly. For allergies, discuss ingredients and accommodation directly with the bakery.
