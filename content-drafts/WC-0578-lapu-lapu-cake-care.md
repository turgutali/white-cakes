## Keep a Lapu-Lapu family cake cool between delivery and serving

When ordering for a family celebration in Lapu-Lapu, plan who can receive the cake and move it to a suitable place. Send the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or venue instructions. White Cakes confirms the delivery charge for the actual address and agrees a delivery window with the customer. Ask a family member to be available rather than expecting the box to wait unattended.

After handover, keep the cake away from direct sun and never leave it in a parked car. The bakery's ordering guidance says to refrigerate buttercream cakes if they will be held for several hours before serving. Tell the person in charge how the cake should be handled and where it will be stored while the family gathers.

If the cake will be served after travel or a long programme, tell White Cakes the planned serving time when you ask for availability. They can confirm the practical arrangements for the chosen design and date. Do not infer a safe wait time or temperature from general online advice; the cake, weather, and holding conditions matter.

For a family table, include the number of people who will eat cake and use the published size guide to discuss portions. Keep this city-specific handover detail on the Lapu-Lapu delivery page and the handling advice in the shared Cebu buttercream-care guide. A city-specific storage page would repeat the same safety guidance without adding a distinct local answer.
