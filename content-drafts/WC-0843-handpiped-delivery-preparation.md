## Prepare for delivery of a cake with piped decoration

Hand-piped details can be delicate, so coordinate the handoff and the place where the cake will wait. Choose a stable, level surface away from direct sunlight and heat. Do not leave the cake in a hot car or stack items on its box. If there will be a delay of several hours, follow White Cakes' refrigeration guidance for the specific cake.

Before ordering, tell the bakery when and where the cake is needed and who will receive it. Provide the complete Cebu address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Confirm the delivery window, route availability, and fee for that address. A driver should not have to guess where a building entrance or event room is.

Send a clear design brief with one reference image and identify which piped detail matters most. Confirm that the finish is feasible for the selected size and date. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed design and delivery plan.

Do not claim a piping style is fragile beyond what the bakery confirms. Keep validated handling advice in the shared delivery and cake-care guides.
