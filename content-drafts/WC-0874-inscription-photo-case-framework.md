## Show how a real cake inscription fits the custom design

A useful photo story should show the relationship between the message and the cake surface. Select an approved White Cakes photo that clearly shows the lettering, then explain one visible decision: why the line was kept short, where it was placed, or how the piped border leaves space around it. Do not invent a customer request or claim that the photo shows a particular event without confirmation.

The inscription needs to be checked against the cake size. A 4-inch Bento has limited space, while a larger custom cake gives a different layout. Share the exact wording early and ask whether it fits the selected design. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; lettering and other details must be planned together rather than copied from an unrelated reference.

This article can help customers send clearer briefs if it includes a real example and permission to use the image. Until then, use the existing custom-brief and cake-message guides. A generic photo caption does not justify a separate search page.
