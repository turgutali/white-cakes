## Get a chocolate-cake price confirmed for your exact order

Chocolate is one of the five flavours White Cakes currently lists for custom cakes. The flavour by itself does not set a separate price. Ask for the quote based on the format, size, and design you want; custom buttercream finishes can affect the final amount, and the current starting prices are maintained on the price guide.

In your enquiry, give the date, size request, flavour, inscription, guest count, and one reference photo if the outside is custom. If you expect a filling or topping beyond the listed flavour, ask whether it is available and whether it changes the quote. Do not assume a menu flavour automatically includes ganache, fruit, or another ingredient.

If delivery is needed, send the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile. Ask for the charge for that address and the delivery window. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery confirms availability before taking the 50% deposit.

Keep current amounts on one price page and flavour-specific information on the chocolate guide. A price page for every flavour would repeat the same size and design pricing and could become inaccurate when the menu changes.
