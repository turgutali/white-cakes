## Explain the piping reference and the deadline before confirming

Hand-piped details can be small but specific, so identify what the reference is meant to show: the border, lettering, floral pattern, or overall style. Sending several unrelated images without explaining your priority can make the brief unclear. Ask the bakery which elements it can adapt to the chosen cake size and date.

Confirm the inscription, spelling, flavour, portions, full price, delivery plan, and timeline in one written summary. Avoid waiting until the last moment to request a detailed design; made-to-order cakes require advance notice, and the requested slot must be confirmed. A 50% deposit is the current stated step for securing the agreed order.

Use these as practical additions to the general ordering and custom-brief guides. Do not describe a specific piping outcome unless a real approved cake shows it.
