## Plan a small celebration around a 4-inch Bento

The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous portions or three modest servings. It can work for a couple or a small family moment, but it is not a substitute for a full party cake. Decide how many people will actually take a slice and whether you expect generous portions before choosing this size.

If more guests will eat cake, ask White Cakes about a 6-inch option, currently estimated at 8–12 party portions, or an 8-inch option at 16–24. These are approximate ranges; how the cake is cut and what else is served matter. Confirm the size with the bakery rather than treating the estimates as guaranteed counts.

Send the event date, flavour, short inscription, and one design reference. Ask whether the design is feasible for a 4-inch surface and request the final quote. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm pickup or delivery details and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Keep Bento serving information on the current product and sizing pages so it is easy to find and stays consistent.
