## English

# Planning 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

A 4-inch Bento works best when the occasion and serving plan match its compact format. Estimate two generous or three modest servings after a meal; if more people need a slice, compare a larger size. Confirm the bakery’s current product, design options, and flavour.

Choose one short message and leave room for the decoration. Confirm quote and pickup or delivery arrangements.

## Tagalog

# Pagpaplano ng 4-inch na Bento cake sa Cebu

Pinakabagay ang 4-inch Bento kung tugma ang okasyon at bilang ng kakain sa compact na format. Tantiya itong dalawang masagana o tatlong maliit pagkatapos kumain; kung mas marami ang kakain, ikumpara ang mas malaking sukat. Kumpirmahin ang produkto, design, at flavor.
