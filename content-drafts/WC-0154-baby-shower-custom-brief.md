## Prepare a baby-shower cake brief the bakery can use

Start with the event date, number of people who will eat cake, preferred size, and flavour. Say whether the cake is the main dessert or part of a larger table. White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; ask the bakery to confirm which size fits your serving plan.

For the design, choose a palette and one focal detail. Attach one or two references and say what to adapt. If the parents have not chosen a theme, a simple colour direction may be enough. Confirm the exact inscription and whether it fits the chosen layout. A reference photo does not guarantee an exact copy.

Include delivery or pickup location, receiving contact, and venue instructions. Confirm date availability and the final quote. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design and terms are settled.

Use this as a baby-shower example in the central custom brief guide and link to the current serving and design pages.
