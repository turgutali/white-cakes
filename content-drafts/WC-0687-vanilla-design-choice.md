## Decide what should stand out on the cake’s surface

For a vanilla cake, choose a design based on the occasion and recipient rather than assuming the flavour determines the look. Pick a colour palette, short inscription, or decorative feature that matters most, then send one reference image that explains it. Ask the bakery which parts can be adapted to the chosen size.

Confirm the current vanilla option and any filling separately from the exterior design. Tell the bakery the date, expected servings, exact wording, and budget. If there are allergies or dietary restrictions, ask directly about ingredients and accommodation; do not rely on the flavour name.

Keep current menu details in the flavour guide and design advice in the existing design page. A standalone vanilla design article needs real photographs and a distinct visual lesson.
