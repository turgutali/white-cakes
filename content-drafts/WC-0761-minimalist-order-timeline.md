## Allow enough time for a minimalist custom cake

A simple-looking design still needs a confirmed production slot. Send the event date and the time you need the cake, then wait for White Cakes to confirm availability. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; contact the bakery earlier for a weekend, holiday, or a date that cannot move.

Describe the design in specific terms: the base colour, the finish you prefer, a short inscription, and any one focal detail. A minimalist look often depends on spacing and clean lettering, so do not add a long list of competing decorations after the design has been agreed. Attach one reference image and say which part should guide the cake.

Include the size, flavour, number of cake eaters, and delivery or pickup plan. For delivery, send the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any building instructions; confirm the fee and window. Ask for the final design and quote in writing. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep the lead-time instructions in the central ordering guide. Do not claim that minimalist cakes take less production time unless the bakery confirms it for the actual design.
