## Choose one family detail and confirm the design can fit the cake

Ask the family what the cake should celebrate and choose a single focal detail, such as a short greeting, favourite colour, or shared symbol. Send one reference and explain what the bakery should adapt. If several relatives suggest ideas, have one person combine them into a coherent brief.

Share the event date, guest estimate, size, flavour, exact wording, and full Mandaue address. Confirm whether delivery to that location is available and ask how the design fits the selected cake. Verify the current quote before ordering.

Add the general design advice to the central guide. A Mandaue-specific close-up story requires an actual cake, approved original photo, and verified local details.
