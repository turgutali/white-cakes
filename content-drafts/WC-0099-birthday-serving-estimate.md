## Count cake eaters and decide how large each slice should be

For a birthday, estimate who will actually be present when the cake is served. Consider whether the party includes a meal, other desserts, children, or guests who may leave early. Tell the bakery whether you prefer modest slices or larger party portions and ask it to confirm the best size.

The current guide lists approximate ranges of 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. A 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous or three modest servings. Cutting style affects the count, so confirm before ordering.

Keep these estimates in the maintained size guide and update them if the bakery changes its portion guidance.
