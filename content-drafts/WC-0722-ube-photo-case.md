## Ube cake case-study framework

**Use only a real White Cakes Ube cake with verified facts and permission.** Confirm the order record, flavour, design, and current product details with the bakery. Secure permission for original photos and any customer or venue information. Do not use a generic purple cake photo or attribute a competitor's work to White Cakes.

The case should explain a specific design decision readers can learn from: colour choices, placement of an inscription, or how a motif was adapted to the selected cake size. Use the actual brief and team confirmation. Do not invent ingredient information, flavour notes, customer reaction, or a celebration story.

Use original full-cake and detail photos with accurate captions. Confirm current size, serving estimate, and price before inclusion, then link to the central cake-design, flavour, and order guides. A standalone article needs a distinct query and useful evidence beyond the menu listing.

If no approved case and photos exist, keep this framework unpublished and improve the current product and design pages instead.
