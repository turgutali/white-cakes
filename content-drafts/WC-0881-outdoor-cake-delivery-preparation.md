## Plan outdoor cake delivery around shade and serving time

Choose a person to receive the cake and move it to a shaded, stable place as soon as it arrives. Give White Cakes the full venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or access instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee and make sure the receiver is available; a cake should not be left outside unattended.

Tell the bakery that the event is outdoors and share the expected serving time. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so ask what handling plan suits the specific cake. Arrange an indoor or refrigerated holding place if there will be a long delay and follow White Cakes' care instructions. Do not leave the cake in a hot car or exposed sun.

Include the date, size, flavour, design, inscription, and serving estimate with the order request. Confirm the delivery route and design feasibility. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep confirmed handoff guidance in the delivery guide. Do not promise outdoor heat tolerance or a fixed safe display time without bakery evidence.
