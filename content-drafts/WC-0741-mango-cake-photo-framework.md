## Build a mango-cake design story from an actual White Cakes photo

A useful photo breakdown should explain something a reader can see in the cake, not simply repeat that mango is a listed flavour. Before writing a standalone case, select an approved photo of an actual White Cakes mango-flavoured cake and confirm what is visible: the outside finish, colour, border, or message. Do not describe fruit pieces, filling, topping, or colour unless those details are confirmed for that cake.

The article should then answer one specific question: which visual detail came from the brief, what was adapted to fit the selected size, or how the message was kept readable. A short account of the decision helps customers send better references for their own order. The photo must show the described cake and have permission for publication.

White Cakes lists mango among its custom cake flavours. The flavour itself does not specify a filling or exterior design. The order guide should remain the source for lead time, payment, and delivery details; this case study should focus on the visual evidence from the image.

Until an approved, mango-specific cake photo is available, keep this as a framework and place the flavour clarification on the existing mango cake guide. Do not publish a photo-led story based on a generic cake image.
