## English

# Common ordering mistakes to avoid with mango cake in Cebu

Do not assume a mango cake shown online is currently offered, or that its name guarantees fresh mango, a particular filling, or an ingredient profile. Confirm the actual product and date availability. Avoid basing servings on a styled image or sending references that ask for incompatible finishes.

Keep one approved brief with size, exact message, design, address, price, delivery window, and payment terms. Ask the bakery for product-specific storage instructions and confirm any dietary details directly. Read the [Cebu order guide](/journal/how-to-order.html) before treating the booking as final.
