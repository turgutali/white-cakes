## Keep a christening cake cool until the family meal

Plan where the cake will wait after delivery. Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm kitchen equipment. If the cake arrives several hours before lunch or the reception, follow White Cakes' care guidance and arrange refrigerator space if the bakery recommends it.

Choose a receiver who can move the cake promptly to a stable place. If the venue is a church hall or function room, confirm that a cool storage area is available and tell the contact when the cake will be served. Do not assume a public hall will hold the cake safely without an arrangement.

Ask the bakery for product-specific care instructions, especially if the design has buttercream details. Avoid generic time limits for leaving it out; room temperature and decoration affect the situation. Confirm delivery window, address, and fee along with the order details. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; its 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified storage advice in the central heat guide, not repeated by event type.
