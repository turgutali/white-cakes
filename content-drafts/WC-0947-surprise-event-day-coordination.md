## Choose one coordinator who can manage the handoff discreetly

For a surprise celebration, designate one organizer to confirm the order and receive the cake. Give the bakery the full address, event timing, a discreet contact number, and any instructions for calling on arrival. Tell the intended recipient’s household or venue contact only what is needed to prevent an accidental reveal.

Confirm the cake’s inscription, size, flavour, design, total cost, and delivery or pickup arrangement in writing. The coordinator should be reachable during the handoff and have a plan for keeping the cake secure until the reveal. Ask the bakery for specific care instructions and avoid leaving the cake in direct heat or sun.
