## Handle an ube-flavoured cake using the same buttercream care basics

Ube is one of the flavours White Cakes lists for its custom cakes. The flavour choice does not change the basic handling instructions for a buttercream cake: keep it out of direct sun and hot cars. If it will be held for several hours before serving, the bakery's ordering guidance says to refrigerate it.

Plan who will receive the cake and where it can be kept until dessert. If it is delivered to a home or venue, share the receiver's name and mobile number with the order and choose someone who can move the box promptly. Do not leave it at a gate, in a parked vehicle, or by a sunny window while guests are gathering.

When placing the order, tell White Cakes the serving time and ask if the schedule presents any concern for the selected cake. Do not infer an exact safe holding time or temperature from general online advice. The cake's surroundings and wait time matter, and the bakery can confirm handling instructions for the order.

Keep storage advice in the shared Cebu buttercream-care guide and flavour details in the ube page. A separate storage post for every flavour would repeat the same handling guidance without adding a different answer.
