## Make the child’s favourite idea clear without overcrowding the cake

Ask the child or family to choose one main theme, character-inspired colour palette, or activity they enjoy. When using a licensed character reference, confirm what the bakery can create and avoid assuming an exact copy is available. A clear focal point and short name or age usually make the design easier for guests to recognize.

Share the child’s name spelling, age, preferred colours, cake size, flavour, and date in the brief. If there are food allergies, ask the bakery directly about ingredients and handling before placing the order. Plan a receiving adult and follow the baker’s storage advice, especially for a warm party venue.
