## Estimate portions for Red Velvet cake

Begin with the number of guests who will actually eat cake and whether there will be other desserts. A meeting, family meal, and birthday party can serve different portion sizes even with the same guest count. Tell White Cakes whether you want a small party slice or a full dessert portion.

Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm the best size for your plan. Red Velvet is on the current flavour menu, but confirm its availability for the requested date and design.

For a small group, the 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. If a guest has an allergy or dietary restriction, ask White Cakes directly about ingredients and preparation rather than inferring from the flavour name.

Keep serving figures in the single central size guide and link to the current flavour listing.
