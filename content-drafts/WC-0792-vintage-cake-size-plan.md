## Plan vintage piping around the cake size and message

Vintage-style borders and rosettes take up space on the cake surface. Before selecting the design, count the people who will eat cake and use White Cakes' size guide to choose a suitable format. Then decide which piped detail should be the focus and how much room is needed for the inscription. A 4-inch Bento has less writing space than a larger custom cake.

Send one reference photo and identify the border, colour, or piping detail you want interpreted. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, and the final design needs to fit the chosen size and available production time. The reference guides the finish but does not guarantee an exact copy. Ask whether a long message should be shortened.

Give the date, flavour, guest count, and pickup or delivery preference in the same enquiry. The bakery requests at least three days' notice; detailed piping or a holiday date is a reason to contact them earlier. Ask for the current quote, since the design can affect the price.

Keep portion facts on the size page and style examples on the vintage cake guide. Add a photo case only when an approved image shows a real White Cakes vintage finish.
