## Confirm a buttercream cake order before sending the deposit

Ask for a written confirmation of the exact cake, not only a payment amount. It should identify the size, flavour, agreed buttercream design, wording, price, event date, and pickup or delivery plan. If you sent reference images, confirm which elements the bakery has agreed to make and which details may be adapted.

Check the schedule next. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm the requested date and time are available, then verify the full destination address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, delivery window, and address-based fee if delivery is planned. Buttercream can soften in heat, so ask about care and make sure someone can receive the cake.

The current White Cakes process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow the payment instructions provided with your quote and keep the receipt alongside the order confirmation. If anything is unclear, settle it with the bakery before paying rather than relying on an old social post.

This checklist belongs in the main order guide. Recheck the deposit amount and process whenever the bakery changes its terms.
