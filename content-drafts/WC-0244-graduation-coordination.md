## Coordinate a graduation cake around the venue schedule

Graduation events can have a fixed program and a busy receiving area. Choose the person who will accept the cake and share their mobile number with the bakery. Confirm the full venue address, landmark, entrance or loading instructions, and a delivery window that leaves time to place the cake before serving. Do not assume the venue can hold a cake safely if no recipient is available.

When requesting the order, state the graduation date, time needed, estimated cake eaters, flavour, design, and inscription. If you are using school colours, name them clearly and send one reference rather than relying on a school logo image alone. Ask White Cakes to confirm which design details are feasible and the price before paying.

Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice. Delivery availability and fees depend on the address, so confirm both before the order is finalized. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order. At the event, keep the boxed cake shaded and away from a hot vehicle; follow the bakery's storage instructions if serving is delayed.

This is a general coordination checklist. Publish a graduation-specific delivery story only when White Cakes can verify an actual route or handoff and has permission for any customer or venue images. Otherwise, place the advice in the central Cebu delivery guide.
