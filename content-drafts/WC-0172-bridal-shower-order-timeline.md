## Set the bridal-shower cake timeline before choosing every detail

Start by checking the event date with White Cakes. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, but a bridal shower involves invitations and venue planning, so enquire earlier if the date is fixed or near a holiday. Wait for the bakery to confirm the production slot before treating the order as booked.

Once the date is available, send the serving estimate, size, flavour, design reference, and exact inscription. If the cake is for a dessert table, estimate how many people will actually have a slice. Current guides suggest 8–12 portions from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches, with actual yield depending on serving style.

Confirm whether delivery or pickup is planned. For delivery, share the full venue address, landmark, receiving contact, and preferred window; ask about the fee and building access. Confirm the design, price, and all details in writing, then pay the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.
