## How to plan the order timeline for bridal shower cake in Cebu

Start with the confirmed event date and the time the cake must be ready. White Cakes requests at least three days’ notice, with earlier contact for detailed piping, multi-tier work and busy dates. Availability is confirmed by the bakery, so an inquiry alone does not hold a slot.

Send the date, venue area, guest estimate, size, flavour, one design reference and delivery or pickup preference together. Ask for the production slot and final quote in writing; pay the stated 50% deposit only after those details are agreed. For a bridal shower, treat the couple’s or host’s palette as a guide and avoid reproducing private or copyrighted details without permission. Confirm who approves the inscription and final design so the surprise stays coordinated.
