## Compare Mango cake options by size and confirmed design

First compare sizes based on the people who will eat cake. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generously or three modestly. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm a size for your serving plan.

Next, check whether Mango is available for the selected size and date. It is on the current flavour list, but availability for a specific configuration should be confirmed. Do not assume a particular fruit filling or ingredient from the flavour name. Ask the bakery if that detail matters to you.

Compare the design scope too. Send one reference image and say what should be adapted. A custom finish may affect the price, so request a quote for the exact size, flavour, design, and inscription. For delivery, confirm the fee and handoff plan.

Keep this option comparison in the central cake-choice guide, with current flavours and prices maintained on the menu page.
