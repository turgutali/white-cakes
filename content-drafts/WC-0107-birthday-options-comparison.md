## Compare birthday-cake options using the guest plan

Start by deciding who will actually eat cake. A private birthday for two may suit the 4-inch Bento, which serves about two generous or three modest portions. A family meal may need a 6-inch cake, estimated at 8–12 party slices, while an 8-inch cake is estimated at 16–24. These are ranges and depend on how the cake is cut.

Compare flavour, design scope, and price for the same size. White Cakes currently lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm availability for the date. A custom reference may add work beyond a listed design, so ask what is included in the quote and what can be adapted.

If the order will be delivered, include the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and preferred window. Confirm the delivery fee and who will accept the cake. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once date, design, flavour, and terms are settled.

Use this comparison in the existing cake-choice guide rather than creating a separate page for each birthday size.
