## Housewarming cake case-study framework

**This article needs a real project before publication.** Choose an actual White Cakes housewarming cake with an order record and original photos. Confirm written permission to use the images and any identifiable home, family member, or address. Do not invent a new-home story or imply that a generic cake was ordered for a real customer.

Document the verified design choices: the palette, any home-related motif, inscription, flavour, and size. Note what the customer requested and what the bakery adapted only when the order notes or team confirm those details. Explain one useful design decision readers can apply, such as keeping a small inscription visible beside decoration.

Show original photographs with accurate alt text and captions that describe visible features. Include only current serving and ordering information, then link to the custom-cake brief and size guides. The case must answer a specific question that the existing design page does not already cover.

If there is no suitable approved cake or the design facts cannot be verified, leave the framework unpublished and merge general ideas into the existing design guide.
