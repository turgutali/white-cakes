## Choose a reference that shows one child-friendly idea clearly

Use one photo for the overall cake style and another only if it explains a distinct detail, such as colours or lettering. Tell the bakery what the child likes and which part of the image should be adapted. If a character is involved, ask what the bakery can create rather than assuming it will copy protected artwork exactly.

Include the child’s name spelling, age, date, serving estimate, size, flavour, and handoff plan. Mention allergies or dietary needs directly and ask whether they can be accommodated. Confirm the final design and current quote in writing.

Add the checklist to the custom brief guide. A separate case requires a real reference-to-finished cake example and family permission.
