## Choose the date and message before asking the bakery to confirm the order

Decide when the cake will be served, how many people will share it, and whether pickup or delivery is needed. Send the requested size, flavour, design reference, and exact inscription together. For a surprise, name one organizer who can approve the order and receive the cake.

White Cakes’ current guidance calls for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Contact earlier for a custom design or busy date and wait for written availability confirmation. Ask for the complete quote and follow the current deposit process to secure the agreed details.

Keep timeline and booking instructions in one current guide. Recheck the bakery’s lead time before publishing an evergreen promise.
