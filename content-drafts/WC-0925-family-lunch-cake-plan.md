## Plan the cake around lunch and the people staying afterward

For a family lunch after church, estimate how many relatives will remain to eat dessert. The number who attend the service may differ from the number at the meal, and lunch may include other sweets. Tell White Cakes the likely cake-eater count, whether you expect small slices, and whether the cake is the main dessert.

Current serving estimates are about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These ranges are approximate and should be confirmed against your serving plan. If the event is small, the 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly; it may not suit a larger family table.

Share the event date, flavour, design, inscription, and delivery or pickup location. If the meal is at a home or function room, include the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep this context in the shared size guide rather than publishing another occasion-specific portion chart.

Ang bilang na dumalo sa serbisyo ay maaaring iba sa bilang sa pagkain, at ang tanghalian ay maaaring may kasamang iba pang matatamis. Sabihin sa White Cakes ang malamang na bilang ng kumakain ng cake, kung inaasahan mo ang maliliit na hiwa, at kung ang cake ang pangunahing dessert.

Ang kasalukuyang pagtatantya ng paghahatid ay humigit-kumulang 8–12 party slice mula sa isang 6-inch na cake, 16–24 mula sa isang 8-inch na cake, at 30–36 mula sa isang 6+8-inch na two-tier na cake. Ang mga saklaw na ito ay tinatayang at dapat kumpirmahin laban sa iyong plano sa paghahatid. Kung ang kaganapan ay maliit, ang 4-pulgada na Bento ay nagsisilbi sa dalawa nang mapagbigay o tatlong katamtaman; maaaring hindi ito angkop sa mas malaking mesa ng pamilya.
