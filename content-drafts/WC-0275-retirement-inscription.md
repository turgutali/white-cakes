## Keep the retirement message warm, specific, and short

Choose whether the message should thank the retiree, mark the years of service, or celebrate the next chapter. A short line such as “Enjoy your next adventure” or “Salamat sa imong serbisyo” can be easier to read than a full speech. Confirm the recipient’s name and title if they will appear.

Send exact wording and spelling to the bakery, and ask whether it fits the requested design and cake size. A company logo or department name should be approved by the organization before it is used. If the group has a longer dedication, place it on a card rather than crowding the cake.

Include retirement examples in the existing message guide. Use real photos only when approved, and avoid presenting sample lines as actual past customer orders.
