## Keep a Bouquet Cake inscription short

A Bouquet Cake is decorated with hand-piped buttercream flowers, so the writing has to share a small, detailed surface with the arrangement. A short name, date, or two- to four-word greeting is usually easier to read than a full paragraph. Treat that as a design suggestion, not a guaranteed character limit: available space depends on the requested layout and the bakery must confirm the final wording.

Choose the message before the design is finalized. Send the exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation in one line; names and dates are easy to mistype when they arrive in separate messages. If you want a particular greeting, explain whether the recipient's name or the occasion should be the main focus. The decorator can advise whether the text will fit without covering the flowers.

For example, a brief “Happy Birthday, Ana” or “Thank You” leaves more room for the floral finish than a long personal note. These are sample phrases, not customer commissions. White Cakes can confirm which wording and design are feasible for the requested cake and quote the current price after reviewing the details.
