## Plan a small-event cake order without leaving details to the last day

Start by checking the date with White Cakes. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, even for a small gathering. Ask early if the date is a weekend or close to a holiday, and wait for the bakery to confirm a production slot.

Send the number of cake eaters, size, flavour, design reference, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery plan in one message. For a small event, the 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. If the group is larger, ask the bakery to confirm a more suitable size.

For delivery, give the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any building instructions. Confirm the window and fee. Ask for the final design and price in writing. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all terms are settled.

Keep one authoritative lead-time guide and add the small-event context as a short example rather than creating another nearly identical schedule page.
