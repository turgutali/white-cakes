## English

# How to match the design of wedding cake in Cebu

Match the wedding cake design to the venue, guest count, cutting time, and finish. White Cakes makes hand-piped buttercream cakes for intimate weddings; it does not build a ballroom display from a catalogue. Share the actual reception setup and ask what the bench can make for the date.

Confirm whether the venue allows outside cake and who will handle cutting; White Cakes does not staff a cutting table.
