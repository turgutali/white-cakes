## Choose a Bouquet Cake design that gives the flowers room

Bouquet Cake is defined by hand-piped buttercream flowers, so begin with the floral palette and arrangement. Decide whether you prefer a soft mix of colours or one main shade, then ask White Cakes what flower shapes and colours it can offer. Do not assume an image of a different bouquet cake can be reproduced exactly.

If you want a message, keep it short and confirm that it fits without covering the flowers. Share one reference image and explain whether you care most about the colours, arrangement, or overall mood. Ask the bakery to confirm the final design and current quote for the selected size and flavour.

Consider where the cake will be displayed and how it will be cared for. Keep it away from direct sunlight and heat, and follow White Cakes' storage advice if it will wait several hours. Include the date and delivery details; made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
