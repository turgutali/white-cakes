## Plan the cake's place in a warm outdoor event

Buttercream is sensitive to heat, direct sun, and time out of refrigeration. If your celebration is outdoors, plan a shaded, cool handover point and a place to keep the cake until it is ready to be served. White Cakes' ordering guidance says not to leave a buttercream cake in direct sun or a hot car; refrigerate it if it will be held for several hours. Do not assume that a display table outside is suitable for the whole event.

Tell the bakery that the celebration is outdoors and share the planned serving time. Ask whether the chosen design and timing are practical. The cake is made to order, and the bakery should confirm its handling advice for the requested date and format. If the weather or venue plan changes, let the person receiving the cake know where it can be moved.

When arranging delivery, provide the full venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and a clear entry instruction. Choose someone who can meet the agreed delivery window and carry the box to the planned cool space. If the venue has no suitable indoor or shaded area, ask the bakery about options before confirming the design.

This practical section belongs in the existing cake-care and delivery guides. Do not promise a specific safe outdoor holding time or temperature without bakery-confirmed instructions for that cake.
