## Avoid a vague reference and an incomplete handoff plan

Do not send multiple buttercream cake photos without explaining which finish or detail you want. Choose one overall direction and identify the essential feature, such as piping, lettering, or colour. Ask the bakery what can be adapted to the requested cake size and date.

Confirm the flavour, guest estimate, exact message, full price, and delivery or pickup arrangement in one written summary. Tell the bakery if the cake will be outdoors or kept in a warm venue and ask for product-specific care advice. Follow the current deposit process only after the details are agreed.

Keep these practical checks in the main ordering and custom-brief guides. Do not claim a buttercream cake is heatproof without confirmation.
