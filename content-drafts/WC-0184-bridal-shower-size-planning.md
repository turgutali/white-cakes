## Planning bridal shower cake in Cebu

Decide the cake’s job first: feed the gathering, mark a candle or photo moment, or do both. Note when it will be served and what other desserts are planned before choosing size and design.

Put one person in charge of the final brief and handoff. Confirm date, serving count, wording, quote, delivery recipient and care plan together so the event team works from one agreed version. For a bridal shower, treat the couple’s or host’s palette as a guide and avoid reproducing private or copyrighted details without permission. Confirm who approves the inscription and final design so the surprise stays coordinated.
