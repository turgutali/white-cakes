## Plan a bridal-shower cake for the guests who will share it

Estimate how many guests will eat cake and whether the shower will offer other desserts. A shower may have a dessert table, so the cake could be served in smaller portions than at a cake-only gathering. Tell White Cakes whether you prefer small tastes or full slices.

The current size guide estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions if the shower is a small get-together. These are approximate; ask the bakery to confirm the size for your event.

Include the event date, flavour, design, inscription, and venue in your enquiry. Check availability and the final quote. If delivery is planned, provide the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep portions in the central cake-size guide and link from bridal-shower content rather than adding another chart.
