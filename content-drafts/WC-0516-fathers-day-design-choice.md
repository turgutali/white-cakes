## Choose Father's Day cake details Dad will recognize

Ask the family what the recipient likes before choosing a generic holiday decoration. A favourite colour, a hobby-related motif, or a short family phrase can make the cake feel personal. Pick one focal idea and keep the inscription readable.

Send one reference photo and state what should be adapted. Ask White Cakes whether the requested design is feasible for the chosen size and what it will cost. If the cake uses a logo or branded image, confirm permission before including it. A reference does not guarantee an exact copy.

Include the date, flavour, serving estimate, and delivery plan. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions; larger gatherings should confirm a bigger size. Check availability early, allow at least three days' notice, and confirm the 50% deposit after the design and terms are agreed.

Use original, approved White Cakes photos to show an actual Father's Day design. Otherwise, merge these general choices into the existing design guide.
