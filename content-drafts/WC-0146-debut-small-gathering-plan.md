## Plan the debut cake for the guests who will eat it

For a smaller debut gathering, estimate the number of people who will actually take cake. The full invitation count may include guests who leave before dessert, while close family may want generous portions. Tell White Cakes the serving plan and whether there are other desserts.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; slice size and design can affect the result. Ask the bakery to confirm the best size for your event rather than stretching the chart.

Include the event date, flavour, size, design, and exact inscription. If delivery is planned, provide the venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and access instructions. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice. Confirm the final quote and handoff details; a 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep the serving ranges in the central size guide and link to the debut page. Verify them with the bakery before publishing.
