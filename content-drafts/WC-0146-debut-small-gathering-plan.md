## Planning debut cake in Cebu

For a small debut, choose the cake’s role before its decoration. If it is the centrepiece for candles and portraits, a smaller cake may be enough; if each guest expects a slice, estimate servings from the actual guest list and other desserts.

Keep the design focused on one personal detail—an initial, favourite colour or meaningful flower—so the cake feels made for the celebrant without competing with the venue décor. Confirm the size, inscription and handoff person together in the final order summary.
