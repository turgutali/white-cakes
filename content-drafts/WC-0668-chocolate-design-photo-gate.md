## Choose a chocolate-cake design that keeps the flavour in focus

Begin by deciding whether the cake should look simple and classic or carry a more elaborate custom finish. A restrained palette and one focal decoration can keep the cake's chocolate character central. If the recipient prefers a particular mood—formal, playful, or floral—describe that in plain language and let the bakery advise which details work on the chosen size.

Send a reference image with one sentence about what you like in it. A photo may show a technique or ingredient White Cakes does not offer, so do not treat it as an exact-copy request. Confirm the available design, flavour, size, and quote. The current menu lists Chocolate among its flavour choices; reconfirm availability when placing the order.

For portion planning, White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches. Actual portions depend on cutting style. Share the guest count, date, inscription, and delivery details. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Add original photographs only when they show real White Cakes chocolate cakes and are approved for use. Without a verified design example, this advice should extend the existing design guide rather than create a flavour-specific page.
