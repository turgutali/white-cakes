## Make the new home easy for the bakery to find and the cake easy to receive

Share the full address, subdivision or building name, gate instructions, requested handoff window, and receiver’s phone number. A new home may not appear clearly in map directions, so include a nearby landmark only if it is accurate. Ask the bakery to confirm route coverage, timing, and any delivery fee.

Have someone ready to receive the cake and follow storage instructions for that specific product. If the home is not yet occupied or access is limited, arrange a reliable alternate handoff before confirming the order.

Add general address and receiver advice to the Cebu delivery guide. Do not imply that every new home or Mandaue/Lapu-Lapu route is covered without checking.

Pag-andam sa usa ka tawo sa pagdawat sa cake ug pagsunod sa mga panudlo sa pagtipig alang sa piho nga produkto. Kung ang balay wala pa giokupahan o ang pag-access limitado, paghikay sa usa ka kasaligan nga alternatibo nga handoff sa dili pa kumpirmahon ang order.
