## Confirm the family date and Mandaue handoff before finalizing the design

Set the celebration date and choose whether the cake will be picked up or delivered to the Mandaue address. Give the bakery the full location, receiver’s name and phone number, and any gate instructions. Ask whether the route is available and confirm the handoff window and fee.

White Cakes’ current guidance asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Contact earlier for a custom design or a busy weekend, then wait for availability confirmation. Settle the size, flavour, message, total quote, and deposit process in writing.

Keep the general timeline in the Cebu order guide. Check both current lead times and delivery coverage before publishing location-specific details.
