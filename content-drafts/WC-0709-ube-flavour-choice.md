## Decide whether Ube suits the people sharing the cake

Ube is one of the flavours on White Cakes' current menu. If you are ordering for a group, consider whether the guests know and enjoy the flavour, and whether the cake will be the only dessert. When tastes are mixed, ask the bakery whether the design or configuration can accommodate another flavour; do not assume a split cake is available without confirmation.

The event size matters as much as flavour. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. These are ranges that vary with the way it is cut. Share how many people will actually eat cake and ask which size is practical.

For a custom Ube order, state the date, size, design, inscription, and delivery plan. Confirm that Ube is available for that date and size, then ask for the final price. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice; its 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the details are settled.

Keep verified flavour details in the central flavour guide and current menu. Do not invent tasting notes, ingredient sourcing, or flavour pairings that the bakery has not confirmed.
