## Get a quote for the vintage-inspired details you want

“Vintage cake” can refer to different piped borders, colours, lettering, and shapes. When asking for a price, send one reference and identify which details you want adapted. Include the size, flavour, event date, inscription, and serving plan so White Cakes can quote the actual request.

The current custom menu lists a 6-inch cake at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16. An 8-inch cake is listed at ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 afterward. These amounts are date-specific and should be checked against the live menu before use. Ask whether detailed piping changes the price.

Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size with the bakery and ask about delivery fee if needed. Allow at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep the rate and quote factors in the central pricing guide. Update temporary price dates when the menu changes.
