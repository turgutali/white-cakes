## Prepare a receiver and storage place for vanilla cake

Choose someone who will receive the cake during the agreed window. Send White Cakes the full address, landmark, local mobile number, and any gate or building instructions. Confirm delivery availability and the address-based fee. The receiver should know where the box will go after handoff.

Set aside a stable, level place away from direct sunlight and a hot vehicle. If the cake will wait for several hours, follow the bakery's storage guidance and ask whether refrigeration is recommended for the specific design. Do not infer care needs from the Vanilla flavour name.

Include the event date, size, flavour, inscription, and delivery plan in the order. Confirm the flavour and design are available for the requested date. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep general handoff instructions in the central delivery guide, with current flavour details linked from the menu page.
