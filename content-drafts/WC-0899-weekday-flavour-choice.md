## Choose for the recipient and the people who will share the cake

A weekday celebration may be a quick office break, a family meal, or an after-school event. Ask the recipient or organizer which flavours people enjoy, then confirm what is available for the requested date. White Cakes’ menu has included Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; check the live list.

Mention allergy or dietary questions directly and ask whether the bakery can accommodate them. A flavour name does not confirm ingredients or safety. Confirm the size, design, message, and office or home handoff separately.

Keep current flavour options in one guide and link to weekday size and delivery advice. Avoid publishing near-identical posts for each workday occasion.
