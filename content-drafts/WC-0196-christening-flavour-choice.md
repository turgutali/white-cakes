## How to choose a flavour for christening cake in Cebu

White Cakes lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango and red velvet. Ask what the selected flavour means in the current menu: the name alone does not promise a separate filling, fruit layer or topping. Confirm the cake construction and raise any ingredient or allergy question directly.

For a mixed-age gathering, choose from the bakery’s confirmed options and tell the host what flavour is being served. Do not assume an unlisted combination or special dietary version is available without written confirmation. For a christening, confirm the child’s name and event date from the family’s final invitation, and ask the venue about the cake handoff point and service time. Keep any religious motif within the family’s requested direction.

## Tagalog translation (editorial review pending)
