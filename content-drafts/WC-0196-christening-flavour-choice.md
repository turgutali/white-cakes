## Choose a christening-cake flavour for the family meal

Ask the family which flavours the child and guests enjoy, then check White Cakes' current menu. Its listed options include Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm that the preferred flavour is available for the selected size and date; do not assume every option fits every custom design.

Consider the rest of the meal. If guests will have other desserts, the cake may be served in smaller portions. Tell the bakery how many people will actually eat cake and ask about the right size. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches.

If anyone has an allergy or dietary restriction, ask White Cakes directly about ingredients and preparation. Do not infer safety from a flavour name. Include the event date, design, inscription, and delivery plan. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified flavour options in one menu guide and link to it from the christening page rather than duplicating the list.
