## Match a Christmas cake design to the celebration

Choose one seasonal idea and one main palette before collecting references. A few coordinated details can make the cake feel festive while keeping the design readable. If the cake will sit beside a decorated table, share the table colours or describe the display so White Cakes can advise on contrast and scale.

Send a reference photo and state which parts you want adapted. Do not assume a character, intricate motif, metallic finish, or seasonal decoration is available or can be copied exactly. Ask the bakery to confirm the feasible design and the final price. Include the flavour and size; both need to be confirmed for the selected date.

Christmas is a busy period, so enquire early. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, but the date may fill earlier. Confirm delivery or pickup details, including the full address, receiving contact, and fee. The 50% deposit secures the agreed order after the design and terms are settled.

Use real, approved White Cakes photos for a seasonal design case. Otherwise, merge the advice into the existing design guide and avoid implying a seasonal product is offered without confirmation.
