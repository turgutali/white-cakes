## Estimate portions for a congratulations cake

The number of people invited may differ from the number who will eat cake. Ask the event host whether cake is the only dessert and whether portions should be full slices or small tastes. For a workplace milestone, the team may share the cake during a brief program rather than serving everyone a full dessert portion.

White Cakes' current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges. Share the real serving plan and ask the bakery to confirm a suitable size. For only two or three people, a 4-inch Bento may be enough.

Include the flavour, date, design, inscription, and delivery or pickup plan in the order request. Confirm size and availability before paying. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit secures the agreed order once the terms are confirmed.
