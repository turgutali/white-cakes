## What to include in a custom brief for First Communion cake in Cebu

A useful brief names the occasion, date, serving time, venue, approximate guest count, budget range and the cake’s role. Add the preferred size, flavour, exact message and one clear design reference; mark which details are essential and which can change.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, so describe the mood and a few specific details instead of asking for an exact copy of a screenshot. Ask the bakery to confirm an achievable interpretation before treating the design as final. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
