## Prepare a First Communion cake brief before asking for a quote

Send the event date, venue or delivery address, number of cake eaters, preferred size, and flavour. If the cake will be served after a meal, tell White Cakes whether there are other desserts and whether you expect small portions. Ask the bakery to confirm a size rather than relying only on the number invited.

For the design, choose a colour palette and one central detail. If the family wants a religious symbol or wording, provide the exact reference and confirm it is appropriate for the design. Include the inscription with spelling checked. A reference photo is guidance, not a guarantee of exact reproduction; ask what can be adapted and what the price includes.

White Cakes estimates 8–12 party portions from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake. These are approximate ranges. Confirm date availability, at least three days' notice, and the delivery plan. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design, price, and terms are settled.

Keep this occasion-specific checklist in the custom-brief guide and link to the current menu and size pages.
