## Compare Father's Day cake formats by the way it will be shared

For a private gift, White Cakes' 4-inch Bento may suit a small celebration and has space for a short message. It serves two generously or three modestly. For a shared family table, compare the 6-inch Signature Marble, 8-inch custom options, or another design using the current serving guide. Ask the bakery to confirm the portion plan for the actual cake rather than relying on a photo.

The finish can also guide the choice. Signature Marble has dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose; Bouquet Cake is decorated with hand-piped flowers; custom buttercream can follow a brief and reference photo. Choose the style based on the recipient and the number of guests, then ask for the current price and whether any requested colour or inscription can be accommodated.

Send the date, flavour, size, guest count, and exact message in one enquiry. Father's Day is a popular holiday, so ask early about availability. If delivery is needed, include the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. The 50% deposit confirms the order after the design, quote, and delivery window are agreed.

Keep Father's Day options on the existing seasonal guide and link to one maintained product comparison. Do not create a new page for each family occasion if the same cake choices and serving facts answer the question.
