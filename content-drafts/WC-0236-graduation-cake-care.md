## Store a graduation cake safely before the program

Choose where the cake will wait before the graduation program or family meal. Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, and warm equipment. If several hours will pass before serving, follow White Cakes' storage instructions and arrange refrigerator space if recommended.

A school, office, or event venue may have limited access to a refrigerator. Confirm the holding plan with the host before delivery and choose someone who can receive the cake. Do not leave it in an exposed lobby or ask a driver to leave it unattended.

Ask the bakery for handling advice based on the specific finish, especially if buttercream details are involved. Include the delivery time and expected serving time in your order. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
