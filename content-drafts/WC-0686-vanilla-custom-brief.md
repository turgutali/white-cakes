## Tell the bakery whether vanilla refers to the cake, filling, or both

Start by confirming the exact vanilla option currently available and what it includes. Then share the event date, cake size, serving estimate, decoration reference, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery plan. If you want a particular combination, ask whether it can be made for the requested date.

Separate required details from inspiration so the bakery knows what can be adapted. Mention allergies or dietary needs and ask directly whether they can be accommodated; a flavour label does not establish ingredients or safety. Confirm the quote and final order summary in writing.

Keep current flavour information in the live flavour guide and the reusable checklist here. Recheck the menu before publication.
