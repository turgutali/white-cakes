## Avoid common mix-ups with a marble cake order

Do not assume “marble” specifies the exact colour or pattern. Describe the base shade, accent colour, and whether you prefer a subtle or more visible effect. Send one reference and ask White Cakes what finish it can make. If you mean Signature Marble, check the current product listing and distinguish it from a custom marble design.

Do not assume a reference photo guarantees an exact copy or fixed price. Ask for the feasible design and confirmed quote for the selected size. If you want to change the listed product's colour, border, or flower, say so before confirming.

Check serving size and date availability. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; ask the bakery to confirm which fits your group. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice. For delivery, verify address, receiver, window, and fee.

The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after terms are settled. Add this practical checklist to the central ordering guide, not to a fabricated customer story.
