## Coordinate the handoff around the event venue and traffic plan

Sinulog celebrations can involve crowded roads, changing access points, and a venue that is difficult to reach. When asking about delivery, provide the complete address, event start time, receiver’s name and phone number, and any gate or building instructions. Ask the bakery what delivery window it can actually support; do not assume a specific arrival time until it is confirmed.

Choose a recipient who can answer the phone and take the cake directly. If the venue has loading or security rules, share them in advance. Keep the cake on a stable, level surface after handoff, and follow the bakery’s storage guidance for the particular design and weather conditions.

Place general logistics in the main Cebu delivery guide. Add a Sinulog-specific note only when current road or venue information has been verified for the relevant year.
