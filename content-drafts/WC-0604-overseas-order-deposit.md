## Confirm an overseas-paid cake order before sending the deposit

Before paying from abroad, make sure White Cakes has confirmed the date, cake design, size, flavour, price, and local delivery plan in writing. Ask which payment method and currency it accepts for this order, whether any transfer fee applies, and how the bakery will confirm receipt. Do not assume an international card, remittance service, or exchange rate is supported.

Check the recipient details: full name, local mobile number, complete Cebu address, landmark, and a trusted person who can receive the cake. If it is a surprise, explain whether the bakery should contact the recipient or only the local contact. Confirm the delivery window and fee for the address.

White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Its current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order, but the exact payment instructions should come from the current quote. Keep the written confirmation and proof of payment, and share the delivery details with the person receiving the cake.
