## Keep the inscription clear on a vintage-inspired cake

Vintage-inspired piping can create a detailed surface, so keep the message brief. A name or short greeting leaves space for borders and other decoration. Decide whether the words or the piping should be the main focus and ask White Cakes how the two can fit together on the selected size.

Send the final wording with spelling, capitalization, and punctuation checked. If you want a particular lettering style, provide one reference and ask whether it can be adapted. A reference is direction, not a guarantee of exact lettering or layout. Confirm the approved wording and design in writing before the cake is made.

Include the event date, flavour, size, and delivery or pickup plan. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once the bakery confirms availability and price.

Add sample phrasing to the existing message guide. A vintage-specific gallery needs original, approved cake photos and examples that add distinct value.
