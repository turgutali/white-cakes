## Use the reception headcount to plan debut cake portions

Estimate how many guests will stay for dessert and whether the cake is served after a full meal. Tell the bakery whether you expect modest slices or party portions. If the reception includes a dessert table, note what else will be available so the size recommendation matches the actual plan.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; confirm the expected yield with the bakery. A large debut reception may need a different arrangement than the standard sizes.
