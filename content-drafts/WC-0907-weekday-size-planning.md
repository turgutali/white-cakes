## Plan weekday cake portions around the people attending

Estimate how many people will actually eat cake at the event. A weekday office celebration may serve small slices during a meeting, while a family dinner may offer fuller portions. Tell White Cakes whether there are other desserts and how you expect the cake to be cut.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from 8 inches, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges. For two or three people, the 4-inch Bento serves about two generously or three modestly. Ask the bakery to confirm the most suitable size.

Include the date, flavour, design, and delivery or pickup plan. If delivery is to an office, confirm who will receive the cake and the building's access instructions. A weekday does not guarantee availability; made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
