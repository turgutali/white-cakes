## Choose the flavour, then confirm the outdoor care plan

Choose a flavour based on the people who will share the cake. White Cakes' current menu lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; ask which are available for the selected size and date. A flavour name alone does not tell you the full ingredients or whether a cake meets a dietary restriction, so ask the bakery directly if that matters.

Because the celebration is outdoors, discuss the location and serving time with White Cakes. Buttercream may soften in warm conditions, and the cake should be kept shaded and away from a hot vehicle. Ask for storage instructions for the specific flavour and design rather than assuming flavour determines safe outdoor time.

Share the guest count, date, design, inscription, and delivery address. Confirm that someone can receive the cake and move it to a cool place until serving. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the final quote; the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep flavour details in the central menu and outdoor handling advice in the heat guide. Link between the two instead of duplicating them.
