## Choose a family message everyone can read

For a Mandaue family celebration, decide whether the cake should name the celebrant, mark the occasion, or carry a short family greeting. One clear line usually works better than fitting every relative's message onto the cake. These are general suggestions, not phrases from actual White Cakes orders.

Send the final words with spelling and punctuation checked. If you want Cebuano or Tagalog wording, ask a fluent family member to confirm the phrase and tone before sending it. Ask White Cakes whether the message fits the cake size and decoration; the available space depends on the layout.

Include the event date, flavour, size, and serving estimate. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. For Mandaue delivery, add the complete address, landmark, receiving mobile number, and access instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
