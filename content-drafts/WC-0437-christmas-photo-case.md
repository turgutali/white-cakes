## Editorial status

Do not invent a Christmas cake order, seasonal menu item, decoration, or customer reaction. A case study needs a real completed order from the relevant year, an original photo approved for publication, verified order details, and customer permission. A previous year’s cake cannot be presented as a current-season order.

## Evidence needed

- Original photo of the actual cake and documented publication permission.
- Order record confirming the year, event, product, and design details.
- Customer approval for any identifying details or quotation.
- Captions and alt text based only on visible, verified facts.
- Cebuano and Tagalog versions reviewed by native editors.

If evidence is unavailable, omit the case and use general seasonal design advice.
