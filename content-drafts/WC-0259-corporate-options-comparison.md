## Compare options by attendees, logo requirements, and serving setup

Estimate the colleagues who will attend at cake time and whether dessert is the main snack. Ask the bakery to match current sizes to that number. Then compare available flavours and designs, including whether the company needs an approved logo or a short milestone message.

Consider office access, display space, budget, and the time the cake will be served. Request the complete price and confirm whether delivery is available to the building. A company’s full headcount may not reflect the portion count for one team event.
