## Keep an outdoor cake shaded until it is served

Do not leave a cake on an exposed table in direct sunlight. Choose a shaded, stable location and keep it in its box until the display is ready. A hot vehicle is not a safe holding place. Ask White Cakes how to store the specific design if the cake will wait for several hours, and use refrigeration if its guidance calls for it.

Plan the serving time with the event host. If the cake arrives early, identify a cooler indoor space or refrigerator and assign someone to move it. Ask the bakery whether it should sit briefly before cutting; do not rely on a universal timing rule because room conditions vary.

When ordering, share the venue, date, expected serving time, flavour, size, design, and delivery contact. Confirm that the route and handoff window are available. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and confirms an agreed order with a 50% deposit.

Use actual, bakery-approved handling instructions in the central heat guide. Avoid claims that any cake can remain outdoors for a set duration without a verified basis.
