## Confirm the date early and discuss the outdoor setup with the baker

Choose the event date and serving time, then tell the bakery the cake will be at a warm outdoor celebration. Share the venue, expected handoff window, requested size, flavour, design, and inscription. Ask which finish and delivery plan are suitable for the actual conditions; the answer may depend on the cake and the time it will wait before serving.

White Cakes’ current guidance asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Inquire earlier for detailed decoration or a busy date, and wait for confirmation of availability. Ask for a complete quote and the product-specific storage advice before sending the stated 50% deposit.
