## Use a Sinulog reference photo to clarify the cake design

Choose one image that communicates the palette or motif you want. Tell White Cakes which element to adapt and which parts can change. If the family sends several festival images, agree on a single direction so the brief does not combine unrelated colours or symbols.

Ask the bakery whether the requested motif is feasible for the cake size and whether it affects the quote. Do not assume a reference guarantees an exact reproduction or that an online image can be used publicly. If you want a logo or branded mark, confirm permission and provide an approved file.

Include the date, size, flavour, inscription, serving plan, and delivery address. Sinulog may affect access and travel time, so confirm the route, receiver, delivery window, and fee early. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once design and logistics are settled.

Keep general reference guidance in the custom brief guide. Add festival-specific access rules only when the bakery verifies them.
