## Choose a cake flavour the Mandaue family will enjoy

Ask the host or celebrant what flavours the family prefers. White Cakes' current menu lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm which flavour is available for the requested size, date, and design; not every option may suit every custom configuration.

If the family has mixed preferences, consider how the cake will be served and whether there are other desserts. Ask White Cakes directly about ingredient questions or dietary restrictions. Do not claim a flavour is allergen-safe or suitable for a diet based only on its name.

For a Mandaue event, include the number of cake eaters, event date, design, and delivery location. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Confirm the size and delivery fee, provide a landmark and receiver's mobile number, and allow at least three days' notice. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour details in the central guide and link to current local delivery information.
