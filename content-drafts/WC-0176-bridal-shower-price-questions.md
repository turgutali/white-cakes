## Questions to ask before budgeting for a bridal-shower cake

Ask for a quote using the event details, not only a photo. Share the date, approximate number of cake eaters, preferred size, flavour, design references, inscription, and delivery or pickup location. A quote can change with size and decoration, so a reference image alone may not capture the full request.

White Cakes' current custom-cake menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 (regular price ₱1,799 from January 16) and 8-inch cakes at ₱1,999 through January 15, 2027 (regular price ₱2,599 from January 16). Treat these date-specific prices as temporary and verify the live menu before publishing or ordering. A complex design, special decoration, or other configuration may require a separate confirmed quote.

Ask whether the chosen size suits the number of guests. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches, with actual portions depending on serving style. Also confirm the minimum three-day notice, date availability, delivery fee and window for the address, and what the 50% deposit covers. The deposit confirms the agreed order after the details are settled.

Keep the current amounts in the central price guide and date them clearly. This bridal-shower section can help readers ask for a comparable quote without creating a price page that goes stale or repeats the same menu table.
