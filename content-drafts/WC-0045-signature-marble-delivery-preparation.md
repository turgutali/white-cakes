# How to prepare for delivery of Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Signature Marble delivery needs a receiver who can accept the cake at the confirmed address. Send the full Cebu destination, building entry, phone number, and delivery window. Keep the 6-inch cake level and follow the bakery’s transport instructions; do not assume a map pin covers gate access.

Confirm route coverage and the current delivery quote before booking.
