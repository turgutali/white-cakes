## Confirm the live product details and receiving point

Check the current Signature Marble product listing before ordering, since its design or size may change. Give the bakery the full address, handoff window, receiver’s name and phone number, and any access instructions. Ask whether delivery to the location is available and confirm the fee and timing.

Have someone ready to receive the cake directly. Ask for handling advice for the actual product and keep its box level while waiting. If the cake will be served later or the venue is warm, mention the schedule so the bakery can give relevant instructions.

Keep delivery logistics in the central guide and current design details on the live product page. Do not publish a route or care promise without confirmation.
