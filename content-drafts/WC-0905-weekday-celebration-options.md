## Compare cake options against the weekday event’s timing and guest count

A weekday cake may be served at an office meeting, after school, or during a family dinner. Estimate how many people will be present at the serving time and whether portions should be modest. Ask the bakery to compare available sizes against that number and confirm the current flavour choices for the date.

Then consider design and handoff. A short message may work well for a quick office celebration, while a family event may call for a more personal reference. Share the delivery address, building access, requested time, and receiver’s phone number; confirm route availability and any fee.

Keep current products in the comparison page and portions in the sizing guide. Link the two pages rather than creating a separate weekday catalogue.

Ang usa ka mubo nga mensahe mahimo’g maayo alang sa usa ka dali nga selebrasyon sa opisina, samtang ang usa ka kalihokan sa pamilya mahimong magkinahanglan usa ka labi ka personal nga pakisayran.
