## English

# How to compare options for cake for a family lunch after church in Cebu

Compare cake options by how they fit the timing between the service and lunch, expected servings, transport, and venue rules. A cake that is difficult to carry or store may not suit a tight transition. Ask the bakery which available format fits the plan.
