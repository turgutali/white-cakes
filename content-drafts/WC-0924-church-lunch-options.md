## Compare cake options for a family lunch after church

Choose the cake based on the number of people who will stay for lunch and eat dessert. The service attendance may not match the lunch group, and the meal may include other sweets. Tell White Cakes whether you want small portions or a full party slice, then compare sizes using the same serving plan.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are ranges only; cut size changes the result. For a very small lunch, the 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly. Ask the bakery to confirm the best option instead of stretching a serving estimate.

Compare flavour and design next. White Cakes currently lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm availability for the date and selected size. Send a short inscription and one reference image; ask for a final quote based on the actual request.

If delivery is needed, share the complete address, landmark, receiver, and agreed window. Allow at least three days' notice and secure the confirmed order with the 50% deposit. Keep this comparison in the shared cake-choice guide.
