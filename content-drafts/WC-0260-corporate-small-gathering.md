## Size the cake for the colleagues who will attend the celebration

Estimate the employees who will be present at serving time, not the company’s total headcount. Tell the bakery whether the cake follows a meal or is the main snack and whether portions should be modest or party-size. Ask it to recommend an available size for the group.

The current guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake. These are approximate and cutting style matters. Confirm the current serving expectations and product options directly. If the gathering is larger, do not assume a standard size is enough.

Keep the portion advice in the central size guide and link to the corporate design and office delivery checklists.
