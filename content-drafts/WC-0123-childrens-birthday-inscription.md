## Keep a child's birthday message short and easy to read

On a cake top, a child's name and a few celebratory words are usually clearer than a long quotation. Send the exact message to White Cakes with spelling checked, and say which word should stand out if the cake is small. The 4-inch Bento has limited writing space, so ask whether the full phrase fits before choosing it.

If the cake is for a child who likes a particular colour or character, describe the visual idea and share one reference photo. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream designs; the reference can guide a colour or decorative interpretation but does not guarantee a copied character, logo, or exact image. Ask what can be made with the selected size and finish.

Give the event date, flavour, guest count, and pickup or delivery plan in the same enquiry. Use the size guide to discuss portions. If delivered, a responsible adult should receive the box and keep it out of direct sun until serving. The bakery asks for at least three days' notice and confirms availability before the order is reserved.

Keep this advice with the children's birthday and cake-message guides. A standalone message gallery should use original lines and show how each example fits a real cake surface; a generic list of phrases would add little beyond the existing guide.
