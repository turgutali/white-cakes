## Ask for the wedding-cake quote that matches your actual brief

Wedding-cake pricing depends on the format, size, design, and delivery address. White Cakes publishes starting prices for its listed custom sizes; a multi-tier design or detailed hand-piped finish may have a different final quote. Bouquet Cake is quoted on request because the scale and finish vary. Check the current price guide instead of relying on an old quotation or a photograph.

For an accurate reply, send the wedding date, venue, expected cake-eaters, serving plan, flavour, reference image, and delivery contact. Ask whether the price you receive is a starting amount or the confirmed total for the selected design. Confirm the address-based delivery charge and delivery window as separate items.

The size guide gives planning estimates for a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier cake, but the bakery should confirm portions for the actual tier dimensions. Weddings and multi-tier requests should be discussed earlier than the three-day minimum when possible, so availability and setup can be planned.

Before the 50% deposit, confirm the final design, amount, date, payment terms, and venue handover. Keep current amounts in the central price guide and wedding-specific portion details on the wedding page.
