## Store a family cake safely before serving in Mandaue

After delivery, place the cake on a stable, level surface away from direct sunlight, a hot vehicle, or warm kitchen equipment. If the meal begins several hours later, follow White Cakes' care instructions and ask whether the specific design should be refrigerated. Do not apply a fixed room-temperature limit without the bakery's guidance.

Choose someone in the family to receive the cake and move it to the planned location. If the gathering is at a home or function room, confirm that there is a cool place available and that the receiver knows when the cake will be served. Avoid leaving the box unattended at a gate or outside.

For delivery in Mandaue, provide the complete address, landmark, local mobile number, and access instructions. Confirm the delivery window and address-based fee. Include the date, size, flavour, and serving estimate; current estimates are 8–12 portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the 50% deposit for the agreed order.
