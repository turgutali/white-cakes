## Ask for the New Year cake price that matches the chosen design

There is no single New Year price for every cake. White Cakes' current amount depends on the format, size, and design. Published starting prices are maintained on the menu and price guide, while a Bouquet Cake is quoted on request because scale and finish vary. A custom design may have a different final quote from the starting price.

Send the date, guest count, flavour, size, inscription, and one reference image. Ask whether the quote covers the exact requested finish and whether any seasonal variation changes the amount. If delivery is needed, request the address-based charge separately and provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile, and delivery window.

Holiday prices and promotions can change by date. Check the live price guide when ordering; do not rely on a screenshot or quote from a previous New Year. The bakery requests at least three days' notice and must confirm availability before the 50% deposit confirms the order.

Keep all current prices in one maintained page. The New Year article can explain the holiday timing and link to it rather than storing a separate set of prices that may become stale.
