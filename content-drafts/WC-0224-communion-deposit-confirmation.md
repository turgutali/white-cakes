## What to confirm before paying a deposit for First Communion cake in Cebu

Before paying, check that the written order summary has the event date, size, flavour, design direction, exact inscription, total price and delivery or pickup arrangement. Correct spelling, address and timing while the details can still be reviewed.

The stated 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the details and price are settled. Ask how the remaining balance is handled and what to do if timing changes; keep the confirmation and payment record together. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
