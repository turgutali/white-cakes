## Check the event schedule, cake details, and final quote together

Before paying a deposit for a First Communion cake, confirm the ceremony and reception dates, requested size, flavour, inscription, design, and delivery or pickup location. If the cake is going to a church hall or restaurant, share the receiving contact and any access instructions. Ask the bakery to confirm availability for the exact handoff window.

Request a written total that includes the design and any delivery charge. White Cakes currently states that a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order; verify the amount and payment channel against the final quote. Save the receipt and order confirmation so the family coordinator can refer to the same details.

Keep payment instructions centralized in the ordering guide. Recheck the current process before publication and do not imply that an inquiry alone reserves the date.
