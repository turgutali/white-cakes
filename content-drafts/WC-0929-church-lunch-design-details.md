## English

# Design details to consider for cake for a family lunch after church in Cebu

Design details should work at the lunch venue, not just match the ceremony. Confirm table space, whether the cake will travel between locations, and how much time is available for setup. Choose decoration that remains practical to carry and serve.

A technique article needs an original approved close-up of real work. Confirm the final design and handling notes with the bakery.
