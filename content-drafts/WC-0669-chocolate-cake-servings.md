## Estimate portions from the cake size, not the chocolate flavour

Chocolate is one of the flavours listed for White Cakes custom cakes, but the flavour does not change the diameter or the serving estimate. The current guide gives about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round and 16–24 from an 8-inch. A common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier cake is planned for roughly 30–36. A 4-inch Bento is a separate small format for two generous or three modest servings.

Treat these numbers as ranges. The actual count depends on the cake height, design, and whether guests want full portions or smaller slices after a meal. Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and whether it is the only dessert. Ask them to confirm the size for the chocolate cake you choose.

When ordering, specify chocolate as the flavour and list any expected filling or topping separately. The menu flavour alone does not confirm those additions. Include the date, size, guest count, and delivery or pickup details in the enquiry.

Keep the portion ranges in the central size guide and flavour information on the chocolate page. Separate serving charts by flavour would repeat the same size estimates without a different answer.
