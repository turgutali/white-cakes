## Confirm the christening-cake handover and design before paying

Before paying a deposit, make sure White Cakes has confirmed the requested date and has the same order details you expect: size, flavour, design, inscription, price, and pickup or delivery plan. A 50% deposit confirms an agreed order; it should follow the bakery's availability reply and a clear quote.

For a christening cake, send the exact name or wording and check the spelling. If you have a colour palette or reference photo, identify the one detail you want the baker to interpret. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; the reference guides the finish but does not guarantee an identical copy or printed artwork. Ask whether the message will fit the chosen cake size.

If the cake is delivered to a church, home, or reception venue, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instructions. Confirm the delivery window and address-based charge. Choose someone who can receive the cake and keep it out of direct sun until the family is ready to serve it.

White Cakes requests at least three days' notice. Ask earlier for weekends and detailed piping, and do not assume the date is reserved until the order is confirmed. Keep this occasion-specific checklist on the existing christening and ordering pages so customers can find one accurate source for payment and logistics.
