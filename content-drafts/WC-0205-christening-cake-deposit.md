## What to confirm before paying a deposit for christening cake in Cebu

Before paying, check that the written order summary has the event date, size, flavour, design direction, exact inscription, total price and delivery or pickup arrangement. Correct spelling, address and timing while the details can still be reviewed.

The stated 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the details and price are settled. Ask how the remaining balance is handled and what to do if timing changes; keep the confirmation and payment record together. For a christening, confirm the child’s name and event date from the family’s final invitation, and ask the venue about the cake handoff point and service time. Keep any religious motif within the family’s requested direction.
