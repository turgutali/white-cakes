## English

# Design details to consider for vanilla cake in Cebu

Design details for vanilla cake should be chosen after the bakery confirms the available size and finish. Decide which visual element matters most—colour, a name, a themed decoration, or an event number—and keep secondary details limited so the cake remains readable.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and does not offer edible printed logo sheets. Ask which details can be piped and send only approved artwork if a mark is involved. The bakery should confirm what is feasible; do not promise exact replication from a reference.

Review the final wording and placement at the chosen size, then approve one written design summary. Verify the live vanilla option and availability before payment.
