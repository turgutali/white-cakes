## Separate the flavour choice from the cake's outside design

Vanilla is one of the flavours listed for White Cakes custom cakes. The flavour name tells the bakery which cake flavour you are requesting; it does not by itself specify a filling, topping, or colour. If those details matter to your design, ask whether they are available for the selected format and date and wait for confirmation before paying.

The outside can be discussed as a separate brief. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream designs, including Signature Marble's dusty-blue marbling and shell border, Bouquet Cake's piped flowers, and fully custom work based on a reference. Those are actual styles to discuss, but a vanilla flavour does not automatically include any one finish. Send one photo and name the detail you want adapted—such as the palette or border—then ask what is possible on the chosen size.

For a family table, give the bakery the number of people who will eat cake and use the size guide to plan portions. Include the date, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery details with the flavour request. At least three days' notice is requested, and availability must be confirmed.

Keep the design and flavour discussion within the existing vanilla and cake-design guides. A separate page should only be added if original photos and distinct vanilla-specific information make it more useful than a section on the current flavour page.
