## English

# Which reference photos help when ordering custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Choose reference photos that show one feature at a time: overall shape, buttercream texture, colour, or lettering. Say what you want to borrow and what may change. White Cakes hand-pipes designs; printed sheets and fondant sculpture are not part of its stated approach.

A reference is not a guarantee of exact replication. Ask the bakery to confirm feasibility at the selected size.

## Tagalog

# Aling mga reference photo ang makatutulong sa pag-order ng custom buttercream cake sa Cebu
