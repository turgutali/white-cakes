## Choosing a retirement-cake design that fits the person

Start with the retiree, not a generic “congratulations” template. Decide whether the cake should feel warm and personal, polished for an office send-off, or light enough for colleagues to share a laugh. One short line and one colour direction usually make a clearer brief than several slogans and unrelated decorations.

White Cakes makes custom designs with hand-piped buttercream. Send a reference that shows the detail you like, then explain whether the important part is the colour, border, lettering, or overall mood. The baker can confirm what translates to the chosen cake size. A long message may be difficult to fit, so send the exact wording early and ask if it should be shortened. Do not assume that a logo or complex image can be printed from a file; the design is interpreted within the available piped finish.

For a workplace gathering, share the expected number of cake-eaters and whether the cake follows a meal. The size guide gives planning estimates for Bento, 6-inch, 8-inch, and a common two-tier arrangement; the actual portions depend on the cut and design. Include the date and delivery or pickup plan so availability and the quote can be confirmed together.

This advice can strengthen the existing cake-design and office-celebration guides. A retirement-specific standalone page needs an approved photo of an actual retirement cake and a distinct design decision to explain; without that example, changing only the occasion name would create a thin page.
