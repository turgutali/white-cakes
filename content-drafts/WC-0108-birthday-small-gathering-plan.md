## Plan a birthday cake for a small gathering

For a small birthday meal, decide how many people will actually share cake and whether you want a full slice or a modest portion. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous portions or three modest servings. If the group is larger, compare it with a 6-inch cake, which White Cakes estimates at 8–12 party portions.

Serving estimates are planning tools, not guarantees. Cake height, slice size, and other desserts can change how many portions you get. Tell White Cakes the number of cake eaters and ask the bakery to confirm the size. If the birthday is at home or at a venue, include the date, delivery or pickup plan, and any receiving instructions.

Choose one flavour from the current menu and keep the design brief simple: colours, one reference, and a short inscription. Confirm the final design and quote. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; its 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once availability and terms are clear.
