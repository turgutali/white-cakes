## Confirm the birthday date and design before the week of the party

Start with the serving date and decide whether you need pickup or delivery. Send the bakery the requested size, flavour, design reference, inscription, and event location. Choose one person to approve the final details so spelling and decoration do not change at the last minute.

White Cakes’ current instructions ask for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Reach out earlier for detailed custom work or a popular weekend, and wait for availability confirmation. Request the complete quote and handoff plan before following the stated 50% deposit process.

Keep current lead times and booking steps in one maintained Cebu guide. Recheck the live ordering instructions before publication.
