## Ask for a quote that includes the cake and the handoff plan

For a family lunch after church, share the date, likely number of cake eaters, preferred size, flavour, and design. If the schedule includes travel between the church and lunch venue, explain the requested handoff location and time. Ask the bakery whether that delivery arrangement is available and whether a fee applies.

Request the complete current price, including any custom decoration, inscription, and delivery charge. Posted prices can change, so confirm the quote for the actual date and design. Ask which deposit secures the order and wait for written confirmation before treating it as booked. White Cakes currently states a 50% deposit process, subject to confirmation with the final order.
