## Avoid last-minute confusion with a Mother's Day cake

The first mistake is waiting until the holiday weekend to ask whether a date is available. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, but a busy Mother's Day date should be checked earlier. Send the date, preferred handoff time, and delivery location before deciding on a final design.

Another mistake is choosing a cake size from the number of relatives invited. Ask how many people will actually eat cake, then use the current portion guide as a range: 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, or 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are estimates, and serving size changes the yield.

Avoid sending a long chain of separate messages with conflicting design references. Combine the flavour, one main photo, colours, exact inscription, and any must-have detail into one brief. Confirm feasibility, price, pickup or delivery, receiving contact, and delivery fee. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the bakery replies with availability and terms.

Use this as a holiday note in the central ordering checklist. Seasonal lead-time claims and capacity should be updated only when the bakery verifies them.
