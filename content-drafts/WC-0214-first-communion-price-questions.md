## What to ask about the price of First Communion cake in Cebu

Request a written quote for the exact date, size, flavour, piping detail, inscription and delivery address. A reference image by itself cannot establish the price because scale and hand-piped detail affect the work; ask what the quoted amount includes.

Prices and availability can change. The listed launch prices for Signature Marble sizes are date-limited through 15 January 2027 and do not set the price for every custom design. Confirm current pricing directly before announcing a budget. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
