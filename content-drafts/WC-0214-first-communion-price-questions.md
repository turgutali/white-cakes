## Get a useful First Communion cake quote

When requesting a price, include more than the occasion. Tell White Cakes the date, number of cake eaters, preferred size, flavour, design idea, inscription, and delivery or pickup location. A simple cake and a custom design may have different costs, so ask for a quote that reflects the actual scope rather than assuming an image matches a listed price.

The current custom menu lists a 6-inch cake at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799 from January 16. An 8-inch cake is ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. These are date-limited published prices and must be checked against the live menu before use; special design work or delivery can affect the final amount. Do not present the temporary rates as permanent.

Ask whether the size will cover the people who plan to eat cake. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Also confirm the minimum three-day notice, delivery fee for the venue, and exact deposit terms. The current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Keep the price in the central menu guide, with its effective dates visible. This FAQ can clarify what affects a quote without creating a separate stale price page for each event.
