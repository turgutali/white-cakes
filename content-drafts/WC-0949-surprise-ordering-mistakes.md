## Avoid surprise-cake problems before delivery day

Do not forget that the driver needs a local person who can answer the phone. If the recipient should not know about the cake, give White Cakes a trusted receiver's mobile number and explain who may be contacted. Provide the complete address, landmark, and access instructions so the surprise does not depend on a driver guessing the location.

Do not assume a request has reserved the date. Confirm availability and allow at least three days for made-to-order cakes. Send the size, flavour, design reference, exact inscription, serving count, and delivery plan together. Ask the bakery to confirm the design and price; a reference photo does not guarantee an exact copy.

Avoid choosing a size based only on the recipient's household count. Tell the bakery how many people will actually share it and whether there are other desserts. Confirm the delivery window, fee, and handoff plan in writing. The 50% deposit secures the agreed order once terms are settled.

These are general safeguards, not stories about real customers. Add them to the central order guide instead of creating a duplicate article.
