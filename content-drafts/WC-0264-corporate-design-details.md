## Prioritize one brand or milestone detail so the cake stays legible

Before ordering, decide whether the cake should feature the company name, a milestone number, a short message, or a brand colour. Trying to fit all of these alongside a complex decoration can make the surface hard to read. Choose one primary feature and ask the bakery how it can fit the cake size.

Provide a clean logo file only after confirming company permission. Share the event date, serving estimate, flavour, and delivery location; ask whether the building access and handoff time are feasible. Confirm spelling and the final design summary in writing, and get approval before publishing the cake or company name in a case study.

Use this as a corporate example in the central design guide. Any standalone case needs a real finished cake, original close-up, and company/customer permission.
