## Decide the flavour and serving plan as separate parts of the order

For a large gathering, estimate how many guests will actually be served cake and whether they will take modest or party slices. Tell the bakery if there are other desserts. A flavour choice such as chocolate does not determine how many portions a cake will provide; the size and cutting style do.

The current size guide lists approximate ranges of 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. Confirm the range and current chocolate availability with the bakery before ordering. If the guest count is uncertain, ask which size best matches the likely number of servings and your budget.

Keep the verified flavour list in the flavour guide and serving ranges in the size guide. This combined query can be answered with links between those two authoritative pages, rather than duplicating both.
