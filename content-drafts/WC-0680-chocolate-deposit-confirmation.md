## Confirm Chocolate cake availability and terms before payment

Check that White Cakes has confirmed Chocolate for the requested date and cake size. The flavour appears on the current menu, but availability should be verified for the specific design and configuration. Do not assume a flavour name includes a certain filling or ingredient; ask the bakery if that detail matters.

Review the order summary for date, size, flavour, agreed design, exact inscription, total price, and pickup or delivery. If you sent a reference, make sure the final interpretation is clear. For delivery, verify the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, time window, and fee.

White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Its current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in the current quote and keep the receipt with the written confirmation.

Keep deposit details in the main order guide and recheck both the menu and payment process before publishing.
