## Understand the Signature Marble design before requesting changes

The current Signature Marble listing describes a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. Those elements already establish the visual direction. If one feature matters most to you, ask the bakery whether it can be emphasized while keeping the design balanced.

Before requesting a different colour, border, flower, or inscription, confirm whether the change is available and whether it changes the price. A reference photo can communicate your preference, but it does not guarantee an exact copy. Ask White Cakes to confirm the final interpretation and current product availability for the event date.

The 6-inch size is currently estimated at 8–12 party portions. Tell the bakery how many people will share it and ask whether that size fits your serving plan. Include the date, flavour, and pickup or delivery details; made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use an original close-up only if it shows the current product and is approved for publication. Otherwise, refresh the product listing and link to it from the existing cake-design guide.
