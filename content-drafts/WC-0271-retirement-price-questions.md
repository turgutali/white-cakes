## Ask for the full quote based on the farewell design and serving plan

Share the retirement date, expected servings, requested size, flavour, message, and design reference. Tell the bakery whether the cake is for an office, restaurant, or home and whether delivery is needed. Ask whether the quote includes lettering, custom decoration, and the requested handoff.

Confirm the current total in writing because prices and product options can change. Ask about any delivery charge and the deposit needed to secure the date. If the group has a budget, share it before finalizing the design so the bakery can explain the available options.

Keep rates centralized in the maintained Cebu price guide and link to it from retirement planning content.
