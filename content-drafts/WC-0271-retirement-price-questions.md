## English

# Questions to Ask About a Retirement Cake Quote in Cebu

A useful retirement-cake quote should make clear what is included and what decisions are still open. Before comparing offers, send each bakery the same brief: event date, cake size or guest estimate, flavour, design direction, inscription, pickup or delivery address, and the time the cake is needed. Different assumptions can make two prices look comparable when they describe different orders.

Ask whether the quoted amount covers the selected size, requested buttercream design, inscription, and any topper or special decoration. If you are considering a logo, edible image, or detailed theme, ask whether that method is available and whether it changes the price. White Cakes decorates in hand-piped buttercream; its current information does not list printed edible logo sheets, so confirm any brand treatment before it is included in the quote.

For delivery, provide the exact address and ask whether the bakery serves that location, what the delivery fee is, and what receiving instructions it needs. For a workplace celebration, include the building name, landmark, floor or lobby, and contact number. Confirm whether pickup is an option and what time the order will be ready. White Cakes provides a delivery window rather than a guaranteed minute of arrival, which matters when planning a program.

Before paying, request the final total, deposit amount, accepted payment method, and the policy for changes or cancellation. Current White Cakes ordering information states a 50% deposit, but check the written quote for the terms that apply to your order. Keep the confirmed details—date, flavour, design, message, price, and handoff—in writing. For broader comparisons, see the [Cebu cake price guide](/journal/cake-prices-cebu.html) and [retirement cake guide](/journal/retirement-cake-cebu.html).
