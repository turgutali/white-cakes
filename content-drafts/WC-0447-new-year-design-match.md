## Choose a New Year design that suits the gathering and stays legible

Decide whether the cake should feel festive, minimal, or personal to the family. Choose one focal idea—such as a short year-end message or a restrained colour palette—and explain which details are flexible. Ask the bakery which seasonal decorations are available for the actual date.

Confirm cake size, flavour, inscription, serving plan, total price, and delivery or pickup separately. If the cake will be served at midnight, provide that schedule and a realistic handoff window. Seasonal stock and schedules may change, so confirm the design in writing.
