## Confirm Ube availability before setting the cake timeline

White Cakes lists Ube on its current flavour menu, but check availability for the specific date and size before finalizing plans. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. For a weekend, holiday, or custom design, contact the bakery sooner so there is time to confirm the flavour and production slot.

Send the event date, size, serving estimate, design reference, inscription, and pickup or delivery location together. Ask the bakery to confirm that Ube is available for the requested configuration and whether the design changes the quote. Do not assume ingredients or flavour details not listed by the business.

If delivery is planned, include the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Wait for written confirmation of the order and terms; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed design and handoff plan.

Keep flavour-specific availability linked from the main order timeline and current menu. Update it when the bakery changes its offerings.
