## Estimate servings for a family gathering in Lapu-Lapu

Count the family members who will have cake and consider whether the celebration includes other desserts. Some guests may take a small portion while others may want a full slice. Tell White Cakes how you plan to serve it so the bakery can help choose a practical size.

The current guide estimates around 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These numbers are approximate and vary with cutting style. Do not turn them into an exact guest guarantee; ask for a recommendation if the count is close to the top of a range.

When ordering for Lapu-Lapu, provide the event date, flavour, design, and complete delivery address, including a landmark and local contact number. Confirm that the route and time are available and ask for the address-based fee. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
