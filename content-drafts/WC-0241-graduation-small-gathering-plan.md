## Planning graduation cake in Cebu

Decide the cake’s job first: feed the gathering, mark a candle or photo moment, or do both. Note when it will be served and what other desserts are planned before choosing size and design.

Put one person in charge of the final brief and handoff. Confirm date, serving count, wording, quote, delivery recipient and care plan together so the event team works from one agreed version. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
