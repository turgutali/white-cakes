## Plan graduation cake portions around the family gathering

For a small graduation meal, count the family members who will actually eat cake after the ceremony. If there are other desserts, you may want smaller portions; if cake is the main dessert, ask about a fuller serving plan. The ceremony guest list alone does not tell you how many slices to order.

White Cakes' current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. A 4-inch Bento is intended for about two generous or three modest servings. These are approximate yields, so tell the bakery how the cake will be cut and ask it to confirm the right size.

Include the graduation date, flavour, design, inscription, and pickup or delivery details in your enquiry. If the event is at a school or office, identify the receiver and any entry instructions. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; confirm availability and final terms before paying the 50% deposit that secures the order.

Keep the small-gathering example inside one verified size guide and update it if product sizes change.
