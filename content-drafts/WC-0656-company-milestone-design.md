## Match a company-milestone cake to the event, not just the logo

Decide what the cake should communicate: a company anniversary, a team achievement, or a new launch. A logo can be one element, but the palette, short message, and event setting can shape the design too. Ask the company for approved brand colours and artwork before sending anything to the bakery.

Choose a clear focal point and keep the message readable. Send one reference image and identify the element you want adapted. White Cakes should confirm whether a logo, lettering, or custom motif is feasible for the selected size and quote. Do not assume a reference image grants permission to use a company's mark or that an online logo file is suitable for printing.

Share the event date, number of cake eaters, flavour, and delivery address. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Confirm the size and office handoff details, allow at least three days' notice, and pay the 50% deposit after the order is agreed.

Use original, permission-cleared photos for a company-specific case. Otherwise, merge the design principles into the existing cake-design guide.
