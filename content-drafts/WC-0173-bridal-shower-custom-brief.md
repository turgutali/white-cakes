## Make a bridal-shower cake brief easy to quote

Put the practical details first: event date, preferred delivery or pickup time, venue address, estimated cake eaters, and flavour. If the cake will be part of a larger dessert table, say so; the cake may not need to serve every guest. White Cakes' guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, with the actual count depending on portions.

Describe the design with a few choices instead of a long mood board. Name the main colours, the most important decorative feature, and any detail to avoid. Attach a reference and say what it illustrates. Include the inscription exactly as it should appear. The bakery must confirm whether the design and wording fit the requested size and what they will cost.

Ask about availability early. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for custom cakes, and popular event dates should be checked sooner. Wait for written agreement on the design, flavour, size, price, delivery details, and timing; the 50% deposit confirms that agreed order.

Use this as the bridal-shower example within the custom brief guide. A separate occasion page needs original evidence or a distinct search intent, not just a new event name attached to the same checklist.
