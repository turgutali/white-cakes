## What to include in a custom brief for bridal shower cake in Cebu

A useful brief names the occasion, date, serving time, venue, approximate guest count, budget range and the cake’s role. Add the preferred size, flavour, exact message and one clear design reference; mark which details are essential and which can change.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, so describe the mood and a few specific details instead of asking for an exact copy of a screenshot. Ask the bakery to confirm an achievable interpretation before treating the design as final. For a bridal shower, treat the couple’s or host’s palette as a guide and avoid reproducing private or copyrighted details without permission. Confirm who approves the inscription and final design so the surprise stays coordinated.
