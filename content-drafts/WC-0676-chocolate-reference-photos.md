## Use references to explain the decoration, not to guess the flavour

If you are ordering chocolate cake, a reference image can help communicate the outside design, colour, or lettering style. Tell the bakery which part of the image matters; the photo alone may not show the cake’s actual flavour or ingredients. Confirm the current chocolate option and what it includes directly.

Include the date, size, serving estimate, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery plan. If the reference is from another bakery, explain which aspects are inspiration and ask how they can be adapted to your order. Confirm the final quote and design summary in writing.

Add this distinction to the shared custom-brief guide and link to current flavour information. Do not treat a visual reference as a guarantee of an exact copy or recipe.
