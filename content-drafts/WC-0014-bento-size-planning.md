## Plan around the 4-inch Bento's small serving size

The 4-inch Bento is for a small cake moment: about two generous servings or three modest ones. It can fit a couple's celebration or a small thank-you, but it is unlikely to suit a large party where everyone expects a full slice. Count the people who will actually eat cake before choosing this size.

For a bigger group, ask White Cakes whether a 6-inch or 8-inch cake is more practical. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. These ranges vary with cutting style and are not guarantees. Share whether there are other desserts and ask the bakery to confirm the best option.

Send the order date, flavour, design, short inscription, and pickup or delivery plan together. A Bento is still made to order, so allow at least three days' notice and wait for date confirmation. If it will be delivered, include a complete address and receiving contact. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep small-size guidance in the central cake-size article and the current product listing. Do not publish a separate portion chart that conflicts with the menu.
