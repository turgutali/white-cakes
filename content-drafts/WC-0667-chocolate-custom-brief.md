## Give the bakery a clear brief for a custom chocolate cake

Start with the event date, preferred size, number of people who will eat cake, and the time you need it. Add Chocolate as the requested flavour and ask whether it is available for that size and design. White Cakes lists Chocolate on its current menu; confirm availability before relying on it for a specific date.

Describe the design in a few points: main colour, one focal decoration, and exact inscription. Attach one reference image and explain which feature you want adapted. A photo may show details that are not practical for the chosen cake, so ask White Cakes to confirm the version it can make and the final quote.

For serving estimates, the current guide suggests 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. These are approximate; tell the bakery whether the cake is the only dessert. For delivery, provide a complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and access instructions. Allow at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour-specific templates in one maintained custom brief guide rather than publishing a separate page for every flavour.
