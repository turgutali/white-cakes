## Write a clear custom brief for Ube cake

Start with the event date, desired size, number of people who will eat cake, and requested Ube flavour. White Cakes lists Ube in its current menu, but confirm availability for the date and design. Do not infer exact ingredients, filling, or taste description from the flavour name; ask the bakery if those facts matter to the order.

For the design, give the main colour, one focal detail, and exact inscription. Attach a reference and identify the element to adapt. Ask White Cakes whether it can create the requested finish for the chosen cake and whether the custom work changes the quote. A reference photo is not a guarantee of an exact replica.

Include the serving plan and delivery or pickup address. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; ask the bakery to confirm the size. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm the written order details, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed cake.

Keep this flavour-specific prompt in the central custom-brief guide, linked to the current menu and sizing information.
