## Choose a weekday cake design from the event, not the calendar label

A weekday celebration can use the same cake formats and hand-piped designs as any other date. Start with the people sharing the cake, then choose a size from the current serving guide. Decide whether a short inscription, a floral finish, or a simple border should lead the design. The weekday itself does not require a separate style or imply a discount.

White Cakes offers a 4-inch Bento, Signature Marble, Bouquet Cake, and custom buttercream options. Send one reference photo and identify the detail you want interpreted. Ask for the current quote and date availability; the design and size can affect the amount.

If the cake is delivered to an office or home, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile, and any lobby or gate instructions. A named person should receive it during the agreed window. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery must confirm the date.

Keep weekday design advice in the general design guide and maintained menu. A weekday-specific page should only be created if real search data shows a distinct question beyond the same design and order choices.
