## Confirm the cake message with someone in Cebu

If you are ordering from abroad, ask a trusted family member in Cebu to check the recipient's name and preferred wording. Names, dates, and local phrases can be easy to mistype across time zones. Send the final inscription in one message with exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation.

Keep the text short enough to fit the chosen cake design. Ask White Cakes whether it works on the selected size and whether it needs to be shortened. If the message is in Cebuano or Tagalog, have a fluent family member confirm the wording and tone before sharing it with the bakery.

Include the event date, flavour, size, design, and serving plan. For a 4-inch Bento, about two generous or three modest servings are planned. Confirm the local delivery address, receiver's mobile number, and handoff details. Ask the bakery directly about current overseas payment options; do not assume a method or currency.
