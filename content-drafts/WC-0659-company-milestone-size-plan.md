## Plan company-milestone portions before choosing a design

Count the employees who will be present for the cake moment and decide whether everyone will gather at one time. A single cake may work for a shared table; several Bento boxes may be simpler when people are at different desks. White Cakes' size guide estimates about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, 16–24 from an 8-inch, and roughly 30–36 from a common two-tier cake. These are ranges and must be confirmed for the actual design and cut.

The corporate-order page asks for the company name, date, contact mobile, delivery address with landmark, lobby or gate instructions, and quantity if ordering more than one. If flavours or inscriptions differ, include the list in the first enquiry so the bakery can confirm what its schedule can accommodate.

For the design, send one reference photo and say whether the company colour, team name, or milestone message matters most. White Cakes can interpret colours in hand-piped buttercream but does not print edible logo sheets from a file. Ask what is possible before agreeing on the final price.

Use the size guide for portions and keep company access details on the corporate-cake page. The cake's date, final quote, delivery charge, and handover window should be confirmed before the 50% deposit.
