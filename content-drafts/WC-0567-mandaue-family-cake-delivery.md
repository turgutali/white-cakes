## Coordinate a Mandaue cake delivery with one clear handover contact

Choose the person who will receive the cake before sending the order. Give White Cakes the full Mandaue address, a nearby landmark, the receiver's mobile number, and any subdivision gate or building instruction. If the celebration is at a venue, name the entrance and the person who can meet the driver. A delivery window is agreed for the order; it is not a promise of arrival at one exact minute.

Tell the receiver when to expect the delivery and where to put the box once it arrives. Buttercream cakes should be kept out of direct sun and hot cars. If the cake will wait for several hours, the bakery's handling guidance says to refrigerate it. Avoid leaving it at a guardhouse or outside while the family is still travelling to the venue.

For the order itself, send the date, cake style, flavour, size, and exact message. Share how many people will eat cake so the bakery can confirm a sensible portion plan. Ask for the address-based delivery charge and current quote before paying. At least three days' notice is requested, and the date must be confirmed by the bakery.

This practical Mandaue handover checklist belongs on the Mandaue delivery page, with the general heat and receiving guidance on the central Cebu delivery guide. A separate article needs a documented route or venue detail that is specific to Mandaue; without that evidence, a city-swapped duplicate would not help the reader.
