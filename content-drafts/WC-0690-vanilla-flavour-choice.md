## Check what “vanilla” means on the current menu

When ordering a vanilla cake, confirm the exact flavour option the bakery currently offers and whether “vanilla” refers to the cake itself, its filling, or both. Ask what choices are available for the requested size and date. Do not assume that a flavour combination is available just because it appears in an old photograph or a past menu.

Think about who will eat the cake and what else is being served. For a mixed group, a familiar flavour may be a straightforward choice; if the recipient has a clear preference, prioritize that. Tell the bakery about allergies or dietary restrictions before confirming and ask whether it can accommodate them. Never infer ingredient or allergen safety from the flavour name alone.

Isipin kung sino ang kakain ng cake at kung ano pa ang ihahain. Para sa isang halo-halong grupo, ang isang pamilyar na lasa ay maaaring isang tapat na pagpipilian; kung ang tatanggap ay may malinaw na kagustuhan, unahin iyon. Sabihin sa panaderya ang tungkol sa mga allergy o mga paghihigpit sa pandiyeta bago kumpirmahin at tanungin kung maaari nitong tanggapin ang mga ito. Huwag kailanman maghinuha ng kaligtasan ng sangkap o allergen mula sa pangalan ng lasa lamang.
