## Get the approved design, logo, and delivery details in writing

Before paying, confirm the corporate event date, cake size, serving estimate, flavour, exact message, approved logo or brand colours, delivery address, and receiver. Ask the company contact to approve branding use before the bakery begins. Request a complete quote that includes custom decoration and delivery.

White Cakes’ stated process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order. Verify the payment channel and amount with the final written quote, then save the receipt and order summary. Confirm office access and handoff timing separately; a booking does not guarantee a building-specific route or time.

Keep the booking process centralized in the order guide and confirm current details before publication.
