## Plan a thank-you cake for a small group

If the cake is a personal thank-you, decide how many people will share it. A 4-inch Bento is intended for two generous servings or three modest portions. If you are thanking a small team or family group, tell White Cakes the actual number of cake eaters and ask whether a 6-inch cake is more appropriate.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Those are approximate yields. Consider whether cake is the only dessert and whether you want full slices or small tastes. Ask the bakery to confirm the size rather than guessing from the number invited.

For a custom thank-you, send the flavour, short inscription, design reference, event date, and pickup or delivery plan. Confirm availability, design, and quote. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all details are settled.

Keep portion advice in the central size guide and link to the message guide for wording ideas.
