## Choose a useful reference for a company milestone cake

For a company milestone, a reference image should explain the visual direction without creating uncertainty about brand use. Share the palette, general layout, or decoration style you like. If you want a company logo, provide an approved high-quality asset and confirm that the company allows it to be reproduced on a cake. Do not use a random web image as a substitute for the official mark.

Send one primary reference and label any optional detail. Include the exact company name, milestone wording, event date, office address, serving estimate, and receiving contact. Ask White Cakes whether the requested text and design will fit the cake size, and confirm the quote before the order is finalized.

For office servings, the current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Tell the bakery how many people are expected to eat cake and whether there are other desserts. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm the delivery window and fee for the address, then pay the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Only publish a reference-to-finished case when both images and company details are cleared for use. Otherwise, keep these instructions in the main custom-cake brief guide.
