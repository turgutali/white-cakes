## Confirm what is included in the Bouquet Cake before requesting changes

White Cakes describes Bouquet Cake as hand-piped buttercream flowers. Check the current product listing and photos to confirm the design, size, and available options before ordering. If you have a preference for the overall palette or a particular floral impression, tell the bakery which detail matters most and ask whether it can be adapted.

Confirm the flavour, message, date, price, and pickup or delivery plan separately. Ask how to transport and store the cake so the decoration is handled according to the baker’s instructions. Do not assume a flower type, colour, or finish from an image without asking.

Keep product-specific details on the Bouquet Cake listing and general design tips in the existing guide. Use original close-ups only when they show actual approved work.
