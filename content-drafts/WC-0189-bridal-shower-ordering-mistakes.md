## Common ordering mistakes to avoid with bridal shower cake in Cebu

Avoid booking assumptions: a message or reference photo is not a confirmed slot. Also avoid a brief with no guest count, venue or serving time, choosing tiers from appearance alone, and leaving spelling or delivery arrangements until event day.

Use one written summary for the final size, flavour, design, wording, quote and handoff. Confirm current availability and care instructions with the bakery; these checks reduce last-minute confusion without adding unnecessary complexity. For a bridal shower, treat the couple’s or host’s palette as a guide and avoid reproducing private or copyrighted details without permission. Confirm who approves the inscription and final design so the surprise stays coordinated.
