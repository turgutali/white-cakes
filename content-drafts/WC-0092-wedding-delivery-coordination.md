## Coordinate wedding-cake delivery with the venue team

Choose a named person who will receive the cake at the venue. Give White Cakes the complete address, landmark, mobile number, delivery window, and instructions for the correct entrance or loading point. Confirm whether the venue allows deliveries at that time and whether someone can bring the cake to the display area.

Agree on where the cake will be set down and who is responsible for keeping it level and away from direct sun or heat. If there will be a delay before serving, ask the bakery about storage for the specific design. Do not assume the venue can refrigerate or hold the cake unless the coordinator confirms it.

Share the event date, cake size, flavour, design, serving plan, and inscription with the order. Confirm delivery availability and fee for the exact location. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design and logistics are settled.
