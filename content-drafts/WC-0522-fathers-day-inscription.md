## Keep a Father's Day cake message personal and brief

Choose a greeting that sounds like the family, not a phrase selected only because it is popular online. A name plus a few words—such as “Best Dad” or “Salamat, Pa”—can be easier to read than a long note. These are sample ideas, not messages from actual White Cakes customers.

Send the exact spelling and punctuation in one message. If you want Cebuano or Tagalog wording, check it with a fluent family member and provide the approved version to the bakery. Ask whether the inscription fits the cake surface and design. A short line can leave more room for a colour or motif that the recipient enjoys.

Include the date, flavour, size, and delivery plan with the design request. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generously or three modestly; a larger gathering may need a 6-inch or 8-inch cake. Confirm availability and quote early, especially around the holiday. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Add wording examples to the existing message guide instead of making a near-duplicate holiday page.
