## Choose a finish that complements the occasion and the chocolate flavour

Decide what the cake should communicate before choosing decoration. A simple border, short greeting, or one colour accent may suit a family gathering, while a milestone may need a clear name or number. Share one reference and point out the feature you want adapted.

Confirm that the current chocolate option is available for your date and ask what it includes. The outside design does not establish filling or ingredients. Specify the size, serving estimate, exact message, and budget, then ask how the finish fits the selected cake.

Keep design ideas in the central guide and current flavour information on the flavour page. Any standalone close-up needs an original image of real work and permission.
