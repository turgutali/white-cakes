## Ask for the current Red Velvet cake price

When asking for a quote, tell White Cakes the size, date, flavour, design, inscription, number of cake eaters, and whether you need delivery. Red Velvet is on the current menu, but confirm availability for the selected cake and date. If the order includes custom decoration, ask for the price of that exact scope rather than assuming a standard listing covers it.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799. The 8-inch price is ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. These rates are date-limited; check the live menu before publication or ordering. Confirm whether decoration or delivery adds to the total.

Current portions are estimated at 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size against the actual guest plan. Allow at least three days' notice and verify delivery fee and timing. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep time-sensitive prices in the central price guide and remove or revise them when the effective dates pass.
