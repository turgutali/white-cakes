## Compare custom buttercream cake options by what matters

Start with servings, then compare the design choices. White Cakes' current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake. Those are ranges, not exact counts. If the event has many guests, ask whether a larger or two-tier configuration is appropriate and explain how cake will be served.

Next, decide which visual detail is essential: a colour palette, piped flowers, a border, or a short inscription. A custom reference may combine features that need different techniques. Send one reference and identify the part you want to preserve; the bakery can say what is feasible and whether the request changes the price. Available flavours currently include Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; reconfirm the menu when ordering.

Compare the confirmed quote and timing for the same size and scope. The current listed custom prices are time-sensitive: 6-inch ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799; 8-inch ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. Verify the live menu before relying on those amounts. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

This comparison belongs beside the existing menu and cake-choice guide. Keep one maintained price table rather than cloning a separate page for every buttercream style.
