## Give the Bouquet Cake order time for design confirmation

Check the event date with White Cakes early. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and Bouquet Cake's hand-piped flower design may need confirmation of colours, arrangement, and inscription. Wait for a clear availability reply before considering the order booked.

Send the requested size, flavour, event date, short message, and one floral reference. Explain which colours or flowers matter most and what can be adapted. Ask the bakery to confirm the feasible design, current quote, and any specific serving or storage guidance.

If delivery is planned, provide the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and access instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee for that location. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order once design and handoff terms are settled.

Keep the three-day minimum and confirmation steps in the central Cebu ordering guide. Do not claim a different production duration for Bouquet Cake unless the bakery verifies it.
