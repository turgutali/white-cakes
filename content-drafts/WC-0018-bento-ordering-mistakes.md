## English

# Common ordering mistakes to avoid with 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

A common mistake is ordering a 4-inch Bento for more people than its estimated two generous or three modest servings after a meal. Another is sending a long message with a detailed design and expecting everything to fit. Confirm servings and surface space before approving.

Also verify the current flavour, price, and delivery arrangement; old Bento photos do not confirm today’s options.

## Cebuano

Kasagarang sayop ang pag-order sa 4-inch Bento para sa sobra sa banabana nga duha ka dagko o tulo ka gagmay nga serving. Sayop usab ang taas nga teksto ug daghang dekorasyon nga gilauman masulod. Kumpirmaha ang servings ug luna.
