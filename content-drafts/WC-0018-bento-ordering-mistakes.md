## Common Bento-order details to settle before the cake is made

**Expecting a large cake from a small box.** The 4-inch Bento is for two generous or three modest servings. If more people will share it, tell the bakery the guest count and ask about a larger format.

**Sending a long inscription.** A small top has limited space. Send the exact words early and ask whether they fit; shorten the message if needed.

**Assuming the date is booked after messaging.** White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice, but the bakery must confirm the date. The 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the details and quote are clear.

**Leaving delivery instructions incomplete.** Provide the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. Include lobby, gate, or venue instructions and make sure somebody can accept the cake during the delivery window.

**Treating a design reference as a promise.** Send one clear photo and identify the colour or piping detail to interpret. The hand-piped finish may need adapting to the 4-inch surface. Use these reminders in the existing Bento and order guides; do not create a second small-cake checklist page.
