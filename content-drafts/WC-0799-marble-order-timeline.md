## English

# How to plan the order timeline for marble cake in Cebu

For a marble cake, confirm the actual product and available size before setting an event timeline. Work backward from serving time and reserve room for design approval, quote, payment, and delivery. White Cakes requests at least three days’ notice; allow more for a custom pattern or busy date.

Send date, guest estimate, chosen design direction, exact message, venue, and receiver in one brief. Ask the bakery to confirm flavour, price, and delivery window in writing before treating the date as reserved.
