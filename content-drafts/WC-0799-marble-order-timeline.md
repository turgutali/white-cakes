## Allow time to confirm a marble cake design

Start by checking the event date with White Cakes. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and a custom marble pattern may need a discussion about colours and layout. Ask early for a weekend or holiday and wait for explicit date confirmation.

Send the requested size, flavour, serving estimate, and design reference together. Describe the base colour and the accent colour, and say whether the marble effect should be subtle or prominent. The bakery must confirm the available finish and quote; do not assume a reference photo is an exact product or a fixed price.

If you mean Signature Marble, confirm the current product listing and whether any changes affect the order. For delivery, include the full address, landmark, receiving mobile number, window, and fee confirmation. The 50% deposit secures the agreed order once the design and terms are settled.

Keep timing in the one central Cebu order guide. Recheck lead-time claims when booking rules change.
