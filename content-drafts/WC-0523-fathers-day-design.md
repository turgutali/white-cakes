## Choose a Father's Day cake design around the recipient

Start with something the recipient actually enjoys, such as a hobby, favourite colour, or simple family message. A cake does not need a complicated scene to feel personal. Pick one focal detail and make the wording short enough to read clearly. If you use a reference photo, explain which part matters and what can be adapted.

Choose the size based on the people who will share it. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; actual portions depend on how it is cut. If the gathering includes a larger group, ask the bakery whether a different size or tiered option fits. Confirm the selected flavour and design are available for the date.

Send the exact inscription, event date, delivery or pickup plan, and full address if applicable. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order work. Confirm feasibility and the quote before the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Do not describe a made-up Father's Day customer story. A standalone design example needs an original White Cakes photo and verified choices with image permission; otherwise, use this advice in the main design guide.
