## Choose a surprise-cake flavour the recipient will enjoy

White Cakes currently lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet. If you know the recipient's preference, choose that flavour and name it in the order. If you do not, select a flavour the group is comfortable sharing and ask about any ingredient or topping that matters. The flavour name alone does not confirm a separate filling or fruit addition.

If you are unsure, the buyer can ask a family member who will not reveal the surprise, or choose a known favourite from previous celebrations. Do not assume a particular cake flavour is less allergenic or suitable for a dietary need; tell the bakery about allergies and wait for a written confirmation before ordering.

Include the event date, size, guest count, exact inscription, and delivery details. For a surprise, give the buyer's contact for questions and a separate receiver's mobile for the handover. Confirm who can receive the cake and keep it cool until serving.

Use the central flavour and order guides for these shared facts. A surprise-specific flavour page would add little unless it includes original, verified information about how to choose for a recipient whose preferences are unknown.
