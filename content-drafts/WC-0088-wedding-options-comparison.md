## Compare the serving plan, venue setup, and design priorities

Start by deciding how many guests will receive cake and whether it is served after a meal. Ask the bakery to confirm portions for the available sizes; small standard size examples may not suit a full wedding guest list. Share the venue’s serving time and how the cake will be cut.

Then compare the design options against the table space, event palette, and budget. Choose a focal detail such as a short message, flower, or clean finish. Confirm the flavour, current price, delivery access, and exact handoff window in writing.

Keep product options on the comparison page and verified portion estimates in the size guide. Link them together, and avoid presenting a product as suitable for a large wedding without direct confirmation.
