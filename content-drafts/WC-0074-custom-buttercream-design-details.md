## Choose the finish and decorative detail that best explain your idea

For a custom buttercream cake, decide whether the main feature is a smooth finish, piped border, flowers, lettering, or a combination. Share one reference and identify the detail the bakery should focus on. Ask how it may be adapted to the size, colour palette, and time available.

Confirm the flavour, filling, serving estimate, message, date, and budget separately. If the cake will be outdoors or in a warm room, tell the bakery and request specific handling instructions. Do not assume a buttercream design is heat resistant.
