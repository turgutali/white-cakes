## Plan who will receive and care for the cake before the family arrives

For a large gathering, choose one person to receive the cake and keep track of the bakery’s handling instructions. Ask what is suitable for the cake’s specific filling, frosting, and decoration. Tell the baker when it will arrive, when it will be served, and whether the venue is warm or outdoors.

Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight while waiting. Follow the bakery’s guidance about refrigeration or room temperature, and do not leave the cake unattended in a vehicle. If the program is delayed or the cake must be moved, contact the bakery for advice.

Keep verified handling information in the central heat-care guide. Do not publish one storage window for every cake type.
