## Confirm the current Bouquet Cake description and handoff plan

Check the live Bouquet Cake listing for its current appearance, size, price, and availability. Confirm the requested date, flavour, inscription, and any design changes with the bakery. Because the cake is described as hand-piped buttercream flowers, ask whether the requested delivery or pickup plan suits its decoration.

Request a complete written quote, including custom changes and delivery if applicable. White Cakes’ stated process uses a 50% deposit to confirm an agreed order; verify the exact amount and payment method before sending it. Save the receipt with the final order summary.

Keep payment steps in the central order guide and update Bouquet Cake facts on its product page. Do not pay based on an old menu or assume the product has not changed.
