## Pick one reference photo for a monthsary cake

For a small monthsary cake, one clear reference usually communicates the idea better than a folder of inspiration. Choose the photo that shows the colour, shape, or decoration you care about, then tell White Cakes what to adapt. If the cake is for two, avoid adding so many details that the message and main feature compete on a compact surface.

State which details are essential and which can change. A reference image does not guarantee an exact copy, and the final design must fit the cake size and the bakery's available techniques. Send the inscription in its final wording and ask whether it is readable on the selected design.

Include the date, flavour, serving plan, and delivery or pickup preference. The 4-inch Bento serves about two generously or three modestly. If friends will join, tell the bakery the number of cake eaters. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the quote before the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep these tips in the central brief guide. A public reference-to-finished example requires original photos and permission.
