## What makes a graduation-cake design feel personal

A graduation cake does not need every school colour, a long quotation, a logo, and several decorations at once. Start with the graduate's name, the milestone, and one visual cue that matters to the family. That might be a colour pair, a short congratulatory line, or a simple hand-piped border. A focused brief gives the baker room to make the cake readable from the table and keeps the decoration from competing with the message.

For a custom cake from White Cakes, send one reference image and say exactly what you want to borrow from it: the palette, lettering style, border, or overall mood. The reference guides a hand-piped buttercream interpretation; it does not promise an identical copy or printed edible artwork. Include the date and guest count as well, because the design needs to fit the selected size and the number of people sharing it.

Keep the inscription short enough to read at a glance. Send the exact spelling and capitalization in the order message. If the cake will be presented after a ceremony, plan who will receive it and where it can be kept out of direct sun until serving. For delivery, provide the venue address, landmark, and a reachable receiver.

This entry is a draft framework, not a claim that a pictured White Cakes graduation commission exists. Before using it as a standalone photo case study, attach an approved image of an actual graduation cake and describe a design decision visible in that cake. Without that first-party evidence, the useful advice belongs in the existing cake-design and custom-brief guides.

## Tagalog translation (machine translated; editorial review pending)

## Ano ang ginagawang personal na disenyo ng graduation-cake

Ang isang graduation cake ay hindi nangangailangan ng bawat kulay ng paaralan, isang mahabang quotation, isang logo, at ilang mga dekorasyon nang sabay-sabay. Magsimula sa pangalan ng nagtapos, ang milestone, at isang visual cue na mahalaga sa pamilya. Maaaring iyon ay isang pares ng kulay, isang maikling linya ng pagbati, o isang simpleng hand-piped na hangganan. Ang isang nakatutok na brief ay nagbibigay sa baker room upang gawing nababasa ang cake mula sa mesa at pinipigilan ang dekorasyon na makipagkumpitensya sa mensahe.

Para sa custom na cake mula sa White Cakes, magpadala ng isang reference na larawan at sabihin nang eksakto kung ano ang gusto mong hiramin mula dito: ang palette, istilo ng pagkakasulat, border, o pangkalahatang mood. Ang sanggunian ay gumagabay sa isang hand-piped buttercream interpretasyon; hindi ito nangangako ng kaparehong kopya o naka-print na nakakain na likhang sining. Isama din ang petsa at bilang ng bisita, dahil kailangang magkasya ang disenyo sa napiling laki at sa bilang ng mga taong nagbabahagi nito.

Panatilihing maikli ang inskripsiyon upang mabasa sa isang sulyap. Ipadala ang eksaktong spelling at capitalization sa mensahe ng order. Kung ang cake ay ipapakita pagkatapos ng isang seremonya, planuhin kung sino ang tatanggap nito at kung saan ito maaaring itago sa direktang araw hanggang sa paghahatid. Para sa paghahatid, ibigay ang address ng venue, landmark, at isang maaabot na receiver.
