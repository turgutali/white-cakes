## Match a buttercream cake design to the event

Choose one focal detail before requesting decoration. It may be a flower, border, colour palette, or short inscription. Then consider how the cake will be used: displayed as a centrepiece, served after a meal, or delivered to an office. The setting helps determine how much detail is useful.

Send one reference image and explain what you want adapted. Ask White Cakes which techniques it can offer for the chosen size and whether the design changes the price. A photo does not guarantee an exact copy. Confirm the final layout and wording before the order is finalized.

Plan portions using the current serving guide: 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Tell the bakery the number of cake eaters and whether other desserts are planned. If the cake will wait in a warm place, ask for specific care guidance because buttercream can soften in heat.

Use original, approved White Cakes photos for a distinct design example. Otherwise, merge the guidance into the existing design page.
