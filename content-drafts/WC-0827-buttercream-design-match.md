## English

# How to match the design of buttercream cake in Cebu

Match the buttercream design to the event, then choose a finish that suits the message and setting. Smooth piping can frame clean lettering; textured piping may be the main feature. Ask the bakery which version works with the selected size and colour direction.

Use references to explain preference, not to promise exact duplication. Confirm the actual finish, wording, price, and delivery details in one written order.
