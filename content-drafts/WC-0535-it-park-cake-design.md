## Choose one company detail to lead the IT Park cake design

An office cake should be easy to read at a shared table or reception. Choose one company colour, team name, or milestone phrase as the main cue. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; it can interpret colours or simple shapes, but it does not print edible sheets from a logo file. Ask what can be recreated by hand before confirming the design.

Send one reference photo and say which part matters: palette, lettering, border, or overall mood. Include the cake size or guest count, flavour, event date, and exact inscription. If there will be several cakes for different teams or names, send the quantities and individual details in one list so the bakery can confirm what fits the date.

For an IT Park tower, give the building address, landmark, lobby or floor access instructions, and a named receiver's mobile number. Arrange any lobby pass in advance. The bakery agrees a delivery window, so the receiver should be available throughout it.

Keep office access details and current cake options on the corporate-order page. A separate IT Park design page should wait for an actual, approved company cake photo and a specific design case.
