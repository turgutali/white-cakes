## English

# Common Housewarming Cake Ordering Mistakes to Avoid in Cebu

A new address can create delivery problems if the bakery receives only a map pin. Send the full address, building or subdivision name, access instructions, unit or floor, and a phone number for someone who will be present. If the host is busy or the house is not ready, name a receiver who can safely accept the cake.

Do not assume a design will be reproduced exactly from a photograph. Explain which colour, motif, or lettering matters and ask how it can be adapted. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and does not offer edible printed logo sheets, so confirm any address, illustration, or printed detail before making it part of the plan.

Another mistake is ordering for the invitation count without deciding who will actually eat cake. Consider the serving time, other desserts, and whether the cake is for display or sharing. Use the site size guide as an estimate and confirm the practical size with the bakery.

Before payment, get the final size, flavour, inscription, price, current deposit terms, and delivery plan in writing. White Cakes confirms a delivery window, so arrange for a receiver rather than relying on an exact arrival minute. For the ordering checklist, see [how to order a cake in Cebu](/journal/how-to-order.html).
