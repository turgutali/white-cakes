## Avoid common housewarming-cake order mistakes

Do not send a delivery request without checking that someone can receive the cake at the new home. A move-in schedule can shift, and a driver may not be able to enter a gate or locate a newly assigned unit. Confirm the date, full address, landmark, local mobile number, and receiving instructions with the host first.

Do not wait until the last minute to ask about availability. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; a weekend date should be checked earlier. Send the serving count, flavour, design, and short inscription together so the bakery can confirm the actual request.

Avoid assuming the cake should serve everyone on the invitation list. Current estimates are 8–12 portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Ask which size suits the expected cake eaters and other desserts. Confirm the price, delivery fee, and final design before paying; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use this checklist in the central order guide, without inventing stories about customers who made these mistakes.
