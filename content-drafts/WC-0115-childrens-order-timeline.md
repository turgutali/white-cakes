## Plan a children's birthday cake order early enough

Check the event date with White Cakes before settling on a design. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. If the birthday falls on a weekend, holiday, or a popular date, ask earlier and wait for the bakery to confirm the production slot.

Ask the child or parent to choose one main idea and send a clear reference photo. Include size, flavour, guest serving estimate, exact inscription, and any details to avoid. Confirm whether the theme can be adapted and what the quote includes; a reference photo does not guarantee an exact copy.

For a small party, the 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. Current 6-inch and 8-inch estimates are 8–12 and 16–24 party slices. Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and whether there are other desserts. Confirm pickup or delivery, receiver, and fee.

Once date, design, and terms are agreed, the 50% deposit confirms the order. Keep this timing guidance in the main Cebu order-timeline article.
