## English

# How to plan the order timeline for children's birthday cake in Cebu

For a child’s birthday cake, confirm the date and venue early; parents often need time to settle the theme, guest count, and party schedule. White Cakes requests at least three days’ notice, but detailed character-inspired piping or a busy weekend should be discussed sooner.

Confirm the cake and delivery are actually booked before sharing the arrival time with guests.
