## Compare the 4-inch Bento with larger cake options

The 4-inch Bento is designed for a small celebration: about two generous portions or three modest slices. Compare it with a 6-inch cake if a few more people will share; the current estimate for that size is 8–12 party portions. An 8-inch cake is estimated at 16–24. These are approximate ranges, so ask White Cakes to confirm the size against the guest and dessert plan.

Compare design and flavour as well. The Bento's compact surface may suit a short inscription and a focused design better than a crowded reference. White Cakes currently lists Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm which flavours are available for the date and size. Ask for a current quote if the design differs from a listed option.

Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. Confirm date availability, pickup or delivery, and the full price before paying. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep the serving estimate and current price in one product and size guide rather than copying them to several competing pages.

Ang 4-inch na Bento ay idinisenyo para sa isang maliit na pagdiriwang: tungkol sa dalawang masaganang bahagi o tatlong maliit na hiwa. Ihambing ito sa isang 6-pulgadang cake kung iilan pang tao ang magsasalo; ang kasalukuyang pagtatantya para sa laki na iyon ay 8–12 bahagi ng partido. Ihambing din ang disenyo at lasa. Ang compact surface ng Bento ay maaaring mas angkop sa isang maikling inskripsiyon at isang nakatutok na disenyo na mas mahusay kaysa sa isang masikip na sanggunian.
