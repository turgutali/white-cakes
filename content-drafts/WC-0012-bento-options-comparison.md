## English

# How to compare options for 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

Compare 4-inch Bento options by flavour, decoration, message space, and serving assumption. A themed design may use most of the available surface, while a plain look leaves more room for text. Ask which options are currently offered and compare the same size and scope.

If two or three people will share it after a meal, confirm whether the estimated portions suit the occasion.
