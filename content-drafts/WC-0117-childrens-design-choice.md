## Let the child choose one main birthday-cake idea

Ask the child what matters most: a favourite colour, character-inspired mood, animal, or simple activity theme. Choose one focal idea and let other details support it. A design that tries to include every favourite may be harder to read and brief, especially on a small cake.

Send one or two references and explain what White Cakes should adapt. Do not assume a copyrighted character or online design can be reproduced exactly; ask the bakery what it can offer. Include the child's name and short inscription exactly as it should appear, then confirm that the message fits the selected size.

Choose size based on cake eaters. The current 6-inch estimate is 8–12 party slices, and the 8-inch estimate is 16–24. These are approximate. Tell the bakery whether children and adults will share it and whether there are other desserts. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the quote before paying the 50% deposit.

Use original, permission-cleared cake photos to show a particular design. Without a real case, merge the advice into the general cake-design guide.
