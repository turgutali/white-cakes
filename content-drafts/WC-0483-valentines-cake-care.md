## Keep a Valentine's cake cool until the gift is ready to share

If the cake is being delivered as a surprise or taken to dinner, choose a receiver who can keep it in a suitable place until serving. White Cakes' guidance says buttercream cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars; refrigerate the cake if it will be held for several hours. Do not leave the box in a vehicle while waiting for the right moment.

If delivery is to a home, restaurant, or venue, give the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any entrance instructions. Agree who will meet the driver during the delivery window. If the venue has no place to keep a cake cool, ask the bakery about the plan before confirming your order.

Send the date, format, flavour, size, and inscription early. A 4-inch Bento has limited message space; Bouquet Cake has a floral finish and may need a quote based on its scale. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice and confirms availability before the 50% deposit.

Keep this handling advice in the shared cake-care guide and link from the Valentine's page. The basic storage advice applies to buttercream cakes generally, not only February orders.
