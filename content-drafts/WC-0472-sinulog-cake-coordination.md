## Coordinate the Sinulog cake with a named receiver and venue plan

Send White Cakes the exact date you need the cake and ask early about availability. The bakery requests at least three days' notice; a festival weekend or venue programme may require more lead time. Wait for the bakery to confirm the slot rather than assuming an online enquiry reserves it.

For a delivery, provide the full venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and the correct entrance or gate. If the event has access rules, passes, or a designated receiving area, confirm those with the venue and share the instructions before delivery day. A named person should meet the driver during the agreed window.

Share the cake format, flavour, size, guest count, and exact inscription. If you want a Sinulog-inspired colour or piped detail, send one reference and name the element to interpret. Avoid assuming a road closure or route condition without checking the event's current arrangements; ask the bakery about the practical delivery plan for your date.

After handover, keep the buttercream cake out of direct sun and hot cars. Keep city-specific access details on the current Sinulog and delivery pages so they can be updated each year.
