## How to coordinate Sinulog cake in Cebu

Assign one cake contact who has the confirmed order summary, delivery window and venue details. That person should answer the rider, notify the venue coordinator and make sure the cake reaches its planned table without being left unattended.

Share a short handoff note with the receiver’s name and phone, landmark, entry instructions, cake-table location and time of service. Avoid parallel revisions from several people; send any change through the single contact and get it reconfirmed. For a Sinulog gathering, confirm venue access, road or event restrictions and the actual service time with the organizer before setting delivery expectations. Use a palette or motif the bakery confirms it can hand-pipe; do not assume a parade route or road is accessible.
