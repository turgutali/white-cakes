## Coordinate a 4-inch Bento cake handoff

The 4-inch Bento is small enough for an intimate celebration, but the delivery still needs a clear receiver. Choose a person who will be at the address during the agreed window and share their mobile number. Give White Cakes the full address, a recognizable landmark, and any gate, lobby, or office instructions so the driver can find the handoff point.

Plan for two generous servings or three modest ones. If more people will share the cake, tell the bakery the actual count and ask whether a larger size is more suitable. Send the event date, flavour, short message, design reference, and delivery or pickup preference together. Confirm the final design and price before paying.

White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order Bento cakes. Check that the date is available and confirm any delivery fee and timing. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order; it does not replace the need to provide accurate delivery details.

On arrival, place the box on a stable surface away from direct sun and heat. This Bento-specific handoff note belongs in the shared Cebu delivery guide, alongside current local service details.
