## English

# How to coordinate 4-inch Bento cake in Cebu

Coordinate Bento delivery with the person who will receive and serve it. Confirm address, contact, delivery window, and a level place to set the box. Its small footprint may fit a table easily, but keep it away from heat and foot traffic until serving.

Confirm exact-address coverage and whether pickup or delivery suits the schedule.

## Cebuano

# Giunsa pag-coordinate ang 4-inch nga Bento cake sa Cebu

I-coordinate ang Bento delivery sa modawat ug mo-serve. Kumpirmaha ang address, contact, window ug patag nga lugar. Gamay kini apan ipahilayo gihapon sa kainit ug agianan.

Kumpirmaha ang coverage sa address ug angay ba ang pickup o delivery sa iskedyul.
