## English

# How to plan the order timeline for custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Custom buttercream cakes are made for the calendar date, not taken from a case. White Cakes asks for at least three days’ notice; weddings, multi-tier cakes, or detailed piping should be discussed earlier. Confirm the date, size, palette, message, and delivery before finalizing the design.

The bakery will confirm what the hand-piped finish can support and quote the agreed scope.

## Cebuano

# Giunsa pagplano ang oras sa pag-order para sa custom buttercream cake sa Cebu

Gihimo alang sa petsa ang custom buttercream cake. Nangayo ang White Cakes og labing menos tulo ka adlaw; sayo pa hisguti ang kasal, daghang tier o detalyadong piping. Kumpirmaha ang petsa, gidak-on, kolor, mensahe ug delivery.

Kumpirmahon sa panaderya ang mahimo sa hand-piped finish ug quote sa gikasabotang scope.

## Tagalog

# Paano planuhin ang oras ng pag-order para sa custom buttercream cake sa Cebu
