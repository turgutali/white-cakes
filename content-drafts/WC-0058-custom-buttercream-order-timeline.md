## Allow time to confirm the brief, design, and delivery plan

Choose the event date first, then send the requested size, flavour, design reference, inscription, and delivery or pickup details. Detailed buttercream piping or a custom illustration may need more planning, so contact the bakery early and ask what is possible for that date. Keep one person responsible for approving the final design.

White Cakes currently asks for at least three days’ notice for made-to-order cakes. Availability still needs confirmation, and busy dates may require earlier inquiry. Ask for the complete quote and final written order summary before using the stated 50% deposit process to confirm.

Keep lead times and booking steps in one authoritative guide. Recheck current policy before publishing this timing advice.
