## How to keep safe and cool until serving corporate cake in Cebu

Estimate portions from the serving plan, not from a photograph. White Cakes’ guide gives about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch round, 16–24 for an 8-inch round, and 30–36 for a common 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate and vary with slice size, height and design.

Tell the bakery whether cake is the main dessert, one of several sweets, or a ceremonial cut. Share the guest count and whether everyone needs a full slice; ask the team to confirm the best size for the exact design. For a corporate cake, send the approved brand colour and exact wording, and confirm who can authorize logo use. Keep the delivery contact reachable at the office or venue; do not assume reception can accept a cake without prior arrangement.
