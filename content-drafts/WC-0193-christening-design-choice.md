## Choose a clear focal detail that suits the family celebration

For a christening cake, begin with the tone of the family gathering. Decide whether you want a simple name and date, a symbolic colour, or a small decorative detail. Choose one main reference rather than combining several unrelated themes, and explain what the bakery may adapt.

Share the child’s name spelling, requested size, guest estimate, flavour, date, and delivery or pickup plan. Ask whether the design fits the cake surface and the available production time. Confirm ingredient or allergy questions directly with the bakery; do not infer them from the cake’s appearance.
