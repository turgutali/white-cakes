## Coordinate the birthday-cake delivery with the person hosting

Choose one contact who can receive the cake during the agreed delivery window. Give White Cakes the full address, nearby landmark, and receiver's mobile number; add gate, lobby, or event-venue instructions. If someone else is hosting, share the handover plan with them before the cake leaves the kitchen.

The person receiving the cake should know where to keep it until the birthday moment. Buttercream cakes should stay out of direct sun and hot cars. If held for several hours, the bakery's guidance says to refrigerate them. Do not leave the box at a guardhouse or outside while guests are still arriving.

When ordering, send the date, cake style, flavour, size, guest count, and exact inscription. Ask whether the message fits the chosen cake top. At least three days' notice is requested, but the bakery must confirm the date and quote before the order is booked. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed details.

Keep general handover instructions on the delivery guide and link them from the birthday page. A city-specific or occasion-specific delivery article should add a verified venue detail rather than repeat the same checklist.
