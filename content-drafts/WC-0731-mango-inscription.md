## Keep the message brief enough to remain readable

For a mango cake, start by deciding whether the inscription should name the occasion, the recipient, or both. A short “Happy Birthday, Ana” or “Salamat, Mama” is easier to read than a full sentence. Send the exact spelling and punctuation with your order, especially when names or words are unfamiliar to the person decorating the cake.

Ask the bakery where the message can fit on the chosen design. Decorative borders and fruit details can reduce the available writing area, so a longer greeting may work better on a separate card. Confirm the cake’s current flavour and decoration separately; an inscription does not establish what ingredients are included.
