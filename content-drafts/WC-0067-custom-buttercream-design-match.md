## Make a custom buttercream design feel cohesive

Choose a clear visual priority before sending inspiration. It might be the palette, piped flowers, a border, or a short inscription. Tell White Cakes which one matters most, then let the remaining details support that feature. This makes the brief easier to interpret than a long list of decorations with equal priority.

Send one reference photo and explain what you want adapted. A reference may show a technique or colour that is not available, so ask the bakery to confirm the feasible design for the selected size. Request the total quote and confirm the wording before paying. Do not describe a photo as a White Cakes product unless it is an original image of the bakery's work.

Plan the serving count at the same time. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches. Actual portions vary, and detailed decoration does not guarantee a particular yield. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use approved photos of real cakes to demonstrate a specific design. If no distinct example exists, merge these choices into the existing design guide rather than making an occasion-free duplicate page.
