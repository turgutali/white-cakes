## How to match the design of corporate cake in Cebu

Choose one visual direction that connects to the occasion: a palette, motif or meaningful detail. A cake does not need to repeat every element in the room; a restrained design often reads more clearly in photographs and keeps the celebrant as the focus.

White Cakes makes custom work with hand-piped buttercream rather than printed sheets or fondant sculpture. Share one overall reference and prioritize the details that matter; confirm what can be adapted to the selected size. For a corporate cake, send the approved brand colour and exact wording, and confirm who can authorize logo use. Keep the delivery contact reachable at the office or venue; do not assume reception can accept a cake without prior arrangement.
