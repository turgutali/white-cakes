## What to write on First Communion cake in Cebu

Keep the message short, legible and correctly spelled. Send the exact text, capitalization and punctuation in writing; compare names and dates with the invitation or programme before approving the final order.

Ask whether the wording fits the selected cake size and hand-piped finish. If several names or a long phrase matter, decide early what can be shortened so the lettering remains readable in person and in photographs. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
