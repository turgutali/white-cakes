## Use references to clarify what you want adapted

When requesting Signature Marble, send a reference only if it helps explain a preference beyond the product’s current description. The listed design is a 6-inch ivory cake with dusty blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose; verify that these details and the product are still current before ordering. If you want a different colour or message, point to the exact change rather than sending several unrelated designs.

Tell the bakery which parts are essential and which are inspiration. Ask whether the requested adjustments fit the product, size, and date, and confirm the final quote before paying. A reference photo should not be treated as a promise of an exact reproduction.

Keep reference-photo advice in the shared custom brief guide, with the live product page as the authority for Signature Marble’s current appearance.
