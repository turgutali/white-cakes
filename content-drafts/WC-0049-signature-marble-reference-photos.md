## English

# Which reference photos help when ordering Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Use a full view of Signature Marble to show the overall 6-inch layout, then a close-up only for a detail you want to discuss. The listed cake has ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. State whether you want it as shown or a custom variation.

Do not let a reference silently add scope; ask for the bakery’s written confirmation.

## Cebuano

Ayaw tugoti nga madugangan ang scope nga wala maklaro; pangayo og sinulat nga kumpirmasyon.
