## Base the cake size on the reception, not just the ceremony attendance

Some people who attend a First Communion ceremony may not join the reception. Estimate how many guests will be present when cake is served and whether a meal or other desserts will come first. Share that number and your preferred slice size with the bakery, then ask it to confirm an appropriate cake size.

Current guide estimates are 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate and may not cover a large reception. Confirm actual servings and current product choices with the bakery.
