## Ask for a christening-cake quote with the full request

Include the event date, cake size, flavour, design, inscription, serving estimate, and delivery or pickup venue. A simple finish and a custom religious motif may have different design requirements. Ask White Cakes for the total price for the actual request rather than assuming a reference photo matches a listed cake.

Current custom-menu prices are time-limited: a 6-inch cake is ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799; an 8-inch cake is ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. Verify the live menu before using these amounts. Confirm whether custom decoration or delivery changes the total.

Ask the bakery to confirm the serving estimate. Current ranges are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the delivery fee and window. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design and terms are settled.

Keep current rates in one price guide and update date-sensitive amounts when the menu changes.
