## What to ask about the price of christening cake in Cebu

Request a written quote for the exact date, size, flavour, piping detail, inscription and delivery address. A reference image by itself cannot establish the price because scale and hand-piped detail affect the work; ask what the quoted amount includes.

Prices and availability can change. The listed launch prices for Signature Marble sizes are date-limited through 15 January 2027 and do not set the price for every custom design. Confirm current pricing directly before announcing a budget. For a christening, confirm the child’s name and event date from the family’s final invitation, and ask the venue about the cake handoff point and service time. Keep any religious motif within the family’s requested direction.
