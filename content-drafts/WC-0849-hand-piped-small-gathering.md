## Choose a cake size that gives the piped detail enough room

For a small gathering, estimate how many people will share cake and whether modest slices are enough. Tell the bakery about the group and ask which size suits the plan. Then describe the hand-piped feature you want—such as lettering, a border, or a flower—and confirm that it can fit the chosen surface.

The 4-inch Bento is described as about two generous servings or three modest ones. Check current availability and ask whether the requested decoration is suitable for that size. Confirm the total price, message, and handoff details before booking.

Keep portions in the central size guide and piping inspiration in the design article. Link the guides rather than repeating the same advice for each finish.
