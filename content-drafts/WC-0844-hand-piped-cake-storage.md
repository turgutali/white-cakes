## Keep a hand-piped cake away from heat before serving

White Cakes uses buttercream for hand-piped designs. Keep the cake out of direct sun and never leave it in a hot or parked car. If the cake will be held for several hours, the ordering guidance says to refrigerate it. Tell the person responsible for the cake where it will be kept and when it should be brought out.

If delivery is part of the order, choose a receiver who can take the box promptly and move it to a suitable place. Share the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile, and gate or lobby instructions. A delivery window is agreed with the bakery; someone should remain reachable during that period.

For a custom design, ask White Cakes whether the piped details and event timing work together. Include the date, size, flavour, serving time, and reference image in the enquiry. Do not rely on general online temperature advice or promise a safe holding duration that the bakery has not confirmed.

Keep storage guidance in the existing Cebu cake-care guide and design details in the custom brief page. The care instructions apply to buttercream cakes generally rather than each piping style.
