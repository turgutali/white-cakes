## Compare cake options by how you will keep them before serving

For a warm outdoor celebration, the most important comparison is not only the design; it is where the cake can be kept until dessert. White Cakes' cakes use buttercream finishes. Its handling guidance says to keep them out of direct sun and hot cars, and to refrigerate them if they will be held for several hours. If the venue has no cool indoor space, ask the bakery whether the requested plan is practical before placing the order.

The 4-inch Bento is compact and suits a small table; a larger custom cake may be more appropriate for a group. The Bouquet Cake has a floral piped finish, while Signature Marble has its established 6-inch design. Choose the size from the guest count and use the current serving guide. Ask for confirmation of the portion plan for the exact design.

When comparing options, include the event date, expected serving time, guest count, and venue access in your enquiry. A receiver should be available during the delivery window and able to move the cake out of the sun. Confirm the current quote and delivery charge before the 50% deposit.

Keep warm-weather handling in the shared cake-care guide and design/size choices in the existing comparison page. Do not promise an outdoor holding duration without bakery-specific confirmation.
