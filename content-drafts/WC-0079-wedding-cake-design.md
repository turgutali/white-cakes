## Choose a wedding-cake design that fits the venue and serving plan

Start with the venue palette and the style of the celebration. Decide whether the cake should blend into the table or act as a focal point, then choose a small set of visual priorities such as colour, floral detail, or a clean finish. Share one or two reference images and explain what you want adapted; do not expect an exact replica without the bakery's confirmation.

The cake's size and design should be discussed together. White Cakes' current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are planning ranges, not guarantees for a wedding. Tell the bakery whether there are other desserts and what slice size you expect, then ask it to confirm the configuration.

Wedding dates deserve early availability checks. Send the date, delivery venue, receiving contact, flavour, inscription, and design brief. Confirm the final quote and delivery plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use real, permission-cleared White Cakes photos for a standalone design example. Otherwise, merge the guidance into the current design guide.
