## Confirm the wedding-cake order and venue plan before paying

Check that White Cakes has confirmed the wedding date and production slot. Weddings should be discussed early; the made-to-order minimum is at least three days' notice, but design and venue coordination may need more time. A quote request is not a reservation.

Review the complete written order: size, flavour, agreed design, inscription, price, delivery venue, receiver, arrival window, and fee. Confirm the serving plan and ask the bakery to verify the size. Current estimates are 8–12 portions for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake; these are approximate ranges.

The current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Follow the payment instructions in the current quote and keep the receipt with the confirmation. Resolve questions about venue access, setup, or storage before paying.

Keep deposit guidance in one current ordering article and recheck all terms against the bakery's current process.
