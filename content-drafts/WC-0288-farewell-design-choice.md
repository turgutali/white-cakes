## Choose a farewell-cake design that reflects the teammate

Ask the team what the departing colleague enjoys or what they will remember together. One shared colour, a hobby motif, or a brief message can feel more personal than a generic farewell decoration. Keep the main idea simple enough to remain clear on the cake.

Send one reference image and explain which detail White Cakes should adapt. If the cake includes a company logo, get approval first. Confirm that the requested finish fits the selected size and ask for the final price. A reference is inspiration, not a guarantee of an exact copy.

Include the event date, number of colleagues who will eat cake, flavour, inscription, and delivery contact. Current size estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use original, permission-cleared photos for a specific farewell design example. Otherwise, merge the general ideas into the existing design guide.
