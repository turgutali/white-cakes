## Compare cakes by guest count, serving style, and the room available

For a housewarming, begin with how many guests are likely to eat cake and whether dessert will be served after a meal. A small cake may suit a close family gathering; a larger cake gives more portions when neighbours or friends are invited. Ask the bakery to confirm the size against the planned slice style rather than relying on diameter alone.

Next consider where the cake will sit. A compact table, outdoor setup, or shared buffet can affect the practical choice of shape and decorations. If the design includes a house illustration, address plaque, or colour theme, send a reference and identify the single feature that matters most.
