## Store an office milestone cake before the program

Corporate celebrations often have a gap between delivery and the program. Ask the office contact to prepare a level, cool place for the cake, away from direct sunlight, warm equipment, or a hot vehicle. If the cake will wait several hours, follow White Cakes' refrigeration instructions and make sure the receiver knows how to handle the box.

Confirm who will receive it and where the driver should go. Provide the building address, tower or entrance, recipient's mobile number, and visitor or loading directions. Check the delivery window and fee, and choose a colleague who can meet the driver rather than expecting the cake to be left unattended.

When ordering, include date, size, flavour, company message, serving estimate, and design. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm that the cake size fits the team and that the design is agreed. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the order.

Use only bakery-confirmed storage details in the central heat-care article. Avoid adding a company-specific holding time without evidence.
