## Choose a bridal-shower design that fits the gathering

Start with the event's scale and the table where the cake will be displayed. A small group might suit a compact Bento and a short message; a larger shower may need a 6-inch or 8-inch format based on the guest count. Use the current size guide for planning ranges and ask White Cakes to confirm portions for the selected design.

For a floral look, Bouquet Cake is decorated with hand-piped buttercream flowers and a wrapped finish. Signature Marble has its distinctive dusty-blue marbling, while a custom cake can follow a colour palette or reference image. Choose one design direction and explain which detail matters most. The reference guides the baker but does not guarantee an identical copy.

Send the date, flavour, exact inscription, size, and one reference photo with your enquiry. Ask for the current quote; Bouquet Cake scale and finish are quoted on request, while custom starting prices are listed on the product page. For delivery, include the venue address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number.

The bakery asks for at least three days' notice and confirms availability before the 50% deposit. Keep bridal-shower-specific design advice on its existing guide and use the shared product pages for current details.
