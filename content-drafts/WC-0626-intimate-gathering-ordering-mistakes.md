## Give a realistic guest estimate and a complete event brief

For an intimate gathering, do not choose a large cake just because the occasion feels important. Count the people who will eat dessert and tell the bakery whether other sweets are planned. Ask which available size matches the portions you want, then confirm the serving estimate directly.

Another avoidable problem is leaving the date, message, or handoff undecided. Share the event date, flavour, design reference, exact inscription, venue, and receiving contact together. Confirm the total price and booking process in writing. An inquiry alone does not secure a production slot.

Keep the portion question in the size guide and the booking checklist in the order guide. This small-event scenario is a useful example, not a reason for a duplicate standalone page.
