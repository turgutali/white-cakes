## Choose one clear detail that fits the small cake surface

The 4-inch Bento is a compact cake, so one short message, colour palette, or small focal decoration may be easier to read than a crowded theme. Decide which element matters most and share one reference with a note explaining what the bakery should adapt. Ask whether the requested detail fits the current Bento design and size.

Confirm the flavour, recipient’s name, date, price, and pickup or delivery plan separately. White Cakes describes the Bento as about two generous servings or three modest ones; verify current product details directly. Keep any allergy or ingredient question separate from the visual brief.

Add this small-surface guidance to the central design article and link to the current Bento listing. Use original photos only for real, approved cakes.

Panatilihing hiwalay ang anumang tanong sa allergy o sangkap sa visual brief.
