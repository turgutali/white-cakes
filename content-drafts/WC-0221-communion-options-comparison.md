## How to compare options for First Communion cake in Cebu

Compare options on the same criteria: servings, current total price, achievable finish and handoff logistics. Ask for one or two sizes that fit the guest count rather than comparing unrelated photographs or designs with different levels of detail.

Clarify whether the cake is meant to feed every guest or serve as a ceremonial centrepiece. That answer can change the useful size more than a decorative detail; confirm all options against the actual event plan. For First Communion, confirm the wording and visual symbols with the family rather than assuming what they want. Check the ceremony and reception schedule so delivery and the cake moment do not conflict with the service.
