## Compare the reception size and design before selecting a product

Estimate how many guests will be present at the reception and whether cake is served after a meal. Ask the bakery to match current sizes to the expected portions. The number attending the ceremony may not be the number joining dessert, so use the reception plan for sizing.

Compare design options based on the family’s preference, message, and table space. Choose one simple visual direction and ask which current flavour and decoration are available for the date. Confirm the total price and handoff details before ordering.

Update the existing comparison page with verified products and link to the main serving guide. Do not create a First Communion product comparison that repeats generic size and design choices.
