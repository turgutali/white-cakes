## English

# What to write on Signature Marble cake in Cebu

The listed Signature Marble has room for a name or a short line on top. Keep the wording concise and send exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation. Ask the bakery to confirm that the message fits without obscuring the marbling or rose.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and does not use edible printed logo sheets. Approve the final wording in writing.

## Cebuano

# Unsa ang isulat sa ibabaw sa Signature Marble cake sa Cebu

Adunay luna sa ibabaw sa listed Signature Marble para sa ngalan o mubo nga linya. Hupti nga mubo ug ipadala ang eksaktong spelling, capitalization ug punctuation. Kumpirmaha nga dili matabonan ang marbling o rose.

Kamot nga gipiping ang buttercream ug walay edible printed logo sheets. Aprubahan sa sinulat ang pulong.
