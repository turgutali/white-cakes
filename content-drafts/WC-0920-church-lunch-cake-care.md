## Keep the cake cool between church and the family lunch

If the cake is delivered before the meal, plan where it will wait. Use a level surface away from direct sunlight, a hot car, and warm kitchen equipment. If several hours will pass before serving, follow White Cakes' specific storage advice and arrange refrigerator space if the bakery recommends it.

Choose a family member to receive the cake and move it promptly to the planned location. If the lunch is at a church hall or function room, confirm that there is a cool, secure place for the box. Do not leave it unattended at the entrance or on an exposed table while guests arrive.

Ask the bakery whether the chosen design needs particular handling, especially if it has buttercream details. Avoid a generic room-temperature time limit; conditions vary. Confirm the delivery window, contact, and fee with the order. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep verified care instructions in one central heat guide and link from the church-lunch article.
