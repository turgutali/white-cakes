## Ask what a hand-piped custom cake quote includes

For an accurate quote, share the size, flavour, event date, design reference, inscription, and number of people who will eat cake. Point out which piped details are essential—such as flowers, borders, or lettering—and which can be simplified. The bakery needs to review the actual request; a reference photo alone does not set the price.

White Cakes' current listed custom prices are date-sensitive: 6-inch cakes are ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, then ₱1,799; 8-inch cakes are ₱1,999 through January 15, then ₱2,599. Verify the live menu before using these rates. Ask whether the requested hand-piped work changes the price and what the quote includes.

Current serving estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size with the bakery. Allow at least three days' notice and verify delivery fee and care instructions if the cake will travel. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep current prices in the central menu guide and update them when rates change; avoid a separate price page for each decorative technique.
