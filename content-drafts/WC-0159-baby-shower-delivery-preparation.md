## How to prepare for delivery of baby shower cake in Cebu

Share the complete venue address, map pin or landmark, event time and latest acceptable arrival. Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu are listed service areas, but the exact route, availability, delivery window and address-based charge need confirmation in the quote.

Name one reachable person who will meet the rider at the door. Include a working number and building-entry instructions; the cake should not be left with security or in a warm lobby. Coordinate a direct handoff to the person responsible for the cake table. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
