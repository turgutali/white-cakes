## Prepare the venue to receive a baby-shower cake

Choose a person who will accept the cake during the agreed delivery window. Give White Cakes the complete venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and instructions for the gate, lobby, or function room. Confirm that someone will be available; do not assume the driver can leave the cake unattended or locate a room without directions.

Plan where the cake will wait before serving. Keep it level and away from direct sunlight and heat. If it will arrive several hours before the shower begins, follow the bakery's storage guidance and arrange refrigerator space if recommended. Ask whether the specific decoration needs any care; do not guess a safe wait time.

Include the event date, size, flavour, short inscription, and delivery contact with the enquiry. Confirm availability, fee, and window. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; its 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep general handoff and storage instructions in the shared Cebu delivery guide. Add venue-specific rules only after they are confirmed.
