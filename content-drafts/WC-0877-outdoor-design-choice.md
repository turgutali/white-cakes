## Choose an outdoor cake design with the holding place in mind

Before selecting a detailed finish, decide where the cake will sit and when it will be served. A shaded, stable table or indoor display may be more practical than leaving the cake in direct sun. Tell White Cakes the venue conditions and ask which design is appropriate for the actual setting.

Choose one visual focal point and send one reference image. Ask what can be adapted and whether the requested finish affects the quote. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions; ask for care instructions for the specific cake rather than assuming it can stay outdoors for a set time.

Include the event date, flavour, size, serving estimate, inscription, and delivery plan. Confirm the receiver can move the cake to a shaded place. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice, and a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use original, approved photos for a specific outdoor design case. Otherwise, add the general advice to the existing design and heat-care guides.
