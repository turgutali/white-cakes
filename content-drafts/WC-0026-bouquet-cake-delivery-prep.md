## Prepare a Bouquet Cake delivery handoff

Choose someone who can receive the cake at the agreed time. Send the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or building instructions to White Cakes. Confirm the delivery area, window, and address-based fee. Do not assume the driver can leave a hand-piped cake unattended in a lobby or outside a home.

After delivery, place the box on a stable surface away from direct sun and heat. Bouquet Cake has hand-piped buttercream flowers, so plan where it will be displayed and how long it will wait before serving. If it will be held for several hours, follow the bakery's refrigeration guidance and ask whether the design has any specific handling needs.

Include the event date, flavour, design, and inscription in the order request. Confirm availability and the final quote; White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after the delivery details are settled.

Keep the verified handoff and care steps in the central delivery guide. Add product-specific handling details only after White Cakes confirms them.
