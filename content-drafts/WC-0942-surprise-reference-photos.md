## Pick a reference that communicates the look without revealing the surprise

Choose one image that shows the cake style and explain which feature matters: colour, message placement, border, or decoration. Avoid forwarding a photo or group chat that the recipient might see. If a reference includes a person or private event, share it only with permission.

Include the event date, size, flavour, serving estimate, exact inscription, and delivery plan. Name a private coordinator who can approve the design and receive the cake. Ask the bakery how the reference can be adapted and confirm the final quote in writing.

Add these tips to the shared custom-brief guide and link to order coordination advice. A separate case requires an actual reference-to-finished example with customer permission.
