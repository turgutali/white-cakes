## Estimate outdoor-event portions and plan for the heat

First count the people who will actually eat cake and decide whether other desserts are served. The current guide estimates 8–12 party portions for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate ranges; ask White Cakes to confirm a size for the event.

An outdoor setting does not change the serving estimate, but it affects the holding and display plan. Tell the bakery the venue, expected serving time, and whether refrigeration or indoor shade is available. Buttercream can soften in warm conditions, so ask about the specific cake rather than assuming it will hold outdoors for a fixed period.

Choose a receiver who can move the cake to a shaded, stable location after delivery. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Include the flavour, design, and inscription in the quote request; allow at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep serving counts in the central size guide and validated heat advice in the care guide; do not publish an outdoor-specific portion chart.
