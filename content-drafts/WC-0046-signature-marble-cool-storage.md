## Ask for handling instructions for the Signature Marble cake you ordered

Check the current product listing first, since its design and details may change. Ask White Cakes how to store that specific cake and tell the bakery when it is expected to arrive and when it will be served. The right care can depend on its filling, frosting, and decorations.

Arrange for someone to receive the cake promptly, keep the box level, and keep it away from direct sunlight. Follow the bakery’s product-specific advice about refrigeration and when to bring the cake out. If the event is delayed or the room is warm, ask the baker what to do.

Keep only confirmed instructions in the central cake-care guide and link to the live product page. Do not use a universal storage time.
