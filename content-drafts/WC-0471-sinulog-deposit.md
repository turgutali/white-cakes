## Confirm a Sinulog cake order before paying

Sinulog week can affect traffic, venue access, and delivery timing. Before you pay, confirm that White Cakes can take the date and that the delivery window and route are workable. Send the complete address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and any event or building access instructions. Do not assume that a normal delivery estimate applies during a city event.

Review the exact order: cake size, flavour, requested colours or festive details, inscription, price, and whether the bakery has confirmed the design. A reference image is a starting point, so ask which elements can actually be included. Check the delivery fee and who will receive the cake at the destination.

White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; for a major festival date, ask sooner. Its current process uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order. Keep the written confirmation and receipt together in case you need to refer back to the agreed time or design.

Festival-specific delivery claims should be added only after the bakery confirms actual route and schedule rules. The core deposit checklist belongs in the central ordering guide.
