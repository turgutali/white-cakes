## What to confirm before paying a deposit for Sinulog cake in Cebu

Before paying, check that the written order summary has the event date, size, flavour, design direction, exact inscription, total price and delivery or pickup arrangement. Correct spelling, address and timing while the details can still be reviewed.

The stated 50% deposit confirms an agreed order after the details and price are settled. Ask how the remaining balance is handled and what to do if timing changes; keep the confirmation and payment record together. For a Sinulog gathering, confirm venue access, road or event restrictions and the actual service time with the organizer before setting delivery expectations. Use a palette or motif the bakery confirms it can hand-pipe; do not assume a parade route or road is accessible.
