## Coordinate the office handoff and ask how long the cake can wait

Tell the bakery when the cake will arrive at the IT Park office and when the team plans to serve it. Ask for storage instructions for the specific cake, since its filling, frosting, and decoration affect care. Share whether the office has a suitable cool place and who can receive the delivery.

Keep the box level and away from direct sunlight while waiting. Follow the bakery’s product-specific guidance about refrigeration and serving time. If the meeting is delayed or the cake will sit out longer than planned, contact the bakery for advice rather than guessing.

Keep confirmed storage details in the central care guide and link to the office delivery checklist. Do not claim a standard safe duration for every cake.
