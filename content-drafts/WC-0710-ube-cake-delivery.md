## Prepare the handover for an ube-flavoured cake

Ube is one of the flavours listed for White Cakes custom cakes. The delivery plan is the same as for the bakery's other buttercream cakes: provide the complete address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instructions. White Cakes confirms the charge for the actual address and agrees a delivery window.

Choose someone who can meet the driver and move the cake to a suitable place. Keep it out of direct sun and hot cars; refrigerate it if it will be held for several hours before serving, following the bakery's guidance. If the cake will wait during a long programme, tell the bakery the planned serving time and ask whether the arrangement is practical.

When placing the order, state ube as the flavour and list any expected filling, topping, or design as a separate request. The flavour name alone does not confirm those details. Include the date, size, guest count, and exact inscription as well.

Keep the shared delivery facts in the Cebu delivery guide and flavour details on the ube page. A separate ube delivery post would repeat the same handover instructions unless a verified local route detail changes them.
