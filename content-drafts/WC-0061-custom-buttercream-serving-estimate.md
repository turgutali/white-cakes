## Estimate custom buttercream cake portions from size and serving style

The design does not determine the exact number of servings. Start with how many people will eat cake and whether there will be other desserts. White Cakes currently estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake.

Treat these figures as ranges, not guarantees. A group may serve small slices after a meal or generous portions as the main dessert. Tell the bakery your plan and ask it to confirm a size, especially if the guest count is near the upper end of a range.

If the custom buttercream design includes substantial piping, ask whether it affects the recommended layout or serving plan. Include the flavour, event date, inscription, and pickup or delivery plan. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice and uses a 50% deposit to confirm the agreed order.

Keep verified portions in the central size guide and custom decoration information in the design guide. Do not publish unverified yields for specific piping styles.
