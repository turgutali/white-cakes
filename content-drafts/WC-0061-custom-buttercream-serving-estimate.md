## English

# How to estimate servings for custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Estimate servings from expected cake eaters and slice style, then ask the bakery about the available custom size. The 6-inch Signature Marble is a design reference, not a fixed serving chart. An 8-inch or two-tier option may be possible depending on the date and brief.

Confirm serving assumptions with the bakery rather than estimating from the cake photo.
