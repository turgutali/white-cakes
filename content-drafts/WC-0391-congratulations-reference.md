## Send one congratulations-cake reference with a clear purpose

Choose a reference photo that shows the design detail you want the baker to understand: colour, lettering, border, or general mood. Then write one sentence explaining which element to keep. A collage of unrelated inspiration can pull the design in different directions, especially when the selected cake has a small surface.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream designs. A reference helps guide an interpretation, but it is not a guarantee of an exact copy or an edible print from a digital file. If the cake is for a school or office milestone, tell the bakery whether a name, team, or colour is the main priority. Keep the inscription short and send the exact wording with spelling checked.

Choose the size from the guest count using the current serving guide. Include the event date, flavour, and pickup or delivery preference. For delivery, provide the full venue address, landmark, a reachable receiver, and any access detail. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice and confirms the date, design, quote, and delivery charge before the 50% deposit.

Use this checklist in the existing custom-brief and cake-choice guides. A separate photo case should wait for a real congratulations cake and a documented choice that distinguishes it from other designs.

Dayon pagsulat og usa ka sentence nga nagpatin-aw kon unsang elemento ang huptan. Ang usa ka collage sa wala'y kalabutan nga inspirasyon makabira sa disenyo sa lain-laing mga direksyon, ilabi na kung ang pinili nga cake adunay gamay nga nawong.

Mga disenyo sa buttercream nga White Cakes nga hand-pipes. Ang usa ka pakisayran makatabang sa paggiya sa usa ka interpretasyon, apan kini dili usa ka garantiya sa usa ka eksaktong kopya o usa ka makaon nga pag-imprinta gikan sa usa ka digital file. Pilia ang gidak-on gikan sa ihap sa bisita gamit ang kasamtangang giya sa pagserbisyo. Ilakip ang petsa sa panghitabo, lami, ug gusto sa pagkuha o paghatud.
