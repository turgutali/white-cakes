## Plan a chocolate-cake order around the date you need it

Start by asking whether White Cakes has a production slot for your event date. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice. Contact the bakery earlier for a weekend, holiday, or custom design so there is time to confirm flavour, decoration, and delivery.

White Cakes currently lists Chocolate as a flavour. Tell the bakery the size, design, exact inscription, and number of people who will eat cake. Ask whether Chocolate is available for that configuration and whether the custom finish affects the price. A reference photo can guide the look, but the bakery must confirm the feasible design.

If delivery is needed, include the full Cebu address, landmark, receiving person's mobile number, and any access instructions. Confirm the delivery window and fee. Keep the written order confirmation with the agreed flavour, design, price, and handoff details. The 50% deposit confirms the order after those terms are settled.

Add flavour-specific timing to the shared Cebu order guide and recheck menu availability before publication; do not imply that Chocolate has a different lead time without confirmation.
