## Ask for a quote that reflects the custom buttercream work

Share the requested size, flavour, event date, design reference, inscription, and number of cake eaters. Identify whether you want a simple finish, piped flowers, a border, or several details. Ask White Cakes to confirm what it can make and what the quoted price includes; a photo alone does not establish the total.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16. The 8-inch price is ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 from January 16 onward. These amounts are time-limited; verify the live menu before publishing. Ask whether the chosen piping or custom colour changes the price.

Current serving estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm the size for your group. Include delivery address, receiver, and fee if needed. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep current prices in the central guide and update them when the menu changes.
