## Estimate portions for a small gathering without over-ordering

Count the people who will actually eat cake and decide whether they want full slices or small tastes. The 4-inch Bento is intended for about two generous servings or three modest portions. If there are a few more people, the 6-inch size is estimated at 8–12 party slices, but slice size changes the count.

For larger options, the current guide estimates 16–24 portions for 8 inches and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are not exact guarantees. Tell White Cakes whether cake is the only dessert and ask the bakery to confirm the right size for your event.

Share the date, flavour, design, and pickup or delivery plan in the enquiry. Confirm current availability and the serving estimate for the exact cake. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after all details are settled.

Keep portion ranges in one central guide and link to it from small-gathering articles; do not create several competing charts.
