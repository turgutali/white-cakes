## Base the cake size on the guests who will be there for the reveal

Estimate how many people will be present when the cake is cut, not everyone who may attend during the full day. Decide whether the cake is the main dessert or one of several and tell the bakery whether you want modest or party portions. Choose one organizer to provide the final headcount and approve the order.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; ask the bakery to confirm servings for the event. If the cake is a surprise, keep the recipient’s contact out of delivery instructions.

Keep size advice centralized and link to the order guide for the private handoff plan. Do not infer exact servings from the occasion name.
