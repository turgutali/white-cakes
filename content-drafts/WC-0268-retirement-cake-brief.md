## Make a retirement-cake brief personal and easy to execute

Begin with the retiree's name, event date, and the mood the team wants: formal, warm, or playful. Choose one short line for the cake and check the spelling before sending it. If the message is long, ask whether it fits the selected cake size or should be shortened.

For the design, share one reference photo and point out the detail you want the baker to interpret—colour, border, lettering, or a piped decoration. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and can adapt a brief within the chosen format. A reference is not a promise of exact duplication, and a digital logo is not printed as an edible sheet. Ask what can be recreated before approving the design.

Tell the bakery how many colleagues will eat cake and whether it will be served after a meal. Use the size guide to compare portions, then ask for confirmation for the actual cake. If delivery is to an office, provide the full address, landmark, building access instructions, and a named receiver's number.

The order needs at least three days' notice and is confirmed only after availability, design, price, and delivery details are agreed. Keep this practical section within the custom-brief and retirement guides.
