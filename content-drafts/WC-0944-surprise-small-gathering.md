## Plan portions for the guests who will be there at the reveal

Count how many people will actually share the cake when the surprise happens. If only a couple of relatives are present, a small cake may be sufficient; if a larger group joins later, ask the bakery which size covers the full plan. Tell the bakery whether you want modest slices or generous portions and whether other desserts are available.

The current guide describes a 4-inch Bento as about two generous or three modest servings. Confirm this estimate and availability for your chosen date. Keep the recipient’s phone number out of the delivery plan if that could reveal the surprise.

Add the portion guidance to the central size guide and link to the order guide for private coordination. Do not infer servings from the word “surprise.”
