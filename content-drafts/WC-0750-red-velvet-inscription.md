## Choose wording that stands out on a red velvet cake

Keep the message short enough to read at a glance. A name and a brief greeting usually leave more room for the cake's decoration than a full sentence. Decide whether the occasion, recipient's name, or date is most important and include only what you need. The bakery should confirm whether the wording fits the chosen design.

Send the final message exactly as it should appear, including spelling, capitalization, and punctuation. Do not send multiple alternate versions without labeling the preferred one. If a particular lettering style or colour matters, include one reference and ask whether it can be used on the requested cake.

Red Velvet appears on White Cakes' current flavour list; confirm it is available for the date and size. The 4-inch Bento is planned for two generous servings or three modest ones. For more people, share the cake-eater count and ask about a larger size. Allow at least three days for a made-to-order cake and confirm the quote, design, and handoff before the 50% deposit secures the order.

These are message-planning suggestions, not real customer orders. Add them to the existing inscription guide; a standalone gallery would need original, approved examples and a distinct reason to exist.
