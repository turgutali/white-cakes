## Choose a family-cake design for a Lapu-Lapu celebration

Begin with the gathering's setting and the recipient's taste. A family lunch may call for a simple design with one colour accent, while a milestone party may need a stronger focal detail. Choose the main idea first and keep the brief to a few clear points so the decoration feels cohesive.

Send one reference photo and describe what you want adapted. Confirm whether the finish can be made for the selected size and ask for the total price. Avoid assuming the bakery can reproduce copyrighted characters, company logos, or another cake design exactly. If the family wants a short inscription, provide the exact spelling and ask whether it fits.

For a Lapu-Lapu celebration, include the date, number of cake eaters, flavour, and delivery location. Confirm the receiving contact, address-based fee, and delivery window. Current size estimates are 8–12 portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; ask the bakery to match the size to the serving plan. Allow three days' notice and confirm the agreed order with the 50% deposit.

Use original approved photos for a local design feature. Otherwise, add only the general advice to the existing design guide.
