## Coordinate the handoff so the finished decoration arrives at the right place

Send the full event address, preferred handoff window, recipient’s name and phone number, and access instructions. Ask whether the bakery can deliver to that location and confirm the timing and fee. If a particular person will set up the event, make sure they can receive the cake and follow the bakery’s care instructions.

Tell the baker if the cake will be outdoors, in a warm room, or waiting before service. Ask how to handle the specific hand-piped design and avoid moving or tilting the box after handoff. Confirm the design details and inscription in writing before the event date.
