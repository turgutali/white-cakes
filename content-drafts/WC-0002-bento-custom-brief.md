## Keep the brief concise so the small design remains clear

For a 4-inch Bento, include the event date, recipient, flavour, exact short message, and one main design idea. Share a reference image only if it clarifies a colour, border, or decoration, and explain which part matters. Ask the bakery what can fit the compact cake surface.

White Cakes describes the Bento as about two generous servings or three modest ones; confirm the live product details and availability. State whether you need pickup or delivery and give the receiving contact. Ask for the current quote and confirm any custom changes before paying.

Keep this checklist in the central custom brief guide and link to the current Bento page. Do not promise that every reference can fit a 4-inch design.
