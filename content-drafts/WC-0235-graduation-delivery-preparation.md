## How to prepare for delivery of graduation cake in Cebu

Share the complete venue address, map pin or landmark, event time and latest acceptable arrival. Cebu City, Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu are listed service areas, but the exact route, availability, delivery window and address-based charge need confirmation in the quote.

Name one reachable person who will meet the rider at the door. Include a working number and building-entry instructions; the cake should not be left with security or in a warm lobby. Coordinate a direct handoff to the person responsible for the cake table. For graduation, check the graduate’s correct name, school spelling and ceremony date before approving lettering. If the cake travels to a venue after the ceremony, include the handoff location and time in the delivery plan.
