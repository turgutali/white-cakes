## Choose one clear design reference for a small gathering

When a cake will be shared by a small group, the design has limited space. A single reference photo is more useful than a collage of different styles. Tell the baker what to take from it—the colour, border, flower piping, or lettering—and what can be adapted. This helps keep a small top readable instead of trying to fit every detail from the image.

Then choose the size based on the people who will actually have cake. White Cakes' 4-inch Bento is intended for two generous or three modest servings. A 6-inch cake is a different shared format, with a planning estimate of about 8–12 party slices. Those estimates vary with the cut and the design, so ask the bakery to confirm the best fit for your guest count.

Include the date, flavour, exact inscription, and pickup or delivery choice. If the cake is delivered, send the full address, a landmark, and the receiver's mobile number. At least three days' notice is requested, and the bakery needs to confirm availability, design, and price before the order is reserved.

This is useful guidance for the custom-cake brief and size pages. A separate photo-led article should wait for a real reference-to-finished-cake example that shows a decision specific to an intimate gathering.
