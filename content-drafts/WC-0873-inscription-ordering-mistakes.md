## Avoid inscription mistakes on a custom cake

The easiest problem to prevent is sending the wording in fragments. Write the final inscription in one message, with the exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation. Mark it clearly as the version to use. Names, dates, and short phrases are easy to misread when several alternatives arrive across different chats.

Do not assume a long message will fit every cake. The available surface depends on size and decoration. Ask White Cakes whether the wording is readable on the chosen design, and shorten it if the bakery recommends doing so. If you want a particular lettering style, provide one reference and confirm what can be adapted.

Include the inscription together with the date, flavour, size, reference photo, and delivery or pickup plan. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes. Confirm the final design and price before the 50% deposit secures the agreed order. Check the written confirmation once more so it matches the text you approved.
