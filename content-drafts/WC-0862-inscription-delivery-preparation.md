## Confirm the delivery contact and inscription before the cake leaves the bakery

Provide the exact address, preferred handoff window, receiver’s name and phone number, and any gate or building instructions. Ask whether the bakery can deliver to that location and confirm the current timing and fee. If the cake is a surprise, tell the bakery which contact it may call.

Before the delivery day, double-check the inscription spelling and the final design summary. The receiver should be available to take the cake directly, keep the box level, and follow the bakery’s care instructions. If the cake will wait before serving or the venue is warm, mention this when requesting handling advice.

Keep these steps in the central delivery guide and link to the message guide. Do not promise delivery coverage or a specific time without confirmation.
