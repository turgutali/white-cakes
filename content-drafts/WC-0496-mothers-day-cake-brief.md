## A Mother's Day brief that gives the baker clear choices

Start with the date, the number of people who will share the cake, and whether the cake is a home-table gift or an office delivery. Then choose a size and one visual direction. White Cakes offers a 4-inch Bento, a 6-inch Signature Marble, a flower-finished Bouquet Cake, and custom buttercream designs. Each option has a different appearance and scale, so the brief should say what the gift needs to do, not simply ask for “something special.”

If you want a custom design, send one reference photo and identify the element that matters—perhaps a colour palette, piped flowers, a border, or a short message. White Cakes interprets references in hand-piped buttercream; it does not print an edible logo or promise an exact copy. Send the exact inscription with spelling checked. If you are unsure whether it will fit, ask before placing the deposit.

For delivery, provide the recipient's full address, a landmark, and a mobile number for the person receiving the cake. Choose a receiver who can meet the agreed window and keep the cake out of direct sun. A holiday date should be requested early; the stated minimum notice is three days, and the bakery must confirm availability before the date is reserved.

Ask for the current quote and delivery charge and wait for the order reply. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed cake. This seasonal brief is a useful subsection for the existing custom-cake brief and Mother's Day pages; do not create a second generic brief guide just for the occasion.
