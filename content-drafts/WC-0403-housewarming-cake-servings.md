## Estimate the cake size for a housewarming table

For a housewarming, count the people who will actually have cake and decide whether the slices should be full party portions or small servings after lunch or dinner. The White Cakes size guide gives planning estimates: a 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly; a 6-inch round gives about 8–12 party slices; an 8-inch round about 16–24; and a common 6-inch-plus-8-inch two-tier cake roughly 30–36. These are general ranges, not guaranteed portions for every design.

The guest list may be larger than the group eating cake. Children, guests leaving early, or another dessert on the table can change the number of slices you need. Tell the bakery how many people are likely to share, when the cake will be served, and whether you expect leftovers. Ask them to confirm the most sensible size for that plan before you order.

If the gathering is small, a Bento may suit the table, but it is not intended to feed a large housewarming party. For more people, compare the 6-inch and 8-inch options using the serving guide. A two-tier cake should be confirmed against the actual tier dimensions and design, not selected from a photograph alone.

Provide the date, flavour, address, landmark, and a reachable receiver if delivery is needed. The confirmed size, quote, delivery charge, and availability should all be agreed before the deposit. Keep this event example inside the existing Cebu size guide so it shares the same serving assumptions.
