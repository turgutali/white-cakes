## Get a quote for a congratulations cake that matches the request

Include the event date, cake size, flavour, design, inscription, number of people who will eat cake, and delivery or pickup plan. A cake with simple lettering may have different requirements from a custom illustration or detailed finish. Ask White Cakes for the price of the actual design rather than assuming an inspiration photo matches a standard listing.

The current custom menu gives 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 and ₱1,799 from January 16. The 8-inch listed price is ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 afterward. These rates are time-sensitive and should be checked on the live menu before publishing. Confirm whether custom decoration or delivery adds to the final total.

Ask whether the size fits the group: 8–12 party slices are estimated for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Confirm date availability, at least three days' notice, and delivery details. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep current rates and quote factors in the central price guide, and update the figures whenever the menu changes.
