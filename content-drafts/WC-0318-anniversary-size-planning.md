## Plan anniversary-cake servings for the celebration

Decide how many people will actually eat cake and whether the event includes other desserts. For an anniversary dinner for two, a 4-inch Bento serves about two generous portions or three modest ones. If family or friends will join, share the cake-eater count with White Cakes and ask about a larger option.

The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake and 16–24 from an 8-inch cake. A 6+8-inch two-tier cake is estimated at 30–36 portions. These are approximate ranges; ask the bakery to confirm the size for the way you plan to serve it.

Include the date, flavour, design, inscription, and pickup or delivery plan. Confirm the date and quote before ordering. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes; the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after details are settled.
