## English

# How to plan the order timeline for wedding cake in Cebu

A wedding cake should be discussed as soon as the date and venue are set. Three days is the minimum for any cake, but wedding and multi-tier designs need more lead time. Share guest count, venue type, cake-cutting time, and whether it is indoor or outdoor.

White Cakes focuses on hand-piped buttercream for intimate weddings and will confirm honestly whether the bench can handle the date and scale.
