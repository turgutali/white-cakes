## Give a wedding cake enough time for design and delivery planning

Wedding dates should be checked with White Cakes as soon as the venue and schedule are set. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice, but a wedding cake often has more design and logistics to confirm. Do not wait until the final week to ask whether the date is available.

Prepare the serving plan, venue address, delivery window, flavour, size, and design references before the enquiry. Explain how many guests are expected to eat cake and whether there are other desserts. The current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from 6 inches, 16–24 from 8 inches, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are estimates, not wedding guarantees.

Confirm the design, final quote, delivery fee, person receiving the cake, and venue access instructions in writing. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after those details are settled. Ask the bakery what happens if the schedule or venue changes.

Keep wedding timing in the central order guide, with a link to the wedding page. Recheck all lead-time information against the current booking process.
