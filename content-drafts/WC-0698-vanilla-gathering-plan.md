## Plan a vanilla cake around your guest count

Before choosing the design, estimate how many people will actually eat cake. A family meal may have guests who prefer other desserts, while an office event may serve a small slice to everyone. Tell the bakery which situation applies and whether you want tasting portions or fuller party slices.

White Cakes lists Vanilla among its current flavours. The available cake sizes have approximate yields: 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are estimates, not an exact serving promise. Confirm which size is suitable for your group and the selected design.

Send the event date, flavour, desired size, short inscription, design reference, and delivery or pickup plan in one enquiry. Ask whether Vanilla is available in the configuration and get the confirmed quote. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep serving estimates in the single size guide and flavour availability in the current menu. A separate vanilla planning page would repeat those facts unless distinct demand and original evidence justify it.
