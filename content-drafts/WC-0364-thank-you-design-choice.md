## Match the design to the person or team being thanked

Decide whether the cake should thank one person, a family, or a group of colleagues. Choose one personal detail—such as a favourite colour, short message, or shared symbol—and explain why it matters. A focused design often reads more clearly than a list of decorations.

Share the date, size, flavour, exact wording, guest estimate, and delivery or pickup plan. Ask whether the chosen detail fits the cake dimensions and current options. If a workplace logo or photo is involved, confirm approval before use.

Add the guidance to the shared design page. A standalone case needs a real finished cake, original photo, and permission, with a distinct design lesson.
