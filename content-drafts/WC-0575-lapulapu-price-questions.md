## Ask for a complete quote for a Lapu-Lapu family cake

The price depends on what the family wants to order. Include the event date, number of cake eaters, preferred size, flavour, design references, and inscription so White Cakes can quote the actual scope. A custom finish can cost differently from a listed size, and delivery to Lapu-Lapu depends on the address and agreed arrangement.

The current custom menu lists 6-inch cakes at ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027, and ₱1,799 from January 16. For 8 inches, the listed amounts are ₱1,999 through January 15 and ₱2,599 from January 16. These are date-sensitive figures; check the current menu before relying on them. Ask whether the chosen design or delivery adds to the quote.

Also request confirmation of the serving estimate. White Cakes currently plans around 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches; serving size affects the yield. Provide the full Lapu-Lapu address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and delivery instructions, and confirm the fee and window. Allow at least three days for made-to-order cakes. A 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep local delivery scope and current prices together in the central pricing guide; do not create a new page for each city and family occasion.
