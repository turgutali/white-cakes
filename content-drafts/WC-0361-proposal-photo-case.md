## Proposal cake case-study framework

**This needs a real, approved White Cakes proposal cake before publication.** Verify that the order exists and that the finished cake photos belong to the business or are licensed for use. Get permission for the images and any identifying event details. A surprise story may be sensitive; anonymize it unless the customer explicitly approves identification.

Use the actual brief to explain one design decision: the colour, inscription placement, size, or a detail chosen for the recipient. Do not reveal the proposal location or the recipient's response unless the customer has approved it and the facts are documented. Never invent a romantic story around a product photo.

Use original full-cake and close-up images with accurate captions. Include current product and serving details only after rechecking the live listing. Link to the proposal, order, and size guides. The article must answer a distinct question that the existing design page does not cover.

If there is no verified case and permission, keep this framework unpublished and add general design advice to the existing guide.
