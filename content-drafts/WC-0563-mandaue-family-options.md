## Compare cake options for a Mandaue family celebration

Compare sizes using the number of relatives expected to eat cake. White Cakes' current estimates are 8–12 party portions for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. They are ranges, not exact yields. If there are several desserts or the family prefers generous slices, tell the bakery before choosing.

Next, compare design scope and flavour. The current flavour list includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet; confirm the choice is available for the requested date and configuration. For a custom design, send one reference and identify the element that matters most. Ask what can be made and whether the request changes the quoted price.

For a Mandaue delivery, provide the full address, landmark, recipient's mobile number, and receiving instructions. Check the delivery window and fee for that address. White Cakes needs at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes and confirms the agreed order with a 50% deposit.

Keep local pricing and options in the shared choice guide. Do not make a city-specific page that only restates the same size and flavour menu.
