## Compare baby-shower cake options by size, finish, and message

For a small baby shower, a 4-inch Bento may suit a compact table and a short message; White Cakes lists it for two generous or three modest servings. For a larger gathering, use the size guide to compare 6-inch, 8-inch, and common two-tier estimates, then ask the bakery to confirm portions for your cake design.

The finish changes the look. Bouquet Cake has hand-piped flowers, Signature Marble has a defined marbled buttercream design, and a fully custom cake can follow a colour palette and reference brief. Ask for the current price of the chosen format: custom starting prices are listed, while Bouquet Cake scale and finish are quoted on request. Do not assume a photo shows the exact size or price you will receive.

Send the event date, expected cake-eaters, flavour, inscription, and one reference image. If you want a particular colour, say whether it is a must-have or simply an inspiration. White Cakes uses hand-piped buttercream and confirms what can be achieved for the selected size.

If delivered, provide the venue address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and any gate or lobby instructions. The bakery requests at least three days' notice and confirms the date and delivery charge with the quote. Keep this options comparison on the baby-shower guide and link to the shared product and size pages.
