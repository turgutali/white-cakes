## How to compare options for baby shower cake in Cebu

Compare options on the same criteria: servings, current total price, achievable finish and handoff logistics. Ask for one or two sizes that fit the guest count rather than comparing unrelated photographs or designs with different levels of detail.

Clarify whether the cake is meant to feed every guest or serve as a ceremonial centrepiece. That answer can change the useful size more than a decorative detail; confirm all options against the actual event plan. For a baby shower, decide who is authorized to share the cake brief and receive delivery, especially if the event is a surprise. Keep the message and colour direction aligned with the host’s confirmed plan.
