## Ask for a current Bouquet Cake quote

When asking about the price, state the requested size, flavour, date, inscription, and whether the hand-piped flower design needs any changes. Bouquet Cake's decoration features buttercream flowers, but the final price and availability should come from the current product listing or a direct quote. Do not rely on an old screenshot or assume a custom variation has the same price.

Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and ask whether the size fits. If the listing does not show a serving estimate, request one rather than guessing from the photo. Confirm whether the chosen flavour is available for that cake and date, and ask whether delivery adds a fee for your address.

For delivery, provide the full address, landmark, receiving mobile number, and preferred window. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice. Confirm the agreed design, amount, and handoff details in writing; the 50% deposit secures the order.

Keep the current amount and serving details on the Bouquet Cake listing and central price guide. Recheck them whenever the product or menu changes.
