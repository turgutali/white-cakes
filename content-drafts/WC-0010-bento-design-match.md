## Keep a 4-inch Bento design simple and focused

The Bento's 4-inch surface is small, so choose one main design idea. A colour, a small motif, or short lettering can communicate the theme without crowding the cake. If you want a message, decide whether the words or decoration should take priority.

Send one clear reference and tell White Cakes which detail to adapt. Ask whether the requested design is feasible at this size and what the confirmed price includes. A reference photo is guidance, not a guarantee of an exact copy. Confirm spelling and ask whether the inscription fits the layout.

The 4-inch Bento serves about two generous or three modest portions. Include the event date, flavour, design, and delivery or pickup plan in the enquiry. White Cakes requires at least three days' notice for made-to-order cakes, and the 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Use original, approved White Cakes photos for a distinct Bento design case. Otherwise, add these small-surface principles to the central cake-design guide.
