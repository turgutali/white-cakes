## Keep the inscription readable and confirm the cake details separately

Ube is one of the flavours currently listed for White Cakes custom cakes. If you want an inscription, send the exact words, spelling, and capitalisation with your order request. The flavour selection and the message are separate instructions: do not assume the cake's outside colour or finish from the word “ube.” Ask the bakery to confirm any colour, filling, or topping you expect before the order is agreed.

Short wording is easier to fit on a cake top, especially on the 4-inch Bento, which has a compact writing area. A name and a brief greeting are more practical than a long quote. If you want several lines, send the text early and ask whether it will remain readable with the requested design.

Include your event date, size, guest count, and delivery or pickup preference in the same message. White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice; availability, final design, and quote should be confirmed before the 50% deposit. If delivery is needed, provide the full address, a landmark, and the receiver's mobile number.

The existing ube guide can link to the cake-message guide for inscription ideas. A standalone ube-message gallery would need original wording examples and evidence that the examples fit actual White Cakes cake sizes; simply changing the flavour in generic message advice would not add a separate answer.
