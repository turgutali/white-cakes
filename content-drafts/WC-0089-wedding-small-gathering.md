## Size a small wedding cake for the number of guests who will actually eat it

For an intimate wedding, estimate how many people will stay for dessert and whether slices will be served after a full meal. Ask the coordinator how the cake will be cut and whether other desserts are available. Share that plan with the bakery before choosing a size.

The current guide estimates about 8–12 party portions from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches; a 4-inch Bento is described as serving two generously or three modestly. These are approximate and may not fit every wedding plan. Have the bakery confirm portions and current product options for the date.

Keep the portion question in the central size guide and link to wedding design inspiration separately. Recheck current figures before publication rather than promising an exact guest count.
