## Coordinate bridal-shower cake details with the event styling

Use the invitation or table palette as a starting point, then decide on one feature the cake should emphasize. A short inscription, a floral finish, or one accent colour can connect the cake to the shower without copying every element of the decor. Ask the host which detail matters most.

Send White Cakes one reference image and describe what to adapt. Ask whether the requested piping, colour, and lettering are feasible for the chosen size and what the quote includes. A reference photo is not a guarantee of exact reproduction. If names or a date are included, send the final spelling and confirm the text fits the design.

Include the serving plan, flavour, and date. Current estimates are 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake; the bakery should confirm which size fits. Allow at least three days' notice and confirm the delivery or pickup details. The 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Use an original close-up only if it shows an actual White Cakes design, the technique is verified, and permission is recorded. Otherwise, add the advice to the existing design guide.
