## Choose one focal detail for a minimalist cake

A minimalist design works best when the brief has a clear priority. Decide whether the focus should be a quiet colour palette, a small piped border, a single flower, or short lettering. Then send one reference image and identify the part you want the baker to interpret. If the reference contains many details, say which ones to leave out so the finished cake stays simple.

White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream, and the finish must suit the selected size. Signature Marble is a defined 6-inch style with dusty-blue marbling, a shell border, and one rose. Fully custom cakes can use a different brief, but the exact design and quote need confirmation. A reference is not a promise of an identical copy.

Pick the size from the number of people who will eat cake and use the current serving guide to plan. Send the date, flavour, inscription, and pickup or delivery details. At least three days' notice is requested; the bakery confirms availability before the order is booked.

Keep this advice in the existing minimalist and cake-design guides. A photo-led article should only be added if it shows an approved minimalist cake and explains a design choice that adds information beyond the general guide.
