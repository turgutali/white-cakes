## English

# What to write on custom buttercream cake in Cebu

Buttercream lettering works best when the message is short and the surface remains clear. Choose one line, check spelling, and send exact capitalization and punctuation. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream and does not use edible printed logo sheets.

Ask how much wording fits the chosen size and approve the placement in writing.
