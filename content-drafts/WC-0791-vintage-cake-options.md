## Compare vintage-inspired cake options by the details you want

“Vintage” can describe different finishes, so identify the exact details you mean. You might be thinking of piped borders, layered colours, or a particular silhouette. Send one reference image and label the parts you want the bakery to adapt. Do not assume a reference photo represents a White Cakes product or that every decoration can be reproduced exactly.

Compare options using the same size and serving plan. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches. These are ranges, and slice size affects yield. The current custom prices are date-limited: 6-inch ₱1,399 through January 15, 2027 then ₱1,799; 8-inch ₱1,999 through January 15 then ₱2,599. Confirm current prices and any design-specific quote before ordering.

Ask whether the requested finish is feasible for the chosen size, confirm the flavour and date, and allow at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit secures the agreed order once the design, price, and handoff details are settled.

Keep the design comparison and current amounts in the maintained cake-choice and price guides. A separate vintage page needs distinct search evidence and original product examples.
