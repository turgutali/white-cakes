## Choose a children's birthday-cake flavour with the group in mind

If the birthday child has a favourite flavour, start there, then consider who else will share the cake. White Cakes' current menu includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Ask which options are available for the date and size rather than assuming the full list is available in every design.

For a mixed-age group, ask the parent what the child likes and whether guests have dietary needs. Do not guess at allergens or claim a cake is suitable for a restriction unless the bakery confirms the ingredients and preparation details. If a particular ingredient must be avoided, contact White Cakes directly before placing an order.

Match the size to the number of cake eaters. The 4-inch Bento serves two generously or three modestly; the current estimate for a 6-inch cake is 8–12 party slices. Actual portions depend on how the cake is cut. Include the date, flavour, design reference, short inscription, and delivery plan in one enquiry.

Allow at least three days for a made-to-order cake and confirm availability, design, and price before the 50% deposit. Keep flavour and food-safety details in the central guide and verify them with the bakery.
