## Choose a congratulations-cake flavour for the group

For a shared office or family cake, start with a flavour the recipient enjoys and consider whether the rest of the group has a preference. White Cakes' current menu includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm what is available for the requested date, design, and size rather than promising the full list.

If the recipient has a dietary restriction, ask the bakery directly about ingredients and preparation. Do not infer allergen safety, ingredient sourcing, or suitability from a flavour name. If White Cakes cannot confirm the requirement, avoid labeling the cake safe for that person.

Include the event date, cake size, design, inscription, and number of people expected to eat cake. Current estimates are 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. Ask which size fits the group. Allow at least three days' notice, confirm delivery details if needed, and use the 50% deposit to secure the agreed order.

Keep flavours and verified menu details in the central guide instead of splitting into event-specific flavour pages.
