## Estimate portions for the children and adults at the party

Count the guests likely to have cake at serving time, including siblings and adults. Decide whether the cake follows a meal and whether other desserts are available. Tell the bakery whether you want smaller dessert slices or larger portions and ask it to recommend a size.

White Cakes describes the 4-inch Bento as about two generous servings or three modest ones. Larger sizes have approximate party-slice ranges in the central size guide. Confirm the current estimates and the best option for the party rather than choosing from the age or theme alone.
