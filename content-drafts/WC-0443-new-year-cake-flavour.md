## Choose a New Year cake flavour from the current menu

White Cakes currently lists chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, and red velvet for custom cakes. Pick the flavour the people at your table enjoy, then tell the bakery the exact name. The flavour selection does not automatically include a separate filling, topping, fruit, or frosting; describe any such request as a separate question and wait for confirmation.

If the celebration includes a large family table, give the guest count and ask whether the chosen cake size suits the way you will serve dessert. A 4-inch Bento is listed for two generous or three modest servings; larger formats use the size guide's planning ranges. Do not assume that every guest will take cake or that a particular size will serve a fixed number regardless of the cut.

Holiday availability should be confirmed early. Send the date, flavour, size, design, message, and delivery details together. The bakery requests at least three days' notice, but New Year's Eve is a busy date; contact them earlier and wait for a production-slot confirmation. Ask for the current price and address-based delivery charge before paying the 50% deposit.

Keep the holiday context on the New Year guide and the menu facts in the shared flavour page. A New Year-by-flavour article would repeat the same five choices unless it adds original, confirmed tasting information.
