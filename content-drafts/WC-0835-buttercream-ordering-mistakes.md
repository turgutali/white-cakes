## Four avoidable gaps in a custom buttercream order

**Giving a date without a time context.** Send the date you need the cake and explain when it will be served. White Cakes requests at least three days' notice, but availability must still be confirmed for that date.

**Sending a folder of conflicting designs.** Choose one reference and identify the colour, piping, or lettering detail you want the baker to interpret. White Cakes hand-pipes buttercream; a photo is guidance, not a guarantee of an identical copy or an edible print from a logo file.

**Leaving the size and guest count out.** Tell the bakery how many people will eat cake and whether you expect party slices or smaller portions after a meal. Use the current size guide and ask for a recommendation; do not assume that a 4-inch Bento can serve a large group.

**Treating delivery as complete with only a city name.** Send the full address, landmark, receiver's mobile number, and gate or lobby instructions. Confirm the address-based charge and delivery window with the quote.

Once these details are clear, review the flavour, message spelling, total price, and payment terms. The order is confirmed by the agreed details and the 50% deposit. Add this short checklist to the existing order and custom-brief guides instead of publishing a standalone page that repeats them.
