## Give a Mother's Day cake order time to be confirmed

White Cakes asks for at least three days' notice for a made-to-order cake. Mother's Day is a popular gift date, so send the enquiry earlier and ask whether the requested date is still available. Do not treat an online enquiry or a message that has not received a reply as an order confirmation.

Include the cake style, size, flavour, message, event date, and whether you need pickup or delivery. If it is a gift delivery, provide the recipient's full address, landmark, and mobile number for the person accepting it. Confirm who will be at home or the venue during the delivery window, then share that time with them if needed.

For design, one reference photo plus a clear colour preference is easier to interpret than several conflicting examples. Send the exact inscription and ask whether it fits the chosen size. Request the current quote and any address-based delivery charge before paying the 50% deposit.

Once White Cakes confirms availability, price, design, and logistics, the deposit confirms the order. Keep the general lead-time steps in the shared Cebu ordering guide and the holiday gift context on the Mother's Day page; a separate date-specific timeline page would repeat the same process.
