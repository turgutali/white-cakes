## Give the bakery a complete wedding-cake brief

Begin with the date, venue, time the cake is needed, expected number of cake eaters, and delivery contact. Include whether the cake is the only dessert and whether you want generous or smaller portions. White Cakes estimates 8–12 party slices for 6 inches, 16–24 for 8 inches, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake; these are ranges and must be matched to your serving plan.

Describe the design in priorities: colour palette, overall mood, essential decoration, and any detail you do not want. Send one or two references and say what to take from each. Confirm whether the requested finish is feasible for the size and what the quote includes. A reference image alone does not establish an exact design or price.

Include flavour preferences, inscription, venue access information, and the person who will receive the cake. Ask about delivery availability and the fee. Weddings should be discussed early; White Cakes' minimum notice for made-to-order cakes is three days. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order after design, price, and logistics are settled.
