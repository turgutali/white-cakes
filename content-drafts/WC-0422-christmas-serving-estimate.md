## Count likely cake eaters, not everyone on the invitation list

Holiday gatherings often include children, shared desserts, and guests who leave before cake is served. Estimate the number of people who will actually take a slice, then tell the bakery whether you expect party-size or modest portions. Mention other desserts too, since they can change how much cake is needed.

As a starting point, the current size guide lists about 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake, 16–24 for an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 for a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are estimates, not guaranteed servings; cutting style and slice size matter. Have the bakery confirm the best fit before paying a deposit.
