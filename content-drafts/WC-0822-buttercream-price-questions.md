## English

# What to ask about the price of buttercream cake in Cebu

Ask the quote to separate cake size, buttercream finish, piping detail, lettering, and delivery. “Buttercream cake” can describe very different amounts of handwork, so reference the exact approved design rather than expecting one standard price.

White Cakes’ published guidance says a 50% deposit follows agreement on final price and details; confirm current terms. Request a written total and a revised quote if decoration, size, or address changes.
