## Ask what the quote includes for the finish and requested decoration

When requesting a buttercream cake price, provide the date, size, flavour, serving estimate, and a description or reference for the decoration. Ask which buttercream finish is included in the quoted product and whether additional details or lettering change the amount. Confirm delivery or pickup costs separately.

Prices and options can change, so ask for a current total for the actual order rather than relying on an older social post. If you have a budget, mention it early and ask which available size or design level can fit. Confirm the final amount in writing before using the deposit process.
