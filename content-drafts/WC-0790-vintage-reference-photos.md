## Send vintage-cake references with clear priorities

Vintage-inspired cakes can use several different borders, colours, and lettering styles. Pick one photo that best communicates the overall direction, then point out one or two details you want to borrow. If you send extra references, explain which element each one illustrates. That gives the bakery a coherent brief instead of several competing designs.

Ask White Cakes to confirm which piping and finish details it can make for your requested size. A reference is not a guarantee of an exact copy, and some images may show techniques or decorations the bakery does not offer. Confirm the final interpretation and quote before paying. If you plan to share the finished cake online, use your own order photos and do not repost another baker's image without permission.

Include the event date, flavour, serving count, inscription, and pickup or delivery details. Current size estimates are 8–12 party slices for 6 inches and 16–24 for 8 inches. Allow at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.
