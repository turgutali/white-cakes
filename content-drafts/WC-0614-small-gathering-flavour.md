## Pick a cake flavour for a small group

For an intimate gathering, start with the preferences of the people who will actually share the cake. White Cakes' current flavour list includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Ask which flavours are available for the selected size and date; do not assume the full menu applies to every custom design.

If there are only two or three people, the 4-inch Bento may be a practical size: it serves about two generously or three modestly. For a few more guests, tell the bakery the number of cake eaters and ask whether a 6-inch cake is more appropriate. A small celebration does not automatically mean every guest wants only a taste.

If someone has a dietary restriction, ask White Cakes directly about ingredients and preparation before ordering. Do not infer that a flavour is allergen-safe or suitable for a diet based on its name. Confirm the serving plan, flavour, design, date, and pickup or delivery details; allow at least three days' notice. The 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep flavour and sizing details in their respective central guides so the information stays accurate.
