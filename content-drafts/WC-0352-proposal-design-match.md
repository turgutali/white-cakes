## Make the design personal while keeping the proposal message clear

Choose one detail that reflects the couple, such as a shared colour, place, or short phrase. Tell the bakery which element is essential and use a reference only to clarify the direction. A clean focal point helps the proposal message remain visible rather than getting lost in decoration.

Confirm the exact wording, size, flavour, date, budget, and discreet handoff plan. Ask how the design fits the cake surface and whether delivery to the proposal location is available. If a surprise is involved, use the organizer’s contact details.

Add general advice to the existing design guide. A standalone proposal portfolio story needs a real cake, original photography, and customer consent.
