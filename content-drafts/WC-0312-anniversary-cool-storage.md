## Ask the bakery how the cake should wait before the anniversary meal

Storage advice depends on the exact cake, including its filling, frosting, and decoration. Ask White Cakes how to care for the order and tell it when the cake will arrive and be served. Mention if the dinner is outdoors or the cake may wait in a warm room.

Arrange for someone to receive the cake directly, keep the box level, and follow the baker’s instructions about refrigeration and serving time. Do not leave it in a parked car or direct sunlight. If the dinner is delayed, check with the bakery before extending the storage plan.

Keep confirmed advice in the central Cebu cake-care page. Avoid universal timing claims for all anniversary cakes.
