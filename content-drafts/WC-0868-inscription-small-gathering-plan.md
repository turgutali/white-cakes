## Plan cake size and inscription together

Choose the size based on the people who will eat cake, then decide how much text needs to fit. For two or three servings, a 4-inch Bento is planned for two generous or three modest portions. A short phrase is easier to place on a compact design; a long message may call for a larger surface or simpler decoration.

For more guests, the current guide estimates 8–12 party slices for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. These are approximate. Tell White Cakes the serving plan and ask whether the message fits the recommended size. Provide the exact wording with spelling checked before the design is confirmed.

Include the event date, flavour, design reference, and pickup or delivery details. Ask what the final design will cost and confirm availability. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep portion estimates in the central sizing guide and link to message ideas rather than repeating the same chart on each inscription page.
