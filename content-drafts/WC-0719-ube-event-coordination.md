## Coordinate the delivery and flavour details in one order plan

First confirm that the bakery currently offers the requested ube cake for your date. Then provide the event address, handoff window, recipient’s name and phone number, and any gate or venue instructions. If the cake will travel to a separate gathering location, ask whether delivery is available and confirm the fee and timing directly.

Name someone to receive the cake and follow the bakery’s handling instructions. Ask how this specific cake should be stored before serving, particularly if it will wait in warm conditions. Confirm the size, inscription, design, and full quote in writing so the receiver knows what to expect.

Keep route details in the delivery guide and current availability in the flavour guide. Do not promise delivery coverage or stock without confirmation from the business.
