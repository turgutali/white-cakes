## Plan the Mandaue family cake from guest count to delivery

Count the family members who will eat cake and decide whether it will be served after a meal or as the main dessert. White Cakes' size guide estimates two generous or three modest portions from a 4-inch Bento, about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, and 16–24 from an 8-inch. A common two-tier setup is planned for roughly 30–36. These figures are ranges; the cut and design affect the result.

Choose the size with the bakery, then share the flavour, inscription, and one design reference. If the gathering is at home, provide the complete Mandaue address, nearby landmark, and a mobile number for the person receiving the cake. Include subdivision or gate instructions and confirm the address-based delivery charge.

White Cakes agrees a delivery window rather than an exact minute. Ask someone to be present and move the cake out of direct sun after handover. The bakery requests at least three days' notice and must confirm the date, size, and quote before the 50% deposit.

Keep the portion planning on the central size guide and local handover details on the Mandaue delivery page. A separate Mandaue family page should only be created if a documented venue or route detail changes the advice.
