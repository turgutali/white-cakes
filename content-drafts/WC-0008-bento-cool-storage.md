## Plan where the Bento cake will wait before you serve it

Ask the bakery for storage instructions for the specific 4-inch Bento cake you ordered. Its frosting, filling, and decoration affect how it should be handled, so do not assume every cake follows the same refrigeration rule. If you are carrying it to a gathering, keep the box level and out of direct sun while traveling.

Tell the bakery how long the cake may wait before serving and whether the location is air-conditioned or warm. Arrange for one person to receive it and place it according to the baker’s guidance. Avoid leaving the box in a parked vehicle or beside a hot window.

Add Bento-specific questions to the central Cebu heat and care guide. The article should give only handling directions confirmed by the bakery, not a universal safe-time promise.

Ang frosting, filling, at dekorasyon nito ay nakakaapekto sa kung paano ito dapat pangasiwaan, kaya huwag ipagpalagay na ang bawat cake ay sumusunod sa parehong panuntunan sa pagpapalamig.
