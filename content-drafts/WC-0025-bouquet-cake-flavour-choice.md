## Choose a Bouquet Cake flavour for the people sharing it

Start with the recipient's preference and the number of people who will eat cake. White Cakes' current flavour list includes Chocolate, Vanilla, Ube, Mango, and Red Velvet. Confirm which flavours are available for the requested Bouquet Cake and date; do not assume every flavour can be paired with every custom configuration.

If you are unsure what the recipient likes, consider whether the cake will be shared after a meal or serve as the main dessert. Ask White Cakes for current flavour details rather than inventing tasting notes or pairings. If a guest has an allergy or dietary restriction, contact the bakery directly about ingredients and preparation; do not infer safety from the flavour name.

The hand-piped buttercream flower design is the main visual feature, so choose a flavour and design combination the bakery confirms it can make. Include the date, size, short message, and delivery plan. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice, and the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Keep flavour information on the central menu and flavour guide. Link from Bouquet Cake details without duplicating the full menu.
