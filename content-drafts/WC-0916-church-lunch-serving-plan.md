## Estimate the cake portions for the people joining lunch

For lunch after church, consider who will stay for the meal and whether cake is served alongside other desserts. Tell the bakery how many people are likely to take a slice and whether you want modest or party portions. This gives a better size estimate than using the full ceremony attendance.

Current guidance lists about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch cake, 16–24 from an 8-inch cake, and 30–36 from a 6+8-inch two-tier cake. These are approximate; cutting style changes the number. Ask the bakery to confirm the right size for your actual plan.

Keep the serving information in the central sizing guide and link to delivery advice if the cake moves from church to lunch. Do not state an exact yield without confirmation.
