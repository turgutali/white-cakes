## English

# What to include in a custom brief for Signature Marble cake in Cebu

A Signature Marble brief should say whether you want the cake as listed or a custom variation. The listed design is 6-inch ivory with dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose. For a custom cake, send mood, palette, message, and one useful reference within hand-piped buttercream capabilities.

Confirm any change, flavour, date, and delivery in writing; printed sheets and fondant sculpture are not the stated approach.

## Cebuano
