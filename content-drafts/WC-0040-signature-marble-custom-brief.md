## Briefing a Signature Marble variation

Signature Marble has a defined look: a 6-inch ivory buttercream cake with dusty-blue marbling, a piped shell border, and one rose. If that is the style you want, name Signature Marble and say whether you are requesting the standard appearance or a change. A custom variation should be confirmed by the bakery and may have a different quote.

To make the request clear, send the date, flavour, size, guest count, inscription, and one reference photo if you want a variation. Identify one element to keep from the reference, such as colour placement or border style. Ask whether the new detail can be hand-piped on the 6-inch surface; the bakery does not promise an exact copy from a photograph.

Use the serving guide for the planning estimate of about 8–12 party slices from a 6-inch round, then ask the bakery to confirm whether it suits your group. The count varies with the cut and design.

The product page should keep the fixed Signature Marble description, while the custom-brief guide explains how to request changes. A separate page for every colour variation would repeat the same order steps unless a real finished cake and a distinct customer question support it.
