## Plan a Mango cake for a small Cebu gathering

For a small group, begin with the number of people who will actually eat cake. A 4-inch Bento is intended for two generous or three modest servings. If there are more guests, current estimates are 8–12 party portions for a 6-inch cake and 16–24 for an 8-inch cake. These are approximate; confirm the size with White Cakes based on the meal and slice style.

Mango appears on the current flavour list. Ask whether it is available for the date, size, and decoration you want. Do not assume a specific ingredient or filling from the flavour name; ask the bakery if you need that information. If a guest has an allergy, confirm ingredients and preparation directly.

Send the event date, serving plan, flavour, short inscription, and one design reference. Confirm the final quote and whether pickup or delivery is available. For delivery, provide the full address, landmark, and receiver's mobile number. Made-to-order cakes need at least three days' notice; a 50% deposit confirms the agreed order.

Keep portion counts in the shared size guide and flavour availability in the menu.
