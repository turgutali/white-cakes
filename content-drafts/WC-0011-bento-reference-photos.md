## Pick a reference that works on the small Bento surface

The 4-inch Bento is a compact cake, so select one photo with a simple focal idea. A colour, small motif, or short lettering style is more useful than a reference full of fine details that may not fit. Tell White Cakes exactly which part you want to adapt.

Send one image and note what can change. A reference photo is guidance, not a promise of an exact copy. Ask whether the decoration will fit the available space and what it costs. If you want an inscription, provide the exact words and ask the bakery to confirm they will remain readable.

The Bento serves about two generous portions or three modest ones. Include the date, flavour, message, and delivery or pickup plan with the request. Made-to-order cakes require at least three days' notice; confirm availability and the final quote before the 50% deposit secures the agreed order.

Use a real, approved White Cakes before-and-after photo only if one exists and adds a distinct lesson. Otherwise, keep these small-surface tips in the central custom-brief guide.
