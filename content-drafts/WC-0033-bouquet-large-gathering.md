## Confirm the Bouquet Cake’s portion plan before choosing it for a large group

For a large gathering, estimate how many people will actually eat cake and whether the cake is the only dessert. Check the current Bouquet Cake listing for its size and product details, then ask the bakery how many portions it can provide. Do not infer servings from the floral appearance or an online photo.

If the cake will be a centerpiece, decide whether you need a second cake or another serving option. Confirm the flavour, current availability, total price, date, and handoff plan. Because the product is described as hand-piped buttercream flowers, request handling advice for the exact order.

Keep verified serving information in the central size guide and current details on the product page. Confirm with the bakery before saying the product suits a specific guest count.

Para sa isang malaking pagtitipon, tantiyahin kung gaano karaming tao ang aktwal na kakain ng cake at kung ang cake ay ang tanging dessert. Tingnan ang kasalukuyang listahan ng Bouquet Cake para sa laki at mga detalye ng produkto nito, pagkatapos ay tanungin ang panaderya kung ilang bahagi ang maibibigay nito.
