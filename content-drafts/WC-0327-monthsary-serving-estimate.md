## Choose a size that fits the number of people celebrating

A monthsary cake may be shared by two people or by a larger family group. Decide who will actually eat cake and whether you want generous portions or a smaller dessert after a meal. Tell the bakery your estimate and ask which current size is a practical match.

White Cakes describes the 4-inch Bento as about two generous portions or three modest ones. For larger gatherings, the current guide estimates 8–12 party slices from 6 inches and 16–24 from 8 inches. These are approximate and depend on cutting style, so confirm them before ordering.

Ang isang monthsary cake ay maaaring pagsaluhan ng dalawang tao o ng mas malaking grupo ng pamilya.
