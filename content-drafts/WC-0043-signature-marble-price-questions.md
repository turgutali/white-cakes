## English

# What to ask about the price of Signature Marble cake in Cebu

Signature Marble is a defined 6-inch cake; ask whether the quote includes the listed ivory buttercream, dusty-blue marbling, shell border, and one rose. Confirm any requested change, delivery fee, and current starting price rather than relying on an old screenshot.

The bakery confirms the final design price before a 50% deposit. Ask for the complete quote in writing.
