About The White Cakes - Cebu

A made-to-order cloud kitchen in Lahug. No storefront, no walk-ins, no published street address.

The White Cakes - Cebu, also called White Cakes and The White Cakes, bakes buttercream cakes for a date you name. The kitchen is in Lahug, Cebu. Orders are confirmed before anyone collects a cake, and delivery runs to Cebu City, Mandaue City, and Lapu-Lapu City.

How to order

Use the order form or message us on WhatsApp. Send the design, the flavour, the date, the words for the top, and whether you want arranged pickup or delivery. For delivery, include a full address, a landmark, and the name of the person who will meet the cake. Launch price — until January 15, 2027. The struck-through amount on the menu is the regular price from January 16, 2027. Bouquet cakes are price on request. A 50% deposit confirms the order.
