Corporate Cake Orders in Cebu

Client gifts, team milestones, and office celebrations — one invoice, one delivery window.

Offices in Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu order from us for client gifts, team milestones, and reception-desk celebrations — one finished cake or several bento boxes, piped by hand rather than pulled from a mall counter.

What we need on the order

Company name, contact mobile, delivery address with landmarks, lobby or gate instructions, date, and quantity if you are ordering more than one cake. Lead time remains at least three days per order unless we tell you otherwise in reply.

Design for desks and boardrooms

Bento sizes suit individual desks; Signature Marble or bouquet finishes suit a shared table. Logo colours can inspire a custom brief — send reference images. We pipe in buttercream; we do not print edible sheets from a file.

Billing

Payment method is confirmed upon order when we reply. Ask on WhatsApp if you need a quotation for procurement; we do not invent formal terms we have not agreed to.

Delivery windows

Traffic to IT Park, downtown, Mandaue, and Mactan offices moves in ranges. We agree a window in confirmation, not a minute-precise drop. Someone must be at reception to receive the cake.

Multiple cakes on one date

Several bento boxes for a team need the same lead time as one marble cake — each one is piped separately. Send a list with names or flavours if each desk is different; we confirm what the bench can accept.

Receiving at IT Park and BPO towers

Lobby passes and floor access should be arranged before the driver arrives. Leave a named contact who can meet the delivery; guards cannot hold buttercream in a warm guardhouse.

Client gifts and team days

A single bouquet or marble cake suits a client meeting and a shared pantry. A set of bento cakes suits named desks when each person should have their own box. Send the count, the date, and whether flavours differ. We confirm what one bench day can finish. We do not promise a same-morning reorder if the list grows after the cakes are already piped.

What a quotation means here

Bento cakes are ₱449 each until January 15, 2027 (regular price ₱599). Other launch prices are on the home menu. Bouquet cakes are price on request. If procurement needs a written quote, ask on WhatsApp with the company name, the date, and the cakes. A 50% deposit confirms the order. We do not invent billing terms or lead times we have not agreed in that reply. The practical note is corporate and office cakes in Cebu.
