Birthday Cakes in Cebu

Ivory buttercream and a name on top — baked for the actual day, not pulled from a case.

Birthday cakes from our Cebu kitchen are mixed and piped for the date you send us. The Signature Marble 6-inch is the usual choice when several people will share one table.

What to send us

Name, mobile number, cake design, flavor, and the words for the top — spelled exactly as you want them piped. We need at least three days before the party.

Smaller birthdays

For two guests or a quiet celebration, a bento cake travels more easily through Cebu traffic and still carries shells, a ribbon, and a rose.

Delivery

We deliver across Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu when someone can receive the cake at the door.
