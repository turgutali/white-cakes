Birthday Cakes in Cebu

Ivory buttercream and a name on top — baked for the actual day, not pulled from a case.

Birthday cakes from our Cebu kitchen are baked for the party date you name, not decorated ahead and stored. For a family table with several guests, Signature Marble at 6 inches is the usual choice. For two people or a desk, a bento carries shells, a ribbon, and one rose without the height of a centerpiece cake.

What to send

Name, mobile number, cake design, flavour, and the inscription — spelled exactly as it should appear on top. We need at least three days before the party. Same-day mall counters are not what we are; shoppers who need tomorrow should know that before they write.

Kids’ birthday cakes in Cebu

For a child’s themed party, send one clear reference and name the colours or simple details that matter most. We pipe by hand in buttercream; printed edible image sheets and fondant sculptures are not available. We review the idea and confirm what can be made before you pay a deposit. Our kids’ birthday cake guide shows how to brief a design, choose a size and plan the delivery.

Tell us the child’s exact name and age, whether candles need space, the guest count and where the party will be held. A 4-inch Bento is a small cake for two generous or three modest servings after a meal. Signature Marble is 6 inches for a family table; ask about an 8-inch or two-tier custom cake for a larger group. We confirm the size against your guest count rather than promise a fixed number of slices.

If the party is in a warm room or outdoors, plan a cool place for the cake before the candles. Our journal note on buttercream in Cebu heat covers the hand-off and storage.

Delivery across metro Cebu

We deliver to Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu when someone can receive the cake. Condos need lobby instructions; villages need a gate note, not only a barangay name.

How to start

Open the order form with Signature Marble or Bento pre-selected, or message us on WhatsApp with the date.

Candles and inscriptions

Say if you need space for candles or a long name on top. We pipe what you send — double-check spelling in the order form. For a milestone debut or 18th, tell us early if the reception is large; we may ask for more lead time.

After-school and office birthdays

Bento cakes suit office desks and classrooms where a tall cake is awkward. Signature Marble suits a home table where everyone is eating together. Either way, three days’ notice applies.

Choosing a size? Our Cebu cake size guide explains the verified Bento portions and how to request a recommendation for a larger guest list.

First birthdays and debuts

A first birthday and an 18th debut are not the same order. Tell us the venue, whether the cake is photographed before it is cut, and how many people will actually eat. Sinulog week and December weekends fill the bench earlier than an ordinary Saturday, so message sooner when your date sits on a holiday. The notes on first birthdays around Sinulog and debut cakes cover those calendars.

Flavour for a mixed table

Chocolate and vanilla stay calm when ages and tastes are mixed. Ube and mango suit a family that already asks for them. Red velvet is the cake someone requests by name. Name one flavour in the order. If you want the filling different from the crumb, ask, and we will say whether that date allows it.
