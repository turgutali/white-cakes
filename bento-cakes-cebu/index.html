Bento Cakes in Cebu

A small cake finished like a full celebration — shells, a ribbon, one rose.

A bento cake from The White Cakes is a 4-inch round, finished in ivory buttercream with piped shells, a blush ribbon, and one rose. We bake it in our Cebu cloud kitchen for the date you name — there is no walk-in counter and no cake waiting in a display case.

Price

Launch price — until January 15, 2027 Regular price ₱599 Launch price ₱449

The struck-through amount is the regular price from January 16, 2027. A 50% deposit confirms the order. The cake is perishable and made to order, so returns are not accepted. We deliver in Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu; order at least three days ahead.

Who it suits

Two people at one table, a desk celebration, a quiet birthday, or a gift you will carry yourself through traffic. In Cebu heat and bridge traffic, a low cake travels more safely than a tall tier. It fits on a passenger seat when the ride is flat and shaded.

Flavours and inscription

Choose chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet. Send the words for the top exactly as you want them piped — spelling included. If the message is long, tell us and we will say whether it fits a 4-inch top.

Lead time

We need at least three days before your date. The cake is mixed for that day, crumb-coated after it cools, then piped. Rush requests the day before do not fit that bench schedule.

Pickup and delivery

Pickup is arranged when we confirm your order. We deliver to Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu with a full address, landmark, and someone at the door. Read how buttercream behaves in Cebu heat before a long ride home.

How to start

Use the order form with “Bento” selected, or message us on WhatsApp with the date and flavour.

Servings

A 4-inch bento is sized for two generous slices, or three modest ones after a meal. It is not a centrepiece for a hall — if the guest list is a family table, see our Signature Marble or message us with the count.

Compared with catalogue bentos

Same-day mall and marketplace bentos are a different product: printed toppers, rush hours, and cases. We bake for your named date and pipe shells and a rose by hand. Shoppers who need a cake tonight should not start here; shoppers who need a finished small cake in three days should.

What to write in the message

Date, flavour, and the line for the top are enough to start. Add whether you will collect in Cebu City or need the cake taken to Mandaue or Lapu-Lapu, and a landmark if someone else opens the door. A long sentence will not fit a 4-inch top; we will say so rather than crowd the rose. We reply within 24 hours. The longer note is what a bento cake is.
