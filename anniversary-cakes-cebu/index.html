Anniversary Cakes in Cebu

Soft piped roses or calm ivory marble — made to order, not from a display shelf.

Anniversary cakes are often softer and more floral than a loud birthday tier. The Bouquet Cake — piped rosettes and a tissue-style wrap — suits an intimate dinner for two or a small family table. For a longer dinner with relatives, Signature Marble still carries a single rose and a calm name line without competing with the meal.

Timing the order

Order at least three days before the evening you celebrate. Buttercream is piped after the cake cools; an anniversary on a Friday night still needs a Tuesday message at the latest for our standard bench.

Gifting across Cebu

We can deliver to the recipient’s home or office in Cebu City, Mandaue, or Lapu-Lapu. Include who the cake is for, the line on top, and a mobile number for whoever meets the driver. Bouquet cakes are the design we suggest when the cake is a gift rather than a party centerpiece.

Flavours

Chocolate, vanilla, ube, mango, or red velvet — match the meal you have planned. If you are unsure, choosing a flavor is the short guide.

Surprise deliveries

If the recipient should not see the message before opening, put the line in the order notes and tell us who answers the door. We confirm the window by SMS to the contact you give — not to the recipient unless you ask.

Photos for reference

Pinterest and Instagram palettes are welcome. Buttercream cannot match every screen colour; we will adjust in reply if a requested blush or blue is outside what we can pipe on your date.

Dinner for two or a family table

A bento is enough when the anniversary is two plates and a quiet evening. A bouquet is the cake to send when it should arrive looking like flowers. Signature Marble holds a longer dinner where relatives share slices. Tell us the evening as a calendar date. We do not guess from “our anniversary” alone.

What to put in the order

Date, design, flavour, the line on top, and whether we deliver to a home or you collect in Cebu City. If the cake is a surprise at a condo or a restaurant, name the person who will meet the driver and a landmark they will recognise. We confirm a window, not a minute, and we reply within 24 hours. How you will pay is settled in that confirmation, not on this page.
