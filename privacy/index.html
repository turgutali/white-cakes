Privacy notice

The White Cakes - Cebu · Updated 17 November 2026

This notice explains what information we receive when you ask about or order a cake, why we use it, and how to contact us about it.

Information you may send

The order form may collect your name, mobile or WhatsApp number, optional email address, cake selection, flavour, size, message for the cake, requested date, pickup or delivery choice, delivery address, notes, and an optional inspiration photo. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to include in that message.

How we use it

We use these details to answer your enquiry, check availability, prepare a quote, confirm and fulfil your order, arrange pickup or delivery, and follow up about the order. We do not use order details for unrelated marketing.

Who processes the information

The website order form is sent through Formspree. Formspree receives and stores form submissions in the account used by the site and provides them to us for handling. Optional photo uploads are stored by Formspree and provided as a link with the submission. See Formspree’s privacy policy for its own processing, storage locations, and retention practices. Email, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook have their own privacy terms when you choose to contact us through those services.

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request those fonts when a page loads. We do not currently use an analytics or advertising pixel in the site code.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry and order information only while it is needed to respond, manage and complete the order, and maintain relevant business records. Formspree’s copy is subject to its current account plan and settings. You can email info@thewhitecakes.com to ask what information we hold, request a correction, or ask us to delete information that is no longer needed. We may retain records where they are needed for an order or required business record.

Under applicable privacy law, you may have rights to be informed, access or correct your information, object to certain processing, and request erasure or blocking subject to legal limits. You may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission about data-subject rights.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@thewhitecakes.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.
